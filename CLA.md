## Why this exists

Modbot is [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE) and is intended to outlive any one contributor.

**Clause 1** exists so the project's licensing stays coherent and can be changed if it ever needs to
be — a security-driven relicence, a compatibility problem with a future dependency — without having
to track down and get agreement from every person who ever contributed. Projects that skip this
discover the problem years later, when it is effectively unsolvable.

**Clause 2** exists because your work stays yours. Nothing here takes your copyright, and nothing
stops you using your own contribution elsewhere.

Nothing in this agreement requires you to contribute your changes upstream. **No licence can compel
that**, and this document does not try to. We would much rather you did, and this is what lets us
accept it cleanly when you do.
