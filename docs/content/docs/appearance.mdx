title: Appearance
description: The light and dark themes, the three densities including VR, and how Modbot works on a phone.
---

Modbot's appearance is set per browser, not per account. Change it on your laptop and your phone
keeps its own setting. Nothing here is a permission and nothing here changes what anybody else
sees.

The controls are at the top right of every page: the density buttons and a button that swaps the
theme. On a phone they are at the foot of the **Menu**.

They are also in the command palette. Press **Ctrl + K** (or **⌘ K**) and type "theme" or
"density".

## Theme

**Dark** and **light**. Dark is the default.

## Density

Three settings, which change row heights, control sizes, text size and border weight together.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dense** | The default. The most rows on screen at once, for scanning a member list of tens of thousands. |
| **Comfy** | Taller rows and larger text. Easier over a long session. |
| **VR** | For the web app seen through a headset. |

VR is not "Comfy, but more". A headset shows your desktop through a virtual panel, and a laser
pointer is far less precise than a mouse, so VR mode raises the size of everything you can click
past the point where you can hit it with a pointer, lifts text above the size where it turns to
mush, thickens the one-pixel borders that otherwise disappear entirely, and pulls the blacks and
whites back from the extremes, because both bloom on a headset panel.

Pick it when you are reading Modbot in VR, whichever theme you use.

## On a phone

The whole app works on a phone. There is no separate mobile version and no page you can reach on a
laptop that you cannot reach on a phone.

Three differences from a wide screen:

- **The sidebar becomes a sheet.** Tap **Menu** at the foot of the screen for the pages, your
  account, the theme and density controls, and sign out.
- **There is a bar at the foot**, not more controls at the top, so the things you use most are
  where your thumb already is: **Menu**, **Search** and **Actions**.
- **Tap targets are larger**, by the same reasoning as VR mode. Dense and Comfy are both raised to
  a size a fingertip can hit; VR keeps its own sizes, which are already larger.

### Actions

**Actions** lists everything the screen you are on can do, and each row does it when you tap it.
It is the same list as the keyboard shortcuts — on the audit log it holds *Open the person the
selected row is about*, which has no other control — so nothing in Modbot is reachable only from a
keyboard.

### Tables

A members row has a name, a Discord account, roles and two dates, and Modbot does not drop any of
them to make the row fit. The table scrolls sideways instead, and the **name column stays put**
while the rest of the row moves under it, so you always know whose row you are reading.

Tap a row to open the person, where everything in it is written out in full.

### Popups

A person, world or instance popup fills the whole screen on a phone, with the identity at the top
and the tabs under it. Everything else works as it does on a laptop — see
[Popups](/moderation/popups).
