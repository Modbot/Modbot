---
title: Roles and permissions
description: What a role is, the three built-in roles, and every permission in Modbot with what it lets a person do.
---

A **role** is a name for a set of permissions. Every staff account holds one or more roles, and
what a person may do is everything their roles allow, added together. There are no extra
permissions set on one person alone: if you want to know why somebody can do something, the
answer is always on the Roles page.

To give people roles, see [Staff accounts](/accounts/staff-accounts).

## The Roles page

Open **Settings**, then the **People and roles** tab, then **Roles**. You need **Manage roles** to see it, and
that permission alone is enough to open Settings — you do not also need **Change settings**, and
you will see only the tabs you may open.

<Callout type="info" title="It used to be in the sidebar">
Roles and Users were sidebar pages until September 2026. The old addresses, `/roles` and `/users`,
still open them, so an old link or bookmark still works.
</Callout>

Each role is a card showing its name, its one-line description, how many permissions it holds and
how many people hold it. Click a card to open it:

- **Name** and **One line about it** (the description).
- A checklist of every permission, grouped under **Reading**, **Evidence**, **Moderation** and
  **Administration**. Each has a short label and a line saying what it covers.
- **Save changes**, and for roles you made yourself, **Delete role** — press it again, in red, to
  confirm, or **Cancel** to back out.

Press **New role** to make one. Give it a name, tick its permissions and press **Create role**.

Rules:

- A role name can be up to 64 characters and must be unique (upper and lower case count as the
  same). A description can be up to 256 characters.
- **You can only put permissions into a role that you have yourself.** Boxes for permissions you do
  not hold are greyed out, and saving a role that contains one is refused, even if the role had it
  before. An administrator can tick anything.
- **A role somebody holds cannot be deleted.** Move those people to another role first. The button
  stays greyed out until nobody holds it.
- A change to a role takes effect for everyone holding it on their next click, not their next
  sign-in.
- Creating, changing and deleting roles are all recorded in the operational log, with the
  permissions before and after.

## Built-in roles

Three roles exist on every Modbot from the start. They cannot be deleted or renamed.

| Role | Holds | Can be changed |
|---|---|---|
| **Administrator** | **Administrator**: everything, including permissions added in future versions. | No. It always means everything. |
| **Moderator** | **See members**, **See profiles**, **See analytics**, **See the audit log**, **Kick**, **Ban**, **Unban**, **Warn**, **View evidence**, **Upload evidence**, **Pair a companion**. | Yes: its description and permissions. |
| **Viewer** | **See members**, **See profiles**, **See analytics**, **See the audit log**. | Yes: its description and permissions. |

Some permissions are deliberately not in Moderator or Viewer, because they reach further than
day-to-day moderation: **See live instances**, **See calendar**, **See giveaways**, **Use AI chat**, **Read Discord
messages**, **Manage Discord links**, **Write notes**, and the evidence and administration permissions not listed above. Grant them on
purpose, to a role of your own or by editing a built-in one.

## The order of roles

Roles are in a line, highest first, like roles in Discord. **Administrator** is always first. The
**Roles** page lists them in that order, and each card has **Move up** and **Move down** buttons
(the arrows at its right end, which work on a phone too). A role you make yourself starts at the
bottom.

A person's **rank** is their highest role. Somebody with no role is at the bottom. Rank follows the
order of the list, not what a role allows, so a role placed low can still hold permissions that
people ranked above it lack. Any role that includes **Administrator** counts as first, wherever it
is in the list.

**Manage users** and **Manage roles** only reach what is below your own highest role. The same rank
doesn't count as below. That is what stops somebody with **Manage users** from making a reset link
for an administrator and signing in as them, and it holds for [API keys](/api/keys) too: a key has
its owner's rank. Buttons for anything you can't change are greyed out. **Administrator** is above
the rule.

When Modbot was updated to have this order, it put the roles in a first one: Administrator, then
Moderator and every custom role, the one that allows the most permissions first, and Viewer last.
Change it on the Roles page if that isn't the order you want.

## Administrator covers everything

A person holding **Administrator** passes every permission check in Modbot, including checks for
permissions that are added in later versions of Modbot. You never need to tick anything else for
an administrator, and updating Modbot never leaves an administrator missing something new.

Modbot always keeps at least one enabled account holding Administrator. See
[the last administrator](/accounts/staff-accounts#the-last-administrator).

## Who can give what

The same rule applies everywhere a permission can be handed out:

- On the **Roles** page you can only put permissions you hold into a role.
- On the **Users** page you can only give people roles whose permissions you all hold, whether by
  invite link, temporary password or changing their roles. You can also only give or take roles
  that sit below your own highest role.
- An **API key** can only be given permissions you hold, and it can never do more than the account
  that made it can do right now. If you lose a permission, your keys lose it too. See
  [API keys](/api/keys).

So **Manage users** and **Manage roles** are never a way to become an administrator.

## Permissions are enforced by the server

The sidebar hides pages a person cannot open, but that is only for tidiness. Every request is
checked on the server, so hiding a link is never what keeps data safe. The same checks apply to the
[API](/api), to [live events over WebSocket](/api/events-websocket) and
[long polling](/api/long-polling), to [webhooks](/api/webhooks), to the tools [Chat](/ai/chat) uses
to answer questions, and to the Discord bot's commands.

The API refers to each permission by its name, shown below after the label (for example
`ViewAuditLog`).

## Reading

### See members

Name: `ViewMembers`

- Opens the **Members** view of the [People](/moderation/people) page (the group's member list) and the **Discord members** page. Without **See profiles**, that view is all of People it opens.
- Shows whether somebody is a member now, and with which roles, in the person popup.
- Lets [Chat](/ai/chat) look up group membership.

Seeing which Discord and VRChat accounts are linked, and filtering the member lists by link, also
needs **See profiles**.

### See profiles

Name: `ViewProfile`

- A VRChat user's profile, history and metrics in the person popup, and refreshing that profile
  from VRChat. See [Profiles](/moderation/profiles).
- The **People** page, which lists everyone Modbot has ever seen, members or not. See
  [People](/moderation/people).
- Reading [case files](/moderation/case-files): the **Cases** tab in the person popup and the case
  files shown on the **Bans** page.
- The **Flags** page, which lists what [AutoMod rules](/moderation/automod) flagged. See
  [Flags](/moderation/flags).
- [Repeat offenders](/moderation/reviews).
- Seeing a member's linked Discord or VRChat account, and their time in world and Discord activity in
  a person's popup.
- The Discord bot's `/lookup` command.

### See analytics

Name: `ViewAnalytics`

- The **Stats**, **VRChat** and **Discord** pages, and the VRChat page's **Instances** tab. See
  [Analytics](/analytics).
- The group's posts, on the **VRChat** page's **Posts** tab.
- The world and instance popups.
- [Insights](/ai/insights) on the **Stats** page.

Seeing who was in a past instance also needs **See the audit log**.

### See live instances

Name: `ViewLiveInstances` (`ViewLiveRooms`, its name before 2026-09-17, is still accepted by the API)

- The **Live** page: the group's open instances right now, how many people are in each, and who.
  See [Live](/moderation/live).
- Live join, leave and avatar events (where somebody is right now) in the
  [event stream](/api/events-websocket) and [webhooks](/api/webhooks). Those events also need
  **See the audit log**.

Not in the built-in Moderator or Viewer roles.

### See calendar

Name: `ViewCalendar`

- The **Calendar** page: planned events, each place an event is published (VRChat's calendar, a
  Discord event, a channel post), and whether that worked. See [Calendar](/moderation/calendar).
- With **See analytics** as well: what each event did, and **Past events**. See
  [What an event did](/moderation/calendar#what-an-event-did).

Not in the built-in Moderator or Viewer roles.

### See giveaways

Name: `ViewGiveaways`

- The **Giveaways** page: every giveaway, its rules, who entered, each draw, the seed, and the
  entrant list with every weight. See [Giveaways](/moderation/giveaways).

Separate from **See members** and **See analytics**: an entrant list is a list of named people with
a number beside each saying how much time they spend in your worlds.

Not in the built-in Moderator or Viewer roles.

### See the audit log

Name: `ViewAuditLog`

- The moderation half of the **Audit log** page: bans, kicks, role changes, joins and leaves,
  Discord moderation, case file changes and other moderation history. See
  [Audit log](/moderation/audit-log).
- The **Bans** page and the group's ban list. See [Members and bans](/moderation/members-and-bans).
- Who was in a past instance, in the instance popup.
- Moderation events in the [event stream](/api/events-websocket), [long polling](/api/long-polling)
  and [webhooks](/api/webhooks).
- The Discord bot's `/recent` command.

### Use AI chat

Name: `UseAiChat`

- Opens the **Chat** page and lets the person ask questions. See [Chat](/ai/chat).

Chat can only use what the person asking can already see: every tool it uses runs with that
person's own permissions, and a tool they lack the permission for is never offered to the model.
[Chat](/ai/chat#tools) lists every lookup and the permission it needs. It adds a way of asking, not
anything more to see. Using Chat sends group data to the AI provider set up in Settings, which is
why it is not in the built-in Moderator or Viewer roles.

Asking about a person through Chat is recorded in the audit log and in that person's own history,
the same as any other lookup.

### Use AI in excess of usage limits

Name: `UseAiPastLimits`

- Chat is not stopped by spend limits set on this person or on any of their roles.
- No AI feature is stopped by this person's [monthly allowance](/ai/limits-and-spend#monthly-ai-allowance-per-team-member).

The limit for everyone together, and each feature's own limit, still apply. See
[Limits and spend](/ai/limits-and-spend).

### Read Discord messages

Name: `ReadDiscordMessages`

- The **Messages** tab in a person's popup: the messages Modbot has stored from that member,
  deleted ones included. See [Discord messages and analytics](/discord/messages-and-analytics).

Not in the built-in roles. Analytics only count messages; this is everything the person wrote.

### See the operational log

Name: `ViewOperationalLog`

- The Modbot half of the **Audit log** page: sign-ins and failed sign-ins, account and role
  changes, settings changes, sync problems and similar. See [Audit log](/moderation/audit-log).
- The **Health** page, and the status rows at the bottom of the sidebar that open it. See
  [Checking a deployment](/self-hosting/health).
- The **Modbot's log** page: Modbot's own log lines, with their properties and exceptions. See
  [Logs](/self-hosting/logs).
- Reading the **Health alerts** card under **Settings**, **Email and alerts**. Changing it needs
  **Change settings**. See [Alerts by email](/self-hosting/alerts).
- Those Modbot events in the [event stream](/api/events-websocket), [long polling](/api/long-polling)
  and [webhooks](/api/webhooks).

The **Audit log** page appears in the sidebar for anyone holding **See the audit log** or **See the
operational log**, and shows only the half each person may see.

## Evidence

See [Evidence](/moderation/evidence) for how evidence works.

### View evidence

Name: `ViewEvidence`

- Opens the screenshots and video attached to a case file, and lists them on the case file.

Without it, a person who can read a case file sees the case file but not its evidence. It is also
needed to read the audit log's entries about evidence, which name the files.

### Upload evidence

Name: `UploadEvidence`

- Attaches screenshots and video to a case file, and takes them off it. In the web app both show on
  case files the person may also edit: ones they wrote, or any case file if they hold **Ban**.

Choosing where evidence is stored is a setting and needs **Change settings**, not this.

### Destroy evidence

Name: `DestroyEvidence`

- Permanently deletes the file behind a piece of evidence. The record that it existed stays: its
  size, type, who destroyed it, when and why. A reason is required, and there is no undo.
- Works from any case file, including a withdrawn one. A file that is still on another case file is
  not destroyed until it has been taken off there.

In the web app this is the **Destroy** button on a file in the case file's **Evidence** section.

## Moderation

### Kick

Name: `Kick`

Remove a person from the VRChat group, from the person's page or the **Members** list. Modbot asks
you to confirm, offers the group's [ban reasons](/moderation/case-files) and a note, and records the
kick only once VRChat has accepted it.

### Warn

Name: `Warn`

Reserved: nothing in Modbot uses this yet. It is in the built-in Moderator role.

### Ban

Name: `Ban`

- Banning a person from the VRChat group, from the person's page or the **Members** list. A reason
  is required, and the ban's [case file](/moderation/case-files) is written from the reason and the
  note.
- Writing a case file for a ban (the **Bans** page offers this for bans with no case file).
- Changing or withdrawing **any** case file. Without **Ban**, a person can only change or withdraw
  case files they wrote themselves.

### Unban

Name: `Unban`

Lift a person's ban, from the **Bans** page or the person's page. Separate from **Ban**, because
letting somebody back in is a different decision from keeping them out.

### Act on many at once

Name: `BulkAction`

Reserved: nothing in Modbot uses this yet.

### Review tickets

Name: `ReviewTickets`

- Opens the **Reviews** page and closes the reviews that open when a moderator's pattern looks
  unusual. See [Reviews and repeat offenders](/moderation/reviews).
- Dismisses flags on the **Flags** page.

Kept separate from the action permissions on purpose: the people being reviewed should not be the
people closing their reviews.

### Edit the reason list

Name: `EditClassifications`

- Adds, changes, reorders and turns off the ban reasons moderators pick from when they write a case
  file.

In the web app the reason list is under **Settings**, **Moderation**, and that tab needs
**Change settings**. A person with only this permission can use it through the
[API](/api/reference).

### Write notes

Name: `WriteNotes`

- Writes a note about a person, on their popup's **Notes** tab, and takes one back. See
  [Notes](/moderation/notes).

Reading notes is **See the audit log**, not this: a note is an entry in that log. Somebody can take
back a note they wrote themselves without holding this. Not in the built-in roles.

### Manage Discord links

Name: `ManageDiscordLinks`

- Unlinks a member's Discord and VRChat accounts. This also removes the Discord roles Modbot gave
  them. See [Account linking](/discord/account-linking).

Seeing a link needs only **See profiles**. Not in the built-in roles.

### Edit 18+ verified

Name: `EditAgeVerification`

- Sets or clears the 18+ verified mark on a VRChat user's profile by hand.

A sync can set the mark but never clears it, so clearing it is always a person's decision, and it is
recorded with their name.

### Pair a companion

Name: `PairCompanion`

- Opens the pairing page, `/pair`, and pairs the [companion](/companion/pairing) to this account.
  Without it the page says *Your account does not have "Pair a companion".*
- Opens **Settings**, **Paired companions**, with this person's own companions and a **Remove**
  button on each. Somebody holding **Manage users** sees everybody's there, with whose each one is,
  and can remove the companions of people whose highest role is below theirs.
- Keeps this person's companions working. It is checked on every request a companion makes, so
  taking it away from a person's roles stops their companions straight away, and giving it back
  starts them again.

In the built-in Moderator role. When it was added, it was also given to every role held by somebody
whose companion was working at the time, so no companion stopped on the update. Take it off a role
you do not want pairing.

## Administration

### Edit the group's profile

Name: `EditGroupProfile`

- Changes the group's **Languages**, **Links** and **About this group** in place on the **VRChat**
  page's **Overview**.
- Opens the **VRChat** page's **Settings** tab and changes everything on it: the group's name,
  description, rules, languages, links and who can join. See
  [Analytics](/analytics#settings).

Each change goes to VRChat as Modbot's VRChat account, which needs the matching group permission in
the group's roles on VRChat. Every change is written to the audit log with who made it.

Needs **See analytics** as well, to open the page.

Not in the built-in Moderator or Viewer roles.

### Manage group posts

Name: `ManageGroupPosts`

- Posts in the group on VRChat, changes a post and deletes one, from the **VRChat** page's
  **Posts** tab. See [Analytics](/analytics#posts).

Reading the posts needs only **See analytics**. Not in the built-in Moderator or Viewer roles.

### Manage group roles

Name: `ManageGroupRoles`

- Opens **Roles** under the **VRChat** page's **Settings** tab, and creates, changes and deletes the
  group's roles on VRChat: their names, descriptions and permissions. See
  [Analytics](/analytics#roles).

These are the group's roles on VRChat, not Modbot's own roles on this page. Modbot's VRChat account
needs **Manage Group Roles** in the group's roles on VRChat. Every change is written to the audit log
with who made it.

Not in the built-in Moderator or Viewer roles.

### Manage group invites

Name: `ManageGroupInvites`

- Opens the **VRChat** page's **Invites** tab, which lists the people the group has invited who have
  not answered yet, and cancels an invite. See [Analytics](/analytics#invites).

Modbot's VRChat account needs **Manage Group Invites** in the group's roles on VRChat. Not in the
built-in Moderator or Viewer roles.

### Manage the group gallery

Name: `ManageGroupGallery`

- Removes an image from one of the group's galleries on VRChat, from the **VRChat** page's
  **Gallery** tab. See [Analytics](/analytics#gallery).

Looking at the gallery needs only **See analytics**. Modbot's VRChat account needs **Manage Group
Galleries** in the group's roles on VRChat. Not in the built-in Moderator or Viewer roles.

### Manage calendar

Name: `ManageCalendar`

- Plan, change, cancel and delete events on the **Calendar** page. An event can publish to VRChat's
  calendar and Discord in the group's name and open a group instance by itself.
- See and replace the calendar feed link. See [Calendar](/moderation/calendar).

Needs **See calendar** as well, to open the page.

Not in the built-in Moderator or Viewer roles.

### Run giveaways

Name: `RunGiveaways`

- Create, change, open, close, draw and cancel giveaways on the **Giveaways** page. A giveaway posts
  in your group's name and a draw decides who gets something. See [Giveaways](/moderation/giveaways).

Needs **See giveaways** as well, to open the page.

Not in the built-in Moderator or Viewer roles.

### Manage users

Name: `ManageUsers`

- Opens **Settings**, **People and roles**, **Users**: add people by invite link or temporary password, take back invite
  links, change people's roles and contact details, make password reset links, and disable or enable
  accounts. See [Staff accounts](/accounts/staff-accounts).

A person with this only reaches accounts **below their own highest role** (see
[The order of roles](#the-order-of-roles)). They can't change, make a reset link for, disable,
enable or delete an account whose highest role is the same as theirs or higher, or remove its
companions, and they can only give or take roles that sit below their own highest role. They see
"You can only change accounts below your highest role." or "You can only change roles below your
highest role." An administrator can change anyone, including other administrators. You can still
change your own contact details, but you can't take your own highest role off yourself.

### Manage roles

Name: `ManageRoles`

- Opens **Settings**, **People and roles**, **Roles**: create roles, change what each allows, move
  roles up and down, and delete roles nobody holds.

A person with this only reaches roles **below their own highest role**: they can't edit, delete or
move a role that sits at their own place or above it. A role they make starts at the bottom.

Separate from **Manage users**: handing somebody a role is a smaller decision than deciding what a
role means.

### Change settings

Name: `ManageSettings`

- Opens the **Settings** page and changes everything on it: **Server** (how long records are kept
  and the public address), **Modbot's VRChat login**, **Email and alerts**, **Discord** (including
  event channels and instance announcements), **Moderation**, **AutoMod**, **Evidence**, **AI**
  (including insights and limits) and **API**.
- Runs the [setup wizard](/setup-wizard) again once it has been completed.
- Opens the **Integrations** page, from the sidebar's **Integrations** heading: the status of
  VRChat, Discord and Email, and where in Settings each is set up. See [Analytics](/analytics).
- Allows webhooks to be sent to private network addresses.

The **API** tab's keys and webhooks also need **Manage API keys and webhooks**, and the reason list
under **Moderation** needs **Edit the reason list**.

It does **not** open the **People and roles** tab, which is **Manage users** and **Manage roles**. Each of those
two opens Settings by itself, and everyone sees only the tabs they may open.

### Manage API keys and webhooks

Name: `ManageApiKeys`

- Makes API keys for yourself, sees every key and revokes any key. See [API keys](/api/keys).
- Sets up webhooks, sees every webhook, and turns off or deletes any of them. Changing a webhook's
  address, events or secret, or sending a test, is only for the person who set it up or an
  administrator. See [Webhooks](/api/webhooks).

In the web app these are under **Settings**, **API**, which also needs **Change settings** to open.

### Use the VRChat proxy

Name: `UseVRChatProxy`

- Sends requests to VRChat's own API through Modbot, as the group's service account, at
  `/api/proxy/vrchat/…`. Any endpoint the service account can reach, read or write. See
  [VRChat proxy](/api/vrchat-proxy).
- The proxy itself is off until an administrator turns it on under **Settings**, **VRChat proxy**,
  which needs **Change settings**.

Not part of the built-in roles. Administrator holds it.

### Set up auto-invites

Name: `ManageAutoInvites`

- Switches [auto-invites](/moderation/auto-invites) on and off, and sets the rules, the minutes
  somebody must spend in an instance, and how long before the same person may be invited again.
  In the web app this is **Settings**, **Auto-invites**.

Not part of the built-in roles, and deliberately not part of **Change settings**. Everything else
under settings changes what Modbot does to its own data; this one decides who ends up inside your
group, without anybody looking at them first.

Administrator holds it.

### Import old data

Name: `ImportOldData`

- Uploads a file of another platform's bans, warnings, notes and audit entries and has Modbot
  write each record into the log as history, and sees every past import. See
  [Importing old data](/self-hosting/importing-old-data).

Not part of the built-in roles, and deliberately not part of **Change settings**, which it used to
be. A setting says what Modbot does next and can be changed back. An import writes history that
did not happen inside Modbot: bans, warnings and notes dated years ago, about named people, in the
same log a moderator reads to decide what somebody has done before. Nothing else Modbot offers can
do that in bulk, and events cannot be deleted afterwards — the only way back is purging the people
concerned or restoring a backup.

There is no screen for importing. It is done with an [API key](/api/keys) holding this permission.

Administrator holds it.

### Administrator

Name: `Administrator`

- Everything, including permissions added in future versions. See
  [Administrator covers everything](#administrator-covers-everything).
