title: New staff checklist
description: Give each new staff member a checklist with a buddy. The new mod fills in written tasks themselves, the buddy signs off the rest and writes a note, the Council is reminded on day 30, and the lines are set per server.
---

The new staff checklist follows one new staff member through their trial. It holds a **buddy**, a
start date, **tasks** the new member answers in writing, and **buddy sign-off** lines the buddy ticks
off. It sits on the person's profile, on the **Account** tab of their [popup](/moderation/popups).
Both lists of lines are yours to set, per server.

## Setting the lines

Under **Settings → People and roles → New staff** (you need **Manage users**), the **New staff
checklist** card has two boxes, one line each:

- **Tasks, one per line:** things the new member does and writes an answer to, such as "Read the
  rules and write down the three you think matter most."
- **Buddy sign-off, one per line:** things the buddy confirms, such as "Has joined a moderation
  shift."

The card also has a **Reminder channel**, the Discord channel for the [reminders](#reminders). Leave it
empty and no reminder is posted. The bot needs to see the channel, send messages there and embed links.

Press **Save checklist**. Blank lines are dropped. A server can have up to 30 lines of each kind, and
a line is at most 300 characters. Saving is recorded in the audit log as a settings change.

A change reaches trials started after it. A trial that has already started keeps the lines it was
given, so answers and sign-offs never end up under a different wording.

## Starting a checklist

Open the new member's popup, go to the **Account** tab and press **Start checklist** (you need
**Manage users**). Pick a **Buddy** from the accounts that are turned on, which can be anyone but the
new member. Modbot copies the current lines, stores today as the start date and records it in the
audit log. An account has at most one checklist.

Like the other account actions, starting, changing the buddy, closing and removing only work on accounts whose
highest role is below yours (an administrator can do it for anyone). You can never start one on your own account.

On a started checklist, someone with **Manage users** can change the buddy, **Close** the checklist or
**Remove** it. Changing the buddy keeps the sign-offs already given. Removing deletes the lines, the
answers, the sign-offs and the note.

## Closing a checklist

**Close** ends the trial once the Council has decided. A closed checklist shows **Closed** with the date, keeps
its lines, answers, sign-offs and note for reading, and stops sending reminders. It also leaves the buddy's
list on their **Account** page. Closing again changes nothing. Close needs **Manage users**, like starting one.

## Reminders

When a **Reminder channel** is chosen under **Settings → People and roles → New staff**, Modbot posts two kinds
of reminder there. It looks every 15 minutes, and a post that Discord refuses is tried again on the next look.
Reminders due while no channel is chosen go out once one is.

- **Day 30, for the Council's vote.** Thirty days after a checklist started, one post for that person shows how
  many tasks they have answered and lines the buddy has signed off, how many events they worked, their buddy, the
  start date and the buddy's note, with a link to their profile. Events worked are the dates since the trial
  started that Modbot opened the event's instance for and invited them to as host or staff; an event run by hand,
  without that invite, is not counted. It is posted once per checklist, and not at all for one that
  is closed. At most five of these go out in one look.
- **Check-in, for any staff member who has gone quiet.** When an account has been inactive for 30 days, a single
  post lists everyone due, each with their name and the day they were last active. The same quiet stretch is
  not reminded again: the account has to be active and then go quiet for 30 days again. One post lists at most 40
  people, fewer if their lines would not fit in one Discord card; anyone left off follows in the next one.

Active means any of these: signing in to Modbot, doing a moderation action (a ban, unban or kick, a join request
refused, an instance kick or warning, as the account or as its linked VRChat or Discord account), or being host or
staff on a date of an event that has started, as counted above for events worked. An account that has done none of them counts from the day it was made. Accounts
that are turned off or deleted are left out.

## Who does what

| | Fills in the tasks | Signs off and writes the buddy note | Reads the checklist |
|---|---|---|---|
| The new member | Yes, only they | No, never their own | Yes |
| The buddy | No | Yes | Yes |
| Someone with **Manage users** | No | Yes, in the buddy's place, so a buddy who has left does not hold the trial up | Yes |
| Someone with **See the operational log** | No | No | Yes |

The tasks are the new member's own words, so only people with a reason to see them can read them:
the new member, their buddy, and people with **Manage users** or **See the operational log**. Everyone
else gets nothing back.

## For the new member

Your own checklist is on your **Account** page, under **Your new staff checklist**. Write an answer
to each task and press **Save answer**. You can change an answer later. An empty answer clears it.
An answer is at most 4000 characters. You can see the buddy's sign-offs and note but not change them.

## For the buddy

The checklists of the people you are buddy to are on your **Account** page too. Press **Sign off** on
a line when it is done, and **Take back** to undo it. Under **Buddy note**, write how the trial went
and press **Save note**; it is at most 4000 characters. Each sign-off shows who gave it and when.

## What Modbot writes down

The [audit log](/moderation/audit-log) records a checklist being started, the buddy being changed, a
line being signed off or taken back, a checklist being closed and a checklist being removed, each under the new member's
account. Written answers and the buddy note are not copied into the log.

Answers, sign-offs and the note are kept with the checklist. They are deleted when the checklist is
removed and when the account is [deleted](/accounts/staff-accounts#deleting-an-account).
