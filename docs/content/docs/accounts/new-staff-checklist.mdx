title: New staff checklist
description: Give each new staff member a checklist with a buddy. The new mod fills in written tasks themselves, the buddy signs off the rest and writes a note, and the lines are set per server.
---

The new staff checklist follows one new staff member through their trial. It holds a **buddy**, a
start date, **tasks** the new member answers in writing, and **buddy sign-off** lines the buddy ticks
off. It sits on the person's profile, on the **Account** tab of their [popup](/moderation/popups).
Both lists of lines are yours to set, per server.

## Setting the lines

Under **Settings → People and roles → New staff** (you need **Manage users**), the **New staff
checklist** card has two boxes, one line each:

- **Tasks, one per line:** things the new member does and writes an answer to, such as "Read the
  rules and write down the three you think matter most."
- **Buddy sign-off, one per line:** things the buddy confirms, such as "Has joined a moderation
  shift."

Press **Save checklist**. Blank lines are dropped. A server can have up to 30 lines of each kind, and
a line is at most 300 characters. Saving is recorded in the audit log as a settings change.

A change reaches trials started after it. A trial that has already started keeps the lines it was
given, so answers and sign-offs never end up under a different wording.

## Starting a checklist

Open the new member's popup, go to the **Account** tab and press **Start checklist** (you need
**Manage users**). Pick a **Buddy** from the accounts that are turned on, which can be anyone but the
new member. Modbot copies the current lines, stores today as the start date and records it in the
audit log. An account has at most one checklist. Like the other account actions, starting, changing the buddy
and removing only work on accounts whose highest role is below yours (an administrator can do it for anyone),
and on your own account.

On a started checklist, someone with **Manage users** can change the buddy or **Remove** the
checklist. Changing the buddy keeps the sign-offs already given. Removing deletes the lines, the
answers, the sign-offs and the note.

## Who does what

| | Fills in the tasks | Signs off and writes the buddy note | Reads the checklist |
|---|---|---|---|
| The new member | Yes, only they | No, never their own | Yes |
| The buddy | No | Yes | Yes |
| Someone with **Manage users** | No | Yes, in the buddy's place, so a buddy who has left does not hold the trial up | Yes |
| Someone with **See the operational log** | No | No | Yes |

The tasks are the new member's own words, so only people with a reason to see them can read them:
the new member, their buddy, and people with **Manage users** or **See the operational log**. Everyone
else gets nothing back.

## For the new member

Your own checklist is on your **Account** page, under **Your new staff checklist**. Write an answer
to each task and press **Save answer**. You can change an answer later. An empty answer clears it.
An answer is at most 4000 characters. You can see the buddy's sign-offs and note but not change them.

## For the buddy

The checklists of the people you are buddy to are on your **Account** page too. Press **Sign off** on
a line when it is done, and **Take back** to undo it. Under **Buddy note**, write how the trial went
and press **Save note**; it is at most 4000 characters. Each sign-off shows who gave it and when.

## What Modbot writes down

The [audit log](/moderation/audit-log) records a checklist being started, the buddy being changed, a
line being signed off or taken back, and a checklist being removed, each under the new member's
account. Written answers and the buddy note are not copied into the log.

Answers, sign-offs and the note are kept with the checklist. They are deleted when the checklist is
removed and when the account is [deleted](/accounts/staff-accounts#deleting-an-account).
