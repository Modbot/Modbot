---
title: Notifications
description: How Modbot decides who it tells about something, the channels it can reach you on, and how to change what you get.
---

Modbot raises a **notification** when something has happened that a person should know about — its
own health, an evidence store that is not there, an instance that closed with people still in it.
Every one of them goes through the same pipeline, so a new kind of event reaches every channel and a
new channel carries every kind of event.

## How serious it is

Every notification is one of three:

| | What it means | What happens by default |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | Modbot cannot do its job | Email and a Discord direct message, straight away |
| **Warning** | Something needs a person before long | A Discord direct message straight away; email in the daily summary |
| **Information** | Worth knowing, not worth interrupting for | The daily summary only |

The defaults are quiet on purpose. A tool that says everything at the same volume gets muted, and a
muted tool misses the one thing that mattered.

## Channels

**Email** needs SMTP set up (Settings, Integrations, Email) and an address on your account.
Notification email counts as other email, so it never uses the twenty sends a day Modbot keeps back
for password resets and invites.

**Discord** needs the bot set up and a Discord account on your Modbot account. Modbot sends you a
direct message; you will not get one if your direct messages are closed to the server.

Web push is not built yet. Your moderators' headsets are covered separately: the SteamVR overlay
raises a card for a flagged join the moment it happens, straight from the live stream, and does not
wait for anything here.

## Changing what you get

Open **your account** from the top of any page, then **Notifications**. For each channel you choose:

- **Off**, **Critical only**, **Critical and warnings**, or **Everything**
- whether the **daily summary** goes out on that channel

Your settings are yours alone. Changing them does not change what anybody else gets.

Anything a channel is set not to interrupt for still turns up in that channel's daily summary when
it is on, so turning email down to **Critical only** does not throw the rest away.

## Saying a thing once

A problem that is still there in a minute is the same problem. Modbot says it once, then counts how
many times it happened again, and says it again when the quiet time is up — six hours, unless the
thing raising it sets its own. A problem that gets more serious while it is being kept quiet goes
out anyway.

## If nothing can reach you

A critical notification that reached you on no channel at all is not thrown away. It waits on your
account and shows as a red bar across the top of every page the next time you sign in, until you
mark it seen.

That is the whole reason Modbot bothers to record who a notification was for before it tries to send
anything.
