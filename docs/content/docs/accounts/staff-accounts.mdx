---
title: Staff accounts, invites and password resets
description: How to add your moderators, link their VRChat accounts, disable them, and get them back in when they forget a password.
---

Modbot has its own accounts, separate from VRChat and Discord. Each person on your team signs in
with their own password and either their username or their email address, so everything Modbot
records says who did it. This page
covers getting people in, keeping their accounts in order, and what each person can do with their
own account.

What a person may do once they are in is decided by their roles. See
[Roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions).

## The Users page

Open **Users** in the sidebar, under **Team**. You need the **Manage users** permission to see it.

The table lists every staff account with its **Username**, the linked **VRChat account** (or
**Not linked yet**), its **Roles**, and its **Last sign-in**. Disabled accounts are greyed out and
marked **Disabled**. Your own row says "you".

Click a row to open that person's panel, where you can change their roles, their contact details,
make a password reset link, and disable or enable the account.

Accounts are never deleted. Every action Modbot records points at the account that did it, and
deleting the account would break that record. When somebody leaves the team, disable them.

## Adding somebody

Press **Add someone**. There are two ways, shown as two tabs.

### Send them a link (recommended)

1. Choose **Send them a link**.
2. Tick the roles the person should have.
3. Press **Make the link**.
4. Copy the link and send it to them on Discord, or however your team talks.

The person opens the link, sees who invited them and with which roles, and fills in their own
**Username**, **Email** and **Password** on the **Create your Modbot account** page. When they
press **Create account** they are signed in straight away and taken to link their VRChat account
(see below).

They type their own email address; the invite does not carry one.

An invite link:

- works **once**,
- expires after **72 hours**,
- stops working if the account that made it is disabled before it is used,
- is shown **only once**. Modbot keeps only a fingerprint of it, so it cannot show it to you again.
  If you lose it, make a new one.

Invite links that have not been used yet are listed under **Invite links not used yet** on the
Users page, with who made each one, its roles and when it expires. Press **Take back** to cancel
one.

This is the way to add people because nobody but the new moderator ever knows their password.

### Set a temporary password

If the person is sitting next to you, choose **Set a temporary password**, type a **Username**, an
**Email** and a **Temporary password** (at least 12 characters) twice, tick their roles and press
**Create the account**. They can sign in right away.

Tell them the password in person and ask them to change it on their account page straight away.
Until they do, two people know it, and Modbot cannot tell which of you did something. Modbot does
not force the change.

### Username, email and password rules

- A username can be up to 64 characters. Two usernames that differ only in upper and lower case
  count as the same name, so `Gunner24` and `gunner24` cannot both exist.
- An email address is required on every account. Two accounts cannot share one, and upper and lower
  case do not make two addresses different.
- A password must be at least 12 characters.

### You can only give what you have

The role list shows every role, but you can only tick roles whose permissions you hold yourself.
The others are greyed out. This applies to invite links, temporary-password accounts and changing
somebody's roles. It stops a person who manages users from making themselves, or a friend, an
administrator. An administrator can give any role.

## Linking a VRChat account (required)

Every account has to be linked to the VRChat account of the person behind it before it can use
Modbot. This includes the first administrator, who does it during the
[setup wizard](/setup-wizard). The link is what lets Modbot connect "Alice in Modbot" with a real
VRChat user.

Until the link is done, signing in takes the person to the **Link your VRChat account** page and
nowhere else.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open your profile

Press **Open my VRChat profile**. Your VRChat profile opens in a new tab.

</Step>
<Step>

### Paste your user id or profile address

Copy your user id (it starts with `usr_`) or the address of the page, paste it into **Your VRChat
user id or profile address**, and press **Continue**. Either is fine.

</Step>
<Step>

### Put the code in your bio

Modbot shows a short code like `modbot-7F3K9Q`. Press **Copy**, paste it anywhere in your VRChat
bio, and save your profile on the VRChat website.

</Step>
<Step>

### Press Check

Back in Modbot, press **Check**. Modbot reads your VRChat profile and, if the code is in the bio,
the link is confirmed. You can take the code out of your bio afterwards.

</Step>
</Steps>

Things to know:

- A code lasts **30 minutes** and can be checked **6 times**. You have to wait **10 seconds**
  between checks. The page shows how many checks are left. If the code runs out, press
  **Start over**.
- One VRChat account can be linked to only one Modbot account. If it is already linked to somebody
  else, Check says so.
- Modbot reads the profile through its own VRChat account. If that account is not set up yet, or
  VRChat is rate limiting Modbot, Check tells you and nothing is retried. Try again later.
- You can link a different VRChat account later from your account page. The new link replaces the
  old one and is recorded.

### What an account without a link can do

Only three things: see who it is signed in as, finish the link, and sign out. Every other part of
Modbot refuses it, whatever roles it holds. The one exception is the first steps of the
[setup wizard](/setup-wizard) for an account allowed to change settings, because the link cannot be
checked until Modbot's own VRChat account is working.

## Changing somebody's roles

Open the person on the Users page, tick or untick roles and press **Save roles**. The change takes
effect on their very next click in Modbot, not the next time they sign in.

## Disabling and enabling an account

In the person's panel, under **Access**:

- **Disable this account** ends every session they have straight away and stops them signing in.
  Nothing they did is removed from history. Invite links they made stop working. You cannot
  disable your own account.
- **Enable this account** lets them sign in again. Sessions that were open before they were
  disabled do not come back; they have to sign in fresh.

A disabled account also cannot use password reset links, and any Discord bot commands it tries are
refused.

## The last administrator

Modbot will not let you disable an account, or change its roles, if that would leave no enabled
account that holds **Administrator**. You see "That would leave nobody who can administer Modbot."
A deployment with no administrator has no way back except editing the database, so make sure a
second person holds Administrator before you step away.

<Callout type="warn">
Anyone with **Manage users** can disable any account other than their own, administrators
included, and can take roles away from people, as long as one enabled administrator is left. Give
that permission to people you trust with the whole team.
</Callout>

## Email and Discord user id

Every account has an **Email** address. A **Discord user id** is optional. You can set them for
somebody under **Contact details** in their panel (then press **Save contact details**), and each
person can set their own on their account page.

The email address is used for three things:

- signing in — the sign-in box takes the address or the username,
- sending a password reset link the person asked for (see below),
- naming the person who runs this Modbot (see below).

The Discord user id is used for recognising the person when they use the Modbot Discord bot's
commands. See [Setting up the bot](/discord/bot-setup). Modbot does not check that it really
belongs to the person. It is typed in.

The email address of the oldest enabled administrator is also the contact address Modbot gives
VRChat on every request, and the one shown as this server's owner. See
[Security](/security#what-modbot-tells-vrchat-about-you) and
[The server page](/api/server).

<Callout type="info">
If you are upgrading a Modbot from before email was required, accounts without an address keep
working. Modbot asks for one on the account page. Where two accounts had the same address, the
older one keeps it and the newer one is asked for its own.
</Callout>

## Emails from the project

When somebody makes their account — the first administrator during setup, or anybody opening an
invite link — the form offers one checkbox: **Receive emails from Modbot about new features and
updates**. It starts unticked, and ticking it sends the address to Modbot Cloud, which is what
sends the mail. To stop receiving them, use the unsubscribe link in one of them.

The checkbox is not shown at all on a Modbot that has Modbot Cloud switched off.

## When somebody forgets their password

There are two ways. You only need one.

### They ask for a link

The sign-in page has **Forgot password?**. The person types their **Username** and presses
**Send me a link**. Modbot sends a reset link:

1. **by email**, if email is set up under **Settings**, **Integrations**, **Email (SMTP)**, and the
   account has an email address,
2. otherwise **by Discord direct message**, if a **Bot token** is saved under **Settings**,
   **Discord**, and the account has a Discord user id.

The message names the account and says nothing has changed if they did not ask. The link lasts
**24 hours**.

For this to work, an administrator must have saved the **Public address** of your Modbot under
**Settings**, **Host & Database**. That is the address people type to reach it, and it is the only
thing a sent link is ever built from. If no public address is saved, or neither email nor Discord
is set up, the forgot-password page says so and sends nothing. Use the second way instead.

To stop this being used to find out which usernames exist, the page always gives the same answer,
whether or not the username is real, disabled, or has no contact details. Behind that answer:

- nothing is sent to a disabled account or one with no email and no Discord user id,
- a person can get at most one link every **10 minutes**,
- many requests from one address slow down, the same way failed sign-ins do.

Nothing is ever sent to a person's email or Discord except a reset link they asked for.

### You make them a link

Open the person on the Users page and press **Make a reset link** under **Password**. Copy the link
and send it to them yourself. It is shown once. This works even when email, Discord and the public
address are not set up, but not for a disabled account (enable it first).

### Using a reset link

Either kind of link opens the **Choose a new password** page. The person types a **New password**
twice. Using the link:

- sets the new password,
- **ends every session the account had**, everywhere,
- uses the link up.

They then sign in with the new password. Making a new reset link for an account cancels any older
unused ones.

## Your own account

Press the button with your name at the top right of any page to open **Your account**. Anyone who
is signed in can use it. It has these parts:

- **You**: who you are signed in as and your roles.
- **Change your username**: type a **New username** and **Your current password**, then press
  **Change username**. The change is recorded with the old and new names.
- **Change your password**: type your **Current password** and a **New password** twice, then
  press **Change password**. Every other session you have is signed out. The browser you did it in
  stays signed in.
- **How you can be reached**: your **Email**, which you can sign in with and which a reset link is
  sent to, and your **Discord user id**. Press **Save**.
- **Your VRChat account**: which VRChat account you are linked to, and **Link a different account**.
- **my.modbot.co**: **Add to my.modbot.co**.
- **Sign out everywhere**: ends every session you have on every browser and machine, including
  this one.

The **Sign out** button at the top right ends only the session in the browser you are using.

## Sessions

The sign-in page has a **Keep me signed in** box. It starts unticked, and what you get depends on
it:

- **Left unticked**, closing the browser signs you out. While the browser stays open, using Modbot
  keeps the session going; two weeks without using it ends it.
- **Ticked**, you stay signed in after closing the browser, for **30 days** from when you signed in.
  Those 30 days do not stretch, however much you use Modbot: after 30 days you sign in again. Leave
  it unticked on a machine that is not yours.

Whichever you choose:

- Modbot checks the account on every request. Disabling an account, using a reset link, changing
  your password (for your other sessions) and **Sign out everywhere** all take effect on the next
  click, not at the next sign-in. **Sign out everywhere** ends kept sessions too.
- Changing your password or your username keeps the browser you did it in signed in, and keeps it
  the same kind: if you had ticked the box, you still have.
- Restarting or updating Modbot does not sign anybody out.

### Failed sign-ins

A wrong password never locks anybody out. Instead each failed attempt makes the next one wait
longer: 1 second, then 2, 4, 8, 16, and at most **20 seconds**, counted per sign-in typed and per
address over 15 minutes. A correct password always works after the wait, so nobody can keep you
out of your own Modbot by hammering your username. The sign-in page gives the same answer for a
wrong password, an unknown username, an unknown email address and a disabled account, so it cannot
be used to find out who has an account here.

## What Modbot writes down

Every account event is recorded in Modbot's own log, which people with **See the operational log**
can read on the [Audit log](/moderation/audit-log) page:

- accounts created (and whether by invite or temporary password), invite links made, used and taken
  back,
- roles changed, and roles created, changed or deleted,
- accounts disabled and enabled,
- usernames changed (old and new name), passwords changed, contact details changed (which fields,
  not the values),
- reset links made (whether you or the person asked, and how it was sent) and used,
- VRChat accounts linked,
- every sign-in, every failed sign-in, and every **Sign out everywhere**,
- who asked for the project's emails when they made their account.

A failed sign-in records what was typed in the sign-in box and the address it came from, never the
password.
