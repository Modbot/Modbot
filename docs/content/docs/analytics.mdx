Analytics is a set of five pages, each answering one question about your community. They are in the
sidebar under **Analytics**:

| Page | The question it answers |
|---|---|
| [**My Group**](#my-group) | Is the VRChat group growing or shrinking, and what changed? |
| [**My Server**](#my-server) | Is the Discord server healthy, and who keeps it going? |
| [**My Team**](#my-team) | Who is doing the moderation work, and when is nobody covering? |
| [**Worlds**](#worlds) | Which of our worlds actually get used? |
| [**Instances**](#instances) | When is the community actually active? |

All five need the **See analytics** permission
([roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-analytics)). The built-in **Moderator**
and **Viewer** roles have it. Without it, the pages are hidden from the sidebar and the server refuses
their data.

## Date ranges

Every page has the same range buttons at the top: **7 days**, **30 days** (the default), **90 days**
and **All time**. The dates the range covers are shown on the right.

- Ranges are whole days in UTC, and include today. Today's figures are still filling in.
- **All time** starts at the first day Modbot has anything recorded.
- Switching range keeps the old figures on screen until the new ones arrive.

A few panels ignore the range because they describe *now*, such as who is a member today. They are
noted below.

## Where the numbers come from

The pages draw on two kinds of stored data.

**Daily totals** are counts per day: joins, leaves, messages, bans, instances opened, actions per
moderator, and so on. Modbot works them out from its record of events about every 15 minutes, so a
chart can be up to about 15 minutes behind. Daily totals are **kept forever**, even if you set
Modbot to delete old events.

**The fact log** is Modbot's record of individual events (a join, a role change, a person seen in an
instance). Panels that need to pair events up, such as "did this invite lead to a join?" or "how
long was this instance open?", read the fact log directly. If you have set
[data retention](/self-hosting/data-retention), old events are deleted, and these panels can only
cover what is left.

### Data covered

At the bottom of every page, the **Data covered** card says:

- **Daily totals cover**: the first and last day with daily totals, and when they were **last
  updated**.
- **The fact log covers**: the first and last day with stored events.
- **Facts kept for**: how long moderation events and presence events are kept, or **forever**.

If a chart looks empty or short, check this card first. A deployment that started recording last
week has no figures for last month, and a short retention window shortens the fact log panels.

### Presence reports

Some figures (time spent in worlds, visitors, how many people were in an instance, coverage gaps)
come from **presence reports**: who the [companion](/companion/install) saw in an
instance while a moderator with the client was there. VRChat does not report this any other way.

So these figures only cover instances where a moderator with a paired companion was present. A
world nobody with the client visited looks empty, however busy it really was.

## My Group

**Is the community growing or shrinking, and what changed?**

The stats along the top:

- **Members**: the group's member count as VRChat last reported it, with the date it was read.
- **Joined** and **Left**: joins and leaves recorded in the range.
- **Net change**: joined minus left in the range.

Below them, the [**Insights**](/ai/insights) panel appears if AI insights are set up.

| Panel | What it shows | Comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Member count** | The member count VRChat reported, reading by reading, with the number of members online in VRChat drawn over it in green on its own scale at the right. Hover a point for both numbers and the time it was read. | Member count readings |
| **Joins and leaves per day** | Joins and leaves, side by side, per day. | Daily totals |
| **Joined minus left, running** | A running total of recorded joins minus recorded leaves, counted from zero on the first day Modbot recorded. This is not the member count: a group that already had 5,000 members when Modbot started still starts at zero. | Daily totals |
| **Invites and join requests** | **Invites sent**; **Invites accepted** (the share of invited people who joined within 7 days); **Join requests**; **Requests decided** (approved / rejected); and a chart of invites and requests per day. | Daily totals, except invites accepted (fact log) |
| **Roles** | Each role VRChat lists for the group, with how many times it was **Given** and **Taken away** in the range. Badges mark **moderation** roles, roles **given on join** and **self-service** roles. Shows when the role list was last read. | Fact log |
| **How long members have been members** | Current members grouped into **Under a week**, **1 to 4 weeks**, **1 to 3 months**, **3 to 12 months** and **Over a year**. | Fact log |

**Member count** has range buttons of its own: **Day**, **Week** (the default), **Month** and
**All**. Modbot reads the group's member count and online member count about every five minutes
and keeps every reading. Long ranges show up to about 500 of them, spread evenly across the range;
before the first stored reading, the chart shows one point per day from the group's recorded
changes. Readings older than the presence [retention](/self-hosting/data-retention) window are
deleted if one is set.

**How long members have been members** ignores the range: it is about who is a member now. It only
counts members whose join Modbot recorded, and says how many that is. Members who joined before
Modbot started recording are not counted here.

## My Server

**Is the Discord server healthy, and who keeps it going?**

My Server needs the Discord bot. The stats along the top are **Members**, **Messages** in the range,
**Active in 30 days** (with the share of members) and **Time in voice** in the range. The page then
shows the member count, joins and leaves, messages and voice minutes per day, active members (per
day, 7 days or 30 days), the busiest channels and hours, moderation actions per day, new members
who stayed, member health and top contributors. Names open that person's popup.

"Active" means the person sent a message or spent time in voice. Bots are never counted.

Most of it comes from daily totals. **New members still here** pairs joins with later leaves, so it
covers what the fact log still holds. **Member health** is about now and ignores the range.

For every panel in detail, and how messages are stored, see
[Messages and My Server](/discord/messages-and-my-server).

## My Team

**Who is doing the moderation work, and when is nobody covering?**

The stats along the top:

- **Moderators active**: how many people took a moderation action in the range.
- **Actions**: all moderation actions in the range.
- **Coverage gaps**: how many gaps were found (see below).
- **Instances nobody watched**: group instances opened with no companion ever in them, out of
  all instances opened.

### Coverage gaps

A coverage gap is a stretch of time when people were in a group instance and no moderator was. It
starts when the last moderator leaves while people are still there, and ends when a moderator is seen
again or the instance closes.

A moderator counts as present when their companion is reporting from the instance, or when
another client sees someone Modbot recognises as a moderator (they hold a role with moderation
permissions, have taken a moderation action, or own the group). A moderator without the client, in
an instance no client is in, cannot be seen at all.

Use the buttons to show only gaps where at least a number of people were left behind:
**Anyone left behind**, **3+ people** (the default), **5+** or **10+**. The table shows:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Began** | When the last moderator left. |
| **Lasted** | How long until the gap ended, or "unknown". |
| **People left behind** | How many people were there when the last moderator left. |
| **Last moderator out** | Who left last, or "a client stopped reporting". |
| **Ended because** | "a moderator arrived", "the instance was closed", or "nothing more was reported". |
| **Instance** | The world (click to open it) and instance number. |

### Actions

- **Actions per moderator**: one row per moderator with a **Total** and a column per kind of action:
  **Instance kicks**, **Warns**, **Bans**, **Unbans**, **Removed from group**, **Invites**, **Requests
  approved**, **Requests rejected** and **Role changes**, plus the day they were **Last active**.
  Click a name to open their [popup](/moderation/popups). People who can review tickets also see a
  **Reviews of unusual patterns →** button that opens [Reviews](/moderation/reviews).
- **Actions per day**: all actions per day.
- **What kind of actions**: each kind of action, largest first.

The action counts come from daily totals. Coverage gaps come from presence reports in the fact log.

## Worlds

**Which of our worlds actually get used?**

If there are no presence reports in the range, the page says **No presence reports in this range.**
If there are fewer than 200, it says how many, as a reminder that the figures are thin.

The stats along the top are **Worlds**, **Time seen**, **Visitors** and **Presence reports**.

**Worlds, by time people were seen in them** lists each world with:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **World** | Name, author and world id. Click the name to open the world. |
| **Holds** | The world's capacity. |
| **Time seen** | Total time people were seen in the world. |
| **Visitors** | Different people seen there. |
| **Arrivals seen** | Times someone was seen arriving. |
| **Instances opened** | Group instances opened in the world. |
| **Last seen** | When someone was last seen there. |

**Visitors per day, busiest worlds** charts visitors per day for the five busiest worlds.

Time, visitors and arrivals come from presence reports. **Instances opened** comes from the group's
audit log and is complete whether or not a client was there. Visitors per day comes from daily totals
and outlasts a short retention window.

## Instances

**When is the community actually active?**

The stats along the top are **Opened** and **Closed** (instances in the range), **Typical time open**
and **Most open at once**.

| Panel | What it shows | Comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Open right now** | Group instances open at this moment. Shown only when there are some, and ignores the range. | Modbot's instance list |
| **Recent instances** | The most recent instances. | Modbot's instance list |
| **When the community is active** | A week-by-hour grid. Switch between **People arriving** and **Instances opened**. Hours are shown in your own time zone (named in the title), rounded to whole hours. | Fact log (arrivals from presence reports) |
| **Opened and closed per day** | Instances opened and closed each day. | Daily totals |
| **Most open at once, per day** | The most group instances open at the same time each day. | Fact log |
| **Most people in one instance, per day** | The biggest crowd seen in a single instance each day. | Presence reports |

VRChat only records an instance closing when a moderator closes it. An instance that emptied out on
its own has no close on record, so Modbot treats it as open until the last thing seen in it (a kick,
a warning or a presence report). That is why **Closed** is often lower than **Opened**.
