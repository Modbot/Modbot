The charts and numbers over time for every part of your community are on one page, **Stats**, at
the top of the sidebar under **Now** and **Chat**. It has a tab for each question:

| Tab | The question it answers |
|---|---|
| [**Growth**](#growth) | Is the community growing or shrinking, and what changed? |
| [**Activity**](#activity) | When is the community busy, and where? |
| [**Moderation**](#moderation) | Who is doing the moderation work, and when is nobody covering? |

In each tab, the VRChat group and the Discord server have a part of their own, under their names.
Nothing adds the two together: a VRChat join and a Discord join are not the same kind of thing.

The [**VRChat**](#vrchat) and [**Discord**](#discord) pages, under **Integrations** in the sidebar,
show the group and the server the way VRChat and Discord do. Each keeps **This week**: four numbers
for the last seven days, each against the seven before. Its heading opens Stats.

Stats and the VRChat and Discord pages need the **See analytics** permission
([roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-analytics)). The built-in **Moderator**
and **Viewer** roles have it. Without it, the pages are hidden from the sidebar and Modbot refuses
their data.

Clicking the **Integrations** heading itself opens a different page, **Integrations**: a card each
for VRChat, Discord and Email, with its status and a **Set up** button that opens the part of
Settings where it is set up. It is about setting things up rather than numbers, so it needs
[**Change settings**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#change-settings) instead of See analytics.
Without Change settings the heading only groups the VRChat and Discord pages.

<Callout type="info" title="Team and Worlds moved">
Until September 2026 the charts were spread over five pages: **Team**, **VRChat**, **Worlds**,
**Instances** and **Discord**. Team is now the Moderation tab and Worlds is part of the Activity
tab. Their old addresses, `/analytics/team` and `/analytics/worlds`, open those tabs.
</Callout>

## Date ranges

Stats has range buttons at the top, shared by its three tabs: **7d**, **30d** (the
default), **90d** and **All time**. The dates the range covers are shown on the right.

- Ranges are whole days in UTC, and include today. Today's figures are still filling in.
- **All time** starts at the first day Modbot has anything recorded.
- Switching range keeps the old figures on screen until the new ones arrive.

A few panels ignore the range because they describe *now*, such as who is a member today. They are
noted below.

## Where the numbers come from

The pages draw on two kinds of stored data.

**Daily totals** are counts per day: joins, leaves, messages, bans, instances opened, actions per
moderator, and so on. Modbot works them out from its record of events about every 15 minutes, so a
chart can be up to about 15 minutes behind. Daily totals are **kept forever**, even if you set
Modbot to delete old events.

**The fact log** is Modbot's record of individual events (a join, a role change, a person seen in an
instance). Panels that need to pair events up, such as "did this invite lead to a join?" or "how
long was this instance open?", read the fact log directly. If you have set
[data retention](/self-hosting/data-retention), old events are deleted, and these panels can only
cover what is left.

### Data covered

At the bottom of every page, the **Data covered** card says:

- **Daily totals cover**: the first and last day with daily totals, and when they were **last
  updated**.
- **The fact log covers**: the first and last day with stored events.
- **Facts kept for**: how long moderation events and presence events are kept, or **forever**.

If a chart looks empty or short, check this card first. A deployment that started recording last
week has no figures for last month, and a short retention window shortens the fact log panels.

### Presence reports

Some figures (time spent in worlds, visitors, who was in an instance, times left without a moderator) come from
**presence reports**: who the [companion](/companion/install) saw in an instance while a moderator
with the client was there. VRChat does not report *who* is in an instance any other way.

**Modbot only knows who was in an instance while a moderator's companion was reporting from it.**
Nobody with the client in the instance means no presence reports at all, and every figure built on
them then reads as nought however busy the instance really was. That is not a fault and it is not
an estimate: it is the limit of what VRChat will tell anybody.

What this means for a figure drawn over a range:

- A world nobody with the client visited looks empty.
- "The most people seen in one instance" is the most **seen**, which is at most the most there were.
- A busy Saturday nobody covered leaves no trace, so it cannot pull an average or a peak up.

Where a page's figures rest on presence reports, it counts them for you and says so when they are
thin: **No presence reports in this range**, or **Only 84 presence reports in this range**. Treat
anything below a few hundred as a sample rather than a measurement.

### Head counts

Some figures (**People in instances**, and the peaks on the [Activity](#activity) tab) come from
**head counts** instead, and these are not limited by who was in the instance. Modbot reads each of
the group's open instances' own page about every thirty seconds and keeps VRChat's own count of how
many people are in it.

No moderator and no companion is involved, so head counts cover instances nobody from the team was
standing in. What they cannot tell you is *who* those people were — only how many. That is why the
two sources sit side by side rather than one replacing the other.

A count is stored only when the number **changes**, so an instance that sits at twelve people all
evening is one stored reading, not a thousand. Charts drawn from head counts are therefore a
staircase: each reading holds until the next one.

Head counts start when Modbot starts reading an instance, so they can still be incomplete:

- An instance that opened before Modbot was set up, or while the group's instance list could not be
  read, has no counts for that part of its life.
- If VRChat rate limits Modbot, reading stops until the limit clears and nothing is counted in
  between. [Health](/self-hosting/health) shows when that is happening.

The Activity tab measures exactly this and says so — see [Coverage](#coverage) below.

## VRChat

**Is the community growing or shrinking, and what changed?**

### The group's header and tabs

The page opens the way your group's own page on vrchat.com does: the banner, the icon, the group's
name, how many members are online and in the group, and the group code (**Copy group code** copies
it). Under that is VRChat's row of tabs, in VRChat's order:

| Tab | Opens |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | This page. |
| **Posts** | The group's posts, at `/analytics/group/posts`. See [Posts](#posts). |
| **Events** | The [Calendar](/moderation/calendar). |
| **Instances** | The [Instances](#instances) page, at `/analytics/instances`. |
| **Gallery** | The group's galleries, at `/analytics/group/gallery`. See [Gallery](#gallery). |
| **Members** | The **Members** view of the [People](/moderation/people) page. |
| **Invites** | The invites the group has sent, at `/analytics/group/invites`. See [Invites](#invites). |
| **Settings** | The group's settings, at `/analytics/group/settings`. See [Settings](#settings). |
| **Banned Users** | The **Bans** page. |

A tab is left out when you may not open the page it leads to, the same as the sidebar. **VRChat**
stays lit in the sidebar on every one of its own tabs.

### Overview

Under the header: the next event on the calendar, the group's **Languages** and **Links**, and
**About this group** (the description and rules).

**About this group** folds away: press its heading. Your browser remembers whether you left it
folded; it starts open.

With **Edit the group's profile**
([roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#edit-the-groups-profile)), each of the three
cards has a pencil that opens its editor in place. **Save** sends the change to VRChat; **Cancel**
puts it back. The limits are VRChat's:

- **Languages**: at most 3, picked from VRChat's list.
- **Links**: at most 3, each a web address starting with `https://` or `http://`.
- **Description**: at most 250 characters. The **Rules** have no limit of Modbot's own.

Everything else on this page asks VRChat nothing when you open it: it is what Modbot last read,
about every five minutes.

### Posts

The group's posts, newest first, with who wrote each, when, its picture if it has one, and who can
see it: **Everyone**, **Members only**, or the roles it is limited to. Modbot reads them from VRChat
when you open the tab, turn a page or press **Refresh**, and at no other time.

With **Manage group posts**:

- **New post**: a title, the text, **Who can see it** (**Members only** or **Everyone**), for a
  members-only post **Only these roles** if it is for some roles, and **Notify members** to have
  VRChat tell them.
- The pencil on a post changes its title, text and who can see it. Members are not notified again,
  and the post keeps its picture.
- The bin deletes it, after you confirm.

Posting a picture is not possible from Modbot yet.

### Gallery

The group's galleries, and the images in one of them, newest first, with who submitted each. An
image still waiting for approval is marked **Pending**. With more than one gallery, the list at the
top picks which one you see. Modbot reads the images from VRChat when you open the tab, pick a
gallery, turn a page or press **Refresh**, and at no other time; the list of galleries comes with
what Modbot already reads about the group every five minutes.

With **Manage the group gallery**
([roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#manage-the-group-gallery)), the bin on an
image removes it from the gallery, after you confirm.

Adding an image is not possible from Modbot yet. Add it on vrchat.com.

### Invites

The people the group has invited who have not answered yet, newest first, with the day each was
invited. Modbot reads them from VRChat when you open the tab, turn a page or press **Refresh**, and
at no other time. **Cancel invite** takes one back, after you confirm.

The tab needs **Manage group invites**
([roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#manage-group-invites)).

### Settings

Everything about the group VRChat lets an admin change on its website, in one form: **Name**
(3 to 64 characters), **Description**, **Rules**, **Languages**, **Links** and **Who can join**
(**Anyone can join**, **Anyone can ask to join**, **Invite only** or **Closed**). **Save** sends only
what you changed; **Cancel** puts the form back. The tab needs **Edit the group's profile**.

Across the top of Settings are **General** (the form above), **Roles** and **Logs**, which opens the
[audit log](/moderation/audit-log). Each is there only if you may open it.

#### Roles

Every role the group has, in VRChat's order, with its description and how many permissions it has.
**Default** marks the role every member has; **Modbot** marks a role Modbot's own VRChat account
holds. Modbot reads the roles from VRChat when you open the tab or press **Refresh**, and at no
other time.

- **New role**: a name, a description, and its **Permissions**, each named the way VRChat's own role
  editor names it.
- The pencil on a role changes its name, description and permissions. **Save** sends only what you
  changed.
- The bin deletes the role, after you confirm. Everyone who had it loses it. The default role has
  no bin: VRChat does not let it be deleted.

Roles needs **Manage group roles**
([roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#manage-group-roles)). Someone with that and
not **Edit the group's profile** finds the **Settings** tab opens at Roles.

The group's icon and banner cannot be changed from Modbot yet. Change them on vrchat.com.

### When VRChat says no

Every **Save**, post, delete, removal and cancel is one request to VRChat, sent as Modbot's VRChat
account. If VRChat refuses, the editor stays open and shows VRChat's own message; nothing is changed
and nothing is tried again. The usual reason is that Modbot's VRChat account lacks the group
permission for it, and then Modbot says which one, the roles the account has, and links to the
group's Roles page on vrchat.com:

| What you did | The VRChat permission it needs |
|---|---|
| Change the profile or settings | Manage Group Data |
| Post, change or delete a post | Manage Group Announcement |
| Create, change or delete a role | Manage Group Roles |
| See or cancel sent invites | Manage Group Invites |
| Remove a gallery image | Manage Group Galleries |

If VRChat is limiting how fast Modbot may ask, try again later.

Every change VRChat accepts is written to the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log) with who made it:
**Group profile changed** (each field, with what it was and what it became), **Posted in the
group**, **Group post changed**, **Group post deleted**, **Group role created**, **Group role
changed** (each field, with what it was and what it became), **Group role deleted**, **Group invite
cancelled** (on the invited person's history) and **Gallery image removed**.

### This week

Under the overview cards, **This week** has four numbers for the last seven days, today included:

- **New members**, **Left** and **Join requests**, each against the seven days before.
- **Most online at once**: the most of the group's members in VRChat at the same moment this week,
  with when it happened. It has no figure from last week beside it: a peak is one moment.

Press **This week** to open [Stats](#stats).

## Discord

The Discord page needs the Discord bot. Under the server's header, **In voice** lists the voice
channels with somebody in them and who is in each, as Discord's channel list does. It is there for
anyone who may open Live, and is read again every 30 seconds. Then **This week** has **New
members**, **Talked**, **Messages** and **Time in voice** for the last seven days, each against the
seven before. Press **This week** to open the [Activity](#activity) tab of Stats.

The server's charts are on [Stats](#stats): who comes and goes on **Growth**, messages and voice on
**Activity**, and moderation actions on **Moderation**.

For every panel in detail, and how messages are stored, see
[Discord messages and analytics](/discord/messages-and-analytics).

## Instances

The **Instances** tab of the VRChat page, in the row under the group's header, shows what
vrchat.com shows there: **Open right now** (group instances open at this moment, when there are
some) and **Recent instances** (the most recent instances of the last 30 days). The command palette
finds it as **Instances**, and `g` then `i` opens it. Its address is `/analytics/instances`.

The charts about when the community is active are on the [Activity](#activity) tab of Stats.

## Stats

The page opens on **Growth**. The tabs are at `/stats/growth`, `/stats/activity` and
`/stats/moderation`; `g` then `t` opens Stats, and `g` then `w` opens Activity. Each tab starts with
the [**Insights**](/ai/insights) that answer its question, if AI insights are set up.

### Growth

**Is the community growing or shrinking, and what changed?**

#### VRChat group

The stats along the top:

- **Members**: the group's member count as VRChat last reported it, with the date it was read.
- **Joined** and **Left**: joins and leaves recorded in the range.
- **Net change**: joined minus left in the range.

Then the peak, with the moment it happened, in your own time zone:

- **Most members**: the highest member count VRChat reported in the range.

It is read from the five-minute member count readings, so the moment shown is a real reading and
not a day. Where the same number was reached more than once, the **earliest** time is shown, so the
figure does not move about between two loads of the same page.

**What the peaks rest on.** Modbot can only find a peak among the readings it has. If the range
reaches back further than Modbot has been running, or [retention](/self-hosting/data-retention) has
deleted the older readings, the peak is the highest Modbot saw and not necessarily the highest there
was. When fewer than half the days in the range carry a reading, the page says so above the stats —
**Readings on 2 of 30 days in this range** — and with no readings at all, **No member count readings
in this range**.

| Panel | What it shows | Comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Member count** | The member count VRChat reported, reading by reading, with the number of members online in VRChat drawn over it in green on its own scale at the right. Hover a point for both numbers and the time it was read. | Member count readings |
| **Joins and leaves per day** | Joins and leaves, side by side, per day. | Daily totals |
| **Joined minus left, running** | A running total of recorded joins minus recorded leaves, counted from zero on the first day Modbot recorded. This is not the member count: a group that already had 5,000 members when Modbot started still starts at zero. | Daily totals |
| **Invites and join requests** | **Invites sent**; **Invites accepted** (the share of invited people who joined within 7 days); **Join requests**; **Requests decided** (approved / rejected); and a chart of invites and requests per day. | Daily totals, except invites accepted (fact log) |
| **Roles** | Each role VRChat lists for the group, with how many times it was **Given** and **Taken away** in the range. Badges mark **moderation** roles, roles **given on join** and **self-service** roles. Shows when the role list was last read. | Fact log |
| **How long members have been members** | Current members grouped into **Under 7d**, **7d to 28d**, **28d to 3mth**, **3mth to 1y** and **Over 1y**. | Fact log |

**Member count** has range buttons of its own: **Day**, **Week** (the default), **Month** and
**All**. Modbot reads the group's member count and online member count about every five minutes
and keeps every reading. Long ranges show up to about 500 of them, spread evenly across the range;
before the first stored reading, the chart shows one point per day from the group's recorded
changes. Readings older than the presence [retention](/self-hosting/data-retention) window are
deleted if one is set.

**How long members have been members** ignores the range: it is about who is a member now. It only
counts members whose join Modbot recorded, and says how many that is. Members who joined before
Modbot started recording are not counted here.

#### Discord

Members, joins and leaves, the member count, new members who stayed, and who the members are now
(links to VRChat, new accounts joining, how long members have been here). A line at the top says how
many channels the bot can read and whether it can read the audit log. See
[Discord messages and analytics](/discord/messages-and-analytics).

### Activity

**When is the community busy, and where?**

#### VRChat instances

The stats along the top are **Most online at once** (the most of the group's members in VRChat at the
same moment, anywhere, not only in your instances), **Opened**, **Manually closed** and **Naturally
ended** (instances in the range), **Typical time open** and **Most open at once**.

##### Peaks

Under them is a second row of peaks, each with the moment it happened, in your own time zone. All
four are built from [head counts](#head-counts) — VRChat's own numbers — so they cover instances
nobody from the team was standing in.

| Stat | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Most people at once** | The most people in all of the group's instances added together, at one moment. |
| **Fullest instance** | The most people in a single instance at one moment, with the world it was in. |
| **Busiest day** | The day with the most **people-time**, shown as the most people in instances at once that day. |
| **Busiest hour** | The single clock hour with the most people-time, on a real date — not "Saturdays at 8". Shown as how many people were there on average, to the nearest person; hover it for the exact figure. |

**People-time** is people added up over the minutes they were there: one person for an hour and
sixty people for one minute are both one hour. Busiest is measured that way rather than by the peak,
because a night that touched forty people for five minutes is not a busier night than one that held
twenty for six hours. For "Saturdays at 8" — the same hour across weeks — use **When the community
is active** below.

Where the same height was reached twice, the **earliest** moment is shown, so the figure does not
move about between two loads of the same page. A range with nothing recorded shows a dash rather
than a zero: nothing was recorded is not the same statement as the peak was nought.

##### Coverage

A peak is only as good as the time Modbot was counting. Two hours of counting in a week gives a
peak that is real and is **not** the week's peak, and nothing about the number itself says which one
you are looking at. So the page measures it.

Modbot compares two amounts of time inside the range:

- how long the group's instances were **open**, added up across instances, and
- how much of that time it actually **had a head count** for — an instance counts from its first
  stored reading onwards, and one Modbot never read contributes nothing.

When the second is less than half the first, the page says so above the stats: **Head counts cover
19% of the time instances were open**. With no head counts at all in the range, it says **No head
counts in this range**.

The peaks are still shown when coverage is thin. They are marked, not hidden — a real peak from a
short window is still worth seeing, as long as you know that is what it is.

##### Panels

| Panel | What it shows | Comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **People in instances** | People in all of the group's instances, moment by moment, with how many instances they were spread across drawn over it in green on its own scale at the right. Hover a point for both numbers and the time. | Head counts |
| **When the community is active** | A week-by-hour grid. Switch between **People arriving** and **Instances opened**. Hours are shown in your own time zone (named in the title), rounded to whole hours. | Presence reports (arrivals), instance list (instances opened) |
| **Opened, manually closed and naturally ended per day** | Instances opened, closed by a moderator and ended on their own each day. | Daily totals (opened and manually closed), instance list (naturally ended) |
| **Most open at once, per day** | The most group instances open at the same time each day. | Instance list |
| **Most people at once, per day** | The most people in all of the group's instances at one moment, each day. | Head counts |
| **Busy time per day** | People-time per day, in hours. A long steady evening reads higher than a short rush. | Head counts |
| **Typical time open, per day** | The middle time from open to end, over the instances that ended that day, however they ended. The stat at the top is the same figure for the whole range. | Instance list |
| **Most people seen in one instance, per day** | The biggest crowd a moderator's companion **saw** in a single instance each day. | Presence reports |

**People in instances** has range buttons of its own: **Day**, **Week** (the default), **Month** and
**All**, because it is drawn from readings taken every thirty seconds rather than from whole days.
Long ranges show up to about 500 readings spread evenly across the range, and every point is a
number VRChat reported at the time shown. The line is a staircase because a count is stored only
when it changes: each reading holds until the next.

**Most people at once, per day** and **Most people seen in one instance, per day** look similar and
are not the same figure. The first is every instance added together, from VRChat's own counts, and
is complete whether or not anyone was watching. The second is one instance at a time, from what a
companion actually saw, and is nought on a day nobody with the client was around. If the range has
fewer than 200 presence reports the page says so, and with none it says so in that panel too.

VRChat only records an instance closing when a moderator closes it by hand, and that is what
**Manually closed** counts. Most instances just empty out and drop off the group's list. Modbot sees
that in its own instance list, and those are **Naturally ended**: the ended instances no moderator
closed. **Typical time open**, **Most open at once**, the opening hours in the grid and the worlds
and team figures all count from that instance list, so an instance that emptied out is counted with
the time it really ended. Each natural end also gets an entry in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log):
"ended on its own", written by Modbot and never worded "closed". It is written only after the audit
log has been read past the end, so a close by hand that VRChat reports late is not labelled that
way. The first time Modbot runs with this, it adds an entry for each instance that ended before,
dated when it ended; those are not posted to Discord. Instances that ended before Modbot began
reading the audit log get "ended on its own" too, because any close by hand from then cannot be known.

#### VRChat worlds

If there are no presence reports in the range, the page says **No presence reports in this range.**
If there are fewer than 200, it says how many, as a reminder that the figures are thin.

The stats along the top are **Worlds**, **Time seen**, **Visitors** and **Presence reports**.

**Worlds, by time people were seen in them** lists each world with:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **World** | Name, author and world id. Click the name to open the world. |
| **Holds** | The world's capacity. |
| **Time seen** | Total time people were seen in the world. |
| **Visitors** | Different people seen there. |
| **Arrivals seen** | Times someone was seen arriving. |
| **Instances** | Group instances Modbot saw open in the world: the same instances the world's popup lists. |
| **Last seen** | When someone was last seen there. |

**Visitors per day, busiest worlds** charts visitors per day for the five busiest worlds.

Time, visitors and arrivals come from presence reports. **Instances** comes from Modbot's own
instance list and is complete whether or not a client was there. Visitors per day comes from daily
totals and outlasts a short retention window.

#### Discord

Messages and voice, active members, the busiest channels and hours, who went quiet and the top
contributors. Names open that person's popup. "Active" means the person sent a message or spent
time in voice; bots are never counted.

### Moderation

**Is moderation keeping up, and is the team OK?**

#### Team

Moderation work is counted in two kinds that are never added together:

- **Actions on people**: instance kicks, warns, bans, removals from the group and join requests
  rejected. These are the actions [Reviews](/moderation/reviews) count.
- **Door and admin**: invites, join requests approved, unbans and role changes.

A moderator who lets people in and hands out roles is not one who throws people out, and one total
would make them look the same.

##### You

Your own numbers come first: **Actions on people**, **Door and admin**, **Days active** (days you
took any action) and **Actions on people per active day**. Beside each is the **team middle**: half
the moderators active in the range are at or below it. Beside the last is also **your usual**: the
same number over the 90 days up to yesterday, the one Reviews compare a busy day against. The team
middle is left out when fewer than four moderators were active, because on a team that small it
would be one person's own number or give it away. Your numbers are found by the VRChat account linked to your Modbot
account.

##### The stats along the top

- **Moderators active**: how many people took a moderation action in the range.
- **Actions on people** and **Door and admin**: the team's totals.
- **Left without a moderator**: how many times the last moderator left an instance while people
  were still in it (see below).
- **Instances with no moderator in them**: group instances (from Modbot's instance list) that
  opened with no companion in them while they ran, out of all instances opened.

##### Left without a moderator

A gap is a stretch of time when people were in a group instance and no moderator was. It
starts when the last moderator leaves while people are still there, and ends when a moderator is seen
again or the instance closes.

A moderator counts as present when their companion is reporting from the instance, or when
another client sees someone Modbot recognises as a moderator (they hold a role with moderation
permissions, have taken a moderation action, or own the group). A moderator without the client, in
an instance no client is in, cannot be seen at all.

The buttons (**1+ people**, **3+**, **5+**, **10+**) say how many people in one instance mean it
wants a moderator. The group's choice is saved: it starts at **3+**, and when somebody with
**Change settings** picks another, it is saved for everybody. Anyone else can pick another to look
at, and it is not saved.

The grid shows the hours of the week, in your own time zone. An hour is **busy** when a group
instance held at least that many people at some point in it, by VRChat's own head count, which
needs no companion. The darker a square, the more of that weekday's busy hours at that time had
**nobody on**: a gap was open in a busy instance. A striped square had busy hours no companion
reported from, so Modbot cannot tell whether a moderator was there. Point at a square, or tap it,
for "Fri 20:00 · 3 of 4 busy hours with nobody on · 1 not seen".

The table lists the gaps with at least that many people left behind:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Began** | When the last moderator left. |
| **Lasted** | How long until the gap ended, or "unknown". |
| **People left behind** | How many people were there when the last moderator left. |
| **Last moderator out** | Who left last, or "a client stopped reporting". Only with **See the audit log**. |
| **Ended because** | "a moderator arrived", "the instance was closed", or "nothing more was reported". |
| **Instance** | The world (click to open it) and instance number. |

##### How long things wait

The middle wait of everything decided in the range, so one request left over a weekend does not
outweigh the rest:

- **Join requests**: from the request to its approval or rejection, from VRChat's audit log. A join
  by invite is not counted: either no request came before it, or the request was already answered.
- **Flags**: from when an [AutoMod](/moderation/automod) rule flagged something to when a moderator
  dismissed it or confirmed it.
- **Reviews**: from when a [review](/moderation/reviews) opened to when it was closed.

Under each is how many were decided. The chart shows the chosen one's middle wait for each day
something was decided.

##### Acted on again and Bans lifted

- **People acted on**: people with an action on them in the range, of the kinds the
  [repeat offender rule](/moderation/reviews) counts. **Acted on again within 30 days**: how many of
  them had another such action in the 30 days before one of those.
- **Bans**: bans in the range. **Lifted within 30 days**: of the bans in the range that are at
  least 30 days old, how many were lifted within 30 days, however they were lifted; under it, that
  as a share and how many bans it is out of. A newer ban has not had its 30 days yet, so it is
  left out rather than counted as one that stood.
- Under them, the reasons picked when a ban was lifted from Modbot in the range, most used first,
  and **No reason given** for lifts without one. A ban lifted in VRChat itself has no reason.

These are the team's numbers. They are never shown per moderator.

##### Actions

- **Actions per moderator**: only with **See the audit log**, which already shows who did what.
  One row per moderator with **Actions on people**, **Door and admin**, **Days active**, a column per
  kind of action (**Instance kicks**, **Warns**, **Bans**, **Removed from group**, **Requests
  rejected**, **Invites**, **Requests approved**, **Unbans** and **Role changes**) and the day they
  were **Last active**. Rows are sorted by last active, most recent first, then by name, so whoever
  has gone quiet is at the bottom; never by how much anybody did. Click a name to open their
  [popup](/moderation/popups).
- **Actions per day**: actions on people or door and admin per day, by the buttons.
- **What kind of actions**: each kind of action, largest first.

People who can review tickets also see a **Reviews of unusual patterns →** button that opens
[Reviews](/moderation/reviews): on **Actions per moderator**, or on **Actions per day** for someone
who cannot see each moderator.

The action counts come from daily totals. Gaps come from presence reports in the fact log, and busy
hours from VRChat's head counts.

#### Discord

**Moderation actions per day**: bans, kicks, timeouts and messages removed. Without the audit log,
kicks and removed messages are missing, and the panel says so.
