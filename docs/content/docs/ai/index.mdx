---
title: AI
description: Every AI page in Modbot. None of it runs until you connect a provider.
---

- **[AI settings and providers](/ai/settings)** turn AI on and connect a provider you choose.
- **[Insights](/ai/insights)** are short AI-written summaries of your group's own figures.
- **[Unusual activity](/ai/alerts)** tells you when a figure Modbot watches runs far outside your
  own normal.
- **[Chat](/ai/chat)** answers questions about your group in plain words.
- **[MCP server](/ai/mcp)** lets Claude, ChatGPT and other apps use Chat's tools with your own account.
- **[Limits and spend](/ai/limits-and-spend)** show what AI costs and set money limits.
- **[Call log](/ai/call-log)** lists every AI call Modbot made.

[AutoMod](/moderation/automod) is built on the same provider and lives with the moderation pages.
