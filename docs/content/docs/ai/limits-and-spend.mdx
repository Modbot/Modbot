Every AI feature in Modbot sends paid requests to your provider. **Settings** → **AI** → **Limits**
shows what each feature has cost, estimates the month's bill, and lets you set money limits so no
feature, and nobody using Chat, can run it past what you are willing to pay.

The page needs the **Change settings** permission, including its list of top Chat users.

## Features

Spend is counted and limited per feature:

| Feature | What counts |
|---|---|
| **Moderation** | AI topic checks, including **Try it** with AI topics ([AutoMod](/moderation/automod)) |
| **Insights** | Scheduled insights and **Generate now** ([insights](/ai/insights)) |
| **Chat** | Every step of every Chat reply ([Chat](/ai/chat)) |
| **Connection test** | The **Test** button on **Base** ([AI settings](/ai/settings)) |

## How spend is counted

AI providers charge by **tokens**: small pieces of text, roughly three quarters of a word each in
English. Input tokens are what is sent to the model, output tokens are what it writes back, and cached
input tokens are input the provider has seen recently and charges less for.

After each request, Modbot records the tokens your provider says it used (input, cached input and
output), the model, the feature, and for Chat, **Generate now** and **Test**, the person who asked.
It turns tokens into money when it shows or checks spend:

1. **OpenRouter's own cost.** When your provider is **OpenRouter**, Modbot asks OpenRouter what each
   request cost and uses that figure exactly.
2. **A price you entered** for the model, on the price list below. It always wins over a fetched
   price.
3. **A price fetched from OpenRouter's public model list.**
4. **No price.** The cost is **unknown**, never zero.

Because prices are applied when spend is read, a price you enter today also prices what that model
used earlier.

Unknown spend is shown as **Unknown**, or beside a known amount as, for example, "$1.20 + unknown".
Hover over a figure to see its input, cached and output tokens and how many have no price.

<Callout type="warn" title="Unknown spend cannot reach a limit">
A model with no price has no cost Modbot can compare with a money limit, so its use is never stopped
by one. Make sure every model you use has a price. Check the **Price from** column for **No price**.
</Callout>

If your AI server does not report token counts at all (some local servers do not), Modbot has
nothing to record, and limits cannot stop it either.

Days, weeks and months are counted in UTC. A week starts on Monday.

## Spend by feature

The **Spend by feature** card has one row per feature and a **Total** row, with columns **Today**,
**This week**, **This month**, **Last month** and **Month-end estimate**. Each shows money with the
token count underneath.

The **Daily spend** card is a chart of the last 30 days, one colour per feature, stacked. It shows
**Part unknown** when some of those days include spend with no price.

## The month-end estimate

The estimate is deliberately simple, so you can check it by hand:

```text
estimate = spent so far this month
         + (spent over the 7 whole days before today ÷ 7) × days left in the month after today
```

The seven days can reach back into last month, which is what makes the estimate usable on the first
of the month. If some of that spend has no price, the estimate says so.

## Spend limits

A limit is an amount in US dollars **per day**, **per month**, or both. You can set limits for:

| Applies to | Compared with | Stops |
|---|---|---|
| **Everyone** | Every feature's spend added together | Every feature |
| A feature (**Moderation**, **Insights**, **Chat** or **Connection test**) | That feature's spend | That feature |
| A role | Each person with that role, separately: their own Chat spend | Chat, for that person |
| A user | That person's own Chat spend | Chat, for that person |

A role limit is not a shared pot. A $2 daily limit on the Moderator role lets each moderator spend $2
a day on Chat.

Role and user limits only apply to **Chat**, because Chat is the one feature a person uses over and
over. They do not apply to **Generate now** or **Test**, even though those also record who pressed
them.

### Adding a limit

<Steps>
<Step>

### Choose what it applies to

In the **Spend limits** card, open **Add a limit for…** and pick **Everyone**, a feature (under
**Features**), a role (under **Chat · roles**) or a user (under **Chat · users**). Press **Add**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Enter the amounts

Fill in **Per day (USD)**, **Per month (USD)**, or both. Leave a box empty for no limit in that
period. Amounts must be zero or more.

</Step>
<Step>

### Save

Press **Save**. The whole list is saved at once: a row you removed with **Remove** is gone once you
save.

</Step>
</Steps>

Each thing can only have one limit row. For each row the card also shows **Spent today**, **Spent
this month** and the **Month-end estimate**, with how far through the limit each one is (for
example "45%"). Role rows show **Each member** instead, because each member is compared separately.

A limit of **0** stops the feature (or the person) completely.

### How a limit stops a request

- Modbot checks limits **before** each request, never during one. A request that starts just under a
  limit can finish a little over it; the next one is refused. Your provider is not called for a
  refused request.
- When several limits apply, the tightest one stops it.
- A limit resets at the start of the next UTC day or month.

What happens when a limit is reached:

| Feature | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Chat** | New replies are refused with a sentence naming the limit. A reply already under way finishes. |
| **Moderation** | AI topics stop and the reason is shown in **Try it**. Term lists keep working. |
| **Insights** | Scheduled insights are skipped (not written late). **Generate now** shows the sentence. |
| **Connection test** | **Test** shows the sentence instead of calling the provider. |

The sentences name the limit:

- "Your daily AI spend limit is reached." (a user limit)
- "The Moderator role's monthly AI spend limit is reached." (a role limit)
- "The monthly AI spend limit for Chat is reached." (a feature limit)
- "This Modbot's daily AI spend limit is reached." (the limit for everyone)

The first time each limit stops a request in its day or month, Modbot records **AI spend limit
reached** in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log), once per limit per period. It is an operational
entry, so seeing it needs **See the operational log**.

## Use AI in excess of usage limits

The **Use AI in excess of usage limits** permission
([roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#use-ai-in-excess-of-usage-limits)) takes away
the limits on a person and on their roles, so they are not stopped by a user or role limit in Chat.

**The limit for everyone and the feature limits still apply to them.** Those are your ceiling on the
bill and each feature's share of it; no permission spends past them.

People with **Administrator** are treated the same way: user and role limits never stop them, but the
limit for everyone and feature limits do.

**Use AI in excess of usage limits** is not part of the built-in **Moderator** or **Viewer** roles.

## Token limits from earlier versions

Earlier versions of Modbot could have a monthly **token** limit on **Moderation** or **Insights**,
from before Modbot knew prices. If your deployment has one, it appears in the **Spend limits** card as,
for example, **Moderation · tokens**, and can only be removed.

Modbot changes each one into a monthly money limit for that feature as soon as the feature's model has
a price, working out the amount from the mix of tokens that feature has used. If the feature already
has a lower monthly money limit, the lower one is kept. Until then the token limit keeps counting and
stops the feature with "The monthly AI token limit for Moderation is reached." (or Insights).

## Top Chat users this month

The **Top Chat users this month** card lists the ten accounts that spent most on Chat this month, with
what they spent and how many tokens they used. It helps decide whether a user or role limit is
needed.

## Prices

The **Prices per million tokens** card lists every model in use: the models set on **Base**, **Chat**
and **Insights**, every model that has been used, and any model you have priced.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Model** | The model name exactly as it is sent to your provider. |
| **Input (USD)** | Price per million input tokens. |
| **Cached input (USD)** | Price per million cached input tokens. Left empty, cached input costs the same as input. |
| **Output (USD)** | Price per million output tokens. |
| **Price from** | **Entered** (your price), **OpenRouter** with how long ago it was fetched, or **No price**. |

The boxes hold prices you entered. When a box is empty, the fetched price shows faintly as its
placeholder.

- **To enter a price**, type the input and output prices (both are needed) and press **Save**.
- **To go back to the fetched price**, press **Clear** on that row and **Save**.
- **To price a model not listed**, type its name in the **Model** box under the table (it suggests
  models that have been used), press **Add**, fill in the prices and **Save**.

Like limits, the whole price list is saved at once.

### Fetched prices

**Fetch prices** downloads OpenRouter's public list of model prices straight away. It needs no key.
The card shows when prices were last fetched, or **Not fetched**.

Modbot also fetches automatically once a day, but only while AI is on and the provider on **Base** is
**OpenRouter**. A deployment using any other provider, or a local model, never contacts OpenRouter
unless someone presses **Fetch prices**. If an automatic fetch fails, Modbot tries again an hour
later; if OpenRouter says it is receiving too many requests, Modbot waits a day.

<Callout type="info" title="Model names must match exactly">
A fetched price is used only when the model name matches OpenRouter's name for it exactly. OpenRouter
names models with the company in front (for example `openai/...` or `anthropic/...`). If you use a
provider directly and its model names are written differently, the fetched prices will not match:
enter prices for those models by hand.
</Callout>

## Warnings on the Health page

The **Sync health** page shows an **AI spend** card when a limit for everyone or for a feature:

- has reached 80% of its amount,
- has a month-end estimate over its monthly amount, or
- is reached.

Each line shows what was spent, the limit, the percentage and, for a monthly limit, the estimate. The
card is amber while close and red once a limit is reached. User and role limits are not listed there.
Seeing the Health page needs **See the operational log**. See
[Checking a deployment](/self-hosting/health).
