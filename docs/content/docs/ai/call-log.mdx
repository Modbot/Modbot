---
title: Call log
description: Every AI call Modbot made — feature, model, tokens, cost, time taken and what went wrong — and what the model was sent for the calls that flagged something.
---

Every time Modbot asks an AI model anything, it writes down what happened. **Settings** → **AI** →
**Call log** is that list.

It is the place to look when a feature has gone quiet, when the bill is higher than you expected, or
when you want to know exactly what the model saw before it flagged somebody.

## What each row says

| Column | What it is |
|---|---|
| **When** | When the call was made |
| **Feature** | Moderation, Insights, Chat or Connection test |
| **Model** | The model that answered, marked **Fallback** when it was the second one |
| **Outcome** | See below, with the error underneath when there was one |
| **In**, **Cached**, **Out** | Tokens sent, tokens the provider had already cached, tokens written back |
| **Cost** | What the call cost, or a dash when the model has no price |
| **Took** | How long the provider took |
| **Asked by** | The person who pressed the button, when a person did |

### Outcomes

| Outcome | What happened |
|---|---|
| **Answered** | The model answered. |
| **Timed out** | The provider did not answer within the feature's time limit. |
| **Error** | Modbot could not reach the provider, or what came back was not an answer it could read. |
| **Refused** | The provider answered with an error — a bad key, a model it will not run, a rate limit. |
| **Limited** | One of your own [spend limits](/ai/limits-and-spend) stopped the call. Nothing was sent and nothing was charged. |

A call that did not answer and could have gone better on another model is tried once more on your
[fallback model](/ai/settings#the-fields). Both attempts get their own row, so the log always shows
which model actually wrote the answer.

## Filters

Filter by feature, by outcome, by model, and by a time range. **Flagged only** narrows the list to
the calls that produced a flag. **Show more** loads the next hundred rows.

## Seeing what the model was sent

Rows with an **Open** button keep the whole prompt and the whole answer. Those are:

- calls that produced a [flag](/moderation/flags)
- calls you started yourself: the **Test** button, **Try it** on
  [AutoMod](/moderation/automod), and **Generate now** on [insights](/ai/insights)

Every other call keeps counts only. The prompt of a routine moderation check is somebody's profile
text or Discord message, and keeping a copy of every check would build a second copy of your members
that nobody asked for.

On the [Flags](/moderation/flags) page, a flag that came from an AI topic links straight to the call
behind it.

## Who can see it

| What | Permission |
|---|---|
| The list, with counts, models, costs and errors | **See operational log** |
| The prompt and the answer behind one call | **Change settings** |

They are different permissions because they are different things to show. The counts are Modbot's own
record of what it did. The prompt and the answer are a member's own words — so reading them needs the
same permission that decides where those words get sent in the first place.

See [roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions).

## How long rows are kept

Thirty days by default. Change it with **Keep the call log for (days)** on
**Settings** → **AI** → **Base**; `0` keeps them for ever. Modbot deletes older rows once a day.
