---
title: Insights
description: Short AI-written summaries of your group's own figures, written on a schedule, shown on My Group and posted to Discord.
---

An **insight** is a short piece of writing by your AI model about Modbot's own figures for a day or a
week: what went up, what went down, and what stands out compared with the stretch before. Each
insight is stored with the exact figures the model was given, so anyone reading it can check every
sentence against the numbers.

Insights are about the group as a whole. The model is only given counts and world names, never a
person's name, id or messages, so an insight cannot turn into a report about somebody.

You set insights up under **Settings** → **AI** → **Insights**, which needs the **Change settings**
permission. AI has to be set up and on under [Base](/ai/settings) first; until then the page shows
**AI is off.**

## The three kinds

| Kind | What it covers | Figures the model gets |
|---|---|---|
| **Group** | The group as a whole: members, how people join, how active it is | Members at the end, as VRChat reported; joined; left; invites sent; join requests; join requests approved and rejected; bans; rooms opened; Discord messages (only on deployments that have ever counted one); busiest worlds by visitors |
| **Moderation team** | How much moderation happened, and of what kind | All moderator actions; how many moderators took any action; kicks from a room; warnings; bans; unbans; removals from the group; invites sent by moderators; join requests approved and rejected; role changes; reviews opened; ban write-ups |
| **Rooms** | The group's instances: how many ran, for how long, how busy | Rooms opened; typical minutes a room stayed open; most people in one room; busiest rooms by most people at once (world and opening time); worlds by rooms opened |

The **Moderation team** kind counts how many moderators acted, never who did the most.

Every figure comes with the same figure for the stretch before, so the model can compare. The
figures come from the same [daily totals](/analytics#where-the-numbers-come-from) as the Analytics
pages.

## What a day and a week mean

Insights cover whole days in UTC, ending **yesterday** (UTC). Today is never included, because it is
not over.

- **Day**: yesterday, compared with the day before.
- **Week**: the seven days ending yesterday, compared with the seven days before that.

The **Time zone** setting only decides *when* a scheduled insight is written. It does not move the
day boundaries of the figures.

## Settings

The first card, **Insights**, has two settings shared by every kind:

- **Time zone** is the zone the hour on each kind is in. Left on **UTC**, hours are UTC.
- **Model** is the model insights use. Left empty, they use the model on **Base** (shown as the
  placeholder).

Then there is one card per kind (**Group**, **Moderation team**, **Rooms**), each with:

- **On**, to write this kind on its schedule.
- **Every**: **Day** or **Week**.
- **Day**: the day of the week, when **Every** is **Week**.
- **At**: the hour it is written.
- **Discord channel**: where to post it. Leave empty to keep it in Modbot only.

**Save** on any card saves the whole page.

## When scheduled insights are written

Modbot checks once a minute for an insight that is due.

- If the daily totals have not yet caught up with the day being summarised, it waits for them, for up
  to an hour. After that it writes the insight anyway.
- If you switch a kind on, or change its time, after today's time has already passed, nothing is
  written straight away. The first one comes at the next scheduled time.
- Each scheduled time is tried once. If the provider fails, Modbot does not try again until the next
  scheduled time.
- If AI is off, or a [spend limit](/ai/limits-and-spend) for everyone or for **Insights** is reached,
  that time is skipped. The insight is not written late once the limit resets.

## Generate now

Each kind's card has **Generate now**. It writes one insight straight away, for the day or week
(following that kind's **Every** setting) ending yesterday, and waits for the model. The result
appears on the card, or the error if the model could not be reached.

**Generate now** is for checking what an insight looks like:

- It is **never posted to Discord**.
- It is stored like any other insight, so a successful one also appears on **My Group**.
- It is a real AI request, and counts towards spend under **Insights**. If a spend limit is reached,
  the card shows that limit's sentence and nothing is written.

The bottom of each card always shows the most recent attempt of that kind, whether it worked or not,
and any problem posting it to Discord.

## Where insights appear

### On My Group

The [My Group](/analytics#my-group) analytics page shows an **Insights** panel above its charts. It
shows the latest insight, with a switch between kinds when there is more than one, and a picker for
earlier ones by date. Open **Figures** under the text to see the table of figures the model was
given, with both stretches side by side.

Reading insights needs **See analytics**. Attempts that failed are not shown there. The panel does
not appear at all until there is at least one insight.

### In Discord

When a kind has a **Discord channel**, each scheduled insight is posted there as one card, titled
with the kind and the days (for example "Group: 14 September 2026"), with the model's name in the
footer.

- The Discord bot must be connected and able to see the channel, send messages and embed links in
  it. The channel picker checks these.
- If the bot is offline when an insight is written, it is posted when the bot comes back, as long as
  that is within a day. An insight more than a day old is not posted.
- If the channel is gone or the bot is not allowed to post there, Modbot records the problem and
  does not try again. Other failures are tried again, for up to a day.

See [Setting up the bot](/discord/bot-setup).

## What the model is told

The model is told to use only the figures given, never to invent a number, cause or event, to lead
with what changed most, to say when a reason is a guess, and to keep to about 220 words of plain
English. It is told never to recommend banning, removing, warning or acting against anyone, and
never to give a score or grade.

These are instructions, and a model can ignore them. That is why the figures are kept beside every
insight: if a sentence and the figures disagree, the figures are right.

For a note the moment something runs far outside your own normal, rather than a summary on a
schedule, see [Unusual activity](/ai/alerts).
