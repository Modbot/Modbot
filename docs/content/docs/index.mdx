Modbot is moderation, analytics and automation for one VRChat group. You host it yourself. It is
open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence.

Modbot keeps a copy of what VRChat's API tells it about your group, records the history VRChat does
not keep, and gives your staff tools VRChat does not have: searchable member and ban lists, a full
audit log, case files with evidence, analytics, a Discord bot, a companion with a SteamVR
overlay, and optional AI features.

One Modbot looks after one group. If you run two groups, you run two Modbots.

## What it does today

Each section below has an overview page that lists every feature in it.

### Moderation

See [who is in your group's instances](/moderation/live) right now, plan [events](/moderation/calendar)
once and publish them to VRChat, Discord and [Google Calendar](/moderation/google-calendar), sort them with
[event categories](/moderation/event-categories), and write
[posts](/moderation/marketing) and [giveaways](/moderation/giveaways) ahead of time. Search
[everyone Modbot has seen](/moderation/people), [members and bans](/moderation/members-and-bans) and
[join requests](/moderation/join-requests), keep [notes](/moderation/notes) and
[case files with evidence](/moderation/case-files), take [reports](/moderation/reports) from members,
and let [AutoMod](/moderation/automod) flag what breaks your rules. See the
[moderation overview](/moderation).

### Discord

A [bot](/discord/bot-setup) that runs inside Modbot. It posts your [events](/discord/event-channels)
and [open instances](/discord/instance-announcements), [links members' accounts](/discord/account-linking),
lets staff [act from Discord](/discord/acting-from-discord), and looks after your server with a
[join gate](/discord/join-gate), [role menus](/discord/role-menus), [tickets](/discord/tickets),
[role and ban sync](/discord/role-and-ban-sync) and [permission watch](/discord/permission-watch).
See the [Discord overview](/discord).

### The companion

A Windows program for moderators. It [reads VRChat's log on your PC](/companion/install) and reports
who is in your group's instances, shows it in [SteamVR](/companion/overlay), lets you leave
[heads-ups](/companion/heads-ups) for other staff, save [clips](/companion/clips), use your
voice with [Listening](/companion/listening) and swap your microphone between a Discord call and VRChat with
[Talk in Discord or VRChat](/companion/talk). See the [companion overview](/companion).

### AI (optional)

[Connect a provider you choose](/ai/settings) and nothing AI-related runs before that. Then you get
[Insights](/ai/insights), [unusual activity alerts](/ai/alerts), [Chat](/ai/chat) (also through
[MCP](/ai/mcp)) and AI rules in [AutoMod](/moderation/automod), with [limits and spend](/ai/limits-and-spend)
to keep the cost under control. See the [AI overview](/ai).

### Accounts and integrations

[Staff accounts](/accounts/staff-accounts), [roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions)
and [notifications](/accounts/notifications) decide who can see, do and hear what; see the
[accounts overview](/accounts). [Twitch](/integrations/twitch) shows when your channel is live; see the
[integrations overview](/integrations).

### Everything else

[Now](/now) is the page Modbot opens on. [Analytics](/analytics) covers your group, your Discord
server, your team, your worlds and your instances, and holds the [VRChat page](/analytics/vrchat-group),
where staff read and change the group's profile, posts, gallery, invites and roles. [Appearance](/appearance) is the themes, Desk and
Headset, and the phone layout. [The API](/api) lets your own tools read Modbot's data, with
[API keys](/api/keys), [live events over WebSocket](/api/events-websocket),
[long polling](/api/long-polling) and [webhooks](/api/webhooks). Things Modbot does not do are on
[Not built yet](/not-built-yet).

## The pieces

| Piece | What it is | Do you need it? |
|---|---|---|
| **The Modbot server** | One program that holds the API, the web app, the VRChat sync and the Discord bot. It runs as one container. | Yes |
| **PostgreSQL** | The database. Everything Modbot knows and every setting lives here. | Yes |
| **The web app** | What you and your staff open in a browser. It is served by the Modbot server, so there is nothing extra to install. | Comes with the server |
| **The companion** | A Windows program each moderator can install to report presence from VRChat and show the overlay. | Optional |
| **The Discord bot** | Part of the server. It starts when you give Modbot a bot token. | Optional |
| **An AI provider** | A service you already have an account with, used for AI moderation rules, insights and chat. | Optional |
| **A storage bucket** | Somewhere to keep evidence files. Modbot can also keep them on disk or in the database. | Optional |

See [What you run](/self-hosting) for more on each one.
