Modbot is moderation, analytics and automation for one VRChat group. You host it yourself. It is
open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence.

Modbot keeps a copy of what VRChat's API tells it about your group, records the history VRChat does
not keep, and gives your staff tools VRChat does not have: searchable member and ban lists, a full
audit log, case files with evidence, analytics, a Discord bot, a companion with a SteamVR
overlay, and optional AI features.

One Modbot looks after one group. If you run two groups, you run two Modbots.

## What it does today

### Moderation

- **[Live](/moderation/live)** shows your group's open instances right now and who is in each one.
- **[Calendar](/moderation/calendar)** plans events once and publishes them to VRChat's group
  calendar, Discord and a calendar feed, and can open the instance on time.
- **[Popups](/moderation/popups)** open a person, a world or an instance on top of whatever page you
  are on, with its history.
- **[Members and bans](/moderation/members-and-bans)** can be searched and sorted, which VRChat's own
  lists cannot.
- **[Profiles](/moderation/profiles)** keep what Modbot has seen of a VRChat user over time.
- **[Audit log](/moderation/audit-log)** shows every event from your group's VRChat audit log, next
  to what happened inside Modbot itself.
- **[Bans and case files](/moderation/case-files)** record why someone was banned, by whom, with the
  details written down.
- **[Evidence](/moderation/evidence)** lets staff attach screenshots and videos to a case file.
- **[Reviews and repeat offenders](/moderation/reviews)** help you look at patterns: people who keep
  coming back, and moderators whose actions need a second look.
- **[Flags](/moderation/flags)** list what your AI moderation rules picked up.

### The companion

- **[The companion](/companion/install)** is a Windows program for moderators. It reads
  VRChat's log on your PC and reports who is in your group's instances.
- **[Pairing](/companion/pairing)** connects a client to your Modbot.
- **[The SteamVR overlay](/companion/overlay)** shows that information while you are in VR.

### Discord

- **[The Discord bot](/discord/bot-setup)** runs inside Modbot. You give it a bot token.
- **[Event channels](/discord/event-channels)** post group events to the Discord channels you choose.
- **[Instance announcements](/discord/instance-announcements)** post your open instances for members.
- **[Account linking](/discord/account-linking)** lets members link their Discord and VRChat accounts.
- **[Messages and My Server](/discord/messages-and-my-server)** and
  **[Discord members](/discord/members)** cover your Discord server itself.

### AI (optional)

- **[AI settings](/ai/settings)** connect Modbot to an AI provider you choose. Nothing AI-related
  runs until you do.
- **[Moderation rules](/ai/moderation-rules)**, **[Insights](/ai/insights)** and
  **[Chat](/ai/chat)** are the features built on it.
- **[Limits and spend](/ai/limits-and-spend)** keep the cost under control.

### Analytics, staff and the API

- **[Analytics](/analytics)** covers your group, your Discord server, your team, your worlds and
  your instances.
- **[Staff accounts](/accounts/staff-accounts)** and **[roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions)**
  decide who can see and do what.
- **[The API](/api)** lets your own tools read Modbot's data, with
  [API keys](/api/keys), [live events over WebSocket](/api/events-websocket),
  [long polling](/api/long-polling) and [webhooks](/api/webhooks).

## The pieces

| Piece | What it is | Do you need it? |
|---|---|---|
| **The Modbot server** | One program that holds the API, the web app, the VRChat sync and the Discord bot. It runs as one container. | Yes |
| **PostgreSQL** | The database. Everything Modbot knows and every setting lives here. | Yes |
| **The web app** | What you and your staff open in a browser. It is served by the Modbot server, so there is nothing extra to install. | Comes with the server |
| **The companion** | A Windows program each moderator can install to report presence from VRChat and show the overlay. | Optional |
| **The Discord bot** | Part of the server. It starts when you give Modbot a bot token. | Optional |
| **An AI provider** | A service you already have an account with, used for AI moderation rules, insights and chat. | Optional |
| **A storage bucket** | Somewhere to keep evidence files. Modbot can also keep them on disk or in the database. | Optional |

See [What you run](/self-hosting) for more on each one.
