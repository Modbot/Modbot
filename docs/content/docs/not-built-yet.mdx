<Callout type="warn" title="None of this works yet">
Everything on this page is **not built**. It is listed so you know it is missing, not because it
is coming on any date. The rest of these docs only describe what Modbot does today.
</Callout>

## Moderation

- **Warning somebody.** Modbot can kick, ban and unban a person in the group; it cannot warn one,
  and it is not going to. A warning in Modbot would only ever have been a record — and that is what
  a [note](/moderation/notes) is. VRChat's group log does hold the warnings moderators give inside
  an instance, and Modbot shows them; it cannot send one. The **Warn** permission still exists and
  does nothing.
- **Acting on many people at once.** Every action is one person at a time. The **Act on many at
  once** permission exists but does nothing yet; see
  [Roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#moderation).
- **A check-back day on a case file or a join request.** Only a [watch](/moderation/notes#watching-a-person)
  carries one. To be reminded about somebody in a case, watch them with a **Check back on** day.
- **A list of everybody being watched.** Each watch is on its person's **Notes** tab, and the ones
  with a check-back day that has come are on [Now](/now).
- **Kicking somebody out of an instance.** **Kick** removes a person from the group. Modbot reads
  instance kicks other moderators make on VRChat from the group log; it cannot make one.
- **Opening an instance whenever you like.** Modbot opens an instance only for a
  [calendar](/moderation/calendar) event. There is no button to open one.
- **Banning or flagging by the avatar somebody wears.** VRChat's log gives an avatar's name and
  never its id, so two avatars with one name cannot be told apart, and no rule looks at the avatar.
- **Acting on a [list](/moderation/lists).** A list can be seen, exported, used by a giveaway or
  auto-invites, and [give a Discord role](/discord/roles-from-lists). It cannot be messaged, in
  Discord or in a VRChat group post, and it cannot be handed to a ban, a kick or a VRChat group role
  change.
- **A rule for days seen in a row.** **Days seen, at least** counts days, not an unbroken run of them.
- **Charting other public groups.** Tracked groups would chart other groups' member counts beside
  your own. There is no page for it.

## Google Calendar

- **Pictures on Google.** An event on [Google Calendar](/moderation/calendar#google-calendar) has
  no picture: Google takes attachments only from Google Drive, and Modbot has no Drive.
- **Reading changes back from Google.** Modbot only writes to the Google calendar. A change made on
  Google is replaced by Modbot's next change to that event, and is never brought into Modbot. Edit
  events in Modbot.
- **An "Add to Google Calendar" link for each event.** Google's add-one-event links ignore repeats,
  and the copy they make never changes or cancels when the event does. Members follow the whole
  calendar instead, with its [public links](/moderation/google-calendar#making-it-public-and-the-links).
- **Signing in with Google.** Modbot uses a key for its own Google address, which you share the
  calendar with. There is no "Sign in with Google" button that lets Modbot use your own Google
  account.

## Bluesky

- **Pictures in a Bluesky post.** A Bluesky post carries the post's picture only on its link card,
  as a small copy, and only when the text has a link. A post with no link goes to Bluesky as text
  only. Pictures shown in the post itself are not built.
- **Choosing a Bluesky post's language.** Bluesky can mark a post's language; Modbot's posts carry
  none.
- **Editing a Bluesky post.** Bluesky cannot edit a post, so there is no **Edit** for one. Delete it
  and send a new one.

## Companion

- **A check, when the client first starts, that VRChat is writing the lines it reads.** The client
  only warns after ten minutes of lines it cannot understand; see
  [Warnings in the window](/companion/install#warnings-in-the-window).
- **Keeping things drawn over VRChat out of a [clip](/companion/clips).** A clip is the part of
  your screen VRChat's window sits in, so a chat overlay or a notification drawn on top of the game
  is in it. Everything outside that window never is, and nothing at all is recorded while you are
  working in another program.
- **Recording clips on Linux.** Windows only, and not planned: the [Clips](/companion/clips) page
  says why.
- **Any program's sound in a clip but VRChat's and Discord's.** Those two, and nothing else your PC
  is playing. There is no setting for the rest and there is not going to be one — see
  [the sound](/companion/clips#the-sound).
- **A volume for each program in a clip.** VRChat and Discord go in at whatever they are playing at.
  If one drowns out the other, change it in Windows' own volume mixer.
- **Sound in a clip on Windows older than version 2004.** That is where Windows learned to hand over
  one program's sound rather than everything the speakers are playing. On an older Windows the clip
  records as usual and is silent, which is better than recording your music.
- **Saving a clip automatically when you ban or kick somebody.** A ban taken in Modbot never
  reaches the companion, so it cannot know one happened. You press **Save a clip**, or say
  [**"Modbot, clip that"**](/companion/listening), and then attach the file to the case yourself.
  With **Tell the group's Modbot when I save a clip** ticked, the case file lists the clip and
  [checks it is the right file](/companion/clips#attaching-a-clip-to-a-ban-a-kick-or-a-case) when you
  attach it, but choosing the file is still yours to do, in a browser.
- **Starting the attach from the companion.** Attaching a clip starts on the case file page. The
  companion does not open a case file, and does not hand the browser the file.
- **Avatar ids in crash details.** [**Copy crash details**](/companion/install#crash-details) names
  each person's avatar by its name only, because VRChat's log never has another person's avatar id.
- **Choosing your own phrases for [Listening](/companion/listening).** The phrase matcher is given
  the small pieces its vocabulary is built from rather than words, and turning a sentence into
  those pieces needs a library the companion does not ship. The name and the commands were worked
  out once, using the model's own tokenizer, and written into the client.
- **Turning the overlay on by voice.** Saying "Modbot, show overlay" puts the headset panel back in
  front of you; it does not switch the overlay on when you have switched it off. It says so rather
  than appearing to work.
- **A second saved microphone for [Listening](/companion/listening), to switch between.** There is
  one list of the microphones your PC has and you pick one, which covers a headset microphone and a
  desk microphone without a second control that does the same job as the first.
- **Listening on Linux.** Windows only, for the same reason recording is: saying "Modbot, clip
  that" saves a clip, and there are no clips anywhere else.
- **A spoken reason when the voice is off.** With the voice on, everything you say is answered out
  loud, including when nothing could be done. With the voice off, something that worked plays the
  notification sound and something that did not plays nothing — one short sound cannot say which
  reason it was. The reason is on the card it belongs to and in the **Events** page either way.

## Discord

- **Inviting somebody to the group when they link their Discord account.** Linking gives them the
  roles you have paired and nothing else. Modbot can invite people who are already spending time in
  your instances — that is [Auto-invites](/moderation/auto-invites) — but it is not tied to linking.
- **A case file for a copied ban.** [Role and ban sync](/discord/role-and-ban-sync) copies the ban
  itself and keeps the reason the moderator typed in Discord, but it picks no reason from your ban
  reason list and writes no case file — nobody here chose one.
- **Discord's own check against a post sent twice.** Discord can refuse a second message with the
  same id sent within a few minutes (`enforce_nonce`), but the library Modbot uses cannot send that
  id. A [post](/moderation/marketing#never-posted-twice) is kept to once by writing it down before
  sending and looking in the channel after an unclear answer instead.

## Notifications

- **Notifications to your browser or phone when the page is closed.** Notifications reach a person
  by email and by Discord message. Web push is not built.

## Word lists

- **Being told a shared word list you imported has a newer version.** A newer version shows on the
  list in **Settings → AutoMod**; nothing is sent to you.
- **Picking word lists during setup.** The [setup wizard](/setup-wizard) has no step for them.

## Across groups

- **Shared warnings**: flags and warnings shared between groups.

## AI

- **Email alerts** when AI spend nears a limit. Limits themselves work; see
  [Limits and spend](/ai/limits-and-spend).
