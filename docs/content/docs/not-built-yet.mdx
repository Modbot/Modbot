<Callout type="warn" title="None of this works yet">
Everything on this page is **not built**. It is listed so you know it is missing, not because it
is coming on any date. The rest of these docs only describe what Modbot does today.
</Callout>

## Moderation

- **Warning somebody.** Modbot can kick, ban and unban a person in the group; it cannot warn one,
  and it is not going to. VRChat has no warning to deliver, so a warning in Modbot would only ever
  have been a record — and that is what a [note](/moderation/notes) is. The **Warn** permission
  still exists and does nothing.
- **Acting on many people at once.** Every action is one person at a time. The **Act on many at
  once** permission exists but does nothing yet; see
  [Roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#moderation).
- **Destroying evidence from the web app.** The server can do it through the
  [API](/api/reference), but there is no button for it.
- **Segments**: saving "our regulars" as a named list you can come back to, export, or act on all at
  once. The rules themselves are built, in [Giveaways](/moderation/giveaways).

## Companion

- **A list of paired devices in the web app.**
- **Keeping things drawn over VRChat out of a [clip](/companion/clips).** A clip is the part of
  your screen VRChat's window sits in, so a chat overlay or a notification drawn on top of the game
  is in it. Everything outside that window never is, and nothing at all is recorded while you are
  working in another program.
- **Recording clips on Linux.** Windows only, and not planned: the [Clips](/companion/clips) page
  says why.
- **Saving a clip automatically when you ban or kick somebody.** A ban taken in Modbot never
  reaches the companion, so it cannot know one happened. You press **Save a clip**, or say
  [**"Modbot, clip that"**](/companion/listening), and then attach the file to the case yourself.
- **Choosing your own phrase for [Listening](/companion/listening).** The phrase matcher is given
  the small pieces its vocabulary is built from rather than words, and turning a sentence into
  those pieces needs a library the companion does not ship. The four spellings of "Modbot, clip
  that" were worked out once and written into the client.
- **A second saved microphone for [Listening](/companion/listening), to switch between.** There is
  one list of the microphones your PC has and you pick one, which covers a headset microphone and a
  desk microphone without a second control that does the same job as the first.
- **Listening on Linux.** Windows only, for the same reason recording is: saying "Modbot, clip
  that" saves a clip, and there are no clips anywhere else.
- **A spoken reason when the voice is off.** With the voice on, saying "Modbot, clip that" is
  answered out loud, including when nothing could be saved. With the voice off, a saved clip plays
  the notification sound and a clip that was not saved plays nothing — one short sound cannot say
  which of six reasons it was. The reason is on the **Clips** card and in the **Events** page
  either way.

## Discord

- **Inviting somebody to the group when they link their Discord account.** Linking gives them the
  roles you have paired and nothing else. Modbot can invite people who are already spending time in
  your instances — that is [Auto-invites](/moderation/auto-invites) — but it is not tied to linking.
- **A reason on a copied ban.** [Role and ban sync](/discord/role-and-ban-sync) copies the ban
  itself, and the copy names the ban that caused it, but it picks no reason from your ban reason
  list and writes no case file — nobody here chose one.

## Across groups

- **Shared warnings**: flags and warnings shared between groups.

## AI

- **Email alerts** when AI spend nears a limit. Limits themselves work; see
