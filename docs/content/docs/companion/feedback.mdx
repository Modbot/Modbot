Something is broken, or you wish Modbot did something else, and you are in the middle of an instance
with no keyboard. **Feedback** lets you say it out loud: tap a button, speak, tap again, check the
draft and send it. Admins read it on the **Feedback** page of the web app.

## Where it is saved

**On your own Modbot server, and nowhere else.** Nothing is sent to Modbot Cloud, to GitHub or to any
other site. If your admins want something fixed, they read it on the Feedback page and decide for
themselves.

## Sending feedback

1. Tap **Feedback**: at the top of the companion's main window, under your group's name; in the strip
   along the top of the [desktop overlay](/companion/settings#desktop-overlay), next to **Dictate**; or
   on the small button panel in the headset. The button reads **Listening** while you speak.
2. Say what you want to tell the admins.
3. Tap the button again. Your words are written out as text on your PC, the same way
   [Dictate](/companion/dictate) does it. Your voice is never saved or sent.
4. A draft shows the **kind** (**Bug**, **Idea** or **Change**), a **title** and the **details**.
   Change any of them.
5. Press **Send**, or **Cancel** to throw it away. Nothing is saved until you press **Send**.

Feedback and Dictate share your microphone, so only one of them listens at a time.

## In the headset

**Feedback** is the third row on the [show and hide button](/companion/overlay#the-show-and-hide-button),
under **Dictate**. It shows even when Dictate is off. The draft opens in its own panel in the headset
when you tapped the button there, and in a window on your monitor when you tapped it anywhere else.

The headset has no keyboard, so the words are shown as they were said. Pick **Bug**, **Idea** or
**Change**, then press **Send**, or **Cancel** or the **X** to throw the draft away. A long title or
long details are cut off in the panel only; **Send** sends all of it.

The panel is held the same way as the other panels:

- **Lock** stops it being picked up, moved or resized.
- **Hand** makes it click-through, so pointing and clicking go to VRChat behind it.
- You can pick it up and move it, and drag its corner to make it bigger or smaller.

The **Lock** and the **Hand** are in the bar under the panel when **Edit mode** is on. Where you put it
is remembered, separately from the Chat panel, and two quick grips send it back to its own spot. See
[the bar under the panel](/companion/overlay#the-bar-under-the-panel) for what the buttons do.

## With AI and without

When [AI is set up](/ai/settings), your server tidies the words into a kind, a title and details, and
takes out the names of people, worlds and groups. You can still edit all of it before you send.

When AI is off, or it cannot answer, the draft is simply what you said, and you pick the kind
yourself. The button shows either way: it does not need [AI Chat](/ai/chat) to be on.

## Asking Chat to file it

If [AI Chat](/ai/chat) is on and an admin has switched on its **File feedback** tool
([Chat settings](/ai/chat#chat-settings)), you can describe a bug, a missing feature or a change in a
chat instead. Chat offers to file it, shows you the draft with names of people, worlds and groups
taken out, and files it only after you say yes. It lands on the same Feedback page, saved the same
way, with you as the sender. You need **Send feedback** for it.

## The Feedback page

Admins open **Feedback** under **System** in the web app's menu, or in the phone's menu. It lists what
staff sent with the date, who sent it, the kind, the title and where it stands. Press an item to read
it in full, and mark it **New**, **Reviewed**, **Done** or **Dropped**. The page can be narrowed to one
status.

Changing a status is written to the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log) with who did it. The log names
the status and never holds a copy of the words.

## Who can do what

| Permission | Lets a person |
|---|---|
| **Send feedback** | See the Feedback button and send feedback. In the built-in Moderator role and Administrator. |
| **Review feedback** | Open the Feedback page, read what was sent and set its status. Administrator holds it; no other built-in role does. |

See [Roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#send-feedback) for both.
