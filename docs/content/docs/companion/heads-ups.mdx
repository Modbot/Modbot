---
title: Heads-ups
description: Short notes a moderator leaves from the overlay for the other staff in the same VRChat instance, what leaves your PC when you place one, and who sees it.
---

A heads-up is a short note you leave, from the [overlay](/companion/overlay) in your headset or on
your monitor, for the other staff standing in the same VRChat instance. Their overlay shows it, and
a card pops up for them when it arrives.

VRChat tells Modbot nothing about where anyone stands in a world, so a heads-up is words and
nothing more. It cannot mark a spot in the world.

## Kinds

| Kind | About | Ends when |
|---|---|---|
| **Pin** | the instance | someone clears it, or the instance closes or empties |
| **Keep an eye** | one person, with an optional short note | someone clears it, or the instance closes or empties. It stays if the person leaves; if they come back, everybody in the instance gets a card. |
| **Message** | one person, or the instance | someone clears it. One about a person also ends when they leave. |
| **Ask for help** | a place you pick from a list: Entrance, Spawn, Bar, Stage, Dance floor, Back room, Outside | someone clears it, or the instance closes or empties |

**Keep an eye** and **Message** have nothing to do with watching a person or the
[notes](/moderation/notes) on a person's page on the website. Those last until someone ends them and
are kept in the audit log. A heads-up is gone when the instance is.

## Placing one

1. On the **Instance** list, press the **+** at the end of a person's row: the controller's
   trigger in the headset, or a click on the desktop overlay.
2. Pick the kind. **Pin** and **Ask for help** are about the instance, so the row you started from
   does not go with them. For **Message**, pick the person or **Instance**.
3. For **Ask for help**, pick the place.
4. Write the note: tap the box for SteamVR's keyboard, or type on the desktop overlay.
   **Pin** and **Message** need words; the other two do not. A headset with no keyboard (WiVRn,
   Monado) can still place the two that need no words.
5. Press **Place** (or Enter on the desktop overlay). **Cancel** drops it, and nothing is sent.

A note is at most 140 characters of plain text. Links are refused. At most 20 heads-ups can stand
in one instance at a time.

Standing heads-ups are listed above the roster, each with **Clear**. Any staff member in the
instance can clear any of them. A person with one on them has a small mark on their row.

## What leaves your PC

Nothing, until you press **Place** or **Clear**. Then one request goes to the paired server whose
group owns the instance you are standing in, with the companion's token:

- **Place** sends the instance's number and world, the kind, your words, the place for **Ask for
  help**, and, for **Keep an eye** or a **Message** about a person, that person's VRChat id and the
  name the roster showed.
- **Clear** sends only which heads-up.

The server notes which Modbot account placed or cleared it, and when.

## Who sees it

Only the staff whose companions the server believes are standing in the same instance: the ones
that have read that instance's roster or reported from it in the last ten minutes. A companion
elsewhere is not told, and cannot place or clear one there.

Heads-ups are not written to the audit log, are not shown anywhere on the website, and are never
sent to Discord. The server keeps each one, with who placed it and when it ended, so that "who
placed this?" can still be answered.

## How fast it arrives

Over the live connection, the other overlays in the instance are told at once and read it straight
away. When a companion has fallen back to ordinary requests (the **Servers** page says
**Polling**), it arrives with the next roster read, at most 20 seconds later.
