---
title: Dictate
description: Speak a question to AI Chat from the headset panel or the desktop overlay. Your words are written out on your PC, sent to AI Chat, and the answer shows in a Chat panel. Your voice is never saved or sent.
---

In a headset you have no keyboard. **Dictate** lets you ask [AI Chat](/ai/chat) out loud instead of
typing: tap a button, say your question, tap again, and the answer appears in your headset. On a
monitor it works the same way from the [desktop overlay](#on-the-desktop).

## Using it

1. Tap **Dictate** on the small button panel, under **Hide** or **Show**. The microphone picture moves
   and the button reads **Listening**.
2. Say your question.
3. Tap the button again. It reads **Writing** while your words are written out as text.
4. Your words are sent to AI Chat, and the answer appears in the **Chat** panel as it is written.

To ask a follow-up, tap **Dictate** again. The new question goes into the same conversation.

The conversation is the same one you see on the website's [Chat page](/ai/chat), so you can carry on
there with a keyboard, or read it again later.

If nothing was said, or nothing could be made out, nothing is sent and the button goes back to
**Dictate**.

## On the desktop

The [desktop overlay](/companion/settings#desktop-overlay) has a **Dictate** button in the strip along
its top, next to **Close**. It works the way the headset button does: click it, say your question,
click it again. The answer shows in a Chat panel next to the overlay.

You can also start and stop Dictate with a key, which is useful while VRChat has the keyboard. The
key is set in the app's **Settings**, on the **Desktop overlay** card, in the **Dictate** row: press
**Record** and then the keys you want. **Clear** takes it away again. There is none until you record
one, and it works while the desktop overlay is switched on. Press it once to start and again to stop,
just like the button.

## When the button is there

The **Dictate** button, on the headset panel and on the desktop overlay, only shows when both of these are true:

- AI Chat is switched on for your server ([Chat](/ai/chat#who-can-use-it)).
- You are allowed to use it: your role has **Use AI chat**
  ([roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#use-ai-chat)).

If either stops being true, the button goes away. The companion checks when it starts, every five
minutes, and when you pair it with a different server.

## The Chat panel

The answer shows in its own panel, in your headset or in a window on your monitor. It is held the
same way as the other panels:

- **Lock** stops it being picked up, moved or resized.
- **Hand** makes it click-through, so pointing and clicking go to VRChat behind it.
- You can pick it up and move it, and drag its corner to make it bigger or smaller.
- The **X** closes it.

In the headset, where you put it is remembered, and two quick grips send it back to its own spot. See
[the bar under the panel](/companion/overlay#the-bar-under-the-panel) for what the buttons do.

On the desktop the Chat panel is its own window beside the overlay. Drag its top strip to move it and
its bottom-right corner to resize it. The **Lock** and the **Hand** are in the strip when **Edit mode**
is on, the same switch as for the other panels. Its place and size are remembered, and the **X**
closes it until the next time Dictate has an answer to show.

## The first time

The first tap downloads a speech model of about 108 MB, once. The button shows how much has come.
When it is done the button goes back to **Dictate**, and you tap it again to start speaking, so you
are never recorded the moment a download ends.

Dictate understands English only.

## What happens to your voice

- **Your voice is turned into text on your own PC.** It is not sent to a speech service.
- **The sound is never saved.** It is held only until your words are written out, then thrown away.
- **Only the written words are sent**, to AI Chat on your own server, the same as if you had typed
  them.
- The microphone is shared the way a voice chat program shares it, so VRChat and Discord keep working.
  It is the one you picked for [Listening](/companion/listening), or the Windows default.
- The microphone is only open between your two taps.

The model comes from GitHub, once, and nothing about you goes with the request. The
[privacy policy](/privacy) says the same.

## How long you can speak

A recording stops by itself after 60 seconds, as if you had tapped again.
