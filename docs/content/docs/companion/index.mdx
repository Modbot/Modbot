---
title: Companion
description: Every page about the Windows companion and its SteamVR overlay.
---

- **[Installing the companion](/companion/install)** covers the download, updates and what it reads
  and sends.
- **[Preview builds](/companion/preview)** cover trying a preview and going back.
- **[Pairing](/companion/pairing)** connects the companion to your Modbot.
- **[Client settings](/companion/settings)** lists every setting in the companion.
- **[The SteamVR overlay](/companion/overlay)** is the headset panel.
- **[Heads-ups](/companion/heads-ups)** are short notes you leave from the overlay for other staff
  in the same instance.
- **[Clips](/companion/clips)** save the last few minutes of VRChat as a video, and can send one to
  your group's Modbot for a ban, kick or warning you made, if you switch that on.
- **[Listening](/companion/listening)** lets you say "Modbot" and a command instead of using a keyboard.
- **[Talk in Discord or VRChat](/companion/talk)** swaps your microphone between a private Discord call
  and VRChat from a button on the headset panel or a key.
- **[Credits in the client](/companion/credits)** are the sponsors and contributors the client shows.
