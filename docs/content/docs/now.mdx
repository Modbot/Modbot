**Now** is the first page after you sign in. It answers two questions: *what needs me?* and *what
changed?* It lists only things somebody can act on. The numbers and charts are on [Stats](/analytics).

**Now** has no permission of its own. Each part is read under the permission of the page it comes
from, and a part you cannot open is not drawn.

## Modbot's health, in one line

At the top right, one line says how Modbot is doing. It reads **All working**, or names the parts
that are not, such as *VRChat needs you*. Press it and the [Health](/self-hosting/health) page opens
at the part it names.

It needs the **See the operational log** permission, the same one Health needs.

## Needs a decision

Three counts across the top of the card, each opening the page it counts, and under them the newest
rows of each. When a follow-up is due, a fourth count says how many.

| Row | Shows | Comes from | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Follow-up** | The person, the reason they are [watched](/moderation/notes#watching-a-person), and how long the check-back day has been due | Watches whose **Check back on** day has come. All of them. | **See the audit log** |
| **Flag** | The person, the rule and what matched, and how long ago | The [Flags](/moderation/flags) page, open flags only. The newest five. | **See profiles** |
| **Review** | The moderator and the pattern that opened it | [Reviews](/moderation/reviews), open ones only. The newest three. | **Review tickets** |
| **Request** | The person, their trust rank and any history with the group | [Join requests](/moderation/join-requests). The newest three. | **See join requests** |

A follow-up row has **Followed up** for whoever started the watch and anyone with **Write notes**.
It clears the check-back day and the row leaves; the watch carries on.

A request row has **Approve** and **Reject** buttons for someone who holds **Answer join requests**.
Each asks you to confirm, exactly as on the Join requests page. A row you answer leaves the list
without another read.

When there is nothing in any of them, the card says **Nothing waiting**.

**Join requests are read from VRChat, once, each time the page opens.** They are never read on a
timer, and a live event does not read them again. VRChat is the one place the whole queue is, so
this is the one part of **Now** that costs a VRChat request. The count is also what the sidebar and
the browser tab's title show.

## In the group's instances

The group's open instances, with the ones that have flagged people in them first. Under an instance
the flagged people are listed by name, with what flagged them. Everybody else is on
[Live](/moderation/live): this is the part of it that needs somebody.

An instance with nobody flagged says **Nobody flagged**, or **Nobody watching** when no moderator's
companion is in it, so Modbot has no list of who is there. The head count is on the right.

It is read from what Modbot already holds, so it asks VRChat nothing. It reads again when something
happens in an instance and every thirty seconds while the page is on screen. It needs the
[**See live instances**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-live-instances) permission.

## Live on Twitch

While your group's Twitch channel is streaming, a **Live on Twitch** card sits above the instances:
the stream's title and category, how long it has been live, the viewer count and **Open** for the
channel. It is not there when the channel is not live. It needs [Twitch set up](/integrations/twitch)
and the same **See live instances** permission as the instances.

## Since you last looked

What happened in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log) since you last had **Now** open, as one count
for each kind of thing that happened:

**bans, unbans, kicks, warns, case files, notes, flags, join requests, joins and leaves.**

Each count opens the audit log filtered to that kind, from that day. Counts of zero are left out, and
the card says **Nothing new** when they all are. Bans, unbans and kicks include the Discord ones and
kicks from an instance. A warn is a warning a moderator gave inside an instance on VRChat: Modbot has
no warning of its own.

- The first time you open **Now**, the card is called **In the last day** and counts the last 24
  hours.
- After that it is **Since you last looked**, with the time it counts from.
- **The starting point moves only after you have been away for 15 minutes.** Reloading the page,
  or opening it in a second tab, does not reset it.
- Modbot keeps two times on your account for this: when you last had **Now** open, and where the
  count starts. Nothing else about what you looked at is kept. A tab in the background does not
  count as looking.

It needs the **See the audit log** permission. Without it the card is left out.
