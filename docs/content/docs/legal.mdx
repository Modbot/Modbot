## The Credits page

**Credits**, from the link at the bottom of every page in Modbot, lists all of this inside the app.
It has four tabs:

- **General** — who made Modbot, the licence, and the privacy policy.
- **People** — the repository's contributors, the project's sponsors, and the groups that used
  Modbot early. Read from Modbot Cloud and shown as it is: a sponsor with a VRChat group is a
  clickable link to that group. It is empty when your Modbot has Cloud turned off, or cannot reach
  it — nothing else changes.
- **Libraries** — every package Modbot ships, with its version and its licence, and a search box.
- **Services** — the services and data Modbot is built on, and who they belong to.

It is open to everyone signed in, whatever their permissions.
