A **flag** is a note Modbot writes when one of your [AutoMod rules](/moderation/automod) matches
some text: a Discord message, or a VRChat user's display name, bio, status or pronouns. The
**Flags** page lists them so a moderator can read each one and either leave it or dismiss it.

| To | You need |
|---|---|
| Open the **Flags** page | [**See profiles**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-profiles) |
| Press **Dismiss**, **Open a review** or **Ask AI** | [**Review tickets**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#review-tickets) |

A flag is a note, not a finding. Nobody is judged by it.

## What raises a flag

Flags only come from AutoMod rules, set up under **Settings**, **AutoMod**. There are two kinds of
rule:

- **Term lists**: words, phrases or patterns. They are matched inside Modbot and send nothing to an
  AI provider.
- **AI topics**: something to catch, described in your own words. Modbot asks your AI model whether
  the text matches.

Nothing is flagged until AutoMod and at least one rule are switched on. Discord
messages are checked as the bot stores them; VRChat profiles are checked when Modbot first records
a profile and whenever part of it changes. The details are on
[AutoMod](/moderation/automod).

The same rule and term on the same message, or on the same words in the same part of a profile,
is only flagged once.

<Callout type="info" title="Not the same as Flagged on Live">
The **Flagged** mark on the [Live](/moderation/live) page counts past removals and bans. It is not
a moderation rule flag.
</Callout>

## Reading the page

There are three tabs: **Open**, with the number waiting, **Dismissed**, and **Confirmed**. Each
shows the newest 200 flags.

When the flags are in more than one language, a row of buttons above the list filters by language,
with the number of flags in each.

Each flag shows:

- **Who.** Click the name to open their popup: a [person popup](/moderation/popups#person-popup)
  for a VRChat user, or that person's popup from their Discord account.
- **Where.** A **VRChat** or **Discord** badge, and what was checked: **Discord messages**,
  **Display name**, **Bio**, **Status** or **Pronouns**.
- **The language** of the text that was checked, or **Unknown** when it was too short to tell.
- **Which rule**, by name.
- **What the rule did**, when it was set to act on Discord messages: **Message deleted** and
  **Timed out** with the number of minutes.
- **The matched words**, in quotes. For a term list, the term that matched follows in grey. When a
  picture matched instead, a **Picture** badge and the picture's name, which links to it.
- **The messages before it**, when the rule was set to send them, so you can see what the model saw.
- **The reason**, when there is one: a Modbot Hub term's note, or the AI model's one-sentence
  explanation.
- **When** it was flagged. On the **Dismissed** and **Confirmed** tabs, also who decided it and when.
- **AI call**, on a flag an AI topic raised: a link to that call in the
  [call log](/ai/call-log), where you can read exactly what the model was sent and what it answered.

The page does not refresh by itself. Switch tabs or reload to see new flags.

## Dismissing a flag

Press **Dismiss** beside an open flag. It moves to the **Dismissed** tab.

<Callout type="warn" title="Dismissing is permanent for that person">
Once dismissed, **that rule and that term** (for an AI topic, **that topic**) never flags that
person again, and never deletes their messages or times them out again. Other terms in the same
list, and other rules, still apply to them. There is no way to undo a dismissal in the app.
</Callout>

Dismissing does not undo anything the rule already did. A deleted Discord message stays deleted
and a timeout runs its course.

Dismiss a flag when the rule got it wrong for this person: a name that only looks like a slur, a
joke between friends, a word that means something else in their language. If the flag was right,
leave it open, or act on it in Discord or VRChat as you normally would.

Every dismissal is recorded in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log) against your account, with
the rule, the term and the matched words.

## Sending a flag to Reviews

Press **Open a review** beside an open flag to hand it to your team's [Reviews](/moderation/reviews)
page. A rule can also be set to open a review for every flag it raises.

Closing that review asks which way it went. **The rule was wrong** dismisses the flag, with
everything that means above. **The rule was right** confirms it: the flag moves to the
**Confirmed** tab, and the rule's card counts it beside its dismissal rate.

## Good to know

- A rule that is mostly dismissed is mostly noise. The **Term lists** and **AI topics** cards in
  Settings show how often each rule has flagged, how many of those were dismissed, how many were
  confirmed, and the dismissal rate for each language separately.
- Deleting a term list or topic keeps the flags it already made.
- A rule starts as flag only, and most rules stay that way. A rule that has been set to act can
  delete a Discord message or time its author out, and can ban somebody from your VRChat group or
  remove them from it. It never kicks or warns anybody, and it never acts across platforms: a bio
  match never deletes a message, and a message never bans anybody from the group. See
  [Actions](/moderation/automod#actions).
