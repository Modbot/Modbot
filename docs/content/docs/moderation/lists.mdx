A **list** is a name and a set of rules: "in the group, and seen on at least four different days in
the last month". It is not a copy of who matched once. Every time you open it, Modbot asks the rules
again, so **Regulars** next month is next month's regulars.

The rules are the [giveaway rules](/moderation/giveaways#rules), and Modbot answers them the same
way. A list and a giveaway with the same rules name the same people.

A list never does anything by itself. Nobody is messaged, invited, banned or given a role because
they are in one. What you can do with a list is look at it, export it, and name it as a rule in a
[giveaway](/moderation/giveaways) or in [auto-invites](/moderation/auto-invites).

## Who can use it

| To do this | You need |
|---|---|
| See the **Lists** page, every list's rules, who is in each, and export | [**See members**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-members) and [**See profiles**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-profiles) |
| Make, change and delete lists, and preview rules | [**Manage lists**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#manage-lists), with **See members** and **See profiles** |
| Change a list a giveaway still being run uses | **Run giveaways** as well |
| Change a list auto-invites use | **Set up auto-invites** as well |

Both **See members** and **See profiles** are needed because a list's rules can ask about bans,
flags and 18+ verification: who is in "banned twice" is part of those people's history, not only
their membership. Without both, **Lists** does not appear in the sidebar. **Manage lists** is not in
the built-in **Moderator** or **Viewer** roles. **Administrator** holds everything.

## The page

**Lists** is in the sidebar, under **Community**, after **Giveaways**. `g` then `u` opens it from any
page. Each row shows the list's name, how many people are in it right now, and what uses it: a
giveaway, or auto-invites.

Click a list to open it. You see its rules in words, what uses it, and **Who is in it**: everybody the
rules let through, by name, fifty to a page. A name with a VRChat account opens their
[person popup](/moderation/popups#person-popup).

Above the names: how many people are in the list, out of how many Modbot looked at, how many are
near the line, and when that was worked out.

Who Modbot looks at is everybody in your VRChat group and everybody in your Discord server, joined
up where their accounts are [linked](/discord/account-linking). Somebody who has left both is not in
any list.

### About, and near the line

A list whose rules count presence (hours in your instances, seen in the last so many days, days
seen, or seen before but not lately) says **about 312** rather than **312**. Presence comes from the
reports your moderators' companions send while they are in an instance, which is a good figure but
not an exact one. People whose figure sits within a whisker of a rule's threshold are counted as
**near the line** and shown in amber. [Giveaways](/moderation/giveaways#preview) explains the
threshold.

### When Modbot cannot answer

If you have set a retention window, a rule reaching further back than the facts Modbot still keeps
cannot be answered. The list says so instead of showing a number that looks complete and is not; see
[When Modbot cannot answer](/moderation/giveaways#when-modbot-cannot-answer). **Seen before, but not
in the last** N days asks about all of your presence history, so it cannot be answered at all while a
presence retention window is set.

## Making a list

Click **New list**. Give it a **Name**, up to 100 characters; two lists cannot share a name. Build the
rules in the rule builder, which works exactly as it does in a giveaway. **Preview** shows how many
people the rules let through and the first fifty of them, before you save anything.

A list cannot use another list.

The rules that suit a list best, beside the giveaway ones:

| Rule | Asks |
|---|---|
| **Joined the group in the last** N days | "New this month". |
| **Joined the group before** a day / **on or after** a day | The join date VRChat states, against a day (UTC). |
| **First seen by Modbot in the last** N days | The first time Modbot saw their VRChat or Discord account. |
| **Days seen, at least** N | Different days they were seen in your instances. "Regulars". |
| **Seen before, but not in the last** N days | They used to come and have stopped. Somebody never seen is not counted. |
| **Times moderated, at least** N | Banned, removed from the group, kicked from an instance, warned, or join request turned down, as many times as you say. You pick which. "Turned down twice". |

## Changing and deleting a list

Open the list and click **Edit**. If a giveaway that is still being run, or auto-invites, uses the
list, changing it changes who that giveaway or auto-invites reaches. So it needs their permission as
well as **Manage lists**.

**Delete** is greyed out while anything uses the list. Take the list out of the giveaway's or
auto-invites' rules first. A drawn or cancelled giveaway does not count: a draw keeps its own copy of
the rules.

## Using a list in a giveaway or auto-invites

In the rule builder of a giveaway or of auto-invites, pick **In the list** and the list. The list's
rules are asked at the moment the giveaway or auto-invites would ask their own, so the list as it is
then decides.

- The giveaway's Discord card shows the list's rules, not its name: your members cannot open your
  lists, and the point of the card is that they can see the rules.
- A draw keeps a copy of the rules with the list written out, so changing the list later does not
  change what an old draw says it was drawn from.

## Exporting

Open a list and click **Export**. Pick **CSV** or **JSON**; the dialog shows how many people the file
will hold and its columns. Click **Export** and your browser saves the file.

| Column | In the file |
|---|---|
| **name** | Their VRChat name, or their Discord name if Modbot knows no VRChat account. |
| **vrchatUserId**, **discordUserId** | Their ids, where Modbot knows them. |
| **linked** | Their Discord and VRChat accounts are linked. |
| **inGroup**, **joinedGroupAt** | In the VRChat group now, and since when. |
| **inDiscord**, **joinedDiscordAt** | In the Discord server now, and since when. |
| **trustRank**, **is18PlusVerified** | Their VRChat trust rank, and whether Modbot has seen them 18+ verified. |
| **vrchatAccountCreated**, **firstSeenAt** | The day their VRChat account was made, and when Modbot first saw them. |

Times are UTC. A name that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` is written with an apostrophe in front,
so a spreadsheet shows it as text instead of running it.

<Callout type="warn" title="An exported file leaves Modbot">
Once the file is on your computer, nothing Modbot does reaches it. Your retention window does not
delete it, and [erasing somebody](/moderation/purge) does not take them out of it. Whoever holds the
file is responsible for it.
</Callout>

Every export is recorded in the audit log: who exported which list, as which file, how many people,
and which columns. The people themselves are not in the record.

## In the audit log

| Entry | When |
|---|---|
| **List made** | Somebody made a list. Carries its name and rules. |
| **List changed** | Somebody changed one. Shows before and after. |
| **List deleted** | Somebody deleted one. |
| **List exported** | Somebody exported one. Carries the format, the number of people and the columns. |

They are **Operational** entries in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log), beside the giveaway
entries, so reading them needs
[**See the operational log**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-the-operational-log).

## What a list cannot do

A list cannot be messaged, in Discord or in a group post, and cannot be handed to a ban, kick or role
change. See [Not built yet](/not-built-yet).
