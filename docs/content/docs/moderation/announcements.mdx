---
title: Instance announcements
description: Send a message that pops up for everyone in one of your group's open instances, now or at a time you pick.
---

An instance announcement is a message VRChat shows to **everyone in that instance** at that moment:
a pop-up in the game with a title and a message. Members, guests and anybody else standing there
all see it. It goes out from Modbot's VRChat account, in your group's name. Nobody outside the
instance sees it, and it is not a group post.

This is not the Discord card Modbot can post when an instance opens; that one is set up under
**Settings → Discord**.

## Sending one

On the [**Live**](/moderation/live) page, every open instance's card has an **Announce** button.

1. Press **Announce** on the instance's card.
2. Write a **Title** (up to 100 characters) and a **Message** (up to 500).
3. Pick **Now** or **Later**. For **Later**, pick the date, time and time zone.
4. Press **Send** (or **Schedule**).

**Now** asks VRChat straight away and the form says what VRChat answered. **Later** is sent once,
within about fifteen seconds of its time.

What is sent is plain text on one line: a line break becomes a space, and other control characters
are left out. Pictures are not sent.

## What each card lists

Under the card, **Announcements** lists the ones sent or scheduled to that instance, newest first:

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **Scheduled** | Waiting for its time. **Cancel** calls it off. |
| **Sending** | On its way. |
| **Sent** | VRChat took it. |
| **Refused** | VRChat said no. Its own words and its status are shown. |
| **Failed** | Not sent: the instance closed, it was more than an hour late, VRChat was too busy, or VRChat gave no clear answer (then it may have gone out). |
| **Cancelled** | Called off before it went. |

Modbot never sends an announcement again by itself, whatever happened. To try again, send a new
one. Every announcement, and what happened to it, is written to the
[audit log](/moderation/audit-log) with who sent or scheduled it.

## What it needs

- [**Announce in instances**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#announce-in-instances), and **See live
  instances** to open the Live page.
- Modbot's VRChat account needs **Create Instance Announcement** in your group's roles on VRChat.
  When Modbot knows the account lacks it, it says so and sends nothing.
- It is not yet known whether VRChat wants Modbot's account to be in the instance. If VRChat
  refuses for that reason, the card shows its refusal.

## Rate limit

One announcement every ten seconds. This number is a placeholder: nobody has measured VRChat's real
limit for this call yet. If VRChat answers "too many requests", Modbot stops sending announcements
for a while and does not try that one again.
