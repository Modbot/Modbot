A **note** is your own words about somebody, kept where every other moderator will see them.
"Says they're new, be patient." "Third time this week." "Their friend vouched for them."

Nothing about a note is required. There is no reason to pick and no form to fill in — you type a
sentence and it is there for the next person who opens that name.

<Callout type="info" title="A note is not a case file">
A [case file](/moderation/case-files) is the write-up of one ban: the reasons, a written
justification, evidence and a copy of the person's profile at the time. A note is not about any
action, and most people with a note have never been banned.
</Callout>

## Where notes are

### On a person

Open anybody's popup — click their name anywhere in Modbot — and go to the **Notes** tab. It lists
every note about them, newest first, each with who wrote it and when.

### Before you kick or ban somebody

The confirmation that opens when you press **Kick**, **Ban** or **Unban** shows that person's three
newest notes above the reason buttons. If nobody has written one, nothing appears there. If Modbot
could not read them, it says **Could not read the notes** instead.

## Writing one

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the person's **Notes** tab

You need the **Write notes** permission for the box to be there.

</Step>

<Step>

### Type it and press **Add note**

Up to 2,000 characters. It appears at the top of the list with your name on it.

</Step>

</Steps>

Your note is written exactly as you typed it. Markdown is not applied — asterisks stay asterisks —
so a note can never be made to look like something Modbot itself said.

## Taking a note back

Press **Take back** on a note. A confirmation names the person, with who wrote the note and when;
press **Take back** again to confirm, or **Cancel**. The note stays on the list, greyed and marked **taken back**, with your
name and the time under it. It stops counting and it stops appearing on kick and ban
confirmations.

You can take back a note you wrote. Anyone with **Write notes** can take back anybody's.

<Callout type="warn" title="Taking a note back is not erasing it">
The note's words are still in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log), where anyone who can read
that log can read them. Taking it back records that it no longer stands; it does not make the text
go away. A [purge](/moderation/purge) of the person does erase their notes along with the rest of
the history records about them, and it cannot be undone.
</Callout>

There is no way to edit a note. If one is wrong, take it back and write another. The log is
append-only, exactly as it is for bans: nothing that was recorded is ever rewritten.

## Watching a person

A **watch** is for somebody you want the team to keep an eye on: "Said they would come back on an
alt." "Check on them after the event." A note waits for somebody to open the person. A watch tells
you.

While somebody is watched:

- They are **Flagged** everywhere Flagged is shown: the red row on the [companion
  overlay](/companion/overlay) and the **Flagged user joined** card when they walk in, [Live](/moderation/live),
  and **In the group's instances** on [Now](/now). They say **Watched** and nothing more: your
  reason is only on the **Notes** tab, for anyone who can see the audit log. See
  [Who is flagged](/moderation/live#who-is-flagged).
- When they walk into one of the group's instances, Modbot raises a **notification** to everybody
  who can see live instances and the audit log, saying that a watched person joined a group
  instance. It names nobody and gives no reason; it opens [Live](/moderation/live), where you see
  who. It goes wherever each person gets warnings; see [Notifications](/accounts/notifications).
  Somebody who leaves and comes back is told about once per instance.
- The row under their name in the popup says **Watched**.

A watch never does anything to the person. Nothing changes on VRChat or Discord.

A [Discord channel](/discord/event-channels) that takes **Watch started**, **Watch stopped** or **Followed up** is told that it happened, and who did it, but never the reason.

### Starting one

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the person's **Notes** tab and press **Watch**

You need **Write notes**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Give a reason

Up to 200 characters. It is shown on the **Notes** tab and in the audit log, to anyone who can see
the audit log.

</Step>

<Step>

### Pick the days, if you want them, and press **Watch**

- **Ends**: the watch stops on its own at the end of that day. Leave it empty to watch until
  somebody stops it.
- **Check back on**: from that day the person is listed on [Now](/now) under **Needs a decision** as
  a **Follow-up**, and you get a notification saying a follow-up is due. Like the one above, it names
  nobody.

</Step>

</Steps>

One watch at a time per account. A watch is put on the person's VRChat account when Modbot knows it,
otherwise on their Discord account. A watch on a Discord account linked to a VRChat one flags the
VRChat person too.

### Following up and stopping

- **Followed up** clears the check-back day once you have looked at the person. The watch carries
  on. It is on the watch in the **Notes** tab and on the row on Now.
- **Stop watching** ends the watch. A confirmation names the person and the reason.

Whoever started the watch can do both, and so can anyone with **Write notes**. Starting, stopping
and following up are each an entry in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log): **Watch started**,
**Watch stopped** and **Followed up**. A watch that reached its end day is closed in the log on its
own, with nobody's name on it.

## Notes in the audit log

Every note is an entry in the audit log — **Note added** — and every take back is another one,
**Note taken back**. They show in the person's timeline beside their joins, bans and everything
else, and they go to your Discord log channel if you route them there. See
[Discord event routes](/discord/event-channels).

## Notes brought in from another platform

If you [imported](/self-hosting/importing-old-data) records from an old bot or a spreadsheet, the notes in
that file are in this same list, marked **imported**. They sit beside the ones written here, in
date order, because they are the same kind of record.

## Permissions

| To | You need |
|---|---|
| Read a person's notes | **See the audit log** |
| Write one | **Write notes** |
| Take one back | **Write notes**, or you wrote it |
| See a watch, and follow-ups on Now | **See the audit log** |
| Start a watch | **Write notes** |
| Stop one, or follow up | **Write notes**, or you started it |

**See the audit log** is what opens notes because a note is an entry in that log. Somebody who can
see profiles but not the log does not get a Notes tab.

See [Roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions).
