---
title: Automatic roles
description: Having Modbot give your moderators a VRChat group role or a Discord role while they meet your conditions, and take it away when they stop.
---

Automatic roles tie a role to how a moderator is doing right now. You write a rule: a VRChat group
role, a Discord role, or one of each, and the conditions a moderator has to meet to hold it. While
they meet every condition, Modbot gives them the role. When one stops holding, Modbot takes it away.

One rule might give a role to moderators whose companion is running and has Clips ready. Another
might give a role that can ban to moderators who have passed the staff trial and have not had more
than two bans overturned in the last 30 days.

<Callout type="warn" title="Nothing happens until you add a rule">
There are no rules on a new Modbot. A rule that is saved starts working on its next check.
</Callout>

## Where to find it

**Settings → Automatic roles**. It needs the
[**Change settings**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#change-settings) permission.

The page has two cards. **Rules** lists your rules, with **Add rule**, and **Edit** and **Delete** on
each. **Ask for a clip on** has the switches **Bans**, **Kicks** and **Warnings**; each one saves as
soon as you press it. Which actions ask for a clip, and what the companion does about it, is on the
[Clips](/companion/clips) page.

## Making a rule

Press **Add rule** and fill in the form.

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Your own label for the rule. Optional. |
| **VRChat role** | The VRChat group role to give. |
| **Discord role** | The Discord role to give. |
| **Whole team** / **Chosen roles** | Who the rule is for (below). |
| Conditions | The switches below. |
| **Take back from** | People whose hand-given role Modbot may take away (below). |

Pick a VRChat role, a Discord role, or one of each. Any role in the list will do: Modbot does not
check what the role allows, so look at it before you pick it.

### Who a rule is for

- **Whole team** is every enabled Modbot account that can kick, ban or warn, or pair a companion, and
  every Administrator.
- **Chosen roles** is every enabled account that holds any of the Discord roles or VRChat group roles
  you pick, whether or not they are on the moderator team.

Modbot can only act on someone whose Modbot account has the matching link. A VRChat role needs the
account's [linked VRChat account](/accounts/staff-accounts) to be in your group. A Discord role needs
a Discord account the person has proven (see
[Staff accounts](/accounts/staff-accounts#email-and-discord-account)) to be in your Discord server.
A role is given on the side where that holds and skipped on the side where it does not.

### The conditions

Switch on the ones you want. **A rule needs at least one switched on to be saved**, and the person
has to meet every one that is on.

| Condition | What it checks |
|---|---|
| **Companion running** | One of their paired [companions](/companion/install) has its live connection to Modbot open. |
| **Clips ready** | One of their paired companions has reported that Clips is switched on and set up to send clips with their actions. |
| **In a group instance** | One of their paired companions is in an open instance of your group. |
| **Minimum level** | They have **Passed the staff trial** (a staff trial that was closed), or hold the **Staff role** you pick (a Discord role), or either. Pick at least one; the person needs only one of them. |
| **Overturned bans** | They have had at most **At most** bans overturned in the last **In days**. A ban is overturned when its [appeal](/moderation/appeals) is upheld, counted from the day the appeal was decided, and it counts for whoever made that ban. |
| **Daily ban cap** | They have made at most **Bans a day** bans in the last 24 hours. Bans made in Modbot count, and so do bans made in VRChat's own menu by the person's linked VRChat account. |
| **Missing clips** | They have at most **At most** missing clips in the last **In days**. A clip is missing when Modbot asked for one with an action and it did not arrive: the companion did not answer, could not cut it, could not upload it, or had Clips off. One still being waited for is not missing yet. |

"At most 3" means three is fine and the fourth takes the role. "At most 0" means the first one takes it.

**Clips ready** and **Missing clips** need evidence storage, because a clip has nowhere to go
without it. Until it is set up in **Settings → Evidence** (see
[Storage buckets](/self-hosting/storage-buckets)) the two switches cannot be turned on, and a rule
that has either on is refused when you save it.

### Take back from

Roles that someone already had, or that you gave them by hand, are left alone. To let a rule take a
role you gave by hand, add the person to its **Take back from** list. Modbot then treats that role
like one it gave itself, for that rule only.

## How and when roles change

- **Checks.** Modbot checks the rules when a companion reports something, and again every
  30 seconds.
- **Taking away is immediate.** The role goes at the next check after a condition stops holding.
- **Giving back waits a minute.** A role is not given again until at least a minute after Modbot
  took it, so switching Clips off and on does not make Modbot change a role every few seconds.
- **A pause after Modbot starts.** For the first 2 minutes after Modbot starts it takes nothing away,
  so a restart does not look like every companion going off at once. Giving carries on.
- **Roles you gave by hand stay.** Modbot remembers the roles it gave. A role a person held before,
  or that you gave by hand, is never taken unless they are on **Take back from**.
- **Two rules, one role.** If two rules give the same role, the person keeps it while any one of
  them holds.
- **Turned-off accounts.** When an account is turned off or deleted, Modbot takes back the roles it
  gave that account.
- **Deleting a rule.** Roles Modbot gave for it are taken back at the next check, as someone who is
  no longer on the rule's list.
- **If VRChat refuses.** Modbot makes each change once. If VRChat refuses one, it makes no more
  VRChat changes until the next check.

VRChat and Discord are asked for the person's roles from Modbot's own copy of your group and server
members, which can be a little behind. A change you can see on one side can take a short while to
show up on the other.

## The audit log

Every give and take is written to the [Audit log](/moderation/audit-log) as **Role given
automatically** or **Role taken away automatically**, with the rule, the role and the reason. In
Discord's own audit log the reason reads `Modbot: automatic role - <reason>`.

| Reason | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Every condition is met | Given. |
| Companion not running | **Companion running** stopped holding. |
| Clips turned off | **Clips ready** stopped holding. |
| Not in a group instance | **In a group instance** stopped holding. |
| Minimum level not met | **Minimum level** is not met. |
| Too many overturned bans | **Overturned bans** went over its number. |
| Daily ban cap reached | **Daily ban cap** went over its number. |
| Too many missed clips | **Missing clips** went over its number. |
| A number on the rule is missing | A switched-on condition has no number. The role is not given until it has. |
| No longer on the rule's list | No rule that gives the role is for this person any more. |
| Account turned off | The account was turned off or deleted. |

When several conditions fail at once, the reason is the first one in the order of the table above.
