---
title: Giveaways
description: Run a raffle your members can check. Decide who may enter with rules about time in your worlds, Discord activity and membership; weight the draw by who actually shows up; and publish the seed so anyone can work the result out again.
---

The **Giveaways** page is where you run a raffle. You say what the prize is, who may enter, how many
people win and when it closes, and Modbot does the rest:

- works out who qualifies, from what it has recorded about your group and your Discord server;
- posts the giveaway in a Discord channel, with the rules on the card;
- takes entries, either automatically or by people reacting;
- draws the winners and names them in the channel;
- keeps the whole entrant list, with every weight, so anyone can check the result.

## Who can use it

| To do this | You need |
|---|---|
| See the **Giveaways** page, the rules, who entered and how each draw went | [**See giveaways**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-giveaways) |
| Create, change, open, close, draw and cancel giveaways | [**Run giveaways**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#run-giveaways) and **See giveaways** |

Without **See giveaways**, **Giveaways** does not appear in the sidebar. **Run giveaways** on its own
is not enough to open the page. Neither is in the built-in **Moderator** or **Viewer** roles.
**Administrator** holds both.

## The page

Giveaways are listed newest first. The buttons at the top filter by status: **All**, **Open**,
**Closed**, **Drawn**, **Drafts**, **Cancelled**.

Each row shows the closing date, the name, the status, how many people have entered (for a giveaway
people react to), the winners once it has been drawn, and how the Discord post is doing.

Click one to open it.

### A giveaway's status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Saved, posted nowhere, nobody can enter. |
| **Open** | Published and running. |
| **Closed** | No more entries, not yet drawn. |
| **Drawn** | Drawn at least once. |
| **Cancelled** | Called off. |

## Planning a giveaway

Click **New giveaway**.

### Name and prize

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Required. Up to 100 characters. |
| **Prize** | Optional. Up to 500 characters. Shown on the Discord card. |

### When

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Opens** | When entries start counting. |
| **Closes** | Must be after it opens. At most 180 days later. |
| **Draw** | **By hand**, or **At a time**. |
| **Draw at** | Shown when the draw is at a time. Not before it closes, and within 30 days of closing. |

### Entering

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **How people enter** | **Everyone who matches** or **React on Discord**. |
| **Emoji** | Shown for **React on Discord**. What people react with. One of your server's own emoji works too. |
| **Winners** | 1 to 100. |

**Everyone who matches** needs nobody to do anything: everyone who passes the rules is in.

**React on Discord** posts the giveaway and puts the emoji on it. A person enters by reacting with
that emoji, and withdraws by taking the reaction off. Picking it ticks **Post to channel**, which
then cannot be unticked: without a post there is nothing to react to.

### Rules

The rule builder is a list of rules inside a group. The group is **All of**, **Any of** or **None
of**. **Add rule** adds a row; **Add group** puts a group inside the group, up to three deep.

These are the rules:

| Rule | Asks |
|---|---|
| **In Discord for at least** N days | Their Discord join date. |
| **In the group for at least** N days | The join date VRChat states. |
| **In the group now** | They are a member right now. |
| **Hours in our instances, at least** N | Every hour they have been seen in your instances, added together. |
| **Hours in one single instance, at least** N | The most they have been seen in any one instance. |
| **Hours in Discord voice, at least** N | Time connected to a voice channel. |
| **Discord messages sent, at least** N | Messages they have sent in your server. |
| **Seen in the last** N days | When they were last seen in one of your instances. |
| **Discord and VRChat accounts linked** | They have [linked their accounts](/discord/account-linking). |
| **Holds the group role** | One of your VRChat group's roles. |
| **Holds the Discord role** | One of your server's roles. |
| **No bans, kicks or flags** | No bans, removals, instance kicks, Discord bans, kicks or timeouts, and no [flag](/moderation/flags) a moderator has not dismissed. |
| **VRChat account at least** N days old | The join date on their VRChat profile. |
| **Trust rank at least** | Their VRChat trust rank, from Visitor up to Legend. Nuisance and VRChat Team are not on that ladder, so neither ever counts as "or better". |
| **18+ verified** | Modbot has seen them 18+ verified at least once. See [Profiles](/moderation/profiles). |

**Hours in our instances** and **hours in one single instance** are different questions. The first
finds your regulars; the second finds the people who stayed all evening.

Five of them — instance hours, one instance's hours, voice hours, messages and no trouble — can be
either **All time** or **In the last** so many days.

There is no "does not hold". Put the rule inside a **None of** group instead.

### Preview

**Preview** says how many people match right now: for example, **about 312 of 4,081 · 7 near the
line**.

Modbot knows how long somebody spent in an instance from the reports your moderators' companions
send while they are there. That is a good figure, not an exact one, so a count that depends on it is
shown as **about**, and people whose hours sit within a whisker of a threshold are counted as **near
the line**. For a rule that asks for 10 hours, anyone between about 9 and 11 is near the line.

Voice hours and message counts are exact.

#### When Modbot cannot answer

If you have set a retention window under **Settings**, **Host &amp; Database**, Modbot eventually deletes old facts.
A rule reaching further back than the facts it still has cannot be answered, and the preview says so
instead of giving a number:

> Modbot cannot answer "10 hours or more in our instances in the last 365 days". Presence history is
> kept for 90 days, and the rule asks about 365.

Shorten the rule's window, or keep presence history for longer. The same message appears if you try
to draw.

Voice hours and message counts are never refused this way: they are counted from daily totals, which
Modbot keeps forever whatever your retention window is.

By default nothing is deleted, so this only happens if you have set a window yourself.

### Not eligible

Tick any of **Staff**, **People who have won before** or **Banned members**.

Anybody kept out this way is still in the entrant list, with the reason beside them. They cannot
win, and they can see why.

### Weighting

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Weighted by** | **Everybody the same**, **Hours in our instances**, **Hours in Discord voice**, **Discord messages** or **Days seen**. |
| **Most one person can hold** | Shown when the weighting is not **Everybody the same**. Leave empty for no cap. |

A weight is how many tickets somebody holds. With **Hours in our instances**, a person seen for 40
hours holds 40 tickets and one seen for 3 holds 3.

Weights are whole numbers, never less than 1 and never more than the cap. The 1 matters: somebody
who passed every rule but has no hours recorded still holds a ticket. The cap matters too, because
without one your most dedicated member can hold most of the raffle.

### Discord

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Post to channel** | Posts the giveaway as a card. Always on for **React on Discord**. |
| **Channel** | The channel to post in. The bot needs View Channel, Send Messages and Embed Links there, and Add Reactions for a giveaway people react to. |

### Saving

- **Open** saves it and starts it.
- **Save draft** saves it without posting anything or letting anybody in. Come back to it from the
  **Drafts** filter.
- **Close** leaves without saving.

## Opening a giveaway

Click one in the list. You see its status, how people enter, how many win, the weighting, the prize,
the times, the rules in plain words, who is not eligible, the seed, and every draw.

With **Run giveaways** you also get **Edit**, **Open**, **Close**, **Draw**, **Cancel giveaway** and
**Delete**.

- **A cancelled giveaway cannot be changed.**
- **A giveaway that has been drawn cannot be changed either.** The draw kept a copy of its rules, so
  changing them now would not change the result — but the page would show today's rules beside last
  week's winners, as though the two went together.
- A published giveaway cannot go back to being a draft.

## The draw

Click **Draw**. Modbot:

1. works out who is in, **checking the rules again**;
2. writes the entrant list down, with every weight, and never changes it;
3. reveals the seed it promised;
4. picks the winners from the two;
5. names them in the Discord channel.

### Why the rules are checked twice

For a giveaway people react to, Modbot checks the rules when the reaction arrives **and** again when
it draws. Somebody can qualify on Monday and not on Friday.

Checking only when they entered would let somebody who has since been banned win. Checking only at
the draw would mean a person who does not qualify hears nothing until it is too late to do anything.
So both happen, and the one at the draw decides.

Somebody who entered and no longer qualifies stays in the entrant list, marked with the rule they
failed. They are never quietly dropped.

### The seed

Before any draw, the giveaway shows a **seed promise** — a long string of letters and numbers. It is
a fingerprint of a secret Modbot generated when the giveaway was created and has not shown anybody.

When the draw happens, Modbot publishes the secret itself. Anyone who noted the promise can check
that it is a fingerprint of that secret, which means Modbot could not have picked a secret after
seeing who would win under it.

Modbot checks this for you: a draw whose seed does not match its promise says **Does not match the
promise** in red.

A fresh promise is made at the same moment, for the next draw.

### Working the result out again

You need two things, both on the page: the draw's **seed**, and its **entrant list** (click
**Entrants** under the draw).

For each winner in turn:

1. Add up the weights of everyone still in the hat.
2. Take `HMAC-SHA256(seed, "round:0")` for the first winner, `"round:1"` for the second, and so on.
   Read the first 8 bytes of the answer as a whole number, most significant byte first.
3. Take that number modulo the total from step 1.
4. Walk the list in position order, adding weights, until the running total passes that number. That
   person wins.
5. Take them out of the hat and repeat for the next winner.

Everything is whole numbers, so the answer is the same on every machine.

### Drawing again

**Draw again** makes a **new draw**, with a new number, a new seed and its own entrant list. It does
not replace the first one. Both stay on the page, both are announced in Discord, and both are in the
audit log.

This is on purpose. A result somebody did not like cannot be made to disappear — it can only be
drawn again where everyone can see that it was.

### The entrant list

Click **Entrants** under a draw. Each row shows the position, the name, the weight, the number the
weight came from, and whether they were in the draw.

People who could not win are here too, with the reason: the rule they failed, **Staff**, **Won
before**, **Banned**, **Kept out by name** or **Withdrew**.

A number that came from presence reports is shown as **about**, and a row near a threshold is
highlighted.

An erased person shows as **(erased)**: their position and weight stay, so the draw can still be
checked, and their name and ids are gone. Nobody can be erased yet — see
[If somebody asks to be erased](#if-somebody-asks-to-be-erased).

## In Discord

### The card

The post shows the name, the prize, when it closes, how to enter, how many win, the weighting and
its cap, the rules in plain words, who is not eligible, how many have entered, and the winners once
it has been drawn. Its colour and the line at the bottom follow the status.

The rules are on the card on purpose: a giveaway whose rules are only on a page your members cannot
open is a giveaway they have to take your word for.

Modbot checks every 20 seconds and makes at most 5 Discord changes each time, and only writes the
post when what it should say has changed.

### Reacting

For **React on Discord**, Modbot puts the emoji on its own post so there is something to click.

- Reacting with that emoji enters you. Reacting with anything else does nothing.
- Taking the reaction off withdraws you. Putting it back enters you again — it is not a second
  entry.
- Reactions after it closes do nothing.
- Anyone can react. Modbot does not have to know who you are; whether you qualify is up to the
  rules, and a rule that needs VRChat data simply fails for somebody Modbot has no VRChat account
  for.

### The announcement

When a draw is made, Modbot posts one line in the same channel naming the winners, with a
**Details** button that opens the giveaway in Modbot. Each draw is announced once; drawing again
gets its own line.

**Nobody is pinged**, not even the winners. A giveaway with four hundred entrants would otherwise be
four hundred notifications for one result.

## Closing and drawing on time

Modbot checks every 30 seconds:

- a giveaway whose **Closes** time has passed is closed;
- a closed giveaway whose **Draw at** time has passed is drawn.

If Modbot was down when a draw was due, it draws when it comes back.

If a draw cannot be made — nobody is in it, or a rule is no longer answerable — Modbot records why
and clears the draw time rather than trying again every half minute against a reason that will not
fix itself. Deal with the reason and press **Draw**.

## If somebody asks to be erased

**Settings → Purge a person** removes everything Modbot stores about somebody. It asks for
Administrator, and it cannot be undone.

It takes the person out of every giveaway: their standing entries deleted so they cannot be drawn,
and their name and ids removed from past draws. Their position and weight
stay, so old draws can still be checked by anyone who kept the seed, and the row then says only
"somebody, with this weight, in this position".

## In the audit log

Every change is recorded in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log):

| Entry | When |
|---|---|
| **Giveaway created** | Somebody created one, as a draft or open. |
| **Giveaway changed** | Somebody edited it. Shows before and after. |
| **Giveaway opened** | It was opened for entries. |
| **Giveaway closed** | Entries closed, by hand or when its time came. |
| **Entered a giveaway** | Somebody reacted. Recorded against them, with whether they qualified. |
| **Withdrew from a giveaway** | Somebody took their reaction off. |
| **Giveaway drawn** | A draw was made. Carries the seed, the promise, the entrant counts and the winners. |
| **Giveaway winner announced** | The winners were named in the channel. |
| **Giveaway cancelled** | Somebody cancelled it. |
| **Giveaway deleted** | Somebody deleted it. |
| **Giveaway failed to publish** | The Discord post could not be written, or a draw could not be made. |

## Common problems

**The preview says Modbot cannot answer.** A rule reaches further back than the facts Modbot still
keeps. Shorten its window, or raise the retention window under **Settings**, **Host &amp; Database**.

**Nobody matches.** Press **Preview** with the rules emptied to see how many people Modbot knows
about at all, then add the rules back one at a time.

**Nothing appears in Discord.** Check that the bot is connected on [Sync
health](/self-hosting/health), that **Post to channel** is ticked, and that the bot can View Channel,
Send Messages and Embed Links in the channel you picked. The error is shown on the giveaway.

**The emoji is not on the post.** The bot needs Add Reactions in that channel, and the emoji has to
be one Discord accepts — a standard emoji, or one of your own server's.

**Somebody says they entered and they are not in the list.** Open the draw's entrant list and find
them. If they are there with a reason, that is the answer. If they are not there at all, they
reacted with a different emoji, or after it closed.

**The winner is not who I expected.** The entrant list and the seed are both on the page, and the
steps for working it out again are [above](#working-the-result-out-again).
