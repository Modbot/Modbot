---
title: Popups
description: The popups that open when you click a person, a world or an instance, how they stack, what each one shows and which permissions they need.
---

Almost every name in Modbot can be clicked: a person in the audit log, a world on Live, an
instance number in a table. Clicking one opens a popup over the page you are on. The page stays
exactly as it was underneath, with its scroll position, filters and loaded rows, so closing the
popup puts you back where you were.

There are three kinds: a **person**, a **world** and an **instance**.

A **person** means one human being, not one account. Somebody can be up to three things in Modbot
— a VRChat account, a Discord account, and the Modbot account they sign in with — and clicking any
of them opens the same popup, showing every account Modbot can tie to the one you clicked. You
never have to know which of somebody's accounts to click.

## How popups stack

- **Opening something from inside a popup puts a new popup on top.** Click the world inside an
  instance popup and the world opens over it.
- **Only the top popup is shown.** Its header has a back control naming the one underneath, such
  as **Back to the instance**. Hover over it to see how many more are open below.
- **Closing goes back one level.** Escape, the close button, a click outside the popup and your
  browser's Back button all do the same thing.
- **Clicking the thing that is already on top does nothing**, so a list that names the same world
  twice does not build a pile of identical popups.

The whole stack is kept in the page address (as one or more `?subject=` values). You can copy the
address and send it to another moderator, and it opens the same popups for them. They still need
the permissions to see what is inside.

## Person popup

The title is **Person**, with the person's id under it.

### Which accounts belong together

Modbot ties accounts together from two things and nothing else:

- the [account link](/discord/account-linking) a member proved between their Discord and VRChat
  accounts;
- the VRChat account a Modbot account proved, and the Discord id an administrator typed onto a
  Modbot account.

Names are never used. Two people called the same thing stay two people, and an account link that
was ended ties nothing.

If an account cannot be tied to the others, the popup still opens on what is known and says which
are missing in one line, such as **Not linked to Discord or Modbot**. You are only
told a Modbot account is missing if you are allowed to know who holds one — otherwise nothing is
said either way.

### Left side

- **Who they are.** Their banner with their picture over it, display name, pronouns and the
  [18+ verified mark](/moderation/profiles#the-18-verified-mark), then a row of badges: the
  [trust rank](/moderation/profiles#trust-ranks), **VRC+** (in gold) if they subscribe, the
  platform they last used (**PC**, **Android** or **iOS**), **VRChat staff**, **Nuisance**,
  **Early adopter**, and one badge per language on their profile. Under that, the group they
  represent (its icon and name; hover for the group id) and how old the stored profile is.
  Opening the popup asks for a fresh copy. See
  [Profiles](/moderation/profiles). The rest of the profile is on the **Overview** tab.
- **Discord.** Only when Modbot knows a Discord account for them: the Discord name and id. When
  the two accounts proved a link, also when they linked, the roles Modbot gave them, and **Not in
  the server** if they have left it, and anybody with **Manage Discord links** sees an **Unlink**
  button. See [Account linking](/discord/account-linking). Where the Discord id was typed onto a
  Modbot account rather than proved, none of that is shown, because none of it exists.
- **Modbot account.** Only for people allowed to know who holds one: the username, **Disabled** if
  it is, its roles, when it was made and when it last signed in.
- **Membership.** Whether they are in your group now and since when, whether they are
  representing the group, who invited them (**Invited by**), who let them in from a join request
  (**Approved by**), how many times they asked to join and how many of those were rejected and by
  whom, **Blocked from asking** if they were, their group roles, the group's manager notes about
  them, and whether they are on the ban list, or when a ban was lifted. The invite and join request
  lines come from the audit log, so they need **See the audit log**. The last line, **Checked**, says how long ago
  the older of the member list and the ban list was last read. If the member list has never been read, it says **Member list not read yet.** Below
  that, **Kick**, **Ban** or **Unban**, for whoever holds those permissions — see
  [Kicking, banning and unbanning](/moderation/members-and-bans#kicking-banning-and-unbanning).

### Tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | **Profile**: their status line, bio, **Joined VRChat**, **Last seen by Modbot**, and the tags that have no badge behind a control that counts them (**12 more tags** / **Hide tags**). Then the **Past actions** block (how many times the person has been acted on, by how many moderators, and their status; see [Reviews and repeat offenders](/moderation/reviews#past-actions-on-a-persons-popup)), their time in world (**Time seen**, **Instances visited**, **Worlds visited**, **Arrivals**, **Last seen** and **First seen**), a table of the instances they were seen in (newest 25), **Visits** (see below), and the newest 8 audit log entries about any of their accounts, with **All activity** to open the **Activity** tab. |
| **Activity** | **Recorded about this person**: every audit log entry about any of their accounts or done by any of them, newest first, 50 at a time with **Load more** at the bottom. It is the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log)'s own list with its filters, narrowed to this person, and new entries arrive while it is open. **Show** starts on **Everything**; pick **Moderation only** for bans, kicks, warnings, rejected join requests, notes, watches, case files, AutoMod and copied bans, **Presence** for arrivals, leaves, avatar changes and clips a moderator saved, or **Discord** for their Discord history and everything Modbot copied between the two. Each row says which account it was found under, so a Discord ban is never mistaken for a group one. Click a row to see everything the entry holds. Where [AI briefs](/ai/chat#ai-briefs) are on, **Brief me** is at the top of the tab. |
| **Notes** | What moderators have written down about them, newest first, and a box to add another. Notes that were taken back are still listed and marked. See [Notes](/moderation/notes). |
| **Profile changes** | Their VRChat profile as it stood after each change Modbot recorded, newest first. Pick a version on the left to see it in full, pictures, banner and represented group included; the fields that change carried are marked. **JSON** under a version shows that version's record as stored; **Hide JSON** puts it away. **Now** is the profile as stored; **First seen** is where the record begins. See [Profile changes](#profile-changes). |
| **Cases** | Their case files, withdrawn ones included and marked as such. Click one to open it. See [Bans and case files](/moderation/case-files). |
| **Flags** | What [AutoMod rules](/moderation/automod) flagged about any of their accounts, switched between **Open**, **Dismissed** and **Confirmed**. **Flags page** opens the [Flags](/moderation/flags) page, where flags are dismissed or taken to a review. |
| **Discord** | Their Discord details, their activity in the server (messages and voice), and their own history there: comings and goings, renames, roles, timeouts, bans, and the bans and roles Modbot copied over from VRChat. See [Discord members](/discord/members). |
| **Messages** | Everything they wrote in Discord that Modbot has stored. Needs **Read Discord messages**. |
| **Account** | Everything their Modbot account did and everything done to it: sign-ins, role changes, settings they changed, and every kick, ban and unban they pressed in Modbot. |
| **Raw data** | The stored records as Modbot's API answers them, as **one document with one copy button**. Its named parts are the profile, the membership and ban standing, the two bodies VRChat last sent (the public profile and the user object), the Discord member row, their Discord activity and the Modbot account. A part you do not hold the permission for is left out rather than shown empty; if you hold none of them the tab says so. Open it from **⋯** in the popup's header; it shows as a tab while it is open. |

A tab is only there when there is an account behind it and you hold its permission.

#### Profile changes

Modbot keeps no separate copies of a profile. When a refresh finds a field changed, it writes an
audit log entry with the old and new values, and the Profile changes tab replays those entries backwards
from the profile as stored now. A version's time is the window between the two reads that noticed
the change. Clicking **VRChat profile** in a *profile changed* entry on the audit log opens the
person on this tab at that version.

#### Visits

**Visits** on **Overview** lists where the person was seen, one row per visit, newest first, 10 at
a time with **Load more**: the instance (click it to open the instance), when they arrived and left
and how long that was, **Already there** when a moderator's companion arrived after they did, **Not
seen leaving** when nobody saw them go (the visit then runs to the last thing reported from that
instance before it closed), the name they had if it was not the one they have now, the avatars they were seen
changing into, and **Seen by** with the moderators whose companions saw them. Visits are made from
the same reports as the time-in-world figures. It needs **See the audit log**, the
permission that reads the same arrivals and leaves in the audit log.

The time-in-world figures on **Overview** only count time a moderator's companion shared an
instance with the person. When no client has seen them they are left out, which does not mean the
person has never been in an instance.

## World popup

The title is the world's name, with the world id under it.

### Left side

The world's picture and name, then:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Made by** | The world's author. Click to open them as a person. |
| **Who can find it** | **Anyone (public)**, **Only people given the link (private)** or **Hidden**. |

**Name not read yet** is normal for a few minutes after a new world turns up, and permanent for a
private or deleted world.

### Tabs

- **Overview**: **Details**, then the four figures (**Time seen**, **Visitors**, **Instances opened**
  with how many are open now, and **Last seen**), the newest 5 instances, and two daily charts:
  **Visitors per day** and **Instances opened per day**. Time and visitors only count time a
  moderator's companion was in the world. The details are:

  | Field | Meaning |
  |---|---|
  | **Holds** | How many people the world page says it holds, and the suggested number if different. This is what the page says; group exemptions can raise the real limit. |
  | **First seen by Modbot** | When the world first turned up. |
  | **Published on VRChat** | When the author published it. |
  | **Page last read** | When Modbot last read the world's VRChat page. |
  | **Description** | The world's description. |

- **Instances**: the newest 25 instances that have run in this world. The number on the tab is the
  total.
- **History**: the newest 50 audit log entries recorded in any of this world's instances.
- **Raw data**: the world's stored record as the API answers it, with a copy button. Open it from
  **⋯** in the popup's header; it shows as a tab while it is open.

Opening a world popup never asks VRChat for anything. It shows what Modbot already has.

## Instance popup

The title is the world name and the instance number. Under it: **Open now** or **Closed**.

Modbot gives every instance its own id, because VRChat hands the same instance number out again
after an instance closes. Two evenings under the same number are two different instances and two
different popups.

### Left side

The world's picture, then **World** (click to open the world popup), **Instance number**,
**Who can join**, and either **Closed** with when (and "went quiet" if Modbot closed it after
hearing nothing for a long time) or **Open now** with when it was last seen.

### Tabs

- **Overview**: **Details** (**Region**, **Opened**, **People now** for an open instance, and
  **Seen by a moderator's client**: how many people a client saw and for how long in total), then
  **Ran for** or **Open for** and **Most at once**, then **Joins** (**Total**: every join a
  moderator's client saw; **Firsts**: the different people who made them, counting someone already
  there when the client arrived; **Rejoins**: the joins that were somebody coming back; **Returning
  members**: how many of those people are group members now), and the six people seen longest.
  In **Busiest others**, another group's instance shows its name and its group's name once
  Modbot has asked VRChat for them, which it does once per instance and once per group, and never
  for an instance only invited people, friends or group members can join.
  - **People over time**, under **Joins**: the line is green where the count went up, dark red
    where it dropped at a kick and light red where it dropped because people left. The keys above it
    turn on dashed lines for **Members** and for each trust rank, counting the people a moderator's
    companion saw there against today's membership and rank; the tooltip lists every line whether or
    not it is drawn.
- **People**: **Who was seen in this instance**, with **Time seen**, **Arrivals**, **First seen**
  and **Last seen** for each person.
- **Activity**: **What happened in this instance**: the audit log narrowed to this instance, with
  the same filter chips as the [Audit log](/moderation/audit-log) page; **When** starts on the
  days the instance ran. **Open in Audit log** opens the page with the same chips. Where
  [AI briefs](/ai/chat#ai-briefs) are on, **Summarise what Modbot recorded** sits beside it.
- **Raw data**: the instance's stored record as the API answers it, with a copy button. Open it
  from **⋯** in the popup's header; it shows as a tab while it is open.

## Size and address

A popup fills the window except for a small margin, with the thing it is about on the left and
the tabs on the right. The tab a link opens on can be part of the address (`?tab=history`), and
for a person the version too (`?version=`); switching tabs by hand does not change the address.

## Who can see what

| Part | Permission |
|---|---|
| Person popup: profile, **Discord** card, **Cases**, **Profile changes** and **Flags** tabs, time in world on **Overview**, Discord activity on the **Discord** tab, **Past actions** block | **See profiles** |
| Person popup: **Membership** card, **Discord** tab | **See members** |
| Person popup: **Unlink** button | **Manage Discord links** |
| Person popup: **Messages** tab | **Read Discord messages** |
| Person popup: **Modbot account** card — knowing who holds which account | **See the operational log** or **Manage staff accounts** |
| Person popup: **Account** tab | **See the audit log** for what they did as a moderator, **See the operational log** for their sign-ins and role changes |
| Person popup: entries in **Activity** | **See the audit log** (and **See the operational log** for Modbot's own entries such as sign-ins) |
| Person popup: **Notes** tab | **See the audit log** to read them, **Write notes** to add one |
| Person popup: **Brief me**, and instance popup: **Summarise what Modbot recorded** | **Use AI chat**, with AI, Chat and AI briefs on; the instance's also needs what its **Activity** tab needs. **Save as note** needs **Write notes** |
| World popup | **See analytics** |
| Instance popup | **See analytics** |
| Instance popup: **People** and **Activity** tabs, **Seen by a moderator's client**, **Joins**, the **Members** and trust rank lines on **People over time**, and the people seen longest | **See analytics** and **See the audit log** |

When you lack a permission, the popup still opens and says **You do not have permission to see
this.** (or a similar line) in the part you cannot see. See
[Roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions) for the full list.
