title: Moderation
description: Every moderation page in Modbot, with a line on what each one is for.
---

Everything staff use to look after the group, from who is in your instances right now to the ban
file written up afterwards.

## What is happening now

- **[Live](/moderation/live)** shows your group's open instances and who is in each one.
- **[Instance announcements](/moderation/announcements)** send a message to everyone in one of your
  open instances, now or at a time you pick.

## Events and posts

- **[Calendar](/moderation/calendar)** plans an event once and puts it on VRChat's group calendar,
  in Discord and in a calendar feed.
- **[Event categories](/moderation/calendar#event-categories)** are your own labels for events, such
  as Karaoke or Movie night, with alerts for members who want to hear about one.
- **[Google Calendar](/moderation/google-calendar)** puts the same events on a Google calendar your
  group owns.
- **[World lists](/moderation/world-lists)** keep lists of worlds for events to pick from.
- **[Marketing](/moderation/marketing)** writes a post once and sends it to Discord, your VRChat
  group and Bluesky at the time you pick.
- **[Bluesky](/moderation/bluesky)** connects Modbot to your group's Bluesky account.
- **[Giveaways](/moderation/giveaways)** run a raffle your members can check.

## People

- **[People](/moderation/people)** is everyone Modbot has ever seen, with search, filters and columns.
  An instance's popup shows the same list for the people seen in that instance.
- **[Members and bans](/moderation/members-and-bans)** are the member list and the Bans page, and
  where you kick, ban and unban.
- **[Join requests](/moderation/join-requests)** are the people waiting to be let into your group.
- **[Profiles](/moderation/profiles)** keep what Modbot has seen of a VRChat user over time.
- **[Names](/moderation/names)** covers names written in fancy lettering or other alphabets.
- **[Popups](/moderation/popups)** open a person, a world or an instance on top of the page you are on.
- **[Lists](/moderation/lists)** save a set of rules, such as "our regulars", to come back to.
- **[Auto-invites](/moderation/auto-invites)** invite people to your group once they have spent
  long enough in your instances.
- **[Partners](/moderation/partners)** keep a record of the other groups you work with.
- **[Availability](/moderation/availability)** shows which hours of the week your team is free.

## Records

- **[Audit log](/moderation/audit-log)** shows your group's VRChat audit log next to what happened
  inside Modbot.
- **[Notes](/moderation/notes)** write something down about a person, or watch them.
- **[Bans and case files](/moderation/case-files)** record why someone was banned.
- **[Evidence](/moderation/evidence)** attaches screenshots and video to a case file.
- **[Appeals and the yearly report](/moderation/appeals)** record appeals and give a year of actions.
- **[Reviews and repeat offenders](/moderation/reviews)** list people acted on more than once and
  moderators whose actions need a second look.
- **[Reports](/moderation/reports)** are what members tell the mods from Discord.
- **[Purge a person](/moderation/purge)** erases the history Modbot holds about one person.

## Rules

- **[AutoMod](/moderation/automod)** checks Discord messages and VRChat profiles against word lists
  and AI topics.
- **[Flags](/moderation/flags)** list what AutoMod picked up.
