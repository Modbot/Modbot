The **Marketing** page is where you write posts for your community and send them at a time you
pick: an announcement, a reminder, news. You write a post once, tick where it goes, and Modbot sends
it at its time.

In this version a post can go to a **Discord channel**. VRChat group posts and Bluesky are planned
and come later; until then the VRChat page's own [**Posts**](/analytics#posts) tab posts in your
group on VRChat.

## Who can use it

| To do this | You need |
|---|---|
| See the **Marketing** page, every post, where each one goes and how it went | [**See posts**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-posts) |
| Write, schedule, change, cancel and send posts, and **Try again** | [**Manage posts**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#manage-posts) and **See posts** |
| **Edit** or **Delete** a post on Discord after it went out | **Manage posts** and **See posts** |
| Turn **Pause all posting** and **Discord posts** on or off | [**Change settings**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#change-settings) |
| See the **Posts** card on **Health** | **See posts** and [**See the operational log**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-the-operational-log) |

Neither permission is in the built-in **Moderator** or **Viewer** roles. **Administrator** holds
both. Give them on purpose: a post goes out in your group's name, in public.

**Manage group posts**, the permission for the VRChat page's **Posts** tab, does not open this page.

## The page

**Marketing** is in the sidebar under **Community**, after **World lists**. `g` then `q` goes there.

The page has four lists, each with how many posts are in it:

- **Scheduled**: posts waiting for their time, or on their way, soonest first.
- **Sent**: posts that went out, newest first.
- **Drafts**: posts saved without a time.
- **Failed**: posts that did not go out somewhere. A post that went to one place and failed on
  another is here as well as in the list it would otherwise be in.

Each list has its own address, such as `/marketing/sent`, so you can send a link to one.
**Cancelled** at the top shows the posts that were called off instead.

Each post shows:

- when it goes, in your own time, with the time zone it was written in after it when that is
  another;
- its title, or the first line of its text;
- who wrote it;
- a badge for each place it goes, with how it stands there:

| Badge | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Waiting** | Not its time yet. |
| **Paused** | **Pause all posting** is on, so nothing is sent. |
| **Off** | **Discord posts** is off in Settings. |
| **Not set up** | Modbot has no Discord server id, or the bot is not connected. |
| **Sending…** | On its way, or Modbot is checking whether Discord took it. |
| **Posted** | In the channel. **Open** opens it in Discord. |
| **Not published** | In the channel, but publishing it to followers did not go through. |
| **Failed** | Not in the channel, with Discord's own words, or Modbot's. |
| **Deleted** | Was in the channel and was deleted from Modbot. |
| **Skipped** | Never went: the post was cancelled first. |

The page updates by itself when a post is sent or fails, so **Sending…** turns into **Posted**
without reloading.

## Writing a post

**New post** opens the form. It has two tabs, **Write** and **Preview**, and its buttons stay at the
bottom.

- **Title** is shown in bold on the first line on Discord. You can leave it empty.
- **Text** is what the post says. Under it a counter shows how long the Discord message will be,
  such as **Discord 212 / 2000**. Discord takes at most 2000 characters, and the counter turns red
  past that. The title and a mentioned role count too.
- **Picture**: **Choose picture** to pick one on your computer, or paste a **Picture link**. It
  opens in a crop box. Pick its shape: **16:9**, **1:1**, or **As it is** for the whole picture.
  Move and size the box, then **Upload**. A picture up to 8 MB is kept by Modbot and sent to Discord
  as a file with the post. Modbot fetches a picture link from your server, so the website it points
  at sees a request from your server; it fetches only `https://` links on public websites.
- **Where it goes**: a chip for each place a post can go. **Discord** is the only one in this version.
  **It is off every time you open the form for a new post.** A post goes nowhere unless you tick it.
  A chip that is on but cannot send says **Off** or **Not set up**, linking to where you fix that.
- **When**: **Now** sends it as soon as you press **Post now**. **Later** sends it at the date and
  time you pick. The time zone is your browser's to start with; pick another and the time is that
  zone's.

Then:

- **Schedule** saves it to go at its time. With **Now** picked, the button says **Post now**.
- **Save draft** keeps it in **Drafts**, with nothing sent.
- **Cancel** closes the form without saving.

If something is wrong, the form lists every problem at once, such as "Pick a channel." or "The
Discord text is longer than 2000 characters.".

### Discord

With **Discord** ticked:

- **Channel**: the one channel the post goes to. A channel where the bot lacks something it needs
  is marked with what is missing.
- **Mention role**: one role to mention on the first line. It is pinged once, when the post first
  goes out, never on an edit. The bot cannot mention @everyone. A role marked **Bot cannot mention**
  will not be pinged unless you let anyone mention it in Discord, or give the bot **Mention
  @everyone, @here and All Roles**.
- **Publish to followers**: only for an **Announcement** channel. Once the post is in, Modbot
  publishes it to the servers that follow the channel.
- **Own text**: tick it to give Discord words of its own. It starts as the post's text, has its own
  counter, and **Reset** puts the post's text back.

### Preview

**Preview** shows the Discord message the way the channel will show it: the role, the title in
bold, the text with its links, and the picture. It is built by the same code that sends the post, so
it is exactly what goes out. If something would stop the post being scheduled, it is listed above.

## Before it goes

Each post in **Scheduled** or **Drafts** has buttons:

- **Edit** opens the form to change the whole post. A post that is on its way cannot be changed: it
  says "This post is being sent.".
- **Post now** sends it now, whatever time it had.
- **Cancel post** asks first, then calls it off. Nothing more is sent, and it moves to **Cancelled**.
- **Delete draft** asks first, then deletes a draft.
- **Duplicate** starts a new draft from the post: the same words, picture and Discord choices, with
  every place unticked again.

There is no repeat. For a post every week, **Duplicate** it. For reminders about an event, the
calendar will make them in a later version.

## After it goes

A post in the channel shows **Open**, **Edit** and **Delete** beside its badge:

- **Edit** changes its title and text on Discord. The picture stays, and nobody is pinged again.
- **Delete** asks "Delete this post on Discord?", then deletes it there. A message already gone
  from Discord counts as deleted.

Both happen at once; the bot has to be connected.

## When a post fails

A failed post shows why, and **Try again**.

| What it says | What happened | What **Try again** does |
|---|---|---|
| Discord's own words, such as "Missing Permissions" | Discord refused it. Nothing was posted. | Sends it again. Fix what Discord said first. |
| Not sent on time. | It was more than an hour late, because Modbot was off, posting was paused or Discord was not set up. It was not sent. | Sends it now. **Post now** does the same. |
| Discord did not take the post. | Discord gave no clear answer, and the post was not in the channel a minute later. | Looks in the channel once more, and sends it only if it is not there. |
| Could not check the channel. | Discord gave no clear answer, and Modbot could not read the channel to look. | Looks again first, the same way. |

A post that is in the channel but was not published to followers shows **Not published**, and **Try
again** publishes it.

### Never posted twice

Modbot writes down that it is sending a post, and exactly what, before it asks Discord. It asks
Discord with the library's own retries turned off, so nothing is sent twice behind its back.

When Discord gives no clear answer (no answer, a timeout, a Discord error), the post shows
**Sending…** and Modbot does not send it again. A minute later it reads the channel for a message by
the bot with the same text and the same picture, sent from a minute before the attempt. If it finds
one, that message is the post: it shows **Posted** with an **Open** link. If not, it is **Failed**
with "Discord did not take the post.". If Modbot cannot read the channel, it tries again every 15
minutes for an hour, then shows "Could not check the channel.". **Modbot never sends a post again by
itself.** Only **Try again** does, and it looks first.

## What the bot needs in Discord

In the post's channel the bot needs:

- **View Channel** and **Send Messages**, to post;
- **Attach Files**, for a post with a picture;
- **Read Message History**, to look for a post Discord gave no clear answer to.

## Settings

**Settings**, **Posts** has two switches:

- **Pause all posting**: while it is on, no post is sent anywhere. Posts wait and show **Paused**; one
  more than an hour late is not sent and shows "Not sent on time." with **Post now**. It does not
  stop the calendar's own Discord event, channel card or VRChat calendar entry.
- **Discord posts**: off, no post goes to Discord; Discord posts wait and show **Off**. It is on
  to start with, because nothing goes out unless somebody ticks **Discord** on a post.

Each change is written to the audit log with who made it.

Modbot sends at most one post a pass, every 20 seconds or so, and at most 10 posts an hour to
Discord, so a mistake cannot flood a channel.

## Health

The **Posts** card on **Health** shows:

- **Paused** while all posting is paused;
- Discord, when posts are waiting on it and it is **Off** or **Not set up**;
- posts that failed in the last 7 days, each leading to **Failed**;
- posts Modbot has been looking for in the channel for more than 15 minutes.

When there is nothing to say, the card is not shown.

## The audit log

Everything done to a post is written to the audit log, under the operational log: who wrote,
changed, cancelled or sent it now, each post Modbot sent with its link and exactly what it said,
each post that failed with the reason, and each edit and delete on Discord with who did it.

## What leaves your server

A post goes only where it is ticked, and every place starts unticked. For Discord, the bot sends the
post's title, text and picture to the channel, mentions the role if one was picked, and publishes it
to the channel's followers if that was ticked. The channel's readers see it, and so do the following
servers' readers. Nothing about your members goes in a post: no names, no ids, no counts. See
[Privacy](/privacy#what-leaves-your-server).
