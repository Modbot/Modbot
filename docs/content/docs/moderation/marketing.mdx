The **Marketing** page is where you write posts for your community and send them at a time you
pick: an announcement, a reminder, news. You write a post once, tick where it goes, and Modbot sends
it at its time.

In this version a post can go to a **Discord channel**, to your **VRChat group's posts** and to your
group's **Bluesky** account, once it is [set up](/moderation/bluesky). The VRChat page's own
[**Posts**](/analytics#posts) tab stays: it posts in your group right away, and posts sent from
**Marketing** show up there like any others.

## Who can use it

| To do this | You need |
|---|---|
| See the **Marketing** page, every post, where each one goes and how it went | [**See posts**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-posts) |
| Write, schedule, change, cancel and send posts, and **Try again** | [**Manage posts**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#manage-posts) and **See posts** |
| **Edit** a post on Discord or VRChat, or **Delete** one on Discord, VRChat or Bluesky, after it went out | **Manage posts** and **See posts** |
| Turn **Pause all posting**, **Discord posts**, **VRChat posts** and **Bluesky posts** on or off, and set up the Bluesky account | [**Change settings**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#change-settings) |
| See the **Posts** card on **Health** | **See posts** and [**See the operational log**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-the-operational-log) |

Neither permission is in the built-in **Moderator** or **Viewer** roles. **Administrator** holds
both. Give them on purpose: a post goes out in your group's name, in public.

**Manage group posts**, the permission for the VRChat page's **Posts** tab, does not open this page.

## The page

**Marketing** is in the sidebar under **Community**, after **World lists**. `g` then `q` goes there.

The page has four lists, each with how many posts are in it:

- **Scheduled**: posts waiting for their time, or on their way, soonest first.
- **Sent**: posts that went out, newest first.
- **Drafts**: posts saved without a time.
- **Failed**: posts that did not go out somewhere. A post that went to one place and failed on
  another is here as well as in the list it would otherwise be in.

Each list has its own address, such as `/marketing/sent`, so you can send a link to one.
**Cancelled** at the top shows the posts that were called off instead.

Each post shows:

- when it goes, in your own time, with the time zone it was written in after it when that is
  another;
- its title, or the first line of its text;
- who wrote it;
- a badge for each place it goes, with how it stands there:

| Badge | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Waiting** | Not its time yet. |
| **Paused** | **Pause all posting** is on, so nothing is sent. |
| **Off** | **Discord posts**, **VRChat posts** or **Bluesky posts** is off in Settings. |
| **Not set up** | Discord: Modbot has no Discord server id, or the bot is not connected. VRChat: no group is picked, or Modbot has no VRChat login. Bluesky: no account has passed **Check**, or Bluesky refused its app password since. |
| **Sending…** | On its way, or Modbot is checking whether the site took it. |
| **Posted** | On the site. **Open** opens it there: the message on Discord, the group's posts on VRChat, the post on Bluesky. |
| **Not published** | In the channel, but publishing it to followers did not go through. |
| **Failed** | Not on the site, with the site's own words, or Modbot's. |
| **Deleted** | Was on the site and was deleted from Modbot. |
| **Skipped** | Never went: the post was cancelled first. |

A VRChat badge also says who sees the post: **Everyone**, **Group**, or the roles it is for.

The page updates by itself when a post is sent or fails, so **Sending…** turns into **Posted**
without reloading.

## Writing a post

**New post** opens the form. It has two tabs, **Write** and **Preview**, and its buttons stay at the
bottom.

- **Title** is shown in bold on the first line on Discord, and as the post's title on VRChat. Discord
  does without one; VRChat needs one.
- **Text** is what the post says. Under it a counter for each place ticked shows how long the post
  will be, such as **Discord 212 / 2000**, **VRChat 212** and **Bluesky 212 / 300**. Discord takes
  at most 2000 characters, and its counter turns red past that; the title and a mentioned role count
  too. VRChat gives no limit, so its counter is never red. Bluesky takes at most 300 characters,
  counted the way a reader sees them (an emoji with its skin tone is one), and the title counts too.
- **Picture**: **Choose picture** to pick one on your computer, or paste a **Picture link**. It
  opens in a crop box. Pick its shape: **16:9**, **1:1**, or **As it is** for the whole picture.
  Move and size the box, then **Upload**. A picture up to 8 MB is kept by Modbot and sent to Discord
  as a file with the post. Modbot fetches a picture link from your server, so the website it points
  at sees a request from your server; it fetches only `https://` links on public websites.
- **Where it goes**: a chip for each place a post can go: **Discord**, **VRChat** and **Bluesky**.
  **Each is off every time you open the form for a new post.** A post goes nowhere unless you tick
  it. A chip that is on but cannot send says **Off** or **Not set up**, linking to where you fix
  that. **VRChat** says **Needs a title** until the post, or VRChat's own title, has one.
- **When**: **Now** sends it as soon as you press **Post now**. **Later** sends it at the date and
  time you pick. The time zone is your browser's to start with; pick another and the time is that
  zone's.

Then:

- **Schedule** saves it to go at its time. With **Now** picked, the button says **Post now**.
- **Save draft** keeps it in **Drafts**, with nothing sent.
- **Cancel** closes the form without saving.

If something is wrong, the form lists every problem at once, such as "Pick a channel.", "VRChat
needs a title.", "The Discord text is longer than 2000 characters." or "The Bluesky text is longer
than 300 characters.".

### Discord

With **Discord** ticked:

- **Channel**: the one channel the post goes to. A channel where the bot lacks something it needs
  is marked with what is missing.
- **Mention role**: one role to mention on the first line. It is pinged once, when the post first
  goes out, never on an edit. The bot cannot mention @everyone. A role marked **Bot cannot mention**
  will not be pinged unless you let anyone mention it in Discord, or give the bot **Mention
  @everyone, @here and All Roles**.
- **Publish to followers**: only for an **Announcement** channel. Once the post is in, Modbot
  publishes it to the servers that follow the channel.
- **Own text**: tick it to give Discord words of its own. It starts as the post's text, has its own
  counter, and **Reset** puts the post's text back.

### VRChat

With **VRChat** ticked, the post goes to your group's posts on VRChat, from the VRChat account
Modbot signs in as:

- **Who sees it**: **Group** (your group's members) or **Everyone** (anyone on vrchat.com). It
  starts as **Group**.
- **Roles**: with **Group**, tick roles to show it only to them. None ticked is every member. The
  roles are your group's as Modbot last read them.
- **Notify members**: has VRChat tell the members about it, once, when it first goes out. It starts
  unticked, every time.
- **Own title** and **Own text**: tick them to give VRChat words of its own. Each starts as the
  post's, and **Reset** puts the post's back.

**The picture goes to VRChat only while uploads are on**: **Settings**, **Modbot's VRChat login**,
**Pictures**, **Upload VRChat pictures**. Then, when you tick **VRChat** on a post with a picture,
the picture is uploaded to VRChat (it must be a PNG or JPEG), on the VRChat account Modbot signs in
as, which needs VRChat+. VRChat takes one upload a minute. If VRChat turns it down, the VRChat
section says why, and VRChat gets the text only. While uploads are off, VRChat gets the text only,
and its preview shows no picture.

### Bluesky

With **Bluesky** ticked, the post goes to your group's Bluesky account, as set up in **Settings**,
**Bluesky** ([how](/moderation/bluesky)):

- Bluesky has no title, so it gets the **title on its own line, then the text**.
- **Own text**: tick it to give Bluesky words of its own, every word, title included. It starts as
  the title and text, has its own counter, and **Reset** puts them back.
- **Links and tags**: a `https://` link and a `#tag` in the text are links on Bluesky. An
  `@handle` stays plain text: Modbot never mentions anyone, because a mention notifies that person.
- **The link card**: when the text has a link, Bluesky shows a card for the first one, with the
  post's title (or the link's website when there is no title) and the post's picture. Bluesky takes
  a card picture of at most 1 MB, so the form makes a small JPEG copy of the picture in your
  browser and keeps it beside the post. With no link in the text, Bluesky gets the text only.

Bluesky cannot edit a post. Once one is on Bluesky it can only be deleted.

### Preview

**Preview** shows each place the post goes, the way that site shows it:

- **Discord**: the message the way the channel will show it: the role, the title in bold, the text
  with its links, and the picture.
- **VRChat**: the group post: its title, who sees it, the picture if VRChat is sent one, and the
  text.
- **Bluesky**: the account's name and handle, the text with its links and tags coloured, the link
  card if there is one, and the counter.

It is built by the same code that sends the post, so it is exactly what goes out. If something
would stop the post being scheduled, it is listed above.

## Before it goes

Each post in **Scheduled** or **Drafts** has buttons:

- **Edit** opens the form to change the whole post. A post that is on its way cannot be changed: it
  says "This post is being sent.".
- **Post now** sends it now, whatever time it had.
- **Cancel post** asks first, then calls it off. Nothing more is sent, and it moves to **Cancelled**.
- **Delete draft** asks first, then deletes a draft.
- **Duplicate** starts a new draft from the post: the same words, picture and each place's choices,
  with every place unticked again and **Notify members** unticked.

There is no repeat. For a post every week, **Duplicate** it. For reminders about an event, the
calendar will make them in a later version.

## After it goes

A post that went out shows **Open**, **Edit** and **Delete** beside its badge (a Bluesky post has
no **Edit**):

- **Edit** changes its title and text there. On Discord the picture stays, and nobody is pinged
  again. On VRChat the picture and who sees it stay, nobody is notified again, and it needs a title.
- **Delete** asks "Delete this post on Discord?" (or on VRChat, or on Bluesky), then deletes it
  there. A post already gone from the site counts as deleted. A Bluesky post is deleted from the
  account in Settings; deleting removes it from Bluesky, not from copies other services already took.

Both happen at once: on Discord the bot has to be connected; on VRChat and Bluesky each is one
request, never sent again, and if the site is busy it says so and you try again later.

## When a post fails

A failed post shows why, and **Try again**.

| What it says | What happened | What **Try again** does |
|---|---|---|
| Discord's own words, such as "Missing Permissions" | Discord refused it. Nothing was posted. | Sends it again. Fix what Discord said first. |
| Not sent on time. | It was more than an hour late, because Modbot was off, posting was paused or Discord was not set up. It was not sent. | Sends it now. **Post now** does the same. |
| Discord did not take the post. | Discord gave no clear answer, and the post was not in the channel a minute later. | Looks in the channel once more, and sends it only if it is not there. |
| Could not check the channel. | Discord gave no clear answer, and Modbot could not read the channel to look. | Looks again first, the same way. |

A post that is in the channel but was not published to followers shows **Not published**, and **Try
again** publishes it.

On VRChat:

| What it says | What happened | What **Try again** does |
|---|---|---|
| VRChat's own words, with **Needs Manage Group Announcement** when Modbot's VRChat account lacks it | VRChat refused it. Nothing was posted. | Sends it again. Give the permission first, in VRChat, under the group's **Roles**. |
| VRChat needs a title. | The post had no title for VRChat. Nothing was sent. | Sends it again. Edit the post to give it a title first. |
| Not sent on time. | It was more than an hour late. It was not sent. | Sends it now. **Post now** does the same. |
| VRChat did not add the post. | VRChat gave no clear answer, and the post was not in the group's posts two minutes later. | Looks in the group's posts once more, and sends it only if it is not there. |
| Could not check the group's posts. | VRChat gave no clear answer, and Modbot could not read the group's posts to look. | Looks again first, the same way. |

On Bluesky:

| What it says | What happened | What **Try again** does |
|---|---|---|
| Bluesky's own words | Bluesky refused it. Nothing was posted. | Sends it again. |
| The Bluesky text is longer than 300 characters. | The text was too long for Bluesky. Nothing was sent. | Sends it again. Edit the post first. |
| That Bluesky account is not the one in Settings. | The account in Settings changed after the post was saved. Nothing was sent. | Sends it again. Edit the post first, so it is for the account in Settings. |
| Not sent on time. | It was more than an hour late. It was not sent. | Sends it now. **Post now** does the same. |
| Could not reach Bluesky. | Bluesky gave no clear answer, and Modbot could not find out for an hour whether the post was made. | Reads it back first, and sends it only if it is not there. |
| Bluesky says this post is already there, but could not show it. | Bluesky said the post's place was taken, then could not show the post. It may well be on Bluesky. | Reads it back, and shows it as **Posted** if Bluesky shows it now. It is never sent again: **Duplicate** it for a new post. |

### Never posted twice

Modbot writes down that it is sending a post, and exactly what, before it asks Discord. It asks
Discord with the library's own retries turned off, so nothing is sent twice behind its back.

When Discord gives no clear answer (no answer, a timeout, a Discord error), the post shows
**Sending…** and Modbot does not send it again. A minute later it reads the channel for a message by
the bot with the same text and the same picture, sent from a minute before the attempt. If it finds
one, that message is the post: it shows **Posted** with an **Open** link. It reads on, page by
page, until it has seen the few minutes after the attempt, or the channel's newest message once
those minutes are over; only then is a post it did not find **Failed** with "Discord did not take
the post.". If Modbot cannot
read the channel, or the channel is so busy that it cannot read those minutes in one look, it tries
again every 15 minutes for an hour, then shows "Could not check the channel.". **Modbot never sends
a post again by itself.** Only **Try again** does, and it looks first: it sends only after a look
that read the whole few minutes and did not find the post.

When Discord turns a post away without making anything (it is rate limiting the bot, or the channel
could not be looked up), the post waits two minutes before it is tried again, so the posts behind
it still go.

VRChat works the same way, with its own look. VRChat has answered with an error and posted all the
same, so when it gives no clear answer (an error on its side, no answer, a timeout) the post shows
**Sending…** and Modbot does not send it again. Two minutes later it reads the group's posts, newest
first, for a post by Modbot's VRChat account with the same title and text, made no earlier than two
minutes before the attempt. VRChat changes some characters it is sent, so the two are compared by
their letters and digits only. If it finds one, that post is the post: it shows **Posted**. It reads
on until it has seen a post older than the attempt, or the end of the list; only then is a post it
did not find **Failed** with "VRChat did not add the post.". The group's audit log counts too: when
Modbot's audit-log reading sees VRChat's own record of that post being made, even up to a day late,
the post shows **Posted**.

When VRChat rate limits Modbot, or Modbot is not signed in yet, nothing was made: the post waits, and
is tried again only once VRChat's own wait is over. **Modbot never sends a rate-limited request
again by itself.**

Bluesky lets Modbot choose a post's id itself, so Bluesky is kept to once more simply. Modbot makes
the id once, writes it down before it sends anything, and sends every try under that same id, asking
Bluesky to make the post only if nothing has that id yet. A second try can therefore never make a
second post. When Bluesky gives no clear answer, the post shows **Sending…**, and a minute later
Modbot asks Bluesky for the post by its id: there, it shows **Posted**; not there, it is sent again
under the same id, with the time of that send, because Bluesky sorts posts by it. When Bluesky rate
limits Modbot, nothing more goes to Bluesky until the time Bluesky names.

## What the bot needs in Discord

In the post's channel the bot needs:

- **View Channel** and **Send Messages**, to post;
- **Attach Files**, for a post with a picture;
- **Read Message History**, to look for a post Discord gave no clear answer to.

## What the Bluesky account needs

An app password, saved in **Settings**, **Bluesky**, and a **Check** that passed. See
[Bluesky](/moderation/bluesky).

## What Modbot's VRChat account needs

In your group, Modbot's VRChat account needs **Manage Group Announcement**, the permission for the
group's posts. A post refused for lacking it says so on the post and on **Health**.

## Settings

**Settings**, **Posts** has four switches:

- **Pause all posting**: while it is on, no post is sent anywhere. Posts wait and show **Paused**; one
  more than an hour late is not sent and shows "Not sent on time." with **Post now**. It does not
  stop the calendar's own Discord event, channel card or VRChat calendar entry.
- **Discord posts**: off, no post goes to Discord; Discord posts wait and show **Off**. It is on
  to start with, because nothing goes out unless somebody ticks **Discord** on a post.
- **VRChat posts**: off, no post goes to the VRChat group; VRChat posts wait and show **Off**. It
  is on to start with, for the same reason.
- **Bluesky posts**: the same switch as **Posting** in **Settings**, **Bluesky**. It is off to start
  with, and goes on only once the Bluesky account has passed **Check**.

Each change is written to the audit log with who made it.

Modbot sends at most one post a pass to each site, every 20 seconds or so for Discord, every 15 for
VRChat and every 30 for Bluesky, and at most 10 posts an hour to each, so a mistake cannot flood a
channel, a group or an account.

## Health

The **Posts** card on **Health** shows:

- **Paused** while all posting is paused;
- Discord, VRChat or Bluesky, when posts are waiting on it and it is **Off** or **Not set up**;
- posts that failed in the last 7 days, each leading to **Failed**, with the VRChat permission
  Modbot's account needs when that is why;
- posts Modbot has been looking for on the site for more than 15 minutes.

When there is nothing to say, the card is not shown.

## The audit log

Everything done to a post is written to the audit log, under the operational log: who wrote,
changed, cancelled or sent it now, each post Modbot sent with its link and exactly what it said,
each post that failed with the reason, each edit and delete on a site with who did it, and each
picture uploaded to VRChat for a post, with who chose it.

## What leaves your server

A post goes only where it is ticked, and every place starts unticked.

- **Discord**: the bot sends the post's title, text and picture to the channel, mentions the role if
  one was picked, and publishes it to the channel's followers if that was ticked. The channel's
  readers see it, and so do the following servers' readers.
- **VRChat**: Modbot's VRChat account posts the title and text in your group, for who was picked
  (the group, some of its roles, or everyone on vrchat.com), and has VRChat notify the members only
  if **Notify members** was ticked. The picture goes only while **Upload VRChat pictures** is on; it
  is then uploaded to that VRChat account and stays there as a file on it.
- **Bluesky**: the post's text, and when it has a link, a card with the post's title, that link and
  a small copy of the picture, go to the account's own server, and from there are public on Bluesky
  and copied by other services. Nobody is mentioned.

Nothing about your members goes in a post: no names, no ids, no counts. See
[Privacy](/privacy#what-leaves-your-server).
