---
title: Auto-invites
description: Having Modbot invite people to your group once they have spent long enough in one of your instances and pass your rules.
---

Auto-invites turn the people who already hang around your instances into members. Somebody stands
in one of your group's instances, stays a while, passes the rules you set, and Modbot sends them a
group invite. Nobody presses anything.

This is the opposite way round from [Join requests](/moderation/join-requests): there, people ask
to join and you answer; here, Modbot asks them.

<Callout type="warn" title="It is off until you turn it on">
Auto-invites are off on every Modbot, including one that has just been updated. Nothing is sent
until somebody switches them on.
</Callout>

## Turning it on

**Settings → Auto-invites**. Switch on **Invite people automatically**, set the rules underneath,
and press **Save**.

The tab needs the **Set up auto-invites** permission, which is not in the built-in Moderator or
Viewer roles — grant it on the [Roles](/accounts/roles-and-permissions) page. Administrators have
it already.

## Nobody is invited unless a moderator is in the instance

<Callout type="warn" title="Read this one before you report a bug">
Modbot only knows who is in an instance, and since when, **while a moderator's companion is running
in it**. With no companion reporting, Modbot has no list of people and no arrival times for that
instance — so nobody meets the "minutes in the instance" rule and **no invites go out at all**.
</Callout>

This is the same limit the [Live](/moderation/live) page has, and the reason it says "nobody is
watching" rather than showing you a stale list. Modbot's own instance poll can tell you an instance
is open and how many people are in it; it cannot tell you who they are or when each of them
arrived, and a duration is never guessed from a head count.

So: if you switch auto-invites on and nothing ever happens, check whether anybody was running the
[companion](/companion/install) in the instance at the time.

One more detail worth knowing. When a moderator walks into an instance that is already busy, Modbot
learns that everybody there is *there*, but not when any of them arrived. It counts their time from
the moment it started watching, not from a guess — so somebody who had already been there an hour
still waits the full five minutes from when the moderator arrived.

## The rules

The rule builder is the same one [Giveaways](/moderation/giveaways#rules) use, and it means the same
thing here. Rules combine with **All of**, **Any of** and **None of**, and can be nested.

| Rule | What it asks |
|---|---|
| **Trust rank at least** | Their VRChat trust rank — Visitor, New User, User, Known User, Trusted User or Legend. Nuisance and VRChat Team are not steps on that ladder, so neither ever counts as "Trusted User or better". |
| **VRChat account at least** N days old | The join date on their VRChat profile. |
| **18+ verified** | Modbot has seen them [18+ verified](/moderation/profiles) at least once. |
| **No bans, kicks or flags** | No bans, removals, instance kicks, Discord bans, kicks or timeouts, and no [flag](/moderation/flags) a moderator has not dismissed. Set it to **All time** or to the last so many days. |
| **In Discord for at least** N days | Their Discord join date. |
| **Hours in our instances, at least** N | Every hour they have been seen in your instances, added up. |
| **Hours in one single instance, at least** N | The most they have been seen in any one instance. |
| **Hours in Discord voice, at least** N | Time connected to a voice channel. |
| **Discord messages sent, at least** N | Messages they have sent in your server. |
| **Seen in the last** N days | When they were last seen in one of your instances. |
| **Discord and VRChat accounts linked** | They have [linked their accounts](/discord/account-linking). |
| **Holds the Discord role** | One of your server's roles. |
| **Holds the group role** | One of your VRChat group's roles. Of limited use here, since people being invited are not members yet. |

A rule about somebody Modbot knows nothing about **fails**. An account whose profile has never been
read has no trust rank and no join date, so "trust rank at least Known User" is not true of them.

With no rules at all, everybody who gets past the checks below is invited.

## Minutes in the instance

**Minutes in the instance** is how long somebody must have been there before Modbot will invite
them. It is **five minutes at the lowest and cannot be set lower** — the box will not take a smaller
number, and neither will the server.

It is a separate box rather than a rule for a reason: rules can go inside a **None of** group, and a
five-minute floor you can turn upside down is not a floor.

## One invite every thirty seconds

Modbot sends **at most one invite every thirty seconds**, for the whole deployment — not per person
and not per instance. A busy night with forty strangers in the instance is forty invites over twenty
minutes, not forty invites at once.

That is deliberate and it is not adjustable. Group invites are a VRChat request like any other, and
a burst of them is the kind of thing that gets an account rate limited.

## Who is never invited

Some checks are not rules and cannot be switched off:

- **Anybody already in the group.** Checked before the invite is sent, not after.
- **Anybody on your ban list.**
- **Anybody your group has ever kicked, removed or banned**, even if the ban was later lifted. An
  unban lets somebody come back; it does not send Modbot to fetch them. Invite them by hand if that
  is what you want.
- **Anybody invited recently** — see below.
- **Modbot's own VRChat account.**

## How long an invite is remembered

Forever. Modbot keeps a row per person saying when it last invited them, and **Invite again after**
says how many days must pass before it may invite the same person again. Thirty days to start with.

So somebody who ignores the invite, or declines it, or leaves and comes back an hour later, is not
asked again that evening. Somebody who joins the group in the meantime is a member, and members are
never invited.

**An invite VRChat refused still counts as an invite.** If it could not be delivered — the account
is gone, or they have blocked the group — Modbot does not keep trying every thirty seconds.

## What happens when Modbot restarts

Nothing is lost, because nothing is held in memory. Modbot does not build a queue of people to
invite: every thirty seconds it works out afresh who is standing in your instances right now and
picks the person who has been there longest.

The two things that have to survive a restart both live in the database — who has already been
invited, and when the last invite went out, which is what the thirty seconds is measured from. A
restart cannot cause a burst of invites and cannot cause anybody to be invited twice.

## What it writes down

Every invite is recorded in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log) and on the person's profile, as
**Invited to the group**, with the instance they were in and how long they had been there. No
moderator is named, because none decided — Modbot did.

An invite that VRChat refused is recorded as **Group invite failed**, which is not a claim that
anybody was invited.

VRChat's own group audit log records the invite too, and it says *Modbot* sent it, because Modbot
signs in as its own account. Modbot's record is the one that says why.
