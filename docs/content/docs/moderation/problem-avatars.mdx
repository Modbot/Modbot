---
title: Problem avatars
description: Mark the avatar somebody is wearing as a problem avatar, and Modbot warns your moderators and your Discord channel when anyone arrives in it or switches to it.
---

A **problem avatar** is an avatar you have told Modbot to watch for. When somebody arrives in one of
your group's instances wearing it, or switches into it, your moderators get the same card and bleep
as for a [flagged person](/moderation/live#who-is-flagged).

| To | You need |
|---|---|
| Mark an avatar, see the list or remove an avatar | [**Change settings**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#change-settings) |

## Marking an avatar

Open a person, for example from [Live](/moderation/live) or [People](/moderation/people). On the
**Overview** tab, **Avatar** shows the avatar Modbot last saw them wearing. Press **Mark avatar**.
The panel then shows **Problem avatar**.

An avatar is only shown once a moderator's [companion](/companion/install) has seen the person in
one.

## The list

**Settings**, **Moderation**, **Problem avatars** lists every avatar you marked. Press **Remove** beside
one to take it off.

## How avatars are matched

VRChat's log gives an avatar's name and never its id, so Modbot matches the **name** only, ignoring
capital letters and spaces at either end. Two avatars that share a name cannot be told apart, and an
avatar that is renamed is no longer on the list. **Mark avatar** stores the name exactly as VRChat
wrote it.

## What happens when somebody wears one

- Every paired [companion](/companion/overlay) gets a card for the person, in the same place as a
  **Flagged user joined** card, saying they are wearing a problem avatar and which one. That includes
  the companion of the moderator who saw them.
- If **Post in Discord** is on under [**Flagged**](/moderation/live#post-in-discord), Modbot also posts
  one line in that channel for each person, such as
  `Flagged: Rin is wearing a problem avatar in a group instance: Name of avatar`.

The same person in the same avatar in the same instance is alerted once, however many moderators see
them, and again after about twelve hours. If Modbot restarts, somebody still there can be alerted
once more.
