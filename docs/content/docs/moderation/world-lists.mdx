---
title: World lists
description: Keep lists of worlds in Modbot, let an event pick its world from one, and use Next game during a game night to pick the next world that fits how many people are there.
---

A **world list** is a name and some worlds, for example **Game night**: Prop Hunt, Murder 4,
Among Us. Each world can say the fewest and most players its game is for.

An event on the [Calendar](/moderation/calendar) can take its world from a list instead of one world.
Modbot then picks a world for each date, and during the event **Next game** picks the next one that
fits the people there.

World lists live in Modbot only. Nothing is read from or written to your VRChat favourites.

## Who can use it

| To do this | You need |
|---|---|
| See the **World lists** page, and **Next game** during an event | [**See calendar**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-calendar) |
| Make, change and delete lists; **Pick again**; **Next game** and **Pick another** | [**Manage calendar**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#manage-calendar) and **See calendar** |

Without **See calendar**, **World lists** does not appear in the sidebar. Without **Manage
calendar**, the page is read-only and **Next game** shows the world picked last, with no buttons.

## The page

**World lists** is in the sidebar, under **Community**, after **Calendar**. Each list shows its name,
how many worlds it has, the events that pick from it, and its worlds: picture, name (click it for the
[world popup](/moderation/popups#world-popup)) and players, such as **4–10**, **2+** or **up to 8**.
**—** means any number.

### Making a list

Click **New list**, or **Edit** on a list.

- **Name**: up to 100 characters.
- **Worlds**: type in the box under the worlds. As you type, worlds Modbot already knows (from your
  group's instances, or anyone a moderator's client saw) are listed; click one to add it. To add a
  world Modbot does not know yet, paste its link from vrchat.com (for example
  `https://vrchat.com/home/world/wrld_…`) or its id, and click **Add** (or press Enter).
- **Fewest players** and **Most players**: both optional, at least 1. Leave a box empty for no limit
  on that side.
- The **×** on a row takes the world out.

Click **Save**. A list can hold up to 500 worlds.

When you add a world Modbot has never seen, it asks VRChat about that world once, so its name and
picture show. If VRChat is busy, the world is added by its id and Modbot fills in the name within a
few minutes. If VRChat says there is no such world, the form says **VRChat has no world with that
id.**

### Deleting a list

**Delete** asks first. A list that a draft, scheduled or open event picks from cannot be deleted
(**Delete** is greyed out); change those events to another world or list first. Finished and
cancelled events keep the last world they had.

## An event that picks from a list

In the event form, under **Where**, set **World** to **Pick from a list**, then choose the **World
list**. Everything else about the event works as usual.

### When the world is picked

Modbot picks the world for a date **when that date becomes the event's next one**:

- for a one-off event, or the first date, when you click **Schedule**;
- for a repeating event, as soon as the date before it ends.

From then on that date's world is on the Discord event, the channel post and the calendar feed, and
it is the world the instance opens in. VRChat's own calendar has no world, so it shows nothing
different. Later dates show the list's name until their turn comes.

Modbot never changes a date's world by itself. Moving the event to another time keeps the same world.
Changing the event to another list picks again from the new list.

### Pick again

Open the event on the calendar. Beside **World list**, **Pick again** (with **Manage calendar**) puts
the date's world back and takes the next one. It is there while the date is **Scheduled**; once the
event is **Open**, use **Next game** instead.

### How Modbot picks

Each event shuffles its list, then goes through the whole list before any world comes round again.
When every world has been played, it shuffles again, and the new round never starts with the world
just played. A world added to the list joins the current round; a world taken out is never picked
again. The shuffle is saved, so a restart carries on where it was.

Each event has its own shuffle: two events using the same list do not share one.

## Next game

While an event that picks from a list is **Open**, the event box on the calendar shows **Next game**.
So does the [instance popup](/moderation/popups#instance-popup) of the event's instance.

- **People now** is how many people are in the event's instance right now, from the group's instance
  list Modbot already reads. It is the instance Modbot opened for the event, or the newest group
  instance opened during the event in one of its picked worlds. From the instance popup, it is that
  instance.
- **Next game** picks the next world in the shuffle that has not been played this round and whose
  players fit **People now**. A world with no players set fits any number. When Modbot does not know
  how many people are there, players are ignored.
- **Pick another** puts the shown world back and picks the next one after it that fits.
- The picked world shows with its picture, name and players, and a **vrchat.com** link to its page.
- When nothing fits: **No world fits 7 people.** (or **No other world fits 7 people.** after **Pick
  another**). Nothing is recorded then.

**Next game** never opens an instance. Open the next world yourself in VRChat.

## In the audit log

With the operational entries (you need **See the operational log**):

| Entry | When |
|---|---|
| **World list made** | Someone made a list. |
| **World list changed** | Someone renamed it, or added, took out or changed worlds. |
| **World list deleted** | Someone deleted it. |
| **World picked for an event** | A world was picked: by Modbot for a date, by someone with **Pick again**, or by someone with **Next game** or **Pick another**. Says the world, the list, and the world it replaced. |

None of these hold anything about your members, so a
[purge](/moderation/purge) has nothing to remove from them.
