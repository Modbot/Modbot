---

Modbot can send your group's posts to a Bluesky account, beside Discord and your VRChat group. You
make an **app password** for the account in Bluesky, give it to Modbot with the account's handle,
and press **Check**: Modbot signs in and tells you who it is signed in as, and whether the account
is marked as automated. Then you turn **Posting** on, and a post on the
[**Marketing**](/moderation/marketing) page can tick **Bluesky**.

When your Modbot has a public `https://` address, you can instead **Sign in with Bluesky** on
Bluesky's own sign-in page, with no app password at all. See
[Sign in with Bluesky](#sign-in-with-bluesky).

Everything here is in **Settings**, **Bluesky**, and needs
[**Change settings**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#change-settings).

## What you need

- A Bluesky account for your group. An account of its own is best, so Modbot never signs in to a
  person's own account.
- An **app password** for it, or a public `https://` address for your Modbot to
  [sign in with Bluesky](#sign-in-with-bluesky). Never give Modbot the account's main password:
  Modbot refuses it.

## Set it up

### 1. Mark the account as automated

Bluesky asks accounts that post by themselves to say so. In the Bluesky app, open the account's
settings and mark it as an automated (bot) account. Modbot's **Check** shows whether the account
says so.

### 2. Make an app password

In the Bluesky app, signed in as your group's account:

1. Open **Settings**, **Privacy and security**, **App passwords**.
2. Click **Add App Password** and name it `Modbot`.
3. Leave access to direct messages off. Modbot never reads or sends messages.
4. Click **Create**. Bluesky shows the password once, as four groups of four letters and digits,
   such as `abcd-efgh-ijkl-mnop`. Copy it.

An app password can post and delete posts as the account, but cannot change the account's email or
its main password. You can revoke it in Bluesky at any time, and Modbot stops posting.

### 3. Give it to Modbot and Check

In Modbot, go to **Settings**, **Bluesky**:

1. Type the **Handle**, such as `ourgroup` (Modbot adds `.bsky.social`), `ourgroup.bsky.social` or `ourgroup.com`. A leading `@` is fine, and so is a profile link from `bsky.app`.
2. Paste the **App password** and click **Save**. The page shows **App password: Saved**. The
   password is never shown again.
3. Click **Check**.

Modbot refuses anything that is not shaped like an app password with **Use an app password.**

## Sign in with Bluesky

When a **Public address** is saved under **Settings**, **Server**, and it starts with `https://`
and has no port (such as `https://modbot.example.com`, not `:8443`), **Settings**, **Bluesky** also
has **Sign in with Bluesky**. Without such an address the button is not there: Bluesky has to reach
your Modbot from the internet to sign it in, and only an `https://` address with no port will do.

1. Type the **Handle**, such as `ourgroup` (Modbot adds `.bsky.social`), `ourgroup.bsky.social` or `ourgroup.com`, and click **Sign in with Bluesky**.
2. Bluesky's sign-in page opens. Sign in as your group's account there, and allow Modbot.
3. Bluesky sends you back to **Settings**, **Bluesky**, which says **Signed in.** and shows
   **Sign-in: Bluesky**, the account and whether it is marked as automated, as **Check** does.

Modbot never sees the account's password this way. It asks Bluesky only for what posting needs:
making and deleting posts, making and deleting the records that say who can reply to a post and
whether it can be quoted, pinning a post on the account's profile, uploading a post's
picture or video, reading the account's replies and mentions, and changing the profile's name, bio,
picture and banner. If Bluesky does not offer that yet, Modbot asks for what an app password can do instead.

**A sign-in made before pinning was added cannot pin, and one made before replies were read cannot
read them.** Click **Sign in with Bluesky** again once, so Bluesky asks you to allow what is missing.
One sign-in covers both. Posting is not affected, and **Health** says
**Sign in with Bluesky again to read replies and mentions.** An app password already allows it.

**A sign-in made before who can reply and quote posts were added cannot set them.** A
[calendar event's](/moderation/calendar#bluesky-settings-on-the-form) post that limits who can reply, or
turns quote posts off, waits, and **Marketing** says **Sign in with Bluesky again to let Modbot set who can
reply.** Click **Sign in with Bluesky** again once. Posts that anyone can reply to and quote go out as
before, and an app password already allows all of it.

**One way of signing in at a time.** Signing in with Bluesky forgets a saved app password, and
saving an app password ends the sign-in with Bluesky.

**Changing the public address ends a sign-in with Bluesky**, because Bluesky knows Modbot by that
address. Sign in with Bluesky again after you change it.

If the page says something went wrong instead:

| What it says | What to do |
|---|---|
| Bluesky sign-in was cancelled. | You said no, or went back, on Bluesky's page. Try again. |
| The Bluesky sign-in took too long. Try again. | Finish on Bluesky's page within ten minutes. |
| You were signed out of Modbot before Bluesky sent you back. | Sign in to Modbot and try again. The person who starts it must finish it. |
| This Bluesky sign-in was already used. | The page was loaded twice. Look at **Settings**, **Bluesky**: the first one may have signed in. |
| Bluesky signed in another account than this handle. | On Bluesky's page, sign in as the account the handle names. |
| Bluesky did not finish the sign-in. | Try again. |
| Could not reach Bluesky. | Try again later. |

## What Check shows

Check finds the account from its handle, signs in, and reads the account's profile. **It never posts
and changes nothing on the account.**

| Line | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Signed in as** | The account's handle and its display name. |
| **Automated** | **Yes** when the account is marked as automated, **No** when it is not. |

When something is wrong, Check says what:

| What it says | What to do |
|---|---|
| Could not find this handle. | Check the spelling. A handle that is a website of your own must still point at the account. |
| Could not reach the account's server. | Bluesky, or the server the account lives on, did not answer. Try again later. |
| Bluesky did not accept the handle or app password. | Make a new app password and save it. Modbot does not sign in again until you do. |
| The Bluesky sign-in has ended. | Bluesky no longer takes Modbot's sign-in with Bluesky. Click **Sign in with Bluesky** again. |
| Too many sign-ins. Try again after … | See **Sign-ins** below. |
| Bluesky is limiting Modbot. Try again after … | Bluesky asked Modbot to slow down. Nothing is sent to Bluesky until then. |

What Check found stays on the page until you change the handle or the app password. Change either
and Check again; posts wait until it passes.

## Posting

Once Check has passed, the **Posting** switch appears. It is off to start. On, posts that tick
**Bluesky** go out at their time; off, they wait and show **Off**. The same switch is in **Settings**,
**Posts**, as **Bluesky posts**.

## Post numbers

A Bluesky post that went out shows its likes, reposts and replies beside its **Bluesky** badge on the
[Marketing](/moderation/marketing#after-it-goes) tab. Modbot asks Bluesky's public API for them, with no
sign-in. A post sent in the last 30 days is counted again every 30 minutes; an older one keeps its
last numbers, and so does a post that is gone from Bluesky. Nothing shows before the first count. If
Bluesky limits Modbot, Modbot waits until the limit resets, and the problem shows on
[Health](/self-hosting/health) under **Posts** until a count goes through.

## Replies and mentions

New replies, mentions and quotes of the group's Bluesky account are saved in the
[audit log](/moderation/audit-log) as entries, and [Now](/now#since-you-last-looked) shows a line
**Bluesky replies and mentions** with a count that opens the audit log at those entries. Each entry
keeps who wrote it (their name and handle), what they wrote and a link to the post on Bluesky; a reply
also names the post it answers when Modbot sent that post. Likes, reposts and follows are not saved:
there are too many to be useful.

While **Posting** is on, Modbot asks the account's own server for them about every 3 minutes, and saves
each one once. With Posting off it does not read them, and Health says nothing about them. The
account's own posts are left out. The first time, it starts from then and saves nothing from before.
Each pass reads at most the newest 300, so more than that in 3 minutes would leave the oldest out.

Reading needs the account's own sign-in: an app password allows it, and so does a Sign in with Bluesky
made after replies were added. An older one has to [sign in again](#sign-in-with-bluesky).

If Bluesky limits Modbot, Modbot waits until the limit resets and never asks again before then. Any
other failure waits 5 minutes. Posting is not held up by either. The problem shows on
[Health](/self-hosting/health) under **Posts**, as **Bluesky · Replies and mentions**, until a read
goes through. Bluesky's limit for this call has not been measured, so Modbot's pace is a cautious guess.

## Profile page

Under **Integrations**, next to VRChat and Discord, the **Bluesky** page shows your group's account
the way Bluesky does: the banner, the round picture, the name and `@handle`, how many follow it, how many
it follows and how many posts it made, and the bio. It has two tabs:

- **Posts**: the posts Modbot sent to the account, newest first, with their likes, reposts and replies.
- **Replies**: the replies and mentions Modbot saved in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log).

You need [**See analytics**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-analytics) to open the page,
[**See posts**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-posts) for the **Posts** tab and
[**See the audit log**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-the-audit-log) for the **Replies** tab.

### Edit profile

**Edit profile** changes four things on Bluesky: the name, the bio, the picture and the banner. It needs
[**Change settings**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#change-settings). Anything you leave alone stays as it
is, and an empty name or bio clears it. It does not touch the pinned post or anything else on the profile.
It does not change your VRChat group.

Bluesky's limits:

| | Limit |
|---|---|
| Name | Up to 64 characters |
| Bio | Up to 256 characters |
| Picture and banner | A PNG or JPEG, under 1 MB each |

Your browser makes a copy of each picture you choose, under 1 MB, the way it does for posts, so any picture your browser can open will do. Modbot checks
the limits before it asks Bluesky anything.

If somebody changed the profile on Bluesky while you were saving, Modbot saves nothing and says so; open
**Edit profile** again and save once more. Saves are a few seconds apart at least. If a save fails, the
page and [Health](/self-hosting/health) under **Posts** say why, until a save goes through. If Bluesky
limits Modbot, nothing is sent until the limit resets. Bluesky's limits for these calls have not been
measured, so Modbot's pace is a cautious guess.

The page reads the numbers from Bluesky's public API, with no sign-in, and keeps them for 10 seconds.
Each change is saved in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log) with the settings changes.

## Calendar events

An event on the [**Calendar**](/moderation/calendar#bluesky) can post itself here: tick **Bluesky**
on the event, pick **Post it** (**1 hour**, **1 day**, **3 days** or **1 week before**), and every
date of the event gets its own post, sent that long before it starts. These posts appear in
[**Marketing**](/moderation/marketing) like any other, and follow the same rules: **Posting**,
**Pause all posting**, the hourly limit and Bluesky's rate limit.

The event's **Bluesky** tab also sets who can reply (**Anyone**, **Nobody**, or your followers, the people
you follow and the people you mention), whether quote posts are allowed, and the post's language (up to
three). The languages go in the post itself; who can reply and quote posts go in two small records Modbot
makes right after the post.

Bluesky cannot edit a post, so when an event changes, Modbot **deletes** the post that already went
out and **posts again**. A cancelled date, a cancelled or deleted event, or **Bluesky** turned off
on the event, deletes its post and makes no new one. **While Posting is off, Modbot neither makes nor
deletes these posts**: a cancelled event's post stays on Bluesky until Posting is on again.

A post ends with the tags of the event's categories, then the tags under **Tags on every post** (up to
five, such as `#VRChat #Karaoke`; type `#` or leave it out, split them with spaces or commas). Changing
any tag changes the words of every post that carries it, so the posts of dates that have not started and
are already up are deleted and posted again. See [Tags](/moderation/calendar#tags).

When the first person is in the instance of a date whose event has **Announce on Bluesky when the first
person joins** ticked, Modbot replies under that date's post: *We're live in* and the world's name, then a
link (the instance's join link when **Who can join** is **Anyone**, otherwise your VRChat group's page).
The reply is in [**Marketing**](/moderation/marketing) too and is sent once. It needs the date's post
to be on Bluesky; without one there is no reply. See
[Announce when the first person joins](/moderation/calendar#announce-when-the-first-person-joins).

A calendar event's post carries up to four pictures as full pictures under the text, each with its
own alt text (the post's title when the alt text is left empty), and has no link card; a link in the
text stays clickable. The event's cover is the first picture until the pictures are changed on the
Bluesky tab. Modbot sends a small copy of each picture, made in your browser when the picture is
chosen: at most 2000 pixels on its longer side and under 1 MB. An event whose cover has no such copy (chosen before this
was added) gets its copy the next time someone who can manage the calendar or templates opens the
Calendar page; their browser makes it without a word on screen. Until then it sends the cover itself
when it is 1 MB or smaller; otherwise the post goes with its link card and no picture.

A calendar event's post can carry one video instead of pictures: Bluesky allows a video no pictures beside
it, so adding a video takes the pictures out and adding a picture is not possible while there is a video.
The video is a file (an MP4, MPEG, WebM or QuickTime file of at most 50 MB) or a link. A file is kept by
Modbot from the moment it is picked, because Bluesky deletes an upload that no post points to within
hours and the post goes out days after the event is saved; it goes up to Bluesky only as the post is made,
and Modbot deletes it once no event, template or post still to be sent uses it. A link is kept only as an
address: Modbot downloads the video from it as the post goes out, and the link must start with `https://`
and point at a public address. Like a picture, the video has an alt text (the post's title when left
empty). Bluesky shows a video a few seconds after the post is up. When the video cannot go (the link does
not answer or is not a video, the file is gone, Bluesky refuses it, or the sign-in is older than video
uploads), the post goes up without it and its line in **Marketing** says why. A sign-in made
before video uploads were added needs **Sign in with Bluesky** done again for a post with a video.

A calendar event's post can carry one GIF from Klipy instead of pictures or a video. The **GIF** button
under the post's box searches Klipy; it is there once the Klipy key is set under **Settings**, **Bluesky**,
**GIF search**, **Klipy key**, and with no key there is no button. The key can only have
letters, numbers, `-` and `_`. The GIF goes up the way Bluesky's own app posts one,
as a link card with the GIF's Klipy address, so Bluesky's apps play it in the feed. Modbot keeps only that
address and the GIF's alt text, never a copy of the GIF, and refuses any address that is not on Klipy's
`static.klipy.com`. The key goes to everyone who can manage the calendar or its templates, because their
browsers do the searching.

### Pin the next event

Under **Settings**, **Bluesky**, the **Pin the next event** switch (shown once Check has passed) keeps
the post of the next date pinned on the account's profile. Of the dates whose post is on Bluesky, it
pins the one that starts first and has not ended. When that date ends it pins the next date's post, and
when no later date has a post it takes the pin off. A pass looks at the profile at most once every 15
minutes unless something changed, and only while **Posting** is on and **Pause all posting** is off.

**A post you pinned by hand stays.** Modbot pins only while nothing is pinned or while the pinned post is
an event post Modbot made; a post written on the Marketing tab and pinned by hand is left alone. Unpin yours on Bluesky and Modbot pins the next date's post at its next look. Modbot
changes nothing else on the profile: it writes the profile back as it read it, only if nobody saved it
in between, with just the pinned post changed. Pinning and unpinning are recorded as
**Post pinned** and **Post unpinned**. When Bluesky refuses a pin (a sign-in made before the profile
right was asked for needs **Sign in with Bluesky** again), the **Posts** card on Health says why, until
a pin goes through. Turning the switch off leaves
the pin where it is.

### Weekly line-up

Under **Settings**, **Bluesky**, the **Weekly line-up** switch (shown once Check has passed) makes one post
a week that lists the next seven days. Pick the **Day**, the time (**At**, on the hour) and the **Time
zone**; it starts on Monday at 12:00 in UTC. Turning it on never posts for a time that has already gone by.

The post starts with *This week at* and the group's name, then one line for each date, in order: the day,
the time in the line-up's time zone on a 24-hour clock, and the title (such as `Mon 20:00 Karaoke`).

- **Which dates.** Every date in the next seven days of an event that everyone can see: **Visible to** is
  **Everyone** and no role is needed. Where the event goes (VRChat, Discord, Google) and whether it goes to
  Bluesky itself do not matter, so a Discord-only event counts. **Visible to** is always shown in the
  form and starts as **Group**, so set it to **Everyone** for an event that belongs in the line-up. A
  members-only event is never in it, and neither is a cancelled date or a cancelled or deleted event.
- **Too long.** A post holds 300 characters. Modbot lists as many dates as fit, then *+N more*.
- **The link.** When the group's [Google calendar](/moderation/google-calendar) is set up and public, the post
  ends with its public page, which gives the post a link card. Without that there is no link.
- **A week with no dates** gets no post.
- **After it is posted** the post is left alone. A date changed or cancelled later does not change it, and
  it is never deleted or posted again.
- **A missed time.** A time more than an hour past (because **Posting** was off, say) is skipped, and the
  next week's post goes out as usual.

It is an ordinary post: it shows in [**Marketing**](/moderation/marketing) and follows **Posting**, **Pause
all posting**, the hourly limit and Bluesky's rate limit, and Modbot never sends it again after a rate limit.
When it fails, the **Posts** card on Health shows it. See
[Weekly line-up](/moderation/calendar#weekly-line-up).

## Remove

**Remove** asks first, then forgets the handle, the app password or the sign-in with Bluesky, and
Modbot's sign-in, and turns **Posting** off. Posts already on Bluesky stay there, calendar events' posts included; delete them from **Marketing** first if you
want them gone. Revoke the app password in Bluesky too.

## Sign-ins

Modbot signs in once and keeps its sign-in, encrypted in its database, so a restart does not sign in
again. It renews the sign-in by itself as Bluesky asks. It signs in with the app password again only
when the kept sign-in stops working, and then at most once every 10 minutes and 20 times a day, far
under Bluesky's own limits. Saving a new app password lets the next sign-in go at once.

A sign-in with Bluesky is renewed the same way, with Bluesky's sign-in server, a little before each
short-lived token ends. It has no app password to fall back on: when Bluesky stops taking it, posts
wait, the page says **The Bluesky sign-in has ended.**, and someone clicks **Sign in with Bluesky**
again.

When Bluesky rate limits Modbot, Modbot stops every request to Bluesky until the time Bluesky says,
and never sends the request again before then.

## What leaves your server

| Sent | To | When |
|---|---|---|
| The handle | Bluesky's public API, or the handle's own website | **Check** |
| The account's lasting id | `plc.directory`, or the account's own website | **Check**, and to find where an older post lives |
| The account's lasting id and the app password, then Modbot's sign-in | The account's own server | Signing in and renewing |
| Modbot's address, the handle, and requests signed with Modbot's own key | Bluesky's sign-in server, which the account's own server names | **Sign in with Bluesky**, and renewing that sign-in |
| A post's text, and either its pictures (a small copy of each, with the alt text you wrote, or the post's title when you wrote none; up to four for a calendar event, one for any other post), or a calendar event's one video (a file or the video found at a link, with its alt text), or a calendar event's one GIF (its Klipy address and alt text, as a link card), or, when it has none of these, its link card (the post's title and the link) | The account's own server, and from there public on Bluesky | Only for posts with **Bluesky** ticked, and for each date of a calendar event with **Bluesky** on |
| The post's languages (up to three codes such as `en`), when you picked some; and, when you limited who can reply or turned quote posts off, a record saying so, with the address of the post it belongs to | The account's own server, and from there public on Bluesky | Only for a calendar event with **Bluesky** on and those choices set, with each date's post |
| A reply: "We're live in" the world's name and a link, with the address and content id of the date's post it answers | The account's own server, and from there public on Bluesky | Only for an event with **Announce on Bluesky when the first person joins** ticked, once for each date, when the first person is in the instance |
| The weekly line-up: the group's name, and the title and time of each public date in the next seven days, with the link to the Google calendar's public page | The account's own server, and from there public on Bluesky | Only with **Weekly line-up** on, once a week |
| The account's profile (its name, description, pictures and pinned post), written back with only the pinned post changed | The account's own server, and from there public on Bluesky | Only with **Pin the next event** on, when the pinned post changes |
| The account's profile (its name, description, pictures and pinned post), written back with only the name, bio, picture and banner you changed, and a picture you chose | The account's own server, and from there public on Bluesky | Only when you press **Save** in **Edit profile** |
| The account's lasting id (to ask for its profile and its numbers) | Bluesky's public API, with no sign-in | When the **Bluesky** page is opened, at most once every 10 seconds |
| The address of a post Modbot made | The account's own server | When Modbot deletes a post, such as one a changed or cancelled calendar event made |
| The addresses of the posts Modbot made in the last 30 days, up to 25 in a request | Bluesky's public API, with no sign-in | About every 30 minutes for each post, to read its likes, reposts and replies |
| Modbot's sign-in, asking for the account's replies, mentions and quotes (a request for the account's own notifications) | The account's own server, which passes it on to Bluesky | About every 3 minutes, once the account has passed **Check** |

**Posts on Bluesky are public**, and other services copy them. Deleting one removes it from Bluesky,
not from copies others already took.

Modbot never mentions anyone in a Bluesky post: an `@handle` in the text stays plain text, because a
mention would notify that person. Nothing about your members goes in a post. See
[Privacy](/privacy#what-leaves-your-server) and [Security](/security#secrets-stored-in-the-database).

With **Sign in with Bluesky**, Bluesky's sign-in server also reads Modbot's client document at
`/oauth/bluesky/client-metadata.json` on your public address: Modbot's name, its address, where to
send you back, and the public half of Modbot's own signing key. The private half never leaves your
server.
