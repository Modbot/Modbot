The **Live** page lists every instance your group has open right now. Each card shows the world,
the instance number, who can join, the region and how many people are inside. When a moderator
with the companion is in an instance, the card also lists who is there.

Live needs the [**See live instances**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-live-instances)
permission. Without it, **Live** does not appear in the sidebar.

## What a card shows

Every open group instance gets a card, whether or not anybody from your team is in it.

- **The world.** Its picture and name. Click either to open the [world popup](/moderation/popups#world-popup).
- **The instance number.** Click it to open the [instance popup](/moderation/popups#instance-popup).
  Beside it: who can join (**Group members**, **Members and friends** or **Anyone**) and the region.
- **The head count**, on the right. A dash means Modbot has no number for it yet.
- **Watching.** The moderators whose companion is in the instance right now, or
  **Nobody watching**.
- **Here now.** Only while somebody is watching: everyone in the instance, with a count. Each name
  opens the [person popup](/moderation/popups#person-popup).
- **Last seen**, with a time. Only when nobody is watching now but somebody was earlier: who was
  there when the last moderator left. This is not who is there now.

**Flagged** people, and anybody kicked or banned before, are listed first. Everybody else is in
the order they came in.

## Live on Twitch

While your group's Twitch channel is streaming, a **Live on Twitch** card sits above the
instances: the stream's title and category, how long it has been live, the viewer count and **Open**
for the channel. It is not there when the channel is not live. It needs
[Twitch set up](/integrations/twitch), and the same permission as the rest of the page.

Next to a name you may see:

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Flagged** | One of your group's [flag rules](#who-is-flagged) matches this person. What matched follows, such as "2 kicks or bans" or "Nuisance". |
| **Member** | The person is in your group. |
| **New** | A moderator's client saw them come in less than five minutes ago. For the first minute their whole row is lit as well. |
| arrived 21:14 | A moderator's client saw them come in at that time. |
| here before 21:02 | They were already there when watching started, so nobody saw them arrive. Modbot does not guess an earlier time. |

### Who is flagged

A person is **Flagged** when any rule that is switched on matches them. The same rules decide the
red row and the **Flagged user joined** card on the [companion overlay](/companion/overlay).

| Rule | Matches | Shown as |
|---|---|---|
| **Watched** | A moderator is [watching](/moderation/notes#watching-a-person) them, or a Discord account linked to them. Always on. | `Watched` |
| **Kicks and bans** | They have been removed from the group or banned at least once. | `2 kicks or bans` |
| **Warns** | They have been warned in an instance at least this many times. 5 unless you change it. | `5 warns` |
| **Nuisance on VRChat** | VRChat marks the account as a nuisance. | `Nuisance` |
| **AutoMod** | An [AutoMod](/moderation/flags) flag on them, or on a Discord account linked to them, that nobody dismissed. Flags from a rule still on trial do not count. | `AutoMod: Slurs` |

When several match, all of them are listed, such as `2 kicks or bans · Nuisance`. A watch is always
listed first, as the word alone. Its reason is only on the person's **Notes** tab, for anyone who
can see the audit log.

All of them are on to start with. To change them: **Settings**, **Moderation**, **Flagged**, for
anyone with [**Change settings**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#change-settings). You can turn each
rule off except **Watched**, set the number of warns (1 to 99), and pick which AutoMod rules count
instead of every rule.

A ban counts for ever unless you tick **Lifted bans stop counting** under **Kicks and bans** and set
**Days after lifting** (1 to 3650). Then a ban that was lifted stops counting that many days after
it was lifted. A ban that still stands, and a kick, always count.

A trust rank is known once Modbot has read the person's profile. For someone Modbot has never seen
before, that can be a little after they join: their row turns red when the rank arrives, but no
join card is sent that late.

## Since the event started

While any instance is open, a line at the top counts what has happened since the oldest of them
opened, such as **Since 20:02: 212 arrivals · 4 warns · 3 kicks · 1 ban**.

| Count | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Arrivals** | People a moderator's client saw walk into an open instance. Each person is counted once, however often they came back and however many moderators saw them. Nobody is counted while nobody is watching. |
| **Warns** | Warns in an instance, from your group's audit log. |
| **Kicks** | Kicks from an instance, from the audit log. Removing somebody from the group is not counted. |
| **Bans** | Bans from the group, from the audit log. |

The line is gone when no instance is open.

## Where the numbers come from

Two kinds of source feed this page, and they answer different questions.

| What | Comes from | How often |
|---|---|---|
| Which instances are open | Modbot asks VRChat for your group's instance list | Every 10 seconds |
| Head count | Each instance's own VRChat page | About every 30 seconds per instance |
| Who is inside | The companions of moderators who are in the instance | As each client reports |
| World name and picture | VRChat's world page, read when Modbot first sees the world | Once, and again if the world turns up in a new instance 30 days or more later |

VRChat tells Modbot how many people are in an instance, but not who they are. So every card has a
head count, and names only appear when a moderator's companion is inside.

If an instance's own page cannot be read, or the last good read is more than two minutes old,
Modbot shows the number from the group's instance list instead. That number may leave some people
out, so treat it as a rough count.

The page redraws the moment somebody joins or leaves, or an instance opens or closes, over a
[live connection](/api/live-updates) to the Modbot server, and asks again every 30 seconds on its
own for the head counts. The word at the top right says how it is connected: **Live**,
**Polling**, **Connecting** or **Off**. The connection is held only while the browser tab is on
screen. Everything on the page comes from Modbot's own database, so leaving Live open does not
use up any of your group's VRChat requests.

An instance leaves the page when it drops off VRChat's group instance list, which Modbot notices
within about 10 seconds.

## When a moderator counts as watching

Watching starts the moment **your** companion reports anything at all in the instance — somebody
arriving, somebody leaving, or you walking in yourself. For that to work:

1. The companion is installed and [paired](/companion/pairing) to your Modbot account.
2. Your Modbot account is [linked to your VRChat account](/accounts/staff-accounts#linking-a-vrchat-account-required).
3. You are in VRChat, in that instance.

A companion only ever reports what its own moderator's VRChat log shows, and that log only shows
the instance they are standing in. So a report from your client in an instance is your client
being in it, whoever the report is about.

A moderator whom somebody else's client sees is in the instance. Whether they are also watching
depends on their own client reporting there.

Watching stops at whichever comes first:

- you leave the instance;
- your client says VRChat's log stopped (for example, you closed VRChat);
- your client reports from a different instance;
- you turn up in a different instance;
- the instance closes.

If you run the companion on two PCs in the same instance, you appear once, from whichever of them
reported first.

When two moderators overlap, the instance counts as watched without a break. If Alice arrives at
eight, Bob at half past and Alice leaves at nine, the list is built from everything reported since
eight.

Watching is also what decides whether a kick in the
[audit log](/moderation/audit-log#how-long-somebody-had-been-in-the-instance) can say how long the
person had been in the instance. A kick in an instance nobody was watching says nothing about how
long they were there, and **arrived** against **here before** is the same difference there as it
is here.

## Common problems

**It says Nobody watching, but I am in the instance.** Check that the companion is running,
paired and not paused, and that your Modbot account is linked to the VRChat account you are
playing on. A client paired to somebody else's Modbot account makes **them** the watcher, not you.
Until 19 September 2026 a companion started after you were already in an instance never counted as
watching it; it does now.

**An instance I can see in VRChat is missing.** Live only lists instances that belong to your
group and appear in VRChat's group instance list. If no instances ever appear, check that Modbot's
VRChat account is working on the [Health](/self-hosting/health) page.

**No open instances.** Your group has no instances open right now, or Modbot has not read the list
yet.
