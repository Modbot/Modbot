Modbot keeps its own copy of your group's member list and ban list, read from VRChat. The
**Members** view of the [People](/moderation/people) page and the **Bans** page show those copies.
Nothing on either is typed in by hand or guessed. When Modbot does not know something yet, the page
says so.

<Callout type="info" title="What Modbot can do from these pages">
Modbot can **kick**, **ban** and **unban**, each behind its own permission. See
[Kicking, banning and unbanning](#kicking-banning-and-unbanning). It cannot **warn** anybody: the
**Warn** permission is on the Roles page but nothing reads it yet, and neither does **Act on many
at once**. Moderation done in VRChat itself still arrives here, because Modbot reads VRChat's group
audit log.
</Callout>

## What "as last swept" means

You will see the words *sweep* and *last synced* on both lists. A **sweep** is Modbot reading a whole
list from VRChat, one page of up to 100 people at a time, from start to finish. When it gets to the
end, it rests, then starts again from the top.

So the lists show **what the last complete read found**. **Last synced 12 minutes ago** means the
last complete read finished 12 minutes ago. Anything that changed in VRChat after that shows up
after the next read.

These are the default timings. You can see the ones your Modbot uses under **Settings**, **Server**,
**Sync timings**, on the **Member list** and **Ban list** cards. They cannot be changed from the app.

| List | **Time between pages** | **Rest between rounds** |
|---|---|---|
| Member list | 2 seconds | 15 minutes |
| Ban list | 3.5 seconds | 30 minutes |

For example, a group of 5,000 members is 50 pages, which takes about 100 seconds to read. With the
15-minute rest, a change on VRChat's side normally shows in the Members view within about 17
minutes.

The pages do not refresh by themselves. To fetch the list again, change a filter, move to another
page, or reload the browser tab.

**The first read.** Until Modbot has read a list all the way through once, the page shows a
warning: **Reading the member list for the first time.** (or **The member list has not been read
yet.**). What you see until then is only the pages read so far, not the whole group.

## Members

**Members** is the [People](/moderation/people) page with the **Membership** filter set to
**Members**. Open it from the **Members** tab on the VRChat page, with `g` then `m`, or by searching
for **Members** (`Ctrl`+`K`). The sidebar lights **People** while it is open. Set **Membership** to
**People who left** for the people a complete read no longer listed.

It needs **See profiles**, or **See members** on its own. With only See members you see members and
people who left, without the **Discord** column.

Above the list: when it was last synced, whether a new sweep is running now, and how many members
the last complete read found.

The columns are **Person**, **Discord**, **Roles**, **Joined**, **Last seen by Modbot** and
**Known for**, with **Left** in place of **Known for** for people who left. The last column on each
row opens a menu with **Kick** and **Ban**; see
[Kicking, banning and unbanning](#kicking-banning-and-unbanning). The search, the filters, the
sorting and the keyboard are People's: see [People](/moderation/people#search-filters-and-sorting).

The Members page was a page of its own until September 2026. Its old address, `/members`, and every
link to it still work: they open this view, with the filters the link carried.

## Bans

The **Bans** page needs **See the audit log**.

There are two tabs, because they answer two different questions.

### Ban list

Everyone VRChat says is banned from your group right now, however long ago the ban was issued.
**This is the list to check before deciding somebody is not banned.**

Above it: when it was last synced and how many bans the last complete read found.

| Control | Options |
|---|---|
| **Search by name or id** | As on [People](/moderation/people#search-filters-and-sorting). |
| Status | **Bans that stand**, **Bans that were lifted** or **Both**. |

**Bans that were lifted** are bans a complete read no longer listed.

The columns are **Person**, **Banned on**, **Modbot first saw it**, **Lifted** (when you are
looking at lifted bans), and **Case file** (with **See profiles**). 50 bans a page, with the page
numbers at the bottom and the page you are on in the address. With **Unban**, each standing ban also
has an **Unban** button.

### People acted on more than once

Everyone who has been kicked, warned, banned, removed from the group or turned away more than
once. See [Reviews and repeat offenders](/moderation/reviews#people-acted-on-more-than-once).

## Kicking, banning and unbanning

Modbot can do three things to a person in your VRChat group, each behind its own permission:

| Action | Needs | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Kick** | **Kick** | The person popup, and the row menu in the **Members** view. |
| **Ban** | **Ban** | The person popup, wherever it is opened from, and the row menu in the **Members** and **People who left** views. |
| **Unban** | **Unban** | The person popup, and the **Bans** page. |

Somebody who is already banned is offered **Unban** instead of **Ban**.

### Who each action works on

**Ban works on anybody, whether or not they are in your group.** You do not have to wait for
somebody to join before you can keep them out. If you have heard about a person from another
group, from a Discord message or from a [flag](/moderation/flags), open them and ban them: VRChat
bans an account, not a membership. Somebody Modbot has never seen before is added to its records
by the ban, and their name and picture appear once the profile refresh reaches them.

The other two only make sense in one direction:

- **Kick** is offered only to somebody the member list says is in the group. There is nothing to
  remove anybody else from, and VRChat answers a kick of somebody who is not in the group with
  **They are not in the group.**
- **Unban** is offered only against a ban that stands. VRChat answers an unban of somebody who is
  not banned with **They are not banned.**

Every action opens a confirmation naming the person, with the group's
[ban reasons](/moderation/case-files#the-reason-list) and an optional note. A reason is required on
a ban and optional on a kick or an unban.

Modbot asks VRChat, waits, and then tells you what happened. If VRChat refuses, the screen says what
VRChat said and **nothing is recorded as done** — there is no case where Modbot shows a ban that did
not happen. A kick or a ban marks the person as gone from the member list straight away, and a ban
adds them to the ban list; the next sweep confirms it.

A ban also writes its [case file](/moderation/case-files) from the reason and the note, so the ban
is written up at the moment it is made rather than chased up afterwards. Once the ban is done, the
confirmation offers **Open the case file**, to add screenshots or more detail straight away.

Pressing the button twice does one thing. Each confirmation carries a key, and Modbot refuses a
second action under the same key and shows you the first one's answer.

Modbot will not act on the VRChat account it signs in as.

## Who banned somebody, and why

- The **Ban list** knows who is banned, but not who banned them.
- A ban **made from Modbot** knows who decided it: VRChat records every action Modbot takes as
  Modbot's own account, so the moderator's name lives in Modbot's audit log and nowhere else.
- The [audit log](/moderation/audit-log) knows who banned them, for every ban Modbot saw happen.
  It reaches back only as far as VRChat's own audit log went when Modbot first synced.
- None of them knows **why**. VRChat's audit log has nowhere to put a reason, so your group writes one
  itself in a [case file](/moderation/case-files).

## How joins, leaves and bans are recorded

When somebody joins, leaves, gets a role, or is banned or unbanned, Modbot adds it to the
[audit log](/moderation/audit-log). Most of the time that entry comes from VRChat's own audit log,
which says exactly who did it and when, and shows **VRChat** as its source.

The sweeps are a backup. If VRChat's audit log missed a change, the next sweep notices the
difference and records it with **Sync** as its source and no "who", because a list cannot say who
changed it. A sweep waits a couple of minutes for the audit log first, so the same change is not
recorded twice.
