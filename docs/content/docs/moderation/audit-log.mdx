The **Audit log** page is one timeline of everything Modbot has recorded: bans, kicks and role
changes from VRChat, people coming and going in instances, Discord events, case files, and
Modbot's own record of sign-ins, settings changes and sync problems. Each row is written as a
sentence, and every name in it opens a [popup](/moderation/popups).

## Two permissions, one page

What you see depends on two separate permissions. The page opens if you have either.

| Permission | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [**See the audit log**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-the-audit-log) | Moderation and member history: who did what to whom. |
| [**See the operational log**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-the-operational-log) | Modbot's own record of how it is run: sign-ins, settings, accounts, sync problems. |

The split is on purpose. A moderator who needs to see bans does not need to see that the owner
changed the email settings or which API keys exist. The server removes what you may not see from
every request, so you cannot get around it by editing the page address.

When you open **More filters**, the page says what is hidden from you: **Hidden from this account:
logins, settings changes and sync failures.** or **Hidden from this account: bans, kicks and role
changes.**

### What each one covers

**See the audit log:**

- joins, leaves, bans, unbans, removals from the group, role changes and invites;
- the rest of VRChat's group audit log: instance kicks and warns, join requests, group posts,
  instances opened and closed, calendar events, changes to group details;
- profile changes and the 18+ verified mark, including changes a moderator made by hand;
- instance joins, leaves, avatar changes and "already here" reports from companions;
- Discord joins, leaves, bans, kicks, timeouts, nickname and role changes, voice, deleted
  messages, and channel and role changes;
- Discord account links, and the roles given or taken because of them;
- case files written, edited, withdrawn and profile snapshots taken again;
- reviews opened and closed;
- moderation rule flags, dismissals, and messages deleted or timeouts given by a rule.

**See the operational log:**

- sign-ins, failed sign-ins, password, username and contact changes, "sign out everywhere";
- staff accounts created, invited, disabled, enabled, and their roles changed; reset links;
- Modbot roles created, changed and deleted;
- API keys and webhooks;
- settings changes, including the ban reason list and moderation rules;
- sync failures, rate limit stops, database updates and old data being removed;
- AI spend limits being reached, and a person's data being purged;
- Discord bot commands run and log messages posted.

Anything Modbot cannot name, if it came from VRChat's group audit log, counts as moderation
history.

## Reading a row

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **When** | The time, with the date under it. Hover for the full date and time. A range such as `~14:05–14:20` means Modbot knows it happened between those times but not exactly when, for example a change a sweep noticed. |
| **Source** | Where the entry came from (see below). |
| **What happened** | A sentence naming who did what to whom, and where. Hover to see the entry's type. |

The sources:

| Source | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **VRChat** | VRChat's own group audit log. Exact. |
| **Sync** | Modbot noticed a difference between two reads of the member or ban list. It has a time window and no "who". |
| **Client** | A moderator's companion. |
| **Discord** | Seen by the Discord bot. |
| **Manual** | Entered by a person through Modbot, such as a case file or a change to the 18+ verified mark. |
| **Modbot** | Something that happened inside Modbot, such as a sign-in or a settings change. |

## Filtering

All sources are on by default. Click a source to show only that one; click more to add them back.

**More filters** opens the rest:

| Filter | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Subject id** | Only entries about this id, for example a VRChat user id. |
| **Actor id** | Only entries done by this id. |
| **From** and **To** | A date range. Both days are included. |
| **Recent actors** | Up to eight people who did the most in the last 90 days, with their count. Click one to filter by them; click again to clear. |
| **Type** | Click entry types to show only those. The list only contains types you are allowed to see. |

The page shows 50 entries at a time. **Load more** at the bottom fetches the next 50.

The page does not refresh by itself. Change a filter or reload to see new entries.

A person's own entries are also on the **Logs** tab of their [person popup](/moderation/popups#person-popup),
and an instance's on the **Logs** tab of its instance popup.

## Where the timeline starts

Above the table: **Oldest recorded entry:** and a date. Anything older than that is not in Modbot.
While Modbot is still reading older entries from VRChat's audit log, it adds **catch-up still
running**.

VRChat's group audit log only reaches back a limited time. Modbot keeps what it has read from then
on, for as long as your retention settings allow. See [Data and retention](/self-hosting/data-retention).

## How quickly things appear

Modbot reads VRChat's group audit log every 8 seconds while things are happening. When nothing new
comes in, it slows down step by step, to once every 5 minutes at the slowest. These are the
defaults; **Settings**, **Sync**, **Group audit log** shows the numbers your Modbot uses.

Companion and Discord entries arrive as they are reported.

## What the audit log does not contain

- **Why** somebody was banned. VRChat's audit log has no reason. Write a
  [case file](/moderation/case-files).
- **Warnings.** Modbot cannot warn anybody yet.

A kick, ban or unban made **from Modbot** appears twice, and the two entries say different things.
Modbot's own entry names the moderator who pressed the button and the reasons they picked; VRChat's
entry, which arrives with the next audit-log sync, says only that Modbot's account did it. VRChat
records every action Modbot takes as Modbot's own account, so the moderator's name exists in
Modbot's entry and nowhere else.
