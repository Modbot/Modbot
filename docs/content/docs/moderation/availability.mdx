**Availability** is where each person on your team says which hours of the week they are free, and
where the team's hours are shown together. It is a week that repeats: "Tuesdays 18:00 to 22:00". It
has no specific dates, and it is not connected to any calendar.

It is for finding a time: when to hold a team meeting, when an event can have staff, and when
somebody is likely to be around.

## Who can use it

| To do this | You need |
|---|---|
| Fill in your own week, on the **Mine** tab | [**Enter availability**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#enter-availability) |
| See the team's heatmap, who is free in each hour and the best times, on the **Team** tab | [**See availability**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-availability) |

The two are separate. Somebody with only **Enter availability** sees only **Mine**, and never anyone
else's times. Somebody with only **See availability** sees only **Team**, and is not on it. Neither is
in the built-in **Moderator** or **Viewer** roles. **Administrator** holds both.

**Availability** is in the sidebar, under **Community**, after **Lists**. `g` then `z` opens it from any
page.

## The team

The people on the team are everyone whose roles include **Enter availability** (or **Administrator**)
and whose account is not disabled or deleted. Somebody with the permission who has not saved a week
yet is on the list with no hours.

## Mine

**Mine** is a grid of the week, seven days of twenty-four hours, from Sunday to Saturday.

1. Pick your **Time zone**. The first time it is the zone your browser is in; after that, the zone you saved.
   Beside it, **Clock** writes the hours as **17:00** (**24h**) or **5 PM** (**12h**). It starts as your
   browser's clock, is remembered in this browser, and is the same on **Team**. It changes only how
   hours are written, not what is saved.
2. Under **Paint**, pick **Free**, **If needed** or **Erase**.
3. Paint the hours. With a mouse, press and drag. On a phone, tap an hour, or touch and hold for a
   moment and then drag. A quick swipe scrolls the page as usual.
4. Press **Save**.

**If needed** means you could do it if you had to; it is drawn striped, and **Free** is drawn solid.
Pressing an hour that already holds the chosen paint clears it, and so does the rest of that
stroke. An hour you leave empty is not free.

Nothing is saved until you press **Save**. Beside it, **Not saved** means you have changes that are
not saved yet, and **Saved** means what you see is what is stored. On a phone the grid turns so the
hours run down and the days run across, which keeps every hour big enough to hit.

You can also use the keyboard: the arrow keys move around the grid, and Enter or Space paints the
hour.

### Your time zone

Your hours are kept in **your own time zone**, as the hour on your clock. "Tuesday 18:00" stays
Tuesday 18:00 for you when your country changes its clocks. If you move, change your **Time zone**
and press **Save**; your hours do not shift, because they are what your clock says.

## Team

**Team** shows the week, Sunday first, with the days down and the hours across. Each hour is shaded by how many of
the people shown are free in it, in four steps from lightest to darkest, and carries that number.
The shades are relative to the number of people shown, so one free of two is darker than one free of
eight. An empty hour has nobody.

Point at an hour, or tap it, to see who is free in it. Somebody who is only free if needed has
**(if needed)** after their name.

### Time zone

The grid is shown in the zone you pick under **Time zone**, which starts as your browser's zone.
Everybody's hours are moved into it for the dates of the current week, so a change of clocks in your
zone or theirs shows on the days after it and not before. In the week the clocks go forward, the hour
that does not happen is empty; in the week they go back, the hour that happens twice counts once.

A zone that is not on a whole hour from yours, such as India, lands between hours. Each of their
hours is shown as the nearest hour of yours, half past rounding up, so an hour of theirs is always
one hour on your grid.

### Filters

All the filters work on what is already on the page, and change the heatmap and the best times
together.

| Filter | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Role** | **All roles**, or one role. |
| **People** | Press names to show only those people. None pressed means everyone. |
| **Day** | **Every day**, **Weekdays** (Monday to Friday) or **Weekend** (Sunday and Saturday). |
| **Hours** | **All hours**, **Daytime** (06:00 to 18:00), **Evening** (18:00 to midnight) or **Night** (midnight to 06:00). |
| **Free at once** | **Any**, or **At least 2**, **3** or **4**. Hours with fewer people free are left blank. |
| **If needed** | Whether **If needed** counts as free. On to begin with. |

### Best times

**Best times** lists the best stretch of each day: the longest run of hours in a row with the most
people free that day, such as **Tue 18:00-22:00 · 5 of 8 free** (**Tue 6 PM-10 PM** on a 12 hour **Clock**). The list is ordered with the most
people first. A day where nobody is free, or fewer than **Free at once** asks for, is left out.

## What is not here

- **Specific dates.** A week repeats. Somebody away next Thursday needs to say so another way.
- **Calendar sync.** Availability is not read from or written to Google Calendar or anywhere else, and
  the [Calendar](/moderation/calendar) does not use it to invite or schedule anyone.
- **A history.** Only the week as last saved is kept.

Deleting an account deletes the times it saved. Nothing here reaches VRChat or Discord.
