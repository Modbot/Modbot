Modbot keeps a copy of the public VRChat profile of everyone it comes across, so moderators can
see who somebody is without leaving Modbot, and so there is a record of what a profile said
before it changed. You see it at the top of the [person popup](/moderation/popups#person-popup).

Reading profiles needs **See profiles**.

## Who gets a profile

Anyone Modbot sees, including:

- everyone on the member list and the ban list;
- anyone who joins, leaves, is banned, kicked, warned, given a role or turned away, and the
  moderator who did it;
- anyone a moderator's companion sees in an instance.

## What is stored

The profile as VRChat returns it. The card shows:

- picture, display name and pronouns;
- status line and bio;
- **Joined VRChat** and **Last platform**;
- **Last seen by Modbot**: the latest time Modbot saw this person in anything it recorded;
- VRChat's tags (trust level and similar);
- the 18+ verified mark, and what VRChat shows about age verification.

Modbot keeps everything else VRChat returns too, **except** these, which are dropped before
anything is saved:

- where the person is right now and where they are travelling to, because that can contain the
  secret part of an instance address;
- the private note Modbot's own VRChat account may have on them;
- the friend key.

Profile pictures are shown from VRChat's servers. Modbot does not keep a copy of the image.

When a watched part of the profile changes (name, bio, status, pronouns, pictures, tags, join date
or age verification), Modbot writes an entry to the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log). The
person's **Logs** tab shows those entries, so you can see what a bio said last week.

## How profiles are refreshed

VRChat answers two different questions about a person, and they do not return the same things:

| | Public profile | User details |
|---|---|---|
| What it gives | Name, bio, pronouns, age verification | Status line, pictures, **Joined VRChat**, tags, **Last platform** |
| How often | On the queue below | Once when Modbot first meets someone, then once a week |

The public profile is the one Modbot reads often, because it carries what moderators look at. The
user details barely change, so reading them once a week is enough.

VRChat hands out profiles one person per request, so Modbot works through a queue. At most 3.5
requests a second go to VRChat for each of the two, and the queue is ordered like this:

1. People a companion has just seen in an instance.
2. People a moderator has just opened in Modbot.
3. People who just did something Modbot recorded (in the last 30 minutes).
4. The oldest profiles, once they are more than 6 hours old.
5. People Modbot has never fetched.

These are the defaults. Some other rules protect the queue:

- A profile fetched in the last 30 seconds is not fetched again just because somebody opened it.
- If a refresh failed, that person is left alone for 30 minutes.
- If VRChat says the account does not exist, Modbot waits a week before asking again.

### Every profile says how old it is

The top line of the card always says **Last refreshed** and how long ago. When the profile is
older than 6 hours it adds **stale — older than 6 hours**. A bio from an hour ago and a bio from
last week are different claims, and the card never shows one without saying which it is.

When you open a person, Modbot shows what it has straight away and asks for a fresh copy.
**refreshing…** appears while it waits, and the card updates when the new copy arrives. If the
refresh cannot happen, the card says **Couldn't refresh:** and the reason (for example, VRChat is
limiting Modbot's requests) and keeps showing what it has. After a minute of waiting it says
**Refresh still waiting.**

Other lines you may see:

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Never refreshed** | Modbot knows the id but has not fetched the profile yet. |
| **Profile not fetched yet.** | The same, in place of the profile. |
| **Not seen before.** | Modbot has no record of this id at all. |
| **No VRChat account with this id as of** a date | VRChat said the account does not exist. Modbot keeps the last profile it saw, and the person's history still mentions them. |

This line only appears when **both** of VRChat's answers say the account does not exist. If one of
them still knows the person, Modbot goes on showing them normally.

## The 18+ verified mark

VRChat lets people choose whether their age verification is visible. Somebody who showed as 18+
yesterday can hide it today, and to anyone who looks them up they then appear unverified.

Modbot treats verification as something it has **seen**, not something it checks again:

- **A refresh can set the mark and can never clear it.** The first time any refresh sees the person
  as 18+ verified, the mark is set and the card says **18+ verified**, **first seen on VRChat** and
  the date. Hiding the badge in VRChat later changes nothing.
- **The card shows both sides.** Under the mark, **VRChat shows this person as** gives what VRChat
  said at the last refresh (for example `18+` or `hidden`). When the two disagree, that is the
  point: hidden is not the same as unverified.
- **Only a moderator can clear it.** Anyone with **Edit 18+ verified** sees a **Clear flag** button
  on a marked person, and **Mark 18+ verified** on an unmarked one.

The **18+** mark on the Members page and in case files is this same mark.

### Changing the mark by hand

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the person

Click their name anywhere to open the [person popup](/moderation/popups#person-popup).

</Step>

<Step>

### Press Clear flag or Mark 18+ verified

The button is at the right of the 18+ block. You need **Edit 18+ verified**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Say why, and confirm

Type a reason (up to 500 characters) and press the button again. The reason is optional but worth
writing: it is kept with the change.

</Step>

</Steps>

The card then says **set by** or **cleared by** your username and the date. The change is written
to the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log) against your account, with the reason.

If you clear the mark and a later refresh sees the person as 18+ verified again, the mark is set
again. A new sighting is new evidence.

## Common problems

**The card keeps saying Couldn't refresh.** Modbot's VRChat account may be rate limited or not
signed in. Check the [Sync health](/self-hosting/health) page. The stored profile stays usable in
the meantime.

**A big group has many ids without names.** After Modbot is first set up, it has to fetch every
profile one at a time. At 3.5 a second, a group of 8,000 takes about 40 minutes, and larger groups
take hours.
