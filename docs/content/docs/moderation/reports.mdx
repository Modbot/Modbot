A member who sees something wrong in your Discord server can tell the mods without telling anyone
else. They run `/report`, or right-click a message and pick **Report to mods**. Their report lands
on the **Reports** page in Modbot, under **Community**, for the staff who handle it.

Both are off until you turn them on: **Settings → Discord → Commands**, **report** and **Report to
mods**. See [the bot's commands](/discord/bot-setup#slash-commands).

## What a member sees

- `/report member: what:` takes a member picked from Discord's list and what happened, 1 to 1,000
  characters.
- **Report to mods**, under **Apps** on a message, opens a form asking **What's wrong?**, 1 to 1,000
  characters. The report is about the message's author, and Modbot keeps a copy of the message.

Every reply is visible only to them. A report is accepted with "Sent to the mods." and is refused,
in plain words, when:

| Why | What they are told |
|---|---|
| It is about themselves | You can't report yourself. |
| It is about Modbot, or about one of Modbot's own messages | You can't report Modbot. |
| They already reported this message | You already reported this. |
| They already have an open report on this person | The mods already have your report. |
| They made 3 reports in the last 10 minutes, or 10 in the last day | Slow down. Try again in a few minutes. |

The limits are per person, counted from the reports Modbot has kept, so restarting Modbot does not
reset them. Nothing is limited for the server as a whole. The person reported is never told, and
nobody in the server can see that a report was made or by whom.

## The Reports page

**Open** and **Closed** tabs; the **Open** tab carries how many are waiting, and so does the
**Reports** entry in the sidebar. Each report shows:

- when it was made;
- who it is about, as a link to their profile;
- what the member wrote;
- the message they reported, with its channel, when it was sent, its words and the names of any
  files attached (Discord's links to the files expire, so only the names are kept);
- **Reported by**, with the member's Discord name and id;
- **Open in Discord**, which opens the message there;
- **Add note**, **Watch** and **Profile**, which open the person's popup, for the people who hold
  those permissions;
- **Close**, with a **Close note** that is required. The note is kept on the report, with who closed it
  and when.

Closing is recorded in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log) as **Member report closed**, naming the
person reported and the staff account that closed it. The note is not in the entry.

### Who sees who reported

**Everyone who can open the page sees who reported**: moderators need to ask follow-up questions and to
spot somebody reporting out of spite. Nobody else does:

- Not Discord: no reply, card or message names the reporter.
- Not a notification: **Modbot: new report** says "Somebody reported a member." with a link to the
  page, and no names. It goes by email, Discord direct message or push, as each person set up their own
  notifications, to people who hold **See reports**, and at most one every 15 minutes.
- Not the audit log: **Member report made** names the person reported and the report's id, and nothing
  the member wrote or quoted. Running `/report` or the menu is not recorded as **Discord command used**,
  because that entry would name the member. Who may see **Member report made** is under
  [Reports about staff](#reports-about-staff).

### Reports about staff

A report about somebody whose Discord account is connected to a Modbot staff account is shown only to
people who also hold **Review tickets**, the permission the people being reviewed are not meant to hold.
For everyone else it is not in the list, not in the count beside **Open**, and not in the sidebar's
number, and the notification for it goes only to people who hold both permissions. It follows the same
rule as [Reviews](/moderation/reviews).

The two entries it leaves, **Member report made** and **Member report closed**, follow the same rule.
When the person reported is a staff account, they are shown only to people who hold **Review tickets** as
well as **See the operational log**: not in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log), not in that person's
timeline, not on the live feed, in webhooks or on the live updates stream. Someone who holds the
operational log alone still sees these entries for everyone else, and every other operational entry.

## Permissions

| Permission | Allows |
|---|---|
| **See reports** | Open the page, read the reports, see who reported, and the count in the sidebar. |
| **Handle reports** | **Close** a report. Needs **See reports** as well. |

Both are in the built-in Moderator role. See [Roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions).

## What Modbot keeps

Each report is one row in Modbot's database:

- the reporter's Discord id and name when they reported;
- the reported person's Discord id and name, and the VRChat account linked to them then;
- what the reporter wrote;
- for the menu: the message's id, the channel's id and name, when it was sent, its words, the names of
  its files, and its link;
- open or closed, who closed it, when, and the close note.

**Closed reports keep the words for a year.** After the number of days in **Settings → Server → Keep
for (days) → Closed reports** (365 unless you change it; 0 keeps them forever) from the day a report
was closed, Modbot removes what the member wrote and the copy of the message. The record stays: who
reported, who it was about, when, who closed it, and the close note. The page then shows **Removed.**
where the words were. An open report is never removed.

A [purge](/moderation/purge) of a person deletes every report they made and every report made about
them. Nothing about a report is sent to Modbot Cloud or to an AI chat tool in this version.
