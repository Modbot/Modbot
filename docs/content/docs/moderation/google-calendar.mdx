---
title: Google Calendar
description: Connecting Modbot to a Google calendar your group owns, putting your events on it, and handing out the calendar's public links.
---

Modbot can put your group's events on a Google calendar that your group's owner owns. You give Modbot
a key for its own Google address, share the calendar with that address, and press **Check**: Modbot
signs in to Google and tells you whether it can see the calendar, whether it may change its events,
and whether the calendar is public. Then you turn **Sending** on, and Modbot keeps the calendar in
line with the events you plan. When the calendar is public, Modbot shows its own links, ready to copy.

Everything here is in **Settings**, **Google Calendar**, and needs
[**Change settings**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#change-settings).

## What you need

- A Google account that owns the calendar, or can share it. A personal Gmail account works.
- A Google Cloud project, with the Google Calendar API turned on. It costs nothing.
- A **service account** in that project, and a **JSON key** for it. The service account is the
  Google address Modbot signs in as. It is not a person, and it has no Gmail inbox.

### Google Workspace accounts

If the calendar belongs to a school or company Google account, its admin decides two things that can
stop this working:

- whether calendars may be shared with addresses outside the organisation;
- whether keys may be made for service accounts at all. Organisations made since May 2024 block
  this until an admin allows it.

If either is blocked, ask the admin, or use a calendar on a personal account.

## Set it up

### 1. Make the key in Google Cloud

1. Go to [console.cloud.google.com](https://console.cloud.google.com) and make a new project, or pick
   one you already have.
2. Open **APIs & Services**, **Library**, find **Google Calendar API** and click **Enable**.
3. Open **IAM & Admin**, **Service accounts**, and click **Create service account**. Give it a name
   such as `modbot`. It needs no roles in the project: skip those steps.
4. Open the new service account, go to **Keys**, **Add key**, **Create new key**, choose **JSON** and
   click **Create**. Your browser downloads a `.json` file.

That file is the key. Anyone who has it can change any calendar shared with that service account, so
keep it like a password, and delete your downloaded copy once Modbot has it.

### 2. Give the key to Modbot

In Modbot, go to **Settings**, **Google Calendar**, and click **Choose key file**. Pick the `.json`
file. Modbot keeps only the parts it needs: the service account's address, the key's id, the
project's id and the private key, which is encrypted and never shown again. The page then shows
**Key file: Saved** and **Modbot's Google address**, which ends in `iam.gserviceaccount.com`. Click
**Copy** beside it.

Modbot refuses a file that is not a service account key with **This is not a Google key file.**

### 3. Share the calendar with Modbot

In [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com) on a computer:

1. Make a new calendar for your group's events, or pick one. A calendar of its own is best, so
   Modbot never sees your personal events.
2. Hover over it under **My calendars**, click the three dots, and choose **Settings and sharing**.
3. Under **Share with specific people or groups**, click **Add people and groups**, paste **Modbot's
   Google address**, and set it to **Make changes to events**. Click **Send**.
4. Further down, under **Integrate calendar**, copy the **Calendar ID**. It looks like
   `c_…@group.calendar.google.com`, or your Gmail address for your main calendar.

### 4. Enter the calendar and Check

Back in Modbot, paste the **Calendar ID** and click **Save**. The calendar's public address, its
embed link or its iCal address work too: Modbot takes the ID out of them.

Then click **Check**.

## What Check shows

Check signs in to Google with the key and reads the calendar. It changes nothing on Google.

| Line | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Calendar** | The calendar's name on Google. |
| **Time zone** | The calendar's own time zone, set in its **Settings and sharing**. |
| **Can change events** | **Yes** when the calendar is shared with Modbot's Google address with **Make changes to events** or more. |
| **Public** | **Yes** when it is public with every detail, **Free or busy only** when the public sees only busy times, **No** when it is not public. **Unknown** when Google would not say. |

What Check found stays on the page until you change the key or the calendar. Change either and Check
again.

### When Check finds a problem

| Modbot says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Google did not accept the key.** | The key was deleted or disabled in Google Cloud, or the service account was. Make a new key and choose it again. |
| **The server's clock is off.** | Google refuses a sign-in whose times are more than a few minutes out. Set the clock on the machine Modbot runs on. |
| **Modbot can't see this calendar.** | The calendar is not shared with Modbot's Google address, or the Calendar ID is wrong. Check both. |
| **Modbot can only read this calendar.** | It is shared as **See all event details**. Change that to **Make changes to events**. |
| **Google is limiting Modbot. Try again after …** | Google answered that Modbot was asking too often. Modbot sends nothing to Google until that time, Check included, and **Check** comes back by itself. |
| **Google did not answer.** | Google could not be reached. Try again later. |

Anything else is Google's own words.

## Making it public, and the links

To let anyone see the calendar, open its **Settings and sharing** in Google Calendar and tick **Make
available to public**, with **See all event details**. Press **Check** again in Modbot: **Public**
says **Yes**, and a **Links** card appears with three of Google's own links, each with **Copy**:

| Link | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Subscribe** | Adds the calendar to someone's own Google Calendar. It works on a computer, not in Google's phone apps. |
| **Public page** | The calendar as a web page anyone can open, in the calendar's time zone. |
| **iCal address** | For any calendar app that subscribes by address: Outlook, Apple Calendar, event directories. It works only while the calendar is public. |

These links show what is on the Google calendar and nothing else. They are safe to hand to members,
put on a website or give to an event directory. Calendar apps that subscribe by address, Google's
own included, check it on their own schedule, so a change can take hours to show there.

A public calendar can be found by search engines once it is embedded on a website they read.

Modbot has a public address of its own too: the calendar's [public feed](/moderation/calendar#the-public-feed),
which lists the public events you plan in Modbot. The Google links list what is on the Google
calendar.

## Sending events

Once **Check** says **Can change events: Yes**, a **Sending** card appears. Turn **Sending** on, and
Modbot adds the events ticked for Google Calendar to the calendar, then keeps them in line: an edit
reaches Google about twenty seconds after the last change, so several quick fixes are one change
there.

| On Google | What Modbot does |
|---|---|
| **New events** | An event everyone may see is ticked for Google Calendar when you make it, and so is one read in from VRChat's calendar. |
| **Events planned before** | **Add all** ticks every scheduled or open event everyone may see. |
| **Events only members see** | Never sent, ticked or not. A public Google calendar would show them to anyone. |
| **A repeating event** | One Google event that repeats, as in Modbot. A date cancelled on its own is left out of it; a date moved or given its own title or description is changed on Google on its own. |
| **An edit** | Sent again whole. A change made on Google itself is replaced by Modbot's next change, so edit events in Modbot. |
| **A cancelled event** | Kept, with **Cancelled:** in front of its title, until a day after the date it was cancelled on; then removed. A repeating one stops at that date. |
| **A deleted event** | Removed from Google at once. |
| **A finished event** | Kept on Google as history. |
| **Turning Sending off** | Nothing more is sent. What is on Google stays as it is. |

What goes on each event: its title, its description, its times in its own time zone, its repeat, the
world's name as the place, and the world's page on vrchat.com. No picture, no join link, and nobody
is invited or emailed.

### Remove Modbot's events

**Remove Modbot's events** turns **Sending** off and deletes every event Modbot put on the calendar.
Modbot asks first: click it again to go ahead. It deletes a few each pass, so a full calendar takes a
minute or two. Events you made on the calendar yourself are left alone. Google keeps deleted events
for a while as cancelled entries you do not see.

### On each event

Each event on the calendar page says how it went on Google: **Google Calendar: Sending…**,
**Published**, **Failed** with Google's own words and **Try again**, or **Removed**. An event ticked
for Google Calendar while Google is not set up, or while **Sending** is off, says **Not set up**.

### When Google says no

| What happens | What Modbot does |
|---|---|
| Google is asking Modbot to slow down | Modbot sends nothing to Google until the time Google asked for, at least 15 minutes, and **Health** says **Google Calendar · limited until …**. Nothing is lost: the events wait and go once it is over. |
| The calendar is no longer shared with **Make changes to events** | The event says **Modbot can't change events on this calendar.**, and Modbot stops sending until you share it again and press **Check**. |
| The key was deleted in Google Cloud | Check shows **Google did not accept the key.**, and Modbot stops sending until you give it a new key and press **Check**. |
| Google did not answer | A minute later Modbot looks on the calendar for the event before adding it again, so it is not added twice. |

### Changing the calendar

Save a new **Calendar ID** and press **Check**: Modbot deletes its events from the old calendar and
adds them to the new one.

## Forget

**Forget** removes the key, the Calendar ID and what Check found from Modbot, and turns **Sending**
off. Modbot asks first: click **Forget** again to go ahead. It changes nothing on Google: the events
Modbot put there stay, unless you used **Remove Modbot's events** first, and the calendar stays shared with
Modbot's Google address until you remove that in **Settings and sharing**, and the key keeps working
until you delete it in Google Cloud. See [Removing the key](/security#the-google-calendar-key).

## What leaves your server

Check sends Google a request signed with the key, naming Modbot's Google address, and the Calendar
ID. While **Sending** is on, Modbot also sends the calendar each event ticked for it, as listed above.
Events only your members see, member names, ids, head counts, pictures and moderation data are never
sent. See [What leaves your server](/privacy#what-leaves-your-server).

## In the audit log

Saving the key or the Calendar ID, turning **Sending** on or off, **Add all**, **Remove Modbot's
events**, **Forget**, and a **Check** that found something different from the last one are each a
**Settings changed** entry in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log). The key is recorded only as
changed, never what it was. Each event added to or removed from Google, and each failure, is an entry
of its own on the event.
