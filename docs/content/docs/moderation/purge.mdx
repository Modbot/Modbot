---
title: Purge a person
description: Erase the history records Modbot holds about one person, what it keeps, and what it leaves behind.
---

**Settings → Purge a person** erases the history records Modbot holds about one VRChat or Discord
account. It exists for the day someone asks to be erased and the law where you live says they may.

**A purge does not remove everything Modbot stores about a person.** Read
[What stays](#what-stays) before you press it. It cannot be undone.

It needs **Administrator**. It has no permission of its own on purpose: a new permission would be a
way to hand the most consequential thing Modbot can do to somebody who holds nothing else.

## How it works

Three steps on one card, each shown only after the one before it has an answer.

1. **Account.** Choose **VRChat** or **Discord**, type the person's id in the box beside it, and
   press **Look up**. The id is used exactly as you type it.
2. **Read what would go.** Nothing has been changed yet. The card shows the name Modbot last saw,
   whether they are in the group or the server, whether they are banned, and two lists.
   **Removed** is a count for each thing a purge erases. **Kept** is the case files and evidence
   files a purge leaves. If a proved account link ties the person to another account, that account
   is named too. **A purge never follows the link:** to erase the other account, purge it as well.
3. **Type the id to confirm.** Type the id again, exactly as it is above, and **Purge** lights up.
   There is no "Are you sure?" and no box for a reason: the only thing you type is the id, and it
   is thrown away once it has been checked. Modbot does not keep a written reason, because a reason
   is the one place a moderator would type the person's name.

When it is done the card says **Purged.** and shows the same two lists as counts of what was
removed and what was kept. The counts of what was kept are shown once and are not saved. The counts
of what was removed are in the audit entry described below.

Purging somebody Modbot has never seen removes nothing and is not an error. Purging the same person
twice is harmless: the second finds nothing.

## What is erased

Everything is done in one go, or not at all.

- **Every history record that is about them.** Joins, leaves, avatar changes, instance
  presence, bans and unbans, kicks, roles, notes, the records of flags raised about them, and
  every other record in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log) whose subject is that person. Their
  names in those records go with them.
- **The counts kept only per person.** Some daily totals have no history record behind them, so they
  would survive a purge. Those are erased.
- **Days are worked out again,** so no total still counts the records that were erased.
- **Every [watch](/moderation/notes#watching-a-person) on them,** standing or ended, with its
  reason and any check-back day. Only watches on the account you purge go: a watch on the Discord or
  VRChat account it is linked to stays, because a purge does not follow a link.
- **The rows that say a record about them was imported** from a file. Without them, uploading the
  same file again would skip exactly the records that were erased.
- **Their Discord messages,** and every earlier version of them, for a Discord account. Messages
  that only mention them or reply to them are somebody else's words and stay.
- **Their entries in giveaways.** Standing entries are deleted, so they cannot be drawn. In a
  draw that has already been made, their row keeps its place and weight and loses its name and ids,
  so anyone who kept the seed can still check the result. See [Giveaways](/moderation/giveaways).
- **Their place on an event's invites,** whether they asked for event invites, and whether Modbot
  noted them as a friend of your group's VRChat account. A purged person gets no event invites until
  they ask again with `/me`. The event's **Invited N of M** counts one fewer. See
  [Inviting people](/moderation/calendar#inviting-people).

  That note is about the friends list of your group's own Modbot VRChat account, not anybody else's.
  If the person is still a friend of that account, VRChat lists them again the next time Modbot signs
  in, and Modbot notes them again. To stop that, remove them as a friend on that VRChat account.

- **The reminders they asked for** with `/remindme`, whether waiting, sent or stopped. For a VRChat
  account, that means the reminders of the Discord account linked to it. A purged person gets no more
  reminders until they ask again. See
  [What Modbot keeps for reminders](/discord/bot-setup#what-modbot-keeps-for-reminders).

## What stays

- **Records where they were the one acting.** A moderator's ban is a record about the person who
  was banned. Erasing it because the moderator asked would delete somebody else's moderation
  history.
- **Case files about them, and the evidence on those case files.** These are kept, with the profile
  copy each case file holds. The card counts them so you can see what is left. A purge does not
  destroy [evidence](/moderation/evidence); somebody with **Destroy evidence** does that, one file
  at a time, from the case file. The audit log's entries about evidence (who attached, viewed,
  downloaded or destroyed a file) are about the case file, so they stay too. A file attached as a
  [companion clip](/companion/clips) keeps, with it, the VRChat id and Modbot username of the
  moderator whose PC saved it, even when that moderator is purged.
- **The rows that say who someone is now:** their VRChat profile, their place in the group, their
  entry on the ban list, their Discord member row and their account link. A purge of somebody who is
  still in the group or the server does not remove them from it, and the next sync writes those
  rows again.
- **Flags** themselves, which still name the person and carry the words that matched, **reviews**,
  entries in the [AI call log](/ai/call-log), and sign-in records.
- **The repeat-offender row,** with how many times they were kicked, banned, removed or rejected,
  the dates and the last moderator's id. A purge does not delete it. The daily review job rebuilds
  these rows from the history records, so it goes the next time the job refreshes it or is rebuilt in
  full, not at the moment of the purge.
- **Modbot's own log** and its log files. See [Logs](/self-hosting/logs).
- **Copies elsewhere:** anything already sent to Modbot Cloud, to an AI provider, to a Discord
  channel by an event route, to a webhook or to Seq, and any backup of your database. A purge only
  reaches this Modbot's database.

## The record it leaves

A purge writes one entry to the audit log, **User data purged**: which account did it, when, which
platform, and how many of each thing were erased. It does not say who the person was. That is why
there is no id in it and no reason. It is an operational entry, so it shows to people who hold
**See the operational log**.

## Where it is on the API

`GET /api/settings/purge` is the look-up and `POST /api/settings/purge` is the purge, both for
Administrators. The `POST` needs `confirmation` to equal `subjectId` exactly; nothing is trimmed and
case counts. See the [API reference](/api/reference).
