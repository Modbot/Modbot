When somebody asks the team to lift their ban, you can write the appeal down in Modbot so the
answer is not lost in a chat. The **Appeals** page, under **Community**, lists them and has the
**Yearly report** beside it. Nothing here is public: members cannot see it, and nothing is posted
anywhere.

## Who can use it

| To | You need |
|---|---|
| Read the list and the report, and download the CSV | **See profiles** |
| Log an appeal, and uphold or turn one down | **Unban** |

Somebody with only **See profiles** sees the buttons gone, not greyed out.

## Logging an appeal

**Log appeal** on the page, or on the **Cases** tab of the person's popup, asks for:

- **Person**: their VRChat id. In the popup it is already filled in.
- **Received**: the day it reached the team. Left empty, it is today. It can't be a day to come.
- **Ban**: one of that person's case files, to link the appeal to the ban. Optional. It has to be
  about the same person.
- **Note**: a line or two, up to 1,000 characters. Optional.

Each appeal starts as **Waiting**.

## Upholding and turning down

**Uphold** and **Turn down** record the answer, who gave it and when. An answer is final: a second
click, or somebody else's click a moment later, is refused.

**Upholding an appeal does not unban anybody.** Nothing is changed on VRChat or Discord. Lift the
ban the usual way, from [Bans and case files](/moderation/members-and-bans).

The list can be narrowed by year and by outcome (**Waiting**, **Upheld**, **Turned down**). A ban
that was linked shows as a link to its case file.

Logging and answering are each an entry in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log): **Appeal logged**
and **Appeal decided**. A [Discord channel](/discord/event-channels) that takes moderation entries
is told that it happened and who did it, never the note.

## The yearly report

**Yearly report** shows one calendar year at a time, newest first, and **Download CSV** saves the
same numbers as a file with one row per number.

| Number | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Warnings, Instance kicks, Removed from group, Bans, Unbans | The daily totals the Team page adds up, so the two always agree |
| Appeals received | Appeals whose **Received** day is in the year |
| Appeals upheld, Appeals turned down | Appeals answered in the year |

So an appeal received in December and answered in January counts as received in one year and as
answered in the next, once each.

Years run from 1 January to 31 December in **UTC**, the same days every analytics page counts.
**Counted since** is the first day Modbot has anything counted for these numbers. A year that starts
before that day is only partly counted.

## Erasing a person

[Purging a person](/moderation/purge) deletes their appeals too. The yearly report then counts one
fewer, which is what erasing them means.
