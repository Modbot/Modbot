title: Calendar
description: Plan your group's events once in Modbot, and let it put them on VRChat's group calendar, in Discord and in a calendar feed, and open the instance on time.
---

The **Calendar** page is where you plan your group's events. You fill in an event once, and
Modbot does the rest:

- puts it on your group's **VRChat calendar**;
- makes a **Discord event** in your server;
- posts it in a **Discord channel**, with a **Join** button once the instance is open;
- lists it in a **calendar feed**, a link people can add to Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple
  Calendar;
- **opens the group instance** a few minutes before the start, if you ask it to.

Each of these is a chip at the top of the event form: click it to turn that place on or off. Leave
one off and Modbot does not touch that place. The calendar feed is always on.

Events made on VRChat itself, on vrchat.com or in the game, show up on the page too, and you can
edit them from Modbot. See [Events made on VRChat](#events-made-on-vrchat).

## Who can use it

| To do this | You need |
|---|---|
| See the **Calendar** page, every event, where each one is published and any errors | [**See calendar**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-calendar) |
| Plan, edit, cancel and delete events | [**Manage calendar**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#manage-calendar) and **See calendar** |
| See, copy or replace the calendar feed link | **Manage calendar** and **See calendar** |
| See [what each event did](#what-an-event-did) and **Past events** | **See calendar** and [**See analytics**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-analytics) |
| See who a moderator's client saw at an event | Those two and [**See the audit log**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-the-audit-log) |
| See calendar problems on **Health** | [**See the operational log**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-the-operational-log) |

Without **See calendar**, **Calendar** does not appear in the sidebar. **Manage calendar** on its own
is not enough to open the page. Without **Manage calendar**, the page is read-only: there is no
**New event** button, no feed link, an event shows no **Edit**, **Duplicate**, **Cancel event** or
**Delete** buttons, and nothing can be dragged or drawn on the calendar.

Neither permission is in the built-in **Moderator** or **Viewer** roles. **Administrator** holds
both. Give them on purpose: an event can open an instance by itself, and it posts to VRChat and
Discord in your group's name.

## The page

The page works like Google Calendar. At the top are **Today**, the arrows for back and forward, the
dates on screen (for example **September 2026** or **Sep 21 – 27, 2026**), and a switch between four
views:

- **Day** and **Week** show the hours down the side, in your own clock, and one column a day, Monday
  first. The page opens scrolled to an hour before the first event on screen, or to 8 AM when there
  is none. A red line marks the time it is now. Events that overlap sit side by side. An event that
  lasts a day or longer is shown in a row across the top instead. Click a date at the top of a
  column to see that day on its own.
- **Month** shows six weeks. Each day lists the events that start on it, with the start time, up to
  three; **+2 more** (or however many) opens that day. Click a date to see that day.
- **Schedule** is a list from the day on screen to about two months ahead. Each row shows the time,
  the title, the event's status, the world, and a badge for each place it is published, such as
  **VRChat calendar: Published** or **Discord event: Failed**.

**Week** is the view the page opens on. On a phone it opens on **Day**.

**Refresh**, beside the views, reads your group's VRChat calendar again straight away. See
[Events made on VRChat](#events-made-on-vrchat).

On a wide screen a small month sits beside the calendar. Click a date in it to jump there; its own
arrows page through months without moving the calendar. It is hidden on phones.

A repeating event appears once for every time it happens.

Times on the page are in your browser's time zone. Each event also has its own time zone, which you
set in the form (see [When](#when)).

Below the calendar, a **Drafts** box lists events saved as drafts, when there are any. Under it,
**Past events** lists every time an event ran in the last 90 days, the busiest first. See
[Past events](#past-events).

The page updates by itself when an event changes, so a place changing from **Waiting** to
**Published** shows up without reloading.

### Colours

An event's colour says where it stands:

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| The accent colour | **Scheduled**, and nothing has failed. |
| Green | **Open** right now. |
| Red edge | **Scheduled** or **Open**, and at least one place it is published has **Failed**. Open the event to see the error. |
| Dashed outline | A **Draft**. |
| Faded | **Finished**. |

### Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>D</kbd>, <kbd>W</kbd>, <kbd>M</kbd>, <kbd>A</kbd> | **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, **Schedule** |
| <kbd>T</kbd> | **Today** |
| <kbd>J</kbd> or <kbd>→</kbd> | Forward a day, a week or a month |
| <kbd>K</kbd> or <kbd>←</kbd> | Back a day, a week or a month |
| <kbd>C</kbd> | **New event** (with **Manage calendar**) |

They are also listed under **Calendar** when you press <kbd>?</kbd>.

### An event's status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Saved, but published nowhere and never opened. |
| **Scheduled** | The next time it happens has not started yet. |
| **Open** | It has started, or its instance has opened early, and it has not ended. |
| **Finished** | It does not happen again. |
| **Cancelled** | Someone cancelled it. |

A repeating event goes from **Scheduled** to **Open** and back to **Scheduled** each time, and only
becomes **Finished** after the last one.

### Opening an event

Click an event in any view. It opens in a small box beside it, showing:

- when it starts and ends (the time you clicked, for a repeating event), and how it repeats;
- **Moved from …** when that one date was [moved on its own](#changing-one-date-of-a-repeating-event);
- the description (that date's own, if it was given one);
- **World**, which opens the [world popup](/moderation/popups#world-popup). For an event that picks
  from a [world list](/moderation/world-lists), the world picked for its next date, and under it the
  **World list**, with **Pick again** while that date is scheduled; other dates show the list's name;
- **Next game**, while an event that picks from a list is open. See
  [Next game](/moderation/world-lists#next-game);
- **Instance**, once Modbot has tried to open one: **Opening** while it waits for VRChat, **Open**
  or **Closed** (click it for the [instance popup](/moderation/popups#instance-popup)), and a
  **Join** link while it is open. If VRChat refused, VRChat's error is shown here instead, in red;
- one line per place it is published, with its state and, if it failed, VRChat's or Discord's error;
- **How it went**, once the time you clicked has started. See [What an event did](#what-an-event-did).

With **Manage calendar** you also get **Edit**, **Duplicate**, **Cancel event** and **Delete**.
**Duplicate** opens the form for a new event filled in with this one's details; nothing is saved
until you save it.

A link to one event (for example from a Chat answer) opens it in the middle of the page instead.

### What an event did

Open a time an event has already run, or one that is running now, and **How it went** shows what it
did. You need **See analytics** as well as **See calendar** for it.

| Figure | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Most at once** | The most people in the event's instance at one moment, over the instance's whole life. The same number as **Most at once** in the [instance popup](/moderation/popups#instance-popup). |
| **Ran for** | How long the instance was open. **Open for** while it still is. |
| **New members** | People who joined your group from when the event opened (its instance's **Minutes early** included) until a day after it ended. |
| **Join requests** | Requests to join your group over the same stretch. |

New members and join requests count until a day after the end because people often join a group the
day after an event they liked. Two times of a daily event share some of that day, and both count it.

**Which instance.** The one Modbot opened for that time, when it opened one. Otherwise, your
**Managed group**'s instance in the event's world that was open for most of the event, which covers
an event someone opened by hand in VRChat. An event with no world,
and no instance opened by Modbot, has no instance: **Most at once** and **Ran for** show **—**.
When the instance is not the one the **Instance** line above already shows (that line is about the
time Modbot is dealing with now), it is named under **How it went**; click it for the instance popup.

For a repeating event, a **Usually** column shows the middle value of its last six times before this
one. Times with no instance leave out **Most at once** and **Ran for**.

**Seen by a moderator's client** shows how many people a moderator's
[desktop client](/companion/install) saw in the instance, their total time there (**People-time**), and the
people seen longest, each with their time. **Everybody** opens the instance popup's **People** tab when
there are more. This needs **See the audit log** too, and counts only while a moderator's client was
in the instance: **Nobody seen.** means no client saw anyone, not that nobody came. For **Usually**,
times nobody was seen are left out.

The numbers are not posted anywhere. They show only on this page.

### Past events

**Past events**, under the calendar, lists every time an event ran in the last 90 days: the event,
when, **Most at once**, **Ran for**, **New members** and **Join requests**. The busiest come first, and
times with no instance come last. It shows at most 50, and only when there is at least one. Click a
row to open that time of the event with **How it went**. On a phone each row shows the event, when it
started and its new members, with the most at once on the right.

Drafts and deleted events are never listed. A cancelled event's times before the cancel are. A date
cancelled on its own is not listed, and a date moved on its own is listed at the time it ran.

A time counts as one the event ran when the event's dates say so, or when Modbot opened an instance
for it. If you move a repeating event, its earlier times are worked out from the new dates, except
those Modbot opened an instance for, which stay at the time they ran.

## Moving events by dragging

With **Manage calendar** you can change the calendar by dragging, the way you would in Google
Calendar. Without it, events open when clicked, but nothing can be dragged and no new event can be
drawn.

- **Move an event:** drag it to another time, or to another day in **Week**. In **Month**, drag it to
  another day; it keeps its time of day. An event in the row across the top of **Week** can be dragged
  to another day the same way.
- **Change when it ends:** drag its bottom edge.
- **Draw a new event:** press on empty time and drag down. A single click on empty time draws one
  hour. In **Month**, click empty space in a day. A small form opens with the time filled in: type a
  title and click **Save draft**, or click **More options** for the full form. The small form only
  saves drafts, so nothing is published until you open the draft and click **Schedule**.

Everything snaps to 15 minutes. On a phone, hold your finger on an event (or on empty time) for a
moment until it picks up, then drag. A finger that moves straight away scrolls the page instead.

A drag is saved once, when you let go. It is the same as opening the event, changing its times in the
form and clicking **Save**: the change reaches VRChat's calendar, the Discord event and the channel
post the same way, and the audit log records it, for example *moved the event “Movie night” from
Sat 26 Sep, 08:00 PM–10:00 PM to Sun 27 Sep, 09:00 PM–11:00 PM*.

After a drag, **Event moved · Undo** shows at the bottom of the screen for a few seconds. **Undo**
puts the event back where it was, through the same save. Because Modbot waits 20 seconds without a
change before writing to VRChat's calendar, an undo straight away usually means VRChat is never
sent the move at all. If the save fails, the bottom of the screen says why instead, for example
**That event has already ended.**

### Repeating events

When you drag one date of a repeating event, Modbot asks **Move “…”?** and shows the date you dragged
and where it is going:

- **This date** moves only that date. The other dates stay where they are. After it saves, **Date
  moved · Undo** shows at the bottom of the screen. See
  [Changing one date of a repeating event](#changing-one-date-of-a-repeating-event).
- **All dates** moves every date by the same amount, the same as editing the whole event. The days of
  the week and the **Last date** move with it: a Monday and Wednesday event dragged one day later
  becomes Tuesday and Thursday.

Close the box to leave the event where it was.

## Planning an event

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the form

Click **New event** (or press <kbd>C</kbd>). The form starts with the next whole hour, two hours long,
in your browser's time zone. You can also draw the event on the calendar and click **More options**
(see [Moving events by dragging](#moving-events-by-dragging)); the form then starts with the time you
drew.
</Step>
<Step>
### Fill it in

Pick [where it goes](#where-it-goes) with the chips at the top, then fill in the fields under
**Details**. Every field is described below. Only **Title** is required, plus a world if the
instance should open by itself, and a channel if **Discord channel post** is on. **Preview** shows
the event the way each place will show it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Save it

- **Schedule** saves it and starts publishing it everywhere that is on.
- **Save draft** saves it without publishing or opening anything. You can come back to it from
  **Drafts** and click **Schedule** later.
- **Cancel** leaves without saving.

The buttons stay at the bottom of the form while you scroll, on a phone too. When you edit an event
that is already scheduled, **Save** takes the place of **Schedule** and **Save draft**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Modbot does not publish while you wait on the form. It saves the event, then publishes it in the
background within a minute or so. Watch the place badges change on the event; they change without
reloading the page.

### Where it goes

The chips at the top of the form say where the event goes. A chip that is on has a tick.

| Chip | What it does | Its own settings |
|---|---|---|
| **VRChat calendar** | Puts the event on your group's [VRChat calendar](#vrchats-group-calendar). On for a new event. | [VRChat calendar](#vrchat-calendar) |
| **Discord event** | Makes a [Discord event](#the-discord-event) in your server. On for a new event. | None |
| **Discord channel post** | Posts a [card](#the-channel-post) in a channel. | [Discord channel post](#discord-channel-post) |
| **Calendar feed** | Lists the event in the [calendar feed](#the-calendar-feed). Always on; it cannot be turned off. | None |
| **Open the instance** | [Opens a group instance](#opening-the-instance) before each start. | [Open the instance](#open-the-instance) |

A place's own settings only show while its chip is on.

**Not set up.** A chip that is on but cannot work yet says **Not set up** beside it. Click it to open
the setting that is missing, in a new tab, so what you typed stays:

| Chip | Needs |
|---|---|
| **VRChat calendar**, **Open the instance** | A **Managed group** and a VRChat account, in **Settings**, **Modbot's VRChat login**. |
| **Discord event**, **Discord channel post** | A **Server id** and the bot connected, in **Settings**, **Discord**. While the bot is connecting or reconnecting, these say **Not set up** too, because nothing is sent until it is back. |

**Discord channel post** also says **Not set up** until you pick a channel in its section.

You can still save an event with a place that is not set up. Nothing is sent there until it is set
up; then Modbot catches up by itself. The event shows the same **Not set up** where that place would
be, and so does [Health](#on-health).

### Preview

**Preview**, next to **Details**, shows the event the way each place that is on will show it:

- **Discord event**: the cover, the time, the title, the location and the description, cut to
  Discord's 100 and 1,000 characters. The location is the world's name (or **VRChat** with no world);
  the join address only appears while an instance Modbot opened is open.
- **Discord channel post**: the card as it will be posted, with the times in your own time.
- **VRChat calendar**: exactly what is sent to VRChat: the title, time, repeat, category, who can see
  it, languages, platforms, tags, picture id, whether members are notified, and the description.
  VRChat may change some characters after it gets them.
- **Calendar feed**: what a phone or desktop calendar shows: the title, time, repeat, location,
  the calendar's name and the notes.

The preview is made by the same code that sends the event, so it matches what goes out. It does not
save anything or send anything to VRChat or Discord. It can draw an event that is not finished yet,
with no title or no channel, but shows the error instead if something would be refused, such as a
title that is too long.

### Title and description

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Required. Up to 100 characters. The count beside it, such as **12 / 100**, turns red when it is too long. |
| **Description** | Optional, except for the VRChat calendar. Up to 1,000 characters, counted the same way. Line breaks are kept. |

### When

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Starts** | The date and time of the first time it happens. |
| **Ends** | Must be after the start. An event can last at most 7 days. |
| **Time zone** | The time zone **Starts** and **Ends** are in. |
| **Repeat** | **Does not repeat**, **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**. |
| Days of the week | Only for **Weekly**: tick **Mon** to **Sun**. The day of the first start is always included, even if you leave it unticked. |
| **Last date** | Only for repeating events. The last day it may start on. Leave it empty to repeat with no end. It cannot be before the start. |

The time zone matters for repeating events. A weekly event at 20:00 in `Europe/London` stays at
20:00 London time in both summer and winter, even though that is a different time for someone in
another country.

A **Monthly** event happens on the same day of the month as its first start. An event on the 31st
skips months that have no 31st.

### Where

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **World** | Pick from the worlds Modbot has seen recently, or choose **World id** and type the world's id. **None** is allowed, unless the instance opens by itself. **Pick from a list** takes the world from a [world list](/moderation/world-lists) instead, a new one for each date. |
| **World list** | Shown for **Pick from a list**. The list to pick from. See [When the world is picked](/moderation/world-lists#when-the-world-is-picked). |
| **Who can join** | **Group members**, **Members and their friends** or **Anyone**. Used for the instance Modbot opens, and shown on the channel post. |
| **Region** | **US West**, **US East**, **Europe** or **Japan**. Used for the instance Modbot opens. |
| **Picture link** | Optional. Must start with `https://` and point at a public website. Used as the Discord event's cover and the channel post's picture. When empty, Discord gets the world's picture instead. |

### VRChat calendar

Shown while the **VRChat calendar** chip is on.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Category** | One of VRChat's categories: **Arts**, **Avatars**, **Dance**, **Education**, **Exploration**, **Film & media**, **Gaming**, **Hangout** (the default), **Music**, **Other**, **Performance**, **Roleplaying** or **Wellness**. |
| **Visible to** | **Group** or **Everyone**. |
| **Languages** | Separate with commas, for example `eng, jpn`. At most 20, each up to 64 characters. |
| **Tags** | Separate with commas. At most 20, each up to 64 characters. |
| **VRChat image id** | Optional. The id of a picture already uploaded to VRChat, starting with `file_`. Modbot does not upload pictures to VRChat for you. |
| **PC**, **Android**, **iOS** | The platforms the event is for. |
| **Notify group members** | Asks VRChat to tell your group members about the event. VRChat is only asked this when the event is first put on its calendar, not when you edit it later. |

### Discord channel post

Shown while the **Discord channel post** chip is on.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Channel** | Required. A channel where the bot is missing View Channel, Send Messages or Embed Links is marked **Missing** followed by what it lacks. |

The **Discord event** has no settings of its own.

### Open the instance

Shown while the **Open the instance** chip is on. It needs a world, or a world list.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Minutes early** | How long before the start the instance opens: 0 to 120. The default is 10. |

When the instance opens early, the event counts as **Open** from that moment, not from the start
time.

### When saving fails

The form shows what is wrong, for example:

- **An event needs a title.**
- **The end must be after the start.**
- **An event can last at most 7 days.**
- **The last date is before the start.**
- **The picture link must start with https://.** or **The picture link points at a private address.**
- **Pick a channel to post to.**
- **Pick a world to open the instance in.**
- **That world list has no worlds.** Add worlds to it on the [World lists](/moderation/world-lists)
  page first, or save the event as a draft.
- **That event has already ended.** You tried to schedule an event whose last time is already over.

## VRChat's group calendar

When the **VRChat calendar** chip is on, Modbot adds the event to the calendar of the group
chosen as **Managed group** (**Settings**, **Modbot's VRChat login**). With no managed group chosen,
nothing is sent.

- **A repeating event is one entry on VRChat**, using VRChat's own repeat, in the event's time zone,
  with the last date when there is one. It is not one entry per week.
- **One date cancelled or changed on its own** is sent to that one date on VRChat. Modbot reads that
  month of your calendar once to find VRChat's id for the date, then removes it (a cancel) or changes
  its time, title and description. If VRChat does not list that date on its own, the date shows
  **VRChat calendar: Failed** when you open it, and the rest of the event is left alone. See
  [Changing one date of a repeating event](#changing-one-date-of-a-repeating-event).
- **Edits are sent as one.** Modbot waits until an event has gone 20 seconds without a change, then
  sends what it says at that moment. Fixing a typo three times in a row costs one change on VRChat.
- **Modbot sends one VRChat calendar change a minute at most**, across all events. VRChat is strict
  about its calendar, so this is kept slow on purpose. If you plan several events at once, they reach
  VRChat one after another.
- **Cancelling or deleting** the event, or turning off **VRChat calendar**, removes it from
  VRChat's calendar.
- **A finished event stays** on VRChat's calendar as a past event.
- **Changes made on VRChat are read back.** See [Events made on VRChat](#events-made-on-vrchat).

### Events made on VRChat

Modbot reads your group's VRChat calendar, so events someone made on vrchat.com or in the game show
up on the Calendar page, in the **Upcoming event** box on the VRChat page's **Overview**, and in the
calendar feed.

**When Modbot reads it.** Only when someone is looking:

- when the **Calendar** page opens, and when you move to dates it has not read yet: one request for
  each month on screen (a **Month** view can touch three);
- when the VRChat page's **Overview** opens: one request for this month, and one for next month when
  nothing on VRChat is left this month;
- when you click **Refresh** on the Calendar page.

A month read in the last five minutes is not asked for again, by anyone, unless you click
**Refresh**. Nothing is read on a timer, so the calendar feed only learns about an event made on
VRChat after someone has opened one of these pages. A repeating event costs one more request the
first time Modbot sees it, and again after it is changed on VRChat, to read how it repeats.

**How they show.** An event made on VRChat has a **VRChat** badge next to its status. It is an
ordinary Modbot event: with **Manage calendar** you can open, edit, drag, cancel and delete it, and
your change is sent to that same event on VRChat, never as a second copy. It arrives with
**VRChat calendar** and **Discord event** on, and **Discord channel post** and **Open the
instance** off; turn them on if you want them. VRChat settings the Modbot form does not
have, such as **Featured** or how early hosts may join, are kept as they are when you edit it.

**Changed on VRChat.** The newest change wins. Modbot copies in a change made on VRChat, for any
event, including ones planned in Modbot. If you changed the event in Modbot more recently and Modbot
has not sent it yet, your change wins and is sent to VRChat.

**Deleted on VRChat.** When an event is missing from a month Modbot read, Modbot asks VRChat about
that one event. If VRChat says it is gone, Modbot deletes it too, which also ends its Discord event
and post. A failed or partial read never deletes anything, and an event that was only moved to
another month is updated, not deleted. One exception: if the event was edited in Modbot and that edit
had not reached VRChat yet, the edit is newer, so Modbot puts the event back on VRChat.

**What Modbot cannot hold.** Modbot keeps one rule per repeating event, so:

- a change on VRChat to **one date only** of a repeating event is left on VRChat, and Modbot keeps
  the series as it was;
- an event repeating **every second week** (or every third, and so on) or **every year** is not
  brought in.

If the read fails, a red line under the page's controls says why, for example
**VRChat's calendar is waiting on its rate limit.** Modbot's own events still show.

### When VRChat says no

The **VRChat calendar** place on the event shows one of:

| State | What is happening |
|---|---|
| **Waiting** | Queued: waiting for its turn, for your edits to settle, or for a rate limit to pass. |
| **Published** | VRChat has the latest version. |
| **Failed** | VRChat refused, or did not answer. VRChat's own error is shown beside it. |
| **Removed** | Taken off VRChat after a cancel, a delete, or turning it off. |

What Modbot does next depends on why it failed:

- **VRChat said "too many requests".** Modbot never retries straight away. It pauses all VRChat
  calendar changes for a while and the place shows **Waiting**, not **Failed**. It carries on by
  itself. You can see the pause under **VRChat request limits** on [Health](/self-hosting/health#vrchat-request-limits).
- **VRChat refused the event** (for example, a field it does not accept). Modbot does not send the
  same thing again. Fix the event in Modbot, and the changed version is sent. Saving it with nothing
  changed does not send it again.
- **VRChat did not answer, or had a problem on its side.** Modbot tries again after 15 minutes.

## Discord

Discord needs the bot to be set up and connected. See [Setting up the bot](/discord/bot-setup).
While the bot is not connected, nothing is sent to Discord, and it catches up once it is.

Modbot checks every 20 seconds and makes at most 5 Discord changes each time, so with many events it
can take a little longer.

### The Discord event

When the **Discord event** chip is on, Modbot makes an event in the server set as **Server id**
(**Settings**, **Discord**, **Bot**). The bot needs the **Manage Events** permission in that server.

- It has the title, description, start and end, and the picture link (or the world's picture) as its
  cover. If the picture cannot be fetched, the event is made without a cover.
- **Before the instance opens**, its location is the world's name.
- **When the event opens**, Modbot starts the Discord event. If Modbot opened the instance for it,
  the join link goes in it until the instance closes. If it did not (**Open the instance**
  is off, or VRChat refused), the location stays the world's name. Discord only
  allows 100 characters for a location, and VRChat's join links are longer. So, while there is a join
  link:
  - if your **Public address** is set (**Settings**, **Server**), the location is a short
    Modbot address that sends people on to the instance;
  - otherwise the location stays the world's name, and the join link goes at the top of the
    description.
- **When it finishes**, Modbot ends the Discord event. **When you cancel or delete it**, or turn off
  **Discord event**, the Discord event is ended too, and disappears from your server.
- **A repeating event gets a new Discord event each time it happens.** Discord cannot move an event
  back from started to scheduled, so after each time, the old one is ended and a new one is made for
  the next time.
- **One date cancelled on its own** ends that date's Discord event, and the next date gets its own.
  **One date moved, or given its own title or description**, has its Discord event changed to match.
- If you schedule an event after its start time, Discord refuses a start in the past, so the Discord
  event starts one minute from when Modbot makes it.
- If someone deletes or ends the Discord event in Discord, Modbot makes it again the next time the
  event needs a change there (an edit, or the instance opening).

The short address works without signing in. It only sends people on while the instance Modbot opened
is still open; at any other time it answers "not found".

### The channel post

When the **Discord channel post** chip is on, Modbot posts a card in the channel you picked, in the same style
as [instance announcements](/discord/instance-announcements).

- The card shows your group's name, the title, the description, **When** and **Ends** (as Discord
  times, so everyone sees their own time zone), **World**, **Who can join**, **Region**, and the
  picture link or the world's picture.
- **World** opens the world's page on vrchat.com, which anyone can open. (Modbot's own world page
  needs a Modbot sign-in, which your members do not have.)
- The line at the bottom says **Scheduled**, then **Open now** with a **Join** button once the
  instance is open.
- The card is edited whenever the event changes.
- When it finishes, the card says **Finished**. When you cancel or delete the event, it says
  **Cancelled**. The **Join** button is removed either way.
- **A repeating event gets a new post each time it happens.** The old post keeps its last word
  (**Finished**, or **Cancelled** for a date cancelled on its own) and a new one is posted for the
  next time.
- Turning off **Discord channel post**, or picking a different channel, deletes the post.
- If someone deletes the post in Discord, Modbot does not post it again until the event changes.

The bot needs View Channel, Send Messages and Embed Links in the channel.

### When Discord says no

The **Discord event** and **Channel post** places use the same states as VRChat: **Waiting**,
**Published**, **Failed** and **Removed**, with Discord's error beside a failure. Common errors:

| Error | Fix |
|---|---|
| **The bot may not manage server events; it needs Manage Events.** | Give the bot's role **Manage Events** in your Discord server. |
| **The bot is not in that server.** | Check **Server id** in **Settings**, **Discord**, and that the bot has been invited. |
| **Discord is rate limiting the bot.** | Nothing. Modbot tries again on its next check. |

A refusal that will not fix itself, such as a missing permission, is not sent again until the event
changes. After fixing the permission, change something Discord shows, such as the description, and
save. Saving with nothing changed does not send it again. Other failures are tried again on the next
check.

## Opening the instance

When the **Open the instance** chip is on, Modbot opens a group instance **Minutes early**
before each start:

- in the event's world, with its **Who can join** and **Region**;
- owned by your **Managed group**.

A date cancelled on its own gets no instance. A date moved on its own opens **Minutes early** before
its new start.

Modbot opens **one instance each time the event happens**, and never more. It notes the attempt
before asking VRChat, so a restart cannot open a second one. If Modbot was down at the opening time,
it still opens the instance when it comes back, as long as that time has not ended yet.

Once the instance is open, its join link goes onto the Discord event and the channel post, and the
event shows it under **Instance**. When the instance closes, the link is taken off.

**If VRChat refuses, Modbot does not try again** for that time. The error shows on the event under
**Instance** and on [Health](#on-health). The next time the event happens gets its own
attempt. If you need the instance now, open it yourself in VRChat.

Modbot opens at most one instance every 5 seconds, which is well within what an event needs.

## The calendar feed

The calendar feed is a link people add to their own calendar app (Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple
Calendar and others) to see your group's events there. It is only on the page for people with
**Manage calendar**, in the **Calendar feed** row below the calendar.

- **Make link** creates the link the first time.
- **Copy** copies it.
- **New link** replaces it. **The old link stops working at once**, for everyone who added it. Use
  this if the link got shared somewhere it should not be. Modbot asks **Make a new feed link?**
  first; click **Make new link** to go ahead.

### The address

The link looks like this:

```text
https://modbot.example.com/api/calendar/feed/<secret>.ics
```

It starts with your **Public address** (**Settings**, **Server**). If that is not set, the
page uses the address you have open in your browser instead, which only works for others if they
can reach that address too.

### Who can see it

**Anyone who has the link.** Opening it needs no Modbot account and no sign-in: the secret part of
the address is the key. Share it where you would share your event list, and make a **New link** if
it leaks.

### What it contains

The feed lists:

- every event that is **Scheduled** or **Open**;
- every **Finished** event for **30 days** after its last time ended, so it stays in people's
  calendars as something that happened;
- every **Cancelled** event for **30 days** after it was cancelled, marked as cancelled, so calendar
  apps can show it as cancelled instead of it quietly disappearing.

For each one:

- the title and description;
- the start and end, in the event's time zone;
- the repeat and last date, so calendar apps show every time it happens;
- dates cancelled on their own left out, and dates moved or reworded on their own at their new time
  and with their own title and description;
- the world's name as the location. A repeating event is one entry in the feed, so for one that picks
  from a world list, the location is the world of its next date;
- a link to the event on your Modbot calendar page, when your **Public address** is set
  (**Settings**, **Server**). The link needs a Modbot sign-in, like the page itself.

The calendar is named after your managed group, or **Modbot** if none is set.

It does **not** contain drafts, deleted events, the join link, the picture, anything about Discord
or VRChat, or anything about your members. When you delete an event, it drops out of the feed. How a
calendar app shows a cancelled event is up to the app: some strike it through, some leave it out.
Calendar apps check a feed on their own schedule, so a change can take a while to show up there.

Reading the feed asks VRChat nothing, so it does not use any of your group's VRChat requests.

## Editing, cancelling and deleting

### Edit

Open the event and click **Edit**. The form opens with **Save** (and **Save draft** too, while it is
still a draft). Changes reach every place by themselves: VRChat after 20 seconds without further
changes, Discord on its next check.

For a repeating event, **Edit** first asks **This date** or **All dates**. **All dates** opens the form
for the whole event. **This date** opens a smaller form for the date you clicked; see
[Changing one date of a repeating event](#changing-one-date-of-a-repeating-event).

- A published event cannot go back to being a draft.
- A cancelled event cannot be edited.
- To run a **Finished** event again, edit it and give it new dates.

### Cancel

**Cancel event** is shown for a **Scheduled** or **Open** event. Cancelling it:

- removes it from VRChat's calendar;
- ends the Discord event, which then disappears from your server;
- changes the channel post to **Cancelled**;
- marks it cancelled in the calendar feed, where it stays for 30 days;
- stops any future instance from opening.

Modbot asks **Cancel “…”?** first; click **Cancel event** to go ahead.

**Telling members.** Changing the old channel post does not notify anyone. When the event has a
channel, the question has a tick, **Post that it's cancelled in the channel**. It starts ticked when
the event has **Discord channel post** on. Leave it ticked, and Modbot posts a short message in that
channel: the event's title, its date and time (in each reader's own time zone), and **Cancelled**.
It is posted once, within about 20 seconds, and never again for that event. On the event it shows as
**Cancelled post: Published**. Untick it to cancel without a message.

A cancelled event no longer appears on the calendar, and cannot be un-cancelled. To run it again,
plan a new event.

For a repeating event, Modbot asks **Cancel “…”?** with **This date** and **All dates**. **All dates**
cancels the whole event, as above. **This date** cancels only the date you clicked; see
[Changing one date of a repeating event](#changing-one-date-of-a-repeating-event).

### Changing one date of a repeating event

You can cancel, move or reword one date of a repeating event and leave every other date as it is:
for example, skip the week of a holiday, or start one week an hour later.

- **Cancel one date:** open the date, click **Cancel event**, then **This date**.
- **Move one date:** drag it and choose **This date**, or open it, click **Edit**, then **This date**,
  and change **Starts** and **Ends**.
- **Give one date its own title or description:** open it, click **Edit**, then **This date**. Leave
  a field as the event's own to keep following the event when you change the event later.

These choices show only for a date that has not ended yet, of an event that is **Scheduled** or
**Open**. A date cannot be moved to start at the same time as another date of the same event. Once a
date has started (or its instance has opened early), only its end can change: **That date has
already started.**

What happens to that one date:

| Place | Cancelled | Moved or reworded |
|---|---|---|
| Calendar page | Shown struck through, with a **Cancelled** badge when opened. | Shown at its new time; opening it shows **Moved from …**. |
| VRChat calendar | That date is removed. | That date is changed. |
| Discord event | Ended; the next date gets its own. | Changed to the new time and words. |
| Channel post | Its last word is **Cancelled**. | Edited to match. |
| Opening the instance | No instance opens. | Opens **Minutes early** before the new start. |
| Calendar feed | Left out. | Shown at the new time, with its own title and description. |
| Past events | Not listed. | Listed at the time it ran. |

**Undo.** After dragging one date, **Date moved · Undo** shows at the bottom of the screen; **Undo**
puts that date back. A date you move back to exactly its planned time, with the event's own title
and description, is a normal date again.

**Cancelling a date cannot be undone from the page.** Its other dates are not affected.

**Then editing the whole event.** If you later change the whole event's times (with **All dates** or
**Edit**), each changed date follows the event to the same day: cancel one Sunday, then move the
event from 8 PM to 9 PM, and that Sunday stays cancelled. A changed date on a day the event no longer
has (for example, the event moves from Sundays to Mondays) is dropped. Dates that are over are left
as they ran.

### Delete

**Delete** is shown for every event, including drafts. For an event that is still **Scheduled** or
**Open**, deleting does everything cancelling does, and also hides it from the Calendar page and
takes it out of the calendar feed at once. A
deleted **Finished** event is also removed from VRChat's calendar.

Modbot asks **Delete “…”?** first; click **Delete** to go ahead. There is no way to bring a deleted
event back from the page. Its history stays in the [audit log](#in-the-audit-log).

## On Health

The [Health](/self-hosting/health) page (**See the operational log**) shows calendar problems
for events that are **Scheduled** or **Open**:

- **A Calendar card** appears when something failed. Each line reads: the event's title, the place
  (**VRChat calendar**, **Discord event**, **Channel post** or **Instance**), the error, and how long
  ago. An **Instance** line means the instance for the current time did not open.
- **Not set up** lines in the same **Calendar** card name each place an event wants that cannot
  work yet (see [Where it goes](#where-it-goes)): for example **Discord event · Not set up**, linking
  to the setting that is missing.
- **Missing Manage Events** appears in the **Discord bot** card when an event has **Discord event**
  on and the bot does not have Manage Events in your server.

A place that is only **Waiting** because of a VRChat rate limit is not a problem and is not listed
here. Look at **VRChat request limits** on the same page instead.

## In the audit log

Every change to the calendar is recorded in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log), with the operational
entries (you need **See the operational log** to see them):

| Entry | When |
|---|---|
| **Event planned** | Someone created an event, as a draft or scheduled. |
| **Planned event changed** | Someone edited or dragged it. Shows what changed; a change of time alone reads as *moved the event … from … to …*. |
| **Planned event cancelled** | Someone cancelled it. Says whether it was posted in the channel. |
| **One date of an event cancelled** | Someone cancelled one date of a repeating event. Shows which date. |
| **One date of an event changed** | Someone moved one date of a repeating event, or gave it its own title or description. |
| **Planned event deleted** | Someone deleted it. |
| **Planned event opened** | One of its times started (or its instance opening time came). |
| **Planned event finished** | Its last time ended. |
| **Instance opened for an event** | Modbot opened the instance. |
| **Instance for an event failed to open** | VRChat refused to open it. Shows VRChat's error. |
| **Event failed to publish** | A place failed. Shows which place and the error. |
| **Event published** | A place has the event now: VRChat's calendar, the Discord event, the channel post, or the message that it is cancelled. Written once when it first gets there, not for every edit after. |
| **Event taken down** | A place that had the event no longer has it, after a cancel, a delete or turning it off. Not written for a place the event never reached. |
| **Calendar feed link replaced** | Someone made a feed link, or a new one. |
| **World picked for an event** | A world was picked from the event's [world list](/moderation/world-lists): by Modbot for a date, with **Pick again**, or with **Next game**. |

For events read from VRChat, the entries say so instead of naming a person: *Modbot added the event
“…” from VRChat's calendar*, *The event “…” was changed on VRChat: …* and *The event “…” was
deleted on VRChat*.

## Common problems

**The VRChat calendar place stays on Waiting.** Modbot sends at most one VRChat calendar change a
minute, and waits 20 seconds after your last edit. With several events queued, give it a few minutes.
If it still waits, VRChat may have asked Modbot to slow down; check **VRChat request limits** on
[Health](/self-hosting/health#vrchat-request-limits). Also check that a **Managed group** is chosen.

**Nothing happens in Discord.** Check that the bot is connected on [Health](/self-hosting/health),
that **Server id** is set, and, for the Discord event, that the bot has **Manage Events**. After fixing
a permission, change something about the event, such as its description, and save, so Modbot tries
again.

**The Discord event shows the world's name instead of a link.** The instance is not open yet, or
Modbot's **Public address** is not set. Without it, the join link is at the top of the Discord
event's description instead.

**The instance did not open.** Check **Instance** on the event for VRChat's error. Modbot does not try
again for that time; open it yourself in VRChat. The next time the event happens, Modbot tries again.

**An event made on vrchat.com is not on the page.** Click **Refresh**. If it repeats every second
week or every year, Modbot cannot hold it (see [What Modbot cannot hold](#events-made-on-vrchat)).
Check also that a **Managed group** is chosen.

**My edit on VRChat's website disappeared.** Someone changed the same event in Modbot more recently,
and the newest change wins.

**The feed link stopped working.** Someone clicked **New link**. Copy the new link from the Calendar
page and add it to your calendar app again.
