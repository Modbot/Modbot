---
title: Calendar
description: Plan your group's events once in Modbot, and let it put them on VRChat's group calendar, in Discord and in a calendar feed, and open the instance on time.
---

The **Calendar** page is where you plan your group's events. You fill in an event once, and
Modbot does the rest:

- puts it on your group's **VRChat calendar**;
- makes a **Discord event** in your server;
- posts it in a **Discord channel**, with a **Join** button once the instance is open;
- lists it in a **calendar feed**, a link people can add to Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple
  Calendar;
- puts it on your group's own **Google calendar**, once one is [connected](/moderation/google-calendar);
- **opens the group instance** a few minutes before the start, if you ask it to.

Each of these is a chip at the top of the event form: click it to turn that place on or off. Leave
one off and Modbot does not touch that place. The calendar feed is always on.

Events made on VRChat itself, on vrchat.com or in the game, show up on the page too, and you can
edit them from Modbot. See [Events made on VRChat](#events-made-on-vrchat).

## Who can use it

| To do this | You need |
|---|---|
| See the **Calendar** page, every event, where each one is published and any errors | [**See calendar**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-calendar) |
| Plan, edit, cancel and delete events | [**Manage calendar**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#manage-calendar) and **See calendar** |
| See, copy or replace the calendar feed link | **Manage calendar** and **See calendar** |
| Turn the [public feed](#the-public-feed) on or off, and copy its address | **Manage calendar** and **See calendar** |
| See [what each event did](#what-an-event-did) and **Past events** | **See calendar** and [**See analytics**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-analytics) |
| See who a moderator's client saw at an event | Those two and [**See the audit log**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-the-audit-log) |
| See calendar problems on **Health** | [**See the operational log**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#see-the-operational-log) |

Without **See calendar**, **Calendar** does not appear in the sidebar. **Manage calendar** on its own
is not enough to open the page. Without **Manage calendar**, the page is read-only: there is no
**New event** button, no feed link or **Public feed** switch, an event shows no **Edit**, **Duplicate**, **Cancel event** or
**Delete** buttons, and nothing can be dragged or drawn on the calendar.

Neither permission is in the built-in **Moderator** or **Viewer** roles. **Administrator** holds
both. Give them on purpose: an event can open an instance by itself, and it posts to VRChat and
Discord in your group's name.

## The page

The page works like Google Calendar. At the top are **Today**, the arrows for back and forward, the
dates on screen (for example **September 2026** or **Sep 21 – 27, 2026**), and a switch between four
views:

- **Day** and **Week** show the hours down the side, in your own clock, and one column a day, Monday
  first. The page opens scrolled to an hour before the first event on screen, or to 8 AM when there
  is none. A red line marks the time it is now. Events that overlap sit side by side. An event that
  lasts a day or longer is shown in a row across the top instead. Click a date at the top of a
  column to see that day on its own.
- **Month** shows six weeks. Each day lists the events that start on it, with the start time, up to
  three; **+2 more** (or however many) opens that day. Click a date to see that day.
- **Schedule** is a list from the day on screen to about two months ahead. Each row shows the time,
  the title, the event's status, the world, and a badge for each place it is published, such as
  **VRChat calendar: Published** or **Discord event: Failed**.

**Week** is the view the page opens on. On a phone it opens on **Day**.

**Refresh**, beside the views, reads your group's VRChat calendar again straight away. See
[Events made on VRChat](#events-made-on-vrchat).

On a wide screen a small month sits beside the calendar. Click a date in it to jump there; its own
arrows page through months without moving the calendar. It is hidden on phones.

A repeating event appears once for every time it happens.

Times on the page are in your browser's time zone. Each event also has its own time zone, which you
set in the form (see [When](#when)).

Below the calendar, **Possible duplicates in Discord** lists Discord events that look like copies of
each other, when there are any (see [Duplicate Discord events](#duplicate-discord-events)). Under it,
a **Drafts** box lists events saved as drafts, when there are any. Under that, **Past events** lists
every time an event ran in the last 90 days, the busiest first. See [Past events](#past-events).

The page updates by itself when an event changes, so a place changing from **Sending…** to
**Published** shows up without reloading. While an open event still has something being sent, it is
also read again every few seconds.

### Colours

An event's colour says where it stands:

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| The accent colour | **Scheduled**, and nothing has failed. |
| Green | **Open** right now. |
| Red edge | **Scheduled** or **Open**, and at least one place it is published has **Failed**, or its instance did not open. Open the event to see the error. |
| Dashed outline | A **Draft**. |
| Faded | **Finished**. |

### Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>D</kbd>, <kbd>W</kbd>, <kbd>M</kbd>, <kbd>A</kbd> | **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, **Schedule** |
| <kbd>T</kbd> | **Today** |
| <kbd>J</kbd> or <kbd>→</kbd> | Forward a day, a week or a month |
| <kbd>K</kbd> or <kbd>←</kbd> | Back a day, a week or a month |
| <kbd>C</kbd> | **New event** (with **Manage calendar**) |

They are also listed under **Calendar** when you press <kbd>?</kbd>.

### An event's status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Saved, but published nowhere and never opened. |
| **Scheduled** | The next time it happens has not started yet. |
| **Open** | It has started, or its instance has opened early, and it has not ended. |
| **Finished** | It does not happen again. |
| **Cancelled** | Someone cancelled it. |

A repeating event goes from **Scheduled** to **Open** and back to **Scheduled** each time, and only
becomes **Finished** after the last one.

### Opening an event

Click an event in any view. It opens in a small box beside it, showing:

- when it starts and ends (the time you clicked, for a repeating event), and how it repeats;
- **Moved from …** when that one date was [moved on its own](#changing-one-date-of-a-repeating-event);
- the description (that date's own, if it was given one);
- **World**, which opens the [world popup](/moderation/popups#world-popup). For an event that picks
  from a [world list](/moderation/world-lists), the world picked for its next date, and under it the
  **World list**, with **Pick again** while that date is scheduled; other dates show the list's name;
- **Next game**, while an event that picks from a list is open. See
  [Next game](/moderation/world-lists#next-game);
- **Instance**, once Modbot has tried to open one: **Opening** while it waits for VRChat, **Open**
  or **Closed** (click it for the [instance popup](/moderation/popups#instance-popup)), and a
  **Join** link while it is open. If VRChat refused, VRChat's error is shown here instead, in red,
  and **Open now** tries again while the instance can still be opened;
- one line per place it is published, with its state: **Sending…** (with a turning mark) from the
  moment you save until it is done, then **Published**, or **Failed** with what went wrong and a
  **Try again** button. See [Try again](#try-again);
- **How it went**, once the time you clicked has started. See [What an event did](#what-an-event-did).

With **Manage calendar** you also get **Edit**, **Duplicate**, **Cancel event** and **Delete**, and
**Open now** from two hours before a time starts until it ends, while no instance of it is open. See
[Opening the instance](#opening-the-instance).
**Duplicate** opens the form for a new event filled in with this one's details; nothing is saved
until you save it.

A link to one event (for example from a Chat answer) opens it in the middle of the page instead.

### Streamed on Twitch

With [Twitch](/integrations/twitch) set up, an event shows **Streamed on Twitch**, with the time and
**Open** for the channel, for each stream that started while it was on (from an hour before it starts
to its end). When two events overlap, Modbot leaves the stream unlinked. With **Manage calendar** you can
click **Remove** on a stream, or **Link a Twitch stream** to choose another from the channel's recent
streams; what you choose Modbot never changes.

### What an event did

Open a time an event has already run, or one that is running now, and **How it went** shows what it
did. You need **See analytics** as well as **See calendar** for it.

| Figure | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Most at once** | The most people in the event's instance at one moment, over the instance's whole life. The same number as **Most at once** in the [instance popup](/moderation/popups#instance-popup). |
| **Ran for** | How long the instance was open. **Open for** while it still is. |
| **New members** | People who joined your group from when the event opened (its instance's **Minutes early** included) until a day after it ended. |
| **Join requests** | Requests to join your group over the same stretch. |

New members and join requests count until a day after the end because people often join a group the
day after an event they liked. Two times of a daily event share some of that day, and both count it.

**Which instance.** The one Modbot opened for that time, when it opened one. Otherwise, your
**Managed group**'s instance in the event's world that was open for most of the event, which covers
an event someone opened by hand in VRChat. An event with no world,
and no instance opened by Modbot, has no instance: **Most at once** and **Ran for** show **—**.
When the instance is not the one the **Instance** line above already shows (that line is about the
time Modbot is dealing with now), it is named under **How it went**; click it for the instance popup.

For a repeating event, a **Usually** column shows the middle value of its last six times before this
one. Times with no instance leave out **Most at once** and **Ran for**.

**Seen by a moderator's client** shows how many people a moderator's
[desktop client](/companion/install) saw in the instance, their total time there (**People-time**), and the
people seen longest, each with their time. **Everybody** opens the instance popup's **People** tab when
there are more. This needs **See the audit log** too, and counts only while a moderator's client was
in the instance: **Nobody seen.** means no client saw anyone, not that nobody came. For **Usually**,
times nobody was seen are left out.

The numbers are not posted anywhere. They show only on this page.

### Past events

**Past events**, under the calendar, lists every time an event ran in the last 90 days: the event,
when, **Most at once**, **Ran for**, **New members** and **Join requests**. The busiest come first, and
times with no instance come last. It shows at most 50, and only when there is at least one. Click a
row to open that time of the event with **How it went**. On a phone each row shows the event, when it
started and its new members, with the most at once on the right.

Drafts and deleted events are never listed. A cancelled event's times before the cancel are. A date
cancelled on its own is not listed, and a date moved on its own is listed at the time it ran.

A time counts as one the event ran when the event's dates say so, or when Modbot opened an instance
for it. If you move a repeating event, its earlier times are worked out from the new dates, except
those Modbot opened an instance for, which stay at the time they ran.

## Moving events by dragging

With **Manage calendar** you can change the calendar by dragging, the way you would in Google
Calendar. Without it, events open when clicked, but nothing can be dragged and no new event can be
drawn.

- **Move an event:** drag it to another time, or to another day in **Week**. In **Month**, drag it to
  another day; it keeps its time of day. An event in the row across the top of **Week** can be dragged
  to another day the same way.
- **Change when it ends:** drag its bottom edge.
- **Draw a new event:** press on empty time and drag down. A single click on empty time draws one
  hour. In **Month**, click empty space in a day. A small form opens with the time filled in: type a
  title and click **Save draft**, or click **More options** for the full form. The small form only
  saves drafts, so nothing is published until you open the draft and click **Schedule**.

Everything snaps to 15 minutes. On a phone, hold your finger on an event (or on empty time) for a
moment until it picks up, then drag. A finger that moves straight away scrolls the page instead.

A drag is saved once, when you let go. It is the same as opening the event, changing its times in the
form and clicking **Save**: the change reaches VRChat's calendar, the Discord event and the channel
post the same way, and the audit log records it, for example *moved the event “Movie night” from
Sat 26 Sep, 08:00 PM–10:00 PM to Sun 27 Sep, 09:00 PM–11:00 PM*.

After a drag, **Event moved · Undo** shows at the bottom of the screen for a few seconds. **Undo**
puts the event back where it was, through the same save. Because Modbot waits 20 seconds without a
change before writing to VRChat's calendar, an undo straight away usually means VRChat is never
sent the move at all. If the save fails, the bottom of the screen says why instead, for example
**That event has already ended.**

### Repeating events

When you drag one date of a repeating event, Modbot asks **Move “…”?** and shows the date you dragged
and where it is going:

- **This date** moves only that date. The other dates stay where they are. After it saves, **Date
  moved · Undo** shows at the bottom of the screen. See
  [Changing one date of a repeating event](#changing-one-date-of-a-repeating-event).
- **All dates** moves every date by the same amount, the same as editing the whole event. The days of
  the week and the **Last date** move with it: a Monday and Wednesday event dragged one day later
  becomes Tuesday and Thursday.

Close the box to leave the event where it was.

## Planning an event

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the form

Click **New event** (or press <kbd>C</kbd>). The form starts with the next whole hour, two hours long,
in your browser's time zone. You can also draw the event on the calendar and click **More options**
(see [Moving events by dragging](#moving-events-by-dragging)); the form then starts with the time you
drew.
</Step>
<Step>
### Fill it in

Pick [where it goes](#where-it-goes) with the chips at the top, then fill in the fields under
**Details**. Every field is described below. Only **Title** is required, plus a world if the
instance should open by itself, and a channel if **Discord channel post** is on. **Preview** shows
the event the way each place will show it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Save it

- **Schedule** saves it and starts publishing it everywhere that is on.
- **Save draft** saves it without publishing or opening anything. You can come back to it from
  **Drafts** and click **Schedule** later.
- **Cancel** leaves without saving.

The buttons stay at the bottom of the form while you scroll, on a phone too. When you edit an event
that is already scheduled, **Save** takes the place of **Schedule** and **Save draft**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Modbot does not publish while you wait on the form. It saves the event, then publishes it in the
background within a minute or so. Watch the place badges change on the event; they change without
reloading the page.

### Where it goes

The chips at the top of the form say where the event goes. A chip that is on has a tick.

| Chip | What it does | Its own settings |
|---|---|---|
| **VRChat calendar** | Puts the event on your group's [VRChat calendar](#vrchats-group-calendar). On for a new event. | [VRChat calendar](#vrchat-calendar) |
| **Discord event** | Makes a [Discord event](#the-discord-event) in your server. On for a new event. | None |
| **Discord channel post** | Posts a [card](#the-channel-post) in a channel. | [Discord channel post](#discord-channel-post) |
| **Calendar feed** | Lists the event in the [calendar feed](#the-calendar-feed). Always on; it cannot be turned off. | None |
| **Google Calendar** | Puts the event on your group's [Google calendar](#google-calendar). On for a new event everyone may see, once Google Calendar is set up. | **Visible to**, under [Where](#where) |
| **Open the instance** | [Opens a group instance](#opening-the-instance) before each start. | [Open the instance](#open-the-instance) |

A place's own settings only show while its chip is on.

**Not set up.** A chip that is on but cannot work yet says **Not set up** beside it. Click it to open
the setting that is missing, in a new tab, so what you typed stays:

| Chip | Needs |
|---|---|
| **VRChat calendar**, **Open the instance** | A **Managed group** and a VRChat account, in **Settings**, **Modbot's VRChat login**. |
| **Discord event**, **Discord channel post** | A **Server id** and the bot connected, in **Settings**, **Discord**. While the bot is connecting or reconnecting, these say **Not set up** too, because nothing is sent until it is back. |
| **Google Calendar** | A key and a calendar that **Check** found Modbot may change, and **Sending** on, in **Settings**, **Google Calendar**. See [Google Calendar](/moderation/google-calendar). This chip says **Not set up** whether it is on or off. |

**Discord channel post** also says **Not set up** until you pick a channel in its section.

**Members only.** The **Google Calendar** chip says **Members only**, and cannot be turned on, for an
event only your members may see: **Visible to** is **Group**, or VRChat shows the event only to some of
your group's roles. Set **Visible to** to **Everyone** to turn it on. An event that had the chip on
keeps it, and goes to Google again once everyone may see it.

You can still save an event with a place that is not set up. Nothing is sent there until it is set
up; then Modbot catches up by itself. The event shows the same **Not set up** where that place would
be, and so does [Health](#on-health).

### Preview

**Preview**, next to **Details**, shows the event the way each place that is on will show it:

- **Discord event**: the cover, the time, the title, the location and the description, cut to
  Discord's 100 and 1,000 characters. The location is the world's name (or **VRChat** with no world);
  the join address only appears while an instance Modbot opened is open.
- **Discord channel post**: the card as it will be posted, with the times in your own time, and
  the role it mentions above it, if you picked one.
- **VRChat calendar**: exactly what is sent to VRChat: the title, time, repeat, category, who can see
  it, languages, platforms, tags, picture id, whether members are notified, and the description.
  VRChat may change some characters after it gets them.
- **Calendar feed**: what a phone or desktop calendar shows: the title, time, repeat, location,
  the calendar's name and the notes.
- **Google Calendar**: what goes on your Google calendar: the title, the time in the event's own
  time zone, the repeat in words, the location, the calendar's name and the description with the
  world's name and page under it. A padlock beside the title means only the people the calendar is
  shared with will see it, because **Check** found the calendar is not public, or shows the public
  only free or busy.

The preview is made by the same code that sends the event, so it matches what goes out. It does not
save anything or send anything to VRChat, Discord or Google. It can draw an event that is not finished yet,
with no title or no channel, but shows the error instead if something would be refused, such as a
title that is too long.

### Title and description

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Required. Up to 100 characters. The count beside it, such as **12 / 100**, turns red when it is too long. |
| **Description** | Optional, except for the VRChat calendar. Up to 1,000 characters, counted the same way. Line breaks are kept. |

### When

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Starts** | The date and time of the first time it happens. |
| **Ends** | Must be after the start. An event can last at most 7 days. |
| **Time zone** | The time zone **Starts** and **Ends** are in. |
| **Repeat** | **Does not repeat**, **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**. |
| Days of the week | Only for **Weekly**: tick **Mon** to **Sun**. The day of the first start is always included, even if you leave it unticked. |
| **Every** | Only for repeating events. How many days, weeks or months apart it happens, from 1 to 52: **Weekly** with **Every 2 weeks** is every other week. |
| **Ends** | Only for repeating events. **Never**, **On a date** or **After a number of times**. |
| **Last date** | For **Ends: On a date**. The last day it may start on. It cannot be before the start. |
| **Times** | For **Ends: After a number of times**. How many times it happens, from 1 to 500, the first one included. After the last one the event is finished. |

The time zone matters for repeating events. A weekly event at 20:00 in `Europe/London` stays at
20:00 London time in both summer and winter, even though that is a different time for someone in
another country.

A **Monthly** event happens on the same day of the month as its first start. An event on the 31st
skips months that have no 31st.

**Every 2 weeks** counts weeks from Monday to Sunday, starting with the week of the first start. A
Monday and Thursday event that starts on a Thursday happens that Thursday, skips the next week, then
happens on the Monday and Thursday of the week after. A skipped month still counts for **Every 2
months**.

**After a number of times** counts every time the repeat gives, including a date you
[cancelled on its own](#changing-one-date-of-a-repeating-event): cancel one of six and five are left.

### Where

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **World** | Pick from the worlds Modbot has seen recently, or choose **World id** and type the world's id. **None** is allowed, unless the instance opens by itself. **Pick from a list** takes the world from a [world list](/moderation/world-lists) instead, a new one for each date. |
| **World list** | Shown for **Pick from a list**. The list to pick from. See [When the world is picked](/moderation/world-lists#when-the-world-is-picked). |
| **Who can join** | **Group members**, **Members and their friends** or **Anyone**. Used for the instance Modbot opens, and shown on the channel post. |
| **Visible to** | **Group** or **Everyone**. Shown while the **VRChat calendar** or **Google Calendar** chip is on, or while **Google Calendar** says **Members only**. Who sees the event on VRChat's calendar, and whether it may go to [Google Calendar](#google-calendar) and the [public feed](#the-public-feed). |
| **Region** | **US West**, **US East**, **Europe** or **Japan**. Used for the instance Modbot opens. |
| **Picture link** | Optional. Must start with `https://` and point at a public website. Used as the Discord event's cover and the channel post's picture, unless a [Discord picture](#the-discord-picture) is set. When empty, Discord gets the world's picture instead. It can be a link to the picture itself, a link to a page that shows it, or a VRChat file link: see [Picture links](#picture-links). |

### VRChat calendar

Shown while the **VRChat calendar** chip is on.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Category** | One of VRChat's categories: **Arts**, **Avatars**, **Dance**, **Education**, **Exploration**, **Film & media**, **Gaming**, **Hangout** (the default), **Music**, **Other**, **Performance**, **Roleplaying** or **Wellness**. |
| **Languages** | Separate with commas, for example `eng, jpn`. At most 20, each up to 64 characters. |
| **Tags** | Separate with commas. At most 20, each up to 64 characters. |
| **VRChat picture** | Optional. The picture on VRChat's calendar. See [The VRChat picture](#the-vrchat-picture). |
| **PC**, **Android**, **iOS** | The platforms the event is for. |
| **Notify group members** | Asks VRChat to tell your group members about the event. VRChat is only asked this when the event is first put on its calendar, not when you edit it later. |
| **Featured** | Asks VRChat to show the event as featured. Off for a new event. Not every VRChat account may feature events. If VRChat refuses Featured, Modbot sends the event again straight away without it, so the event still goes onto VRChat's calendar, and leaves Featured out of everything it sends for the next 7 days; after that the next change asks again. The box stays ticked in Modbot while VRChat's copy is not featured. |

Some of VRChat's own event settings are not in the form: how early hosts and guests may join, closing
the instance after the end, instance overflow and which group roles see the event. For an event
[made on VRChat](#events-made-on-vrchat), Modbot keeps what VRChat has for them and sends them back
with every change.

### Picture links

The **Picture link** can be more than a link that ends in `.png` or `.jpg`:

- **A link to a page** that shows the picture (a gallery page, a post): Modbot uses the picture the
  page names for link previews, the same one Discord or a chat app would show.
- **A link that does not end in a picture name**, like a CDN link with `?ex=…` after it, or a
  picture in another format such as WebP: Modbot looks at what the link sends back, not at its name.
- **A VRChat file link**, like `https://api.vrchat.cloud/api/1/file/file_…/1/file`, from a picture
  you uploaded to VRChat: the **VRChat picture** fills itself in from it, so you do not have to copy
  the `file_` part across. Change or clear the link and a VRChat picture that came from it follows.
  A VRChat picture you chose yourself stays.

To use a link, Modbot's server fetches it. It only fetches `https://` links on public websites, never
an address on your own network, follows at most three redirects, stops after 10 MB or 15 seconds, and
keeps nothing. A VRChat file link is fetched with Modbot's VRChat login, and only while **Settings**,
**VRChat proxy**, **Proxy VRChat images through Modbot** is on.

Discord takes PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP for an event cover. A link to a picture in another format
still works for the **VRChat picture**, which is turned into a PNG in your browser, but Discord's
event gets no cover from it.

A Discord attachment link (`cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/…?ex=…`) stops working after about a
day. Modbot fetches the link again each time it updates the Discord event, so use a link that lasts.

### The Discord picture

Shown while the **Discord event** or **Discord channel post** chip is on. Discord shows an event's
cover in a wide 2.5:1 strip, so a picture made for VRChat's calendar loses its top and bottom there.
**Discord picture** lets you choose which part shows.

- **Choose picture**, or give a **Picture link**: the picture opens in a crop box in Discord's shape.
  A picture chosen here or under **VRChat picture** opens in both crop boxes, each in its own shape.
- Drag the box, and use **Size**. The **Preview** tab shows the Discord event and the channel post
  with the crop, before anything is uploaded.
- **Upload** keeps the cropped picture in Modbot. From then on Discord gets it as the event's cover
  and on the channel post, instead of the picture link. Pressing **Save** or **Schedule** with a crop
  still open uploads it first.
- **Remove** goes back to the picture link. **Crop** opens the picture link again to crop it anew.
- Modbot keeps the picture (at most 8 MB) until the event is deleted or given another one. A
  picture uploaded but never saved with an event is deleted once it is a day old, within the hour
  after.

### The VRChat picture

VRChat's calendar does not take a picture link, so its picture is a separate field from **Picture
link**. A VRChat file link in **Picture link** fills it in (see [Picture links](#picture-links)).

**Uploading a picture is off until you turn it on, and it needs VRChat+.** VRChat takes the upload
only from an account that has [VRChat+](https://hello.vrchat.com/vrchatplus), and the account that
has to have it is the VRChat account Modbot signs in as for your group. Without it VRChat refuses
the upload with *You don't have permission to use tag: gallery.* Modbot shows that sentence under the
field with a **Get VRChat+** link, and nothing is uploaded.

To turn it on, go to **Settings**, **Modbot's VRChat login**, **Pictures**, and turn on **Upload
VRChat pictures**. A **Get VRChat+** link sits beside the switch. While it is off the form has a
box for a VRChat image id instead: the id of a picture already on VRChat. Paste the whole VRChat
link if you like (`https://api.vrchat.cloud/api/1/file/file_…/1/file`, with `_blob` or anything else
on the end): Modbot keeps only the `file_…` id inside it. Text with no VRChat file id in it is
refused with an example of one.

With it on:

- **Choose picture** and pick any picture your browser can open (PNG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, and
  more). Or give a **Picture link** in this form: its picture opens the same way.
- **The picture opens in a crop box** in the shape VRChat's calendar uses, 16:9. Drag the box to
  choose the part to keep, and use **Size** to make it larger or smaller. The **Preview** tab shows
  the cropped picture before anything is uploaded.
- **Upload** sends the cropped picture to VRChat, as a PNG (or a JPEG if a PNG would be over
  **10 MB**), and a small copy of it shows in the form once VRChat has it. Pressing **Save** or
  **Schedule** with a crop still open uploads it first. While it uploads, **Save** and **Schedule**
  wait.
- **Crop** on a VRChat picture that came from a VRChat file link opens that picture in the crop box,
  to upload a cropped copy in its place.
- **The picture is uploaded to VRChat, to the VRChat account Modbot signs in as for your group.**
  It is a file on that account from then on, like a picture uploaded on vrchat.com. Modbot does not
  keep a copy; it keeps only the id VRChat gives the picture, and sends that id with the event.
- **Remove** takes the picture off the event. The file stays on VRChat.
- An event saved with a picture, opened again later, shows **VRChat picture set** instead of the
  small copy, because Modbot keeps only the id. So does an event made on VRChat with a picture.
- Modbot uploads **one picture a minute at most**, the same pace as its VRChat calendar changes. A
  second picture chosen within the minute waits for its turn.
- **To turn the upload off again**, turn off **Upload VRChat pictures**. Modbot then never sends a
  picture to VRChat: the form has no **Choose picture** button (an event that already has a
  picture keeps it, and the box for a VRChat image id is back), and the server answers **Picture
  uploads are off.** to anyone who asks it to upload. Turning it on or off is in the audit log as a
  settings change.

If the upload fails, the reason shows under the field:

- **Could not open the picture.** Your browser cannot read that kind of picture (some browsers
  cannot read HEIC, for example). Save it as a PNG or JPEG and choose it again.
- **The picture is larger than 10 MB.**
- **The link is not a picture.**, **Could not fetch the picture link.** or **The link took too long
  to answer.** The picture link gave Modbot nothing it could use.
- **VRChat is not taking uploads right now. Try again in a few minutes.** VRChat asked Modbot to
  slow down. Modbot does not try again by itself; choose the picture again later.
- **You don't have permission to use tag: gallery.** The account Modbot signs in as does not have
  [VRChat+](https://hello.vrchat.com/vrchatplus). The **Get VRChat+** link is beside it.
- Anything else VRChat says, in VRChat's own words.

Who uploaded which picture, and for which event, is in the [audit log](#in-the-audit-log).

### Discord channel post

Shown while the **Discord channel post** chip is on.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Channel** | Required. A channel where the bot is missing View Channel, Send Messages or Embed Links is marked **Missing** followed by what it lacks. |
| **Mention role** | Optional, **None** to start with. A role to ping when the post goes up, such as **@GameNight**. See [Mentioning a role](#mentioning-a-role). |

The **Discord event** has no settings of its own.

### Open the instance

Shown while the **Open the instance** chip is on. It needs a world, or a world list.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Minutes early** | How long before the start the instance opens: 0 to 120. The default is 10. |
| **Host** | A staff account Modbot invites first once the instance is open. See [Inviting people](#inviting-people). |
| **Invite list** | A [saved list](/moderation/lists) whose people Modbot invites last. Picking one needs **See members** and **See profiles**. |
| **Staff** | Staff accounts Modbot invites after the host. |
| **Announce in Discord when the first person joins** | Posts once in the **Channel** when the first person is in the instance. Can only be ticked with **Post to channel** and a channel. |
| **Announce in VRChat when the first person joins** | Posts once in your VRChat group's posts when the first person is in the instance. |

When the instance opens early, the event counts as **Open** from that moment, not from the start
time.

### When saving fails

The form lists everything that is wrong at once, one line each, so one fix-and-save round is enough.
For example:

- **Modbot's VRChat account needs Manage Group Calendar in this group.** Shown first, when
  **VRChat calendar** is on and Modbot's last look at your group says its account lacks that
  permission. On its own it does not stop the save: the event is saved, and the
  [VRChat calendar place](#when-vrchat-says-no) waits for the permission.
- **An event needs a title.**
- **VRChat's calendar needs a description.**
- **The VRChat image id is not a VRChat file id.** It has a space in it, or it is a link to
  somewhere other than VRChat. See [The VRChat picture](#the-vrchat-picture).
- **The end must be after the start.**
- **An event can last at most 7 days.**
- **The last date is before the start.**
- **Pick a last date or a number of times, not both.**
- **Repeat every must be between 1 and 52.** or **The number of times must be between 1 and 500.**
- **The picture link must start with https://.** or **The picture link points at a private address.**
- **Pick a channel to post to.**
- **Pick a world to open the instance in.**
- **That world list has no worlds.** Add worlds to it on the [World lists](/moderation/world-lists)
  page first, or save the event as a draft.
- **That event has already ended.** You tried to schedule an event whose last time is already over.

## VRChat's group calendar

When the **VRChat calendar** chip is on, Modbot adds the event to the calendar of the group
chosen as **Managed group** (**Settings**, **Modbot's VRChat login**). With no managed group chosen,
nothing is sent.

- **A repeating event is one entry on VRChat**, using VRChat's own repeat, in the event's time zone,
  with **Every**, and the last date or the number of times when there is one. It is not one entry
  per week. Once some of its times have passed, VRChat's entry starts at the next one, so VRChat is
  sent only the number of times that are left: six times with two over shows as four on VRChat.
- **One date cancelled or changed on its own** is sent to that one date on VRChat. Modbot reads that
  month of your calendar (up to three pages) before each change to find VRChat's id for the date, then removes it
  (a cancel) or changes its time, title and description. If Modbot cannot find that date on its own,
  the date shows **VRChat calendar: Failed** when you open it, and the rest of the event is left
  alone; check the date on VRChat yourself. Cancelling an event's last date finishes the event, and
  that date is still taken off VRChat. See
  [Changing one date of a repeating event](#changing-one-date-of-a-repeating-event).
- **Edits are sent as one.** Modbot waits until an event has gone 20 seconds without a change, then
  sends what it says at that moment. Fixing a typo three times in a row costs one change on VRChat.
- **Modbot sends one VRChat calendar change a minute at most**, across all events. VRChat is strict
  about its calendar, so this is kept slow on purpose. If you plan several events at once, they reach
  VRChat one after another.
- **Cancelling or deleting** the event, or turning off **VRChat calendar**, removes it from
  VRChat's calendar.
- **A finished event stays** on VRChat's calendar as a past event.
- **Changes made on VRChat are read back.** See [Events made on VRChat](#events-made-on-vrchat).

### Events made on VRChat

Modbot reads your group's VRChat calendar, so events someone made on vrchat.com or in the game show
up on the Calendar page, in the **Upcoming event** box on the VRChat page's **Overview**, and in the
calendar feed.

**When Modbot reads it.** Only when someone is looking:

- when the **Calendar** page opens, and when you move to dates it has not read yet: one request for
  each month on screen (a **Month** view can touch three);
- when the VRChat page's **Overview** opens: one request for this month, and one for next month when
  nothing on VRChat is left this month;
- when you click **Refresh** on the Calendar page.

A month read in the last five minutes is not asked for again, by anyone, unless you click
**Refresh**. Nothing is read on a timer, so the calendar feed only learns about an event made on
VRChat after someone has opened one of these pages. A repeating event costs one more request the
first time Modbot sees it, and again after it is changed on VRChat, to read how it repeats.

**How they show.** An event made on VRChat has a **VRChat** badge next to its status. It is an
ordinary Modbot event: with **Manage calendar** you can open, edit, drag, cancel and delete it, and
your change is sent to that same event on VRChat, never as a second copy. It arrives with
**VRChat calendar** and **Discord event** on, and **Discord channel post** and **Open the
instance** off; turn them on if you want them. **Featured** is copied in with the rest. While you leave it as it
came, Modbot does not send Featured back to VRChat, so an event VRChat features is not refused when
you edit it; changing the box sends it. VRChat settings the Modbot form does not have, such as how early hosts may join, are kept as they are when
you edit it.

**Changed on VRChat.** The newest change wins. Modbot copies in a change made on VRChat, for any
event, including ones planned in Modbot. If you changed the event in Modbot more recently and Modbot
has not sent it yet, your change wins and is sent to VRChat. One exception: for an event planned in
Modbot, the title and description are always Modbot's. VRChat changes some characters in the text it
is given (for example it drops an en dash), so its copy of the words is not taken as an edit; change
them in Modbot.

**Deleted on VRChat.** When an event is missing from a month Modbot read, Modbot asks VRChat about
that one event. If VRChat says it is gone, Modbot deletes it too, which also ends its Discord event
and post. A failed or partial read never deletes anything, and an event that was only moved to
another month is updated, not deleted. One exception: if the event was edited in Modbot and that edit
had not reached VRChat yet, the edit is newer, so Modbot puts the event back on VRChat.

**What Modbot cannot hold.** Modbot keeps one rule per repeating event, so:

- a change on VRChat to **one date only** of a repeating event is left on VRChat, and Modbot keeps
  the series as it was;
- an event repeating **every year**, or more than 52 days, weeks or months apart, or more than 500
  times, is not brought in.

An event that repeats **every second week** (or every third, and so on) or **a number of times** on
VRChat is brought in with the same **Every** and **Times**.

If the read fails, a red line under the page's controls says why, for example
**VRChat's calendar is waiting on its rate limit.** Modbot's own events still show.

### When VRChat says no

The **VRChat calendar** place on the event shows one of:

| State | What is happening |
|---|---|
| **Sending…** | Being sent: waiting for your edits to settle, for its turn, or for a rate limit to pass. |
| **Published** | VRChat has the latest version. |
| **Failed** | Something stopped it. What went wrong is shown beside it, with **Try again**. |
| **Removed** | Taken off VRChat after a cancel, a delete, or turning it off. |

**Before sending, Modbot checks what it can**, and lists every problem it finds together, so you can
fix them all at once:

1. that Modbot's VRChat account has **Manage Group Calendar** in your group, with a link to your
   group's roles on VRChat. Modbot looks at your group every few minutes; when its last look says
   the permission is missing, Modbot looks once more before saying so, so a permission you just gave
   is not missed;
2. a title and a description;
3. that the VRChat image id could be one (see [The VRChat picture](#the-vrchat-picture)).

When any of these is wrong, nothing is sent to VRChat, so none of its strict limit is spent.

What Modbot does next depends on why it failed:

- **Modbot's VRChat account lacks Manage Group Calendar.** Give the permission to a role Modbot's
  account has in VRChat (**Group settings**, **Roles**). Modbot sends the event by itself the next
  time it looks at your group, within a few minutes, with no edit. Press **Try again** to have it
  look straight away.
- **VRChat said "too many requests".** Modbot never retries straight away. It pauses all VRChat
  calendar changes for a while and the place shows **Sending…**, not **Failed**. It carries on by
  itself. You can see the pause under **VRChat request limits** on [Health](/self-hosting/health#vrchat-request-limits).
- **VRChat refused the event** (for example, a field it does not accept). Modbot does not send the
  same thing again by itself. Fix the event in Modbot and save, and it is sent. Saving with nothing
  changed, or pressing **Try again**, sends it again as it is.
- **VRChat did not answer, or had a problem on its side.** For a change to an event already on
  VRChat, Modbot tries again after 15 minutes. For a new event, VRChat sometimes adds it anyway, so
  Modbot looks on VRChat's calendar instead of sending it again: if it is there, the place shows
  **Published**; if not, it shows **Failed** with *VRChat did not add the event.* and a **Try
  again** button, and Modbot sends nothing until you press it, not even after an edit. **Try again**
  looks once more first, in case VRChat added it late.

### Try again

Every place that failed has a **Try again** button, for people with **Manage calendar**: the VRChat
calendar, the Discord event, the channel post, the post saying the event is cancelled, and one
date's own change on VRChat. It sends it again as it is, without editing the event, and
the place shows **Sending…** until it is done. Use it after fixing the cause outside Modbot, such
as a permission in VRChat or Discord.

- One press is enough. The button is off while it sends, and a second press finds the place already
  being sent.
- Saving the event, even with nothing changed, does the same for every failed place at once: the old
  failure goes away as soon as you save, and the place shows **Sending…**. The one exception is
  *VRChat did not add the event.*, which only its own **Try again** sends.
- An instance that did not open is tried again with [Open now](#open-now).

## Discord

Discord needs the bot to be set up and connected. See [Setting up the bot](/discord/bot-setup).
While the bot is not connected, nothing is sent to Discord, and it catches up once it is.

Modbot checks every 20 seconds and makes at most 5 Discord changes each time, so with many events it
can take a little longer.

### The Discord event

When the **Discord event** chip is on, Modbot makes an event in the server set as **Server id**
(**Settings**, **Discord**, **Bot**). The bot needs the **Manage Events** permission in that server.

- It has the title, description, start and end, and the picture link (or the world's picture) as its
  cover. If the picture cannot be fetched, the event is made without a cover.
- **Before the instance opens**, its location is the world's name.
- **When the event opens**, Modbot starts the Discord event. If Modbot opened the instance for it,
  the join link goes in it until the instance closes. If it did not (**Open the instance**
  is off, or VRChat refused), the location stays the world's name. Discord only
  allows 100 characters for a location, and VRChat's join links are longer. So, while there is a join
  link:
  - if your **Public address** is set (**Settings**, **Server**), the location is a short
    Modbot address that sends people on to the instance;
  - otherwise the location stays the world's name, and the join link goes at the top of the
    description.
- **When it finishes**, Modbot ends the Discord event. **When you cancel or delete it**, or turn off
  **Discord event**, the Discord event is ended too, and disappears from your server.
- **A repeating event gets a new Discord event each time it happens.** Discord cannot move an event
  back from started to scheduled, so after each time, the old one is ended and a new one is made for
  the next time.
- **One date cancelled on its own** ends that date's Discord event, and the next date gets its own.
  **One date moved, or given its own title or description**, has its Discord event changed to match.
- If you schedule an event after its start time, Discord refuses a start in the past, so the Discord
  event starts one minute from when Modbot makes it.
- If someone deletes or ends the Discord event in Discord, Modbot makes it again the next time the
  event needs a change there (an edit, or the instance opening).

The short address works without signing in. It only sends people on while the instance Modbot opened
is still open; at any other time it answers "not found".

### The channel post

When the **Discord channel post** chip is on, Modbot posts a card in the channel you picked, in the same style
as [instance announcements](/discord/instance-announcements).

- The card shows your group's name, the title, the description, **When** and **Ends** (as Discord
  times, so everyone sees their own time zone), **World**, **Who can join**, **Region**, and the
  picture link or the world's picture.
- **World** opens the world's page on vrchat.com, which anyone can open. (Modbot's own world page
  needs a Modbot sign-in, which your members do not have.)
- The line at the bottom says **Scheduled**, then **Open now** with a **Join** button once the
  instance is open.
- The card is edited whenever the event changes.
- When it finishes, the card says **Finished**. When you cancel or delete the event, it says
  **Cancelled**. The **Join** button is removed either way.
- **A repeating event gets a new post each time it happens.** The old post keeps its last word
  (**Finished**, or **Cancelled** for a date cancelled on its own) and a new one is posted for the
  next time.
- **Old posts are deleted a day after the event, or that date, was due to end.** That covers every
  card, whether it says **Finished** or **Cancelled**, and the short **Cancelled** messages from
  [cancelling](#cancel). Modbot only deletes messages it posted for the calendar. It never
  touches anyone else's messages, or its own instance announcements and giveaway posts.
- Turning off **Discord channel post**, or picking a different channel, deletes the post.
- If someone deletes the post in Discord, Modbot does not post it again until the event changes.

Cards from before October 2026 are not deleted, because Modbot did not keep track of them once they
said **Finished** or **Cancelled**. Delete those by hand. Older **Cancelled** messages are deleted,
a couple at a time.

The bot needs View Channel, Send Messages and Embed Links in the channel.

### Mentioning a role

An event can ping one role when its channel post goes up, for example **@GameNight** for the people
who asked to hear about game nights. It is off until you pick a role in **Mention role**.

- The post shows the role above the card, and **pings it once for each date**: when that date's post
  first goes up. A repeating event pings again for each new date.
- **Edits never ping.** When the event changes, opens or finishes, the post is edited and keeps
  showing the role, but nobody is notified. If someone deletes the post and Modbot posts it again,
  or you turn **Discord channel post** off and on, or you move the event to another date and back,
  that date is not pinged a second time.
- **Only roles the bot may ping.** That is a role with **Allow anyone to @mention this role** turned
  on (Discord, **Server Settings**, **Roles**, the role, **Display**), or any role if the bot has the
  **Mention @everyone, @here and All Roles** permission. Other roles are marked **Bot cannot
  mention** in the list, and saving with one says **The bot may not mention that role.**
- **@everyone and @here are never offered**, and an event cannot ping them.
- Nothing else Modbot posts pings the role: not the [cancel message](#cancel), and not the post when
  the [first person joins](#announce-when-the-first-person-joins).

The role list is the one Modbot keeps for **Settings**, **Discord**. Like the channel list, it needs
[**Change settings**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#change-settings) to open.

### Duplicate Discord events

When several tools make Discord events (another bot that copies VRChat's calendar, a bot of your
own, Modbot's calendar, or people by hand), your server's event list can show the same event twice.
Modbot looks for this in every Discord event in your server, whoever made it, and lists what it finds
under the calendar as **Possible duplicates in Discord**, and on [Health](#on-health).

Two events count as possible duplicates when:

- **their titles match**, ignoring capitals, spaces, punctuation, emoji and a bracketed tag at the front (so
  **[VRChat, Group Public] Movie night** matches **Movie night 🎬**, but **Game Night (Among Us)**
  does not match **Game Night (Minecraft)**), or one title contains the
  whole of the other and the shorter one has at least six letters; and
- **they start at most 15 minutes apart.**

Each set shows the title and time, then every copy with who made it: the bot's or app's name,
**Modbot's calendar** for the Discord event of one of your Modbot events (with **Open** to open it),
**Modbot** for another event Modbot's bot made, or **A person**. People are never named.

Modbot does not delete or change anything. Decide which tool should make your Discord events, turn
the others off, and delete the extra events in Discord. To stop Modbot making one, turn off
**Discord event** on that Modbot event.

Modbot reads your server's event list when you open the Calendar page or Health, at most once every
five minutes, so a duplicate you just deleted can stay listed for a few minutes. The list needs the
bot to be connected; while it is not, nothing is listed.

### When Discord says no

The **Discord event** and **Channel post** places use the same states as VRChat: **Sending…**,
**Published**, **Failed** and **Removed**, with Discord's error beside a failure. Common errors:

| Error | Fix |
|---|---|
| **The bot may not manage server events; it needs Manage Events.** | Give the bot's role **Manage Events** in your Discord server. |
| **The bot is not in that server.** | Check **Server id** in **Settings**, **Discord**, and that the bot has been invited. |
| **Discord is rate limiting the bot.** | Nothing. Modbot tries again on its next check. |

A refusal that will not fix itself, such as a missing permission, is not sent again by itself. After
fixing the permission, press **Try again** on the place, or save the event; nothing has to change.
See [Try again](#try-again). Other failures are tried again on the next check.

## Opening the instance

When the **Open the instance** chip is on, Modbot opens a group instance **Minutes early**
before each start:

- in the event's world, with its **Who can join** and **Region**;
- owned by your **Managed group**.

A date cancelled on its own gets no instance. A date moved on its own opens **Minutes early** before
its new start.

Modbot opens **one instance each time the event happens**, and never more. It notes the attempt
before asking VRChat, so a restart cannot open a second one. If Modbot was down at the opening time,
it still opens the instance when it comes back, as long as that time has not ended yet.

Once the instance is open, its join link goes onto the Discord event and the channel post, and the
event shows it under **Instance**. When the instance closes, the link is taken off.

**If VRChat refuses, Modbot does not try again** for that time by itself. The error shows on the
event under **Instance**, with **Open now** to try again while the time has not ended, and on
[Health](#on-health), and the event turns red. The next time the event happens gets its own attempt.

**If the request never reached VRChat**, Modbot sends it again while the time has not ended: when it
was waiting out a VRChat request limit or signing in, when Cloudflare stopped it, or when VRChat asked
Modbot to slow down (only once the limit allows). The event shows **Opening** meanwhile.

**If VRChat failed on its side or did not answer**, it may have opened the instance anyway, so Modbot
does not send the request again. The event shows **Checking…** while Modbot looks for a new instance
of the event's world in your group. If one shows up, it becomes the event's instance. If none does,
the event shows the error, turns red, and it is up to you to press **Open now**.

### Open now

**Open now**, on the event, opens the instance straight away, through the same path and the same
limit. It appears for people with **Manage calendar** from two hours before a time starts until it
ends, while no instance of that time is open: before the opening time, after VRChat refused, or after
the instance closed. It does not appear while an earlier attempt is under way or **Checking…**. It works whether or not **Open the instance automatically** is ticked. The audit
log names who pressed it. Invites and the posts for the first person then follow as usual; after a
closed instance, the people not invited yet are invited to the new one.

Modbot opens at most one instance every 5 seconds, which is well within what an event needs.

## Inviting people

Once Modbot has opened an event's instance, it can invite people to it, in this order:

1. the **Host**;
2. the **Staff**;
3. the people on the **Invite list**, as the list holds them at the moment the instance opens.

Not every group member: only who you picked. Somebody who is host, staff and on the list is invited
once.

**Only people who asked get an invite.** Nothing is sent to anybody else:

| Who | Gets invites when | How to stop |
|---|---|---|
| The **Host** and **Staff** | Their **Get event invites** switch is on, on their **Account** page. It is on for every staff account until they turn it off. | Untick **Get event invites** on the Account page. |
| People on the **Invite list** | They pressed **Get event invites** under [`/me`](/discord/account-linking#get-event-invites) in your Discord server. | Press **Stop event invites** under `/me`. |

Turning invites off stops them at once, even for an event whose invites are already going out. While
**/me** is off on the Commands card in your server, members have no way to ask or to stop, so no member
counts as having asked, even one who asked before: an invite list reaches its staff only. A member
is matched by their Discord account, or by the VRChat account linked to it now.
People who did not ask count as **Didn't ask for invites**.

**What is sent, and from where.** Everything is sent from your group's own accounts:

- a **VRChat invite** to the instance, from your group's Modbot VRChat account (the one under
  **Settings → VRChat**). VRChat only lets an account invite its **friends**, so somebody who is not a
  friend of that account cannot get one;
- otherwise, a **Discord direct message** from your server's Modbot bot, to people whose Discord
  account Modbot knows (linked to their VRChat account, or proven on their staff account). It holds
  the event's title and a **Join** button, nothing else.

Somebody with neither counts as **No way to reach**. Somebody whose direct messages are closed counts
as **Couldn't reach**, and is not asked again.

**Who is left out:** people banned from the group or from the Discord server, and people a moderator's
client already sees in the instance.

**Who is a friend.** VRChat does not give Modbot the whole friends list, so Modbot remembers what it
learns: an invite VRChat accepts means a friend, a refused one means not, and the friends VRChat lists
when Modbot signs in count as friends. Somebody Modbot knows is not a friend gets the Discord message
straight away; after a week, Modbot tries a VRChat invite once more in case they added the account.
Somebody Modbot knows nothing about gets one VRChat invite first.

**How fast.** One VRChat invite every 30 seconds, and a direct message every few seconds. Sending stops
when the instance closes or the event ends, so a long list may not get through in time. If VRChat asks
Modbot to slow down, Modbot does not ask again for that person: it sends the direct message instead,
and waits before the next VRChat invite. If VRChat fails on its side, the invite may have arrived
anyway, so that person counts as **Couldn't reach** and gets no direct message on top.

**Nobody is invited twice.** Modbot notes each person before it sends, so a restart picks up where it
stopped and never sends the same invite again.

The event shows **Invited N of M**, and under it how many by **VRChat invite**, by **Discord
message**, **Couldn't reach**, **No way to reach** and **Didn't ask for invites**.

### Announce when the first person joins

As soon as VRChat counts somebody in the instance, Modbot posts once each time the event happens:

- with **Announce in Discord when the first person joins**, in the event's **Channel**: the event's
  title and a **Join** button, from your server's Modbot bot;
- with **Announce in VRChat when the first person joins**, in your VRChat group's posts, from your
  group's Modbot VRChat account: the event's title, *… has started.* and the join link. It is visible
  to the group, and VRChat does **not** notify members about it.

If Discord or VRChat refuses, the event shows what it said under **Instance**, and Modbot does not try
again for that time.

## The calendar feed

The calendar feed is a link people add to their own calendar app (Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple
Calendar and others) to see your group's events there. It is only on the page for people with
**Manage calendar**, in the **Calendar feed** row below the calendar.

- **Make link** creates the link the first time.
- **Copy** copies it.
- **Add to Google Calendar** opens Google Calendar's own page for adding a calendar by its address,
  with the feed filled in, in a new tab. It adds the feed to the Google account you are signed in to
  in that browser, for your own use. Google then holds the feed's secret address and reads the feed
  from its own servers, so the address must be one the internet can reach. It works on a computer,
  not in Google's phone app, and Google checks the feed on its own schedule, so a change can take a
  while to show there. To put events on a Google calendar your members follow, use
  [Google Calendar](#google-calendar) instead.
- **New link** replaces it. **The old link stops working at once**, for everyone who added it. Use
  this if the link got shared somewhere it should not be. Modbot asks **Make a new feed link?**
  first; click **Make new link** to go ahead.

### The address

The link looks like this:

```text
https://modbot.example.com/api/calendar/feed/<secret>.ics
```

It starts with your **Public address** (**Settings**, **Server**). If that is not set, the
page uses the address you have open in your browser instead, which only works for others if they
can reach that address too.

### Who can see it

**Anyone who has the link.** Opening it needs no Modbot account and no sign-in: the secret part of
the address is the key. Share it where you would share your event list, and make a **New link** if
it leaks.

### What it contains

The feed lists:

- every event that is **Scheduled** or **Open**;
- every **Finished** event for **30 days** after its last time ended, so it stays in people's
  calendars as something that happened;
- every **Cancelled** event for **30 days** after it was cancelled, marked as cancelled, so calendar
  apps can show it as cancelled instead of it quietly disappearing.

For each one:

- the title and description;
- the start and end, in the event's time zone;
- the repeat and last date, so calendar apps show every time it happens;
- dates cancelled on their own left out, and dates moved or reworded on their own at their new time
  and with their own title and description;
- the world's name as the location. A repeating event is one entry in the feed, so for one that picks
  from a world list, the location is the world of its next date;
- a link to the event on your Modbot calendar page, when your **Public address** is set
  (**Settings**, **Server**). The link needs a Modbot sign-in, like the page itself.

The calendar is named after your managed group, or **Modbot** if none is set.

It does **not** contain drafts, deleted events, the join link, the picture, anything about Discord
or VRChat, or anything about your members. When you delete an event, it drops out of the feed. How a
calendar app shows a cancelled event is up to the app: some strike it through, some leave it out.
Calendar apps check a feed on their own schedule, so a change can take a while to show up there.

Reading the feed asks VRChat nothing, so it does not use any of your group's VRChat requests.

## The public feed

The public feed is a second feed that lists only your public events, at an address with no secret
in it. Hand it to event directories, put it on your website, or add it in Google Calendar with
**From URL**. Directories that only read VRChat's group calendar, or that want a form filled in, do
not need it.

It is **off** until someone with **Manage calendar** turns it on, with the **Public feed** switch
below the **Calendar feed** row. While it is on, its address is beside the switch, with **Copy**:

```text
https://modbot.example.com/api/calendar/public.ics
```

Like the calendar feed's link, it starts with your **Public address** (**Settings**, **Server**), or
the address you have open in your browser when that is not set.

### Who can see it

**Anyone.** Opening it needs no Modbot account, no sign-in and no secret. There is no link to replace:
to stop it, turn the switch off. The address then answers "not found" at once, and calendar apps that
read it stop getting updates. Turning it on or off is in the
[audit log](/moderation/audit-log), like any other setting.

### What it contains

Only events that go to your group's VRChat calendar (the **VRChat calendar** chip is on) with
**Visible to** set to **Everyone**, in the form's [Where](#where) section. Turn the chip off and the
event leaves the public feed, whatever **Visible to** said. An event that goes only to
[Google Calendar](#google-calendar) is not in the public feed; it is on the Google calendar's own
public links instead. An event that VRChat shows only to some of your group's roles is left out too.

Of those, it lists the same events as the calendar feed: **Scheduled** and **Open** ones, **Finished**
ones for 30 days after their last time, and **Cancelled** ones for 30 days after the cancel, marked
as cancelled. Never drafts or deleted events.

Each entry has the same title, description, times, repeat, changed and cancelled dates, world name
and calendar name as in the calendar feed, with two differences:

- **No link to your Modbot calendar page.** Nobody reading a public feed can sign in to it.
- **The join link, only for an event anyone can join** (**Who can join** set to **Anyone**). It is
  your **Public address** followed by `/api/calendar/join/` and the event, the same link a Discord
  event shows. It leads to the instance while one Modbot opened is open, and to "not found" the rest
  of the time. An event for **Group members** or **Members and their friends** has no link.

It does **not** contain events visible only to your group, drafts, deleted events, the picture,
anything about Discord, or anything about your members.

A change you make in Modbot is in the public feed at once. A change made on VRChat, or an event
finishing, can take up to a minute. Calendar apps check a feed on their own schedule, so it can take
longer to show there. Reading the feed asks VRChat nothing.

## Google Calendar

When the **Google Calendar** chip is on, Modbot puts the event on the Google calendar named in
**Settings**, **Google Calendar**, and keeps it in line with the event. Setting it up, and the
calendar's own public links, are on the [Google Calendar](/moderation/google-calendar) page.

### What goes

- The title and description, with a blank line and the world's name and its page on vrchat.com
  under the description.
- The start and end, in the event's own time zone.
- The repeat and its last date, as one Google event that repeats.
- The world's name as the location, or **VRChat** with no world.

No picture, no join link, nothing about Discord and nothing about your members. Nobody is invited or
emailed, and no reminders are set: each person's own Google settings decide those.

An edit reaches Google about twenty seconds after your last change, so several quick fixes are one
change there.

### Members only

An event only your members may see never goes to Google, with the chip on or not: **Visible to** is
**Group**, or VRChat shows the event only to some of your group's roles. A public Google calendar
would show it to anyone. The chip says **Members only**. Change an event that is on Google to
**Group**, and Modbot takes it off Google.

### Cancel, delete and finished events

| In Modbot | On Google |
|---|---|
| **Cancel event** | Kept, with **Cancelled:** in front of its title, until a day after the date it was cancelled on; then removed. A repeating event stops at that date. |
| **Delete** | Removed at once. |
| **Finished** | Kept, as something that happened. |
| The chip turned off | Removed. Turned on again later, the event is added again. |

### One date of a repeating event

What happens on Google when you [change one date](#changing-one-date-of-a-repeating-event):

| Change | On Google |
|---|---|
| Cancelled | Left out of the repeat. |
| Moved or reworded | That date is changed on its own, to the new time and words. |
| Put back as planned | That date goes back to the repeat's time and the event's own words. |

When you then edit the whole event, Modbot writes the whole repeat again and sends every changed date
again after it, so they stay as you left them.

### Changes made on Google

Modbot does not read anything back from Google. A change made to the event on Google itself is
replaced the next time the event changes in Modbot, and an event deleted on Google is added again
then. Edit your events in Modbot.

### On the event

Each event says how it went on Google: **Google Calendar: Sending…**, **Published** with **Open**,
which opens the event on Google, **Failed** with Google's own words and **Try again**, or
**Removed**. A changed date Google refused shows under the date, with **Try again**.

## Editing, cancelling and deleting

### Edit

Open the event and click **Edit**. The form opens with **Save** (and **Save draft** too, while it is
still a draft). Changes reach every place by themselves: VRChat after 20 seconds without further
changes, Discord on its next check. A place that had failed shows **Sending…** as soon as you save,
and is sent again even if nothing it shows changed.

For a repeating event, **Edit** first asks **This date** or **All dates**. **All dates** opens the form
for the whole event. **This date** opens a smaller form for the date you clicked; see
[Changing one date of a repeating event](#changing-one-date-of-a-repeating-event).

- A published event cannot go back to being a draft.
- A cancelled event cannot be edited.
- To run a **Finished** event again, edit it and give it new dates.

### Cancel

**Cancel event** is shown for a **Scheduled** or **Open** event. Cancelling it:

- removes it from VRChat's calendar;
- ends the Discord event, which then disappears from your server;
- changes the channel post to **Cancelled**;
- marks it cancelled in the calendar feed, where it stays for 30 days;
- puts **Cancelled:** in front of its title on [Google Calendar](#google-calendar), until a day after
  its date;
- stops any future instance from opening.

Modbot asks **Cancel “…”?** first; click **Cancel event** to go ahead.

**Telling members.** Changing the old channel post does not notify anyone. When the event has a
channel, the question has a tick, **Post that it's cancelled in the channel**. It starts ticked when
the event has **Discord channel post** on. Leave it ticked, and Modbot posts a short message in that
channel: the event's title, its date and time (in each reader's own time zone), and **Cancelled**.
It is posted once, within about 20 seconds, and never again for that event. On the event it shows as
**Cancelled post: Published**. A day after the event was due to end, Modbot deletes it, together with
the old card, and it shows as **Cancelled post: Removed**. Untick it to cancel without a message.

A cancelled event no longer appears on the calendar, and cannot be un-cancelled. To run it again,
plan a new event.

For a repeating event, Modbot asks **Cancel “…”?** with **This date** and **All dates**. **All dates**
cancels the whole event, as above. **This date** cancels only the date you clicked; see
[Changing one date of a repeating event](#changing-one-date-of-a-repeating-event). The **Post that
it's cancelled in the channel** tick is there too, for either choice: with **This date**, the message
gives that date's time.

### Changing one date of a repeating event

You can cancel, move or reword one date of a repeating event and leave every other date as it is:
for example, skip the week of a holiday, or start one week an hour later.

- **Cancel one date:** open the date, click **Cancel event**, then **This date**.
- **Move one date:** drag it and choose **This date**, or open it, click **Edit**, then **This date**,
  and change **Starts** and **Ends**.
- **Give one date its own title or description:** open it, click **Edit**, then **This date**. Leave
  a field as the event's own to keep following the event when you change the event later.

These choices show only for a date that has not ended yet, of an event that is **Scheduled** or
**Open**. A date cannot be moved to start at the same time as another date of the same event. Once a
date has started (or its instance has opened early), only its end can change: **That date has
already started.** For an event on VRChat's calendar, a date cannot move to or past the date before
or after it: **Can't move a date past the next date on VRChat.** Cancel the date and plan a separate
event instead.

What happens to that one date:

| Place | Cancelled | Moved or reworded |
|---|---|---|
| Calendar page | Shown struck through, with a **Cancelled** badge when opened. | Shown at its new time; opening it shows **Moved from …**. |
| VRChat calendar | That date is removed. | That date is changed. |
| Discord event | Ended; the next date gets its own. | Changed to the new time and words. |
| Channel post | Its last word is **Cancelled**. Deleted a day after the date was due to end, with the short **Cancelled** message. | Edited to match. |
| Opening the instance | No instance opens. | Opens **Minutes early** before the new start. |
| Calendar feed | Left out. | Shown at the new time, with its own title and description. |
| Google Calendar | Left out of the repeat. | That date is changed on its own. |
| Past events | Not listed. | Listed at the time it ran. |

**Undo.** After dragging one date, **Date moved · Undo** shows at the bottom of the screen; **Undo**
puts that date back. A date you move back to exactly its planned time, with the event's own title
and description, is a normal date again.

**Cancelling a date cannot be undone from the page.** Its other dates are not affected.

**Then editing the whole event.** If you later change the whole event's times (with **All dates** or
**Edit**), each changed date follows the event to the same day: cancel one Sunday, then move the
event from 8 PM to 9 PM, and that Sunday stays cancelled. A changed date on a day the event no longer
has (for example, the event moves from Sundays to Mondays) is dropped. Dates that are over are left
as they ran.

### Delete

**Delete** is shown for every event, including drafts. For an event that is still **Scheduled** or
**Open**, deleting does everything cancelling does, and also hides it from the Calendar page and
takes it out of the calendar feed at once. Its channel post changes to **Cancelled** straight away,
and is deleted a day after the event was due to end, like any other old post. A
deleted **Finished** event is also removed from VRChat's calendar.

Modbot asks **Delete “…”?** first; click **Delete** to go ahead. There is no way to bring a deleted
event back from the page. Its history stays in the [audit log](#in-the-audit-log).

## On Health

The [Health](/self-hosting/health) page (**See the operational log**) shows calendar problems
for events that are **Scheduled** or **Open**:

- **A Calendar card** appears when something failed. Each line reads: the event's title, the place
  (**VRChat calendar**, **Discord event**, **Channel post**, **Google Calendar** or **Instance**), the
  error, and how long ago. An **Instance** line means the instance for the current time did not open.
- **Not set up** lines in the same **Calendar** card name each place an event wants that cannot
  work yet (see [Where it goes](#where-it-goes)): for example **Discord event · Not set up**, linking
  to the setting that is missing. **Google Calendar · Not set up** shows while events are ticked for
  it and Google Calendar is not set up, or **Sending** is off.
- **Google Calendar · limited until …** in the same card means Google asked Modbot to slow down.
  Nothing is sent to Google until then; the events wait and go after.
- **Possible duplicates in Discord** lines in the same **Calendar** card name each set of
  [duplicate Discord events](#duplicate-discord-events), its time and who made the copies, and lead
  to the Calendar page. These show even when Modbot has no events of its own.
- **Missing Manage Events** appears in the **Discord bot** card when an event has **Discord event**
  on and the bot does not have Manage Events in your server.

A place that is only **Sending…** because of a VRChat rate limit is not a problem and is not listed
here. Look at **VRChat request limits** on the same page instead.

## In the audit log

Every change to the calendar is recorded in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log), with the operational
entries (you need **See the operational log** to see them):

| Entry | When |
|---|---|
| **Event planned** | Someone created an event, as a draft or scheduled. |
| **Planned event changed** | Someone edited or dragged it. Shows what changed; a change of time alone reads as *moved the event … from … to …*. |
| **Planned event cancelled** | Someone cancelled it. Says whether it was posted in the channel. |
| **One date of an event cancelled** | Someone cancelled one date of a repeating event. Shows which date. |
| **One date of an event changed** | Someone moved one date of a repeating event, or gave it its own title or description. |
| **Planned event deleted** | Someone deleted it. |
| **Planned event opened** | One of its times started (or its instance opening time came). |
| **Planned event finished** | Its last time ended. |
| **Instance opened for an event** | Modbot opened the instance. |
| **Instance for an event failed to open** | VRChat refused to open it. Shows VRChat's error. |
| **Event failed to publish** | A place failed. Shows which place and the error. |
| **Event published** | A place has the event now: VRChat's calendar, the Discord event, the channel post, Google Calendar, or the message that it is cancelled. Written once when it first gets there, not for every edit after. |
| **Event taken down** | A place that had the event no longer has it, after a cancel, a delete or turning it off. Not written for a place the event never reached. |
| **Event picture uploaded to VRChat** | Someone uploaded a VRChat picture. Names the event when it was already saved; for a new event, the event's own **Event planned** entry carries the same picture. |
| **Calendar feed link replaced** | Someone made a feed link, or a new one. |
| **World picked for an event** | A world was picked from the event's [world list](/moderation/world-lists): by Modbot for a date, with **Pick again**, or with **Next game**. |

Each invite is recorded too, about the person who was invited, with the moderation entries (you need
**See the audit log**):

| Entry | When |
|---|---|
| **Invited to an event** | Modbot sent a VRChat invite or a Discord message. |
| **Event invite failed** | Neither got through. Shows what VRChat or Discord said. |

For events read from VRChat, the entries say so instead of naming a person: *Modbot added the event
“…” from VRChat's calendar*, *The event “…” was changed on VRChat: …* and *The event “…” was
deleted on VRChat*.

## Common problems

**The VRChat calendar place stays on Sending….** Modbot sends at most one VRChat calendar change a
minute, and waits 20 seconds after your last edit. With several events queued, give it a few minutes.
If it still waits, VRChat may have asked Modbot to slow down; check **VRChat request limits** on
[Health](/self-hosting/health#vrchat-request-limits). Also check that a **Managed group** is chosen.

**Nothing happens in Discord.** Check that the bot is connected on [Health](/self-hosting/health),
that **Server id** is set, and, for the Discord event, that the bot has **Manage Events**. After fixing
a permission, press **Try again** on the place.

**VRChat calendar still says Modbot needs Manage Group Calendar after you gave it.** Check that the
role you changed is one Modbot's VRChat account has: the message names the account's roles. Then
press **Try again**, which looks at your group's roles again before sending.

**The same event shows twice in Discord's event list.** More than one tool is making Discord events.
Check **Possible duplicates in Discord** under the calendar to see which ones, then see
[Duplicate Discord events](#duplicate-discord-events).

**The role was not pinged.** Check that the role is open to mentions, or that the bot has **Mention
@everyone, @here and All Roles** (see [Mentioning a role](#mentioning-a-role)). A date is pinged only
once: an edit, or a post made again for the same date, does not ping.

**The Discord event shows the world's name instead of a link.** The instance is not open yet, or
Modbot's **Public address** is not set. Without it, the join link is at the top of the Discord
event's description instead.

**The instance did not open.** Check **Instance** on the event for VRChat's error. Modbot does not try
again for that time by itself; fix the cause and press **Open now**, or open it yourself in VRChat.
The next time the event happens, Modbot tries again.

**An event made on vrchat.com is not on the page.** Click **Refresh**. If it repeats every second
week or every year, Modbot cannot hold it (see [What Modbot cannot hold](#events-made-on-vrchat)).
Check also that a **Managed group** is chosen.

**My edit on VRChat's website disappeared.** Someone changed the same event in Modbot more recently,
and the newest change wins.

**The feed link stopped working.** Someone clicked **New link**. Copy the new link from the Calendar
page and add it to your calendar app again.
