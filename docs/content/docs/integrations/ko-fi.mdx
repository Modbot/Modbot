title: Ko-fi supporters
description: Giving supporters a Discord role and a VRChat group role for the tier they pay at, thanking them, and taking the roles back when a monthly payment stops.
---

Modbot can give your Ko-fi supporters their perks. When somebody pays monthly, Modbot gives them the
**Discord role** and the **VRChat group role** you chose for their tier and sends them a thank-you
message. When a monthly payment does not come, Modbot takes the roles back.

Ko-fi calls Modbot, not the other way round. Modbot never signs in to Ko-fi and never asks Ko-fi for
anything, so the only thing it needs from Ko-fi is a **verification token**. Your Modbot must be
reachable from the internet at the **Public address** in **Settings**, **Server**, because Ko-fi sends
each payment to it.

You set it up in **Settings**, **Ko-fi**, and look after the tiers and people on the **Supporters**
page (**Community**). Both need
[**Change settings**](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#change-settings).

## Set it up

1. In Ko-fi, open your page's settings, **API**, and find the **Webhooks** section. Copy the
   **Verification Token**.
2. In Modbot, go to **Settings**, **Ko-fi**. Paste the token into **Verification token** and click
   **Save**. The page shows **Verification token: Saved**, and never shows it again. It is kept
   encrypted.
3. Copy the **Webhook address** shown on that page into Ko-fi's **Webhook URL** box and save it there.
   The address is built from your Modbot's public address. If it says **Public address not set**, set
   the public address first.
4. Turn the **Ko-fi** switch on. Until it is on, the address answers as if it were not there.
5. Ko-fi has a button to send a test message. Use it, then look at the **Supporters** page.

A message with the wrong token is refused. Nothing else about a message is trusted until its token
matches.

Ko-fi's field names (the tier's name, the kind of payment, the Discord account) come from published
descriptions of the webhook, and the **Discord account** field has not been checked against a live
payload from Ko-fi. If supporters you know are in your server show up as **Not matched**, the
[matching](#matching-a-supporter-to-their-discord-account) below still works.

## Tiers and roles

On the **Supporters** page, each tier has a **Discord role** and a **VRChat group role**. Either can
be left empty.

- A tier appears by itself the first time a payment names it, with no roles. Pick its roles to start
  giving them. You can also add a tier by hand with the name Ko-fi uses for it.
- Changing a tier's roles applies to everyone on it within a minute.
- A tier cannot be deleted while paid-up supporters are on it.

Only a **monthly** payment counts. A one-off donation, a shop order or a commission is answered and
ignored.

## The three-day rule

Ko-fi sends nothing when a monthly payment stops. So Modbot counts instead. A payment covers a month.
If the next one has not come **3 days after that month is up**, the supporter's state becomes **Ended**
and Modbot takes back the roles it gave. The page shows **Roles end** for each supporter. A payment that
arrives late, after the roles came off, gives them back.

Modbot only takes back roles it gave itself. A role you gave by hand is never touched.

## Matching a supporter to their Discord account

Roles need a Discord account. Modbot finds it two ways:

- **Ko-fi's Discord link.** A supporter who connected their Discord account in Ko-fi is matched as soon
  as they pay.
- **The code on their receipt.** Anybody else shows as **Not matched**. They give staff the
  **transaction id** from their Ko-fi receipt. Type it into the search box on the **Supporters** page
  to find them, press **Match**, search for their name in the Discord server and press **Match**
  again.

The VRChat group role goes to the VRChat account linked to their Discord account (see
[Account links](/discord/account-links)). A supporter with no linked VRChat account shows
**No linked VRChat**; their Discord role is given, and the VRChat role follows when they link.

## The thank-you message

The message is a direct message on Discord, sent once, the first time a supporter has a Discord
account and a tier. It is set in **Settings**, **Ko-fi**. `{name}` becomes their name and `{tier}`
the tier's name. Leave it empty to send none. A supporter who changes tier is thanked again. If
somebody does not accept direct messages from the server, the page shows that on their row.

## Permissions

Giving a Discord role needs the **Manage Roles** permission, and the bot's own role must sit above the
roles it gives. The VRChat side uses Modbot's own VRChat login, which needs the group permission to give
and take away roles; a refusal from VRChat is shown on the supporter's row. The **Bot invite link** asks for Manage Roles while
Ko-fi is on. A bot added earlier has to be added again from the new link.

A role the bot cannot give is marked in the picker, and a refusal is shown on the supporter's row.

## What Modbot keeps

For each supporter: the name they paid under, their tier, their Discord account, when they paid, which
roles Modbot gave, and the transaction ids of their payments. Their **email address is never stored**:
Modbot keeps only a one-way fingerprint of it, to tell one person's payments from another's. Nothing
is sent to Modbot Cloud. A [purge](/moderation/purge) deletes the supporter matched to that Discord
account, with their payments; no retention window touches it.
