---
title: Integrations
description: Every outside service Modbot connects to, apart from VRChat, Discord and your AI provider.
---

- **[Twitch](/integrations/twitch)** lets Modbot see when your group's channel is live and post
  about it.
- **[Ko-fi](/integrations/ko-fi)** gives supporters a Discord role and a VRChat group role for their
  tier, thanks them, and takes the roles back when a monthly payment stops.
- **[Patreon](/integrations/patreon)** does the same for patrons, and takes the roles back when a pledge
  ends or a charge is declined.

Google Calendar and Bluesky are set up from the moderation pages: see
[Google Calendar](/moderation/google-calendar) and [Bluesky](/moderation/bluesky).
