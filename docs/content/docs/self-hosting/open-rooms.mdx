---
title: Open rooms
description: Listing your group's rooms that anyone can join on modbot.co, and how to turn it off.
---

Modbot can tell Modbot Cloud which of your group's rooms are **open to everyone**, so they appear on
[modbot.co/rooms](https://modbot.co/rooms) alongside every other group that has this on. It is a way
for people to find your events without being in your Discord.

It is **on** when you install Modbot.

## What is sent

| | |
|---|---|
| Your group | Its VRChat id, its name, its icon and its banner |
| Each open public room | The world's id, name and picture; the VRChat join link; the region; when the room opened |

## What is not sent

- **Any room that is not open to everyone.** A room set to group members only, or to members and
  their friends, is filtered out before the report is built. Only `groupAccessType(public)` is sent,
  and a room whose access type VRChat did not state is treated as not public.
- **Any count of people.** No head count, no member count. The report has no field for one.
- **Anybody's name or id.** No members, no moderators, no visitors.
- **Your server's address**, and anything at all from your moderation records.

## How often

Every five minutes, and again the moment a room opens or closes.

Each report replaces the whole list, so:

- A room that closes is gone from the page on the next report.
- A Modbot that stops reporting — switched off, redeployed, offline — has its rooms dropped after
  twenty minutes, and is forgotten entirely after seven days.

## Turning it off

**Settings → Integrations → Modbot Cloud**, the switch **List this group's public rooms on
modbot.co**.

Turning it off asks Cloud to drop what it already has, so the group leaves the page straight away
rather than waiting to be dropped.

Setting `MODBOT_CLOUD_DISABLED=1` stops it too, along with everything else this server would send to
Modbot Cloud. See [Environment variables](/self-hosting/environment-variables).

A public demo (`MODBOT_DEMO`) never reports anything: its rooms are made up.

## Running your own

The whole path is in this repository, so you can run it end to end yourself.

- `MODBOT_CLOUD_ENDPOINT` on your Modbot points it at your own Cloud.
- Your Cloud takes reports at `PUT /api/v1/public-rooms` and serves them at
  `GET /api/v1/public-rooms`, which needs `ROOMS_API_KEY` (or `ROOT_API_KEY`).
- Your landing page reads that feed with `MODBOT_CLOUD_PROXY_URL` and `MODBOT_CLOUD_API_KEY`, holds
  the answer for a minute, and serves it to browsers at `/api/rooms`. The key stays on the server.

A Modbot claims its place on Cloud with an id and a secret it makes up on its first report. Cloud
keeps only a hash of the secret, and one group can only be reported by one Modbot at a time.
