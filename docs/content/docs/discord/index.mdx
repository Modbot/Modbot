title: Discord

---

## Set up

- **[Setting up the bot](/discord/bot-setup)** creates the bot and connects it to Modbot.
- **[Account linking](/discord/account-linking)** lets members link their Discord and VRChat accounts.
- **[Acting from Discord](/discord/acting-from-discord)** lets staff look people up and act on them
  from Discord.

## Posts in your server

- **[Event channels and filters](/discord/event-channels)** send bans, kicks, joins and other events
  to the channels you choose.
- **[Instance announcements](/discord/instance-announcements)** post a card for each instance your
  group opens.
- **[Event recaps](/discord/event-recaps)** post a summary once an event's instance has closed.
- **[Birthdays](/discord/birthdays)** post the day's birthdays and give a role for the day.
- **[Member milestones](/discord/member-milestones)** count the events each person came to and
  mark the moments that matter.
- **[Terms and rules](/discord/terms-and-rules)** let members look up your community's words and
  rules with `/term` and `/rule`.

## New and existing members

- **[Join gate](/discord/join-gate)** lets new members in once they have agreed to your rules.
- **[Welcome steps](/discord/welcome-steps)** send each new member their next steps.
- **[Role menus](/discord/role-menus)** let members pick their own roles.
- **[18+ check](/discord/age-check)** marks a member 18+ verified.
- **[Tickets](/discord/tickets)** let members open a private thread with staff.
- **[Gallery pictures](/discord/gallery-pictures)** let members post pictures that staff add to the
  group's gallery.

## Keeping Discord and VRChat in step

- **[Role and ban sync](/discord/role-and-ban-sync)** keeps VRChat group roles and Discord roles in
  step and copies bans between the two.
- **[Modbot roles from Discord](/discord/staff-roles)** gives staff Modbot permissions from their
  Discord roles.
- **[Roles from lists](/discord/roles-from-lists)** gives a Discord role to everybody in a saved list.

## Keeping the server safe and tidy

- **[Permission watch](/discord/permission-watch)** tells you when a role or member gains a
  dangerous permission.
- **[Roles and channels](/discord/tidy-up)** list roles nobody holds and channels nobody writes in.

## Your server in numbers

- **[Messages and analytics](/discord/messages-and-analytics)** covers what the bot keeps and the
  charts built from it.
- **[Discord members](/discord/members)** is the server's member list and the popup for each member.
