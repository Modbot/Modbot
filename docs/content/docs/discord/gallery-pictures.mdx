---
title: Gallery pictures
description: Let members post pictures in a Discord channel, and have staff add the good ones to the group's gallery on VRChat.
---

Members post a picture in one Discord channel. Modbot copies it into a queue on the **Gallery** tab of
the **VRChat** page. Staff look at the queue and add a picture to one of the group's galleries on
VRChat, or decline it.

Nothing is collected until you choose the channel.

## Set it up

You need the **Change settings** permission. The bot should be set up first
([Setting up the bot](/discord/bot-setup)).

Open **Settings → Discord** and find the **Gallery pictures** card. Pick the **Picture channel** and
press **Save gallery pictures**. The bot needs to see the channel and read its message history. To
put the tick on a picture once it is added, it also needs **Add Reactions** in that channel.

Messages in a thread under the channel count too. Messages from bots and webhooks are ignored.

## Which pictures are taken

- Only **PNG and JPEG** files. VRChat takes nothing else, so a GIF or a file of any other kind is left
  out. Modbot checks the file itself, not just what Discord calls it.
- Up to **10 MB** each.
- A message with several pictures gives several entries.
- Up to **100** pictures wait at a time. Once the queue is full, new pictures are not kept until
  somebody adds or declines some.

Modbot copies the picture when it is posted, because Discord's own link to it stops working after a
day. It keeps the copy only while the picture is waiting.

## Adding a picture

On the **VRChat** page, open the **Gallery** tab. **Pictures from Discord** lists what is waiting,
oldest first, with who posted it and a link to their message. With **Manage the group gallery**
([roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#manage-the-group-gallery)), choose the
gallery in **Add to** and press **Add**.

Adding is two requests to VRChat: Modbot uploads the picture, then adds it to the gallery. When VRChat
accepts it:

- The picture shows in the gallery. If the gallery needs approval, it shows as **Pending** until a
  group manager approves it on VRChat.
- The [audit log](/moderation/audit-log) records **Gallery image added**, with who added it and who
  posted it.
- Modbot puts a ✅ on the member's message in Discord.
- Modbot's copy of the picture is deleted.

If VRChat refuses at the gallery, the picture stays in the queue with the reason under it, and the
upload is kept. Pressing **Add** again does not upload it a second time.

### What it needs

Uploading is the same switch as every other picture Modbot sends to VRChat: **Upload VRChat
pictures** must be on, under **Settings**, **Modbot's VRChat login**, **Pictures**, and Modbot's VRChat
account must have VRChat+ (see [calendar pictures](/moderation/calendar#the-vrchat-picture)). If
the switch is off, or Modbot knows the account has no VRChat+, **Add** is refused and nothing is sent to
VRChat.

Whether adding to a gallery needs VRChat+ itself is not known. Modbot follows the picture-uploads
switch and the VRChat+ check, which cover the upload.

Modbot's VRChat account also needs **Manage Group Galleries** in the group's roles on VRChat.

VRChat limits how fast Modbot may upload (one a minute), and adding to a gallery shares its limit with
removing an image. If VRChat is limiting, try again later.

## Declining a picture

**Decline** takes the picture out of the queue, after you confirm. Modbot sends nothing to VRChat,
tells the member nothing, and leaves their message in Discord alone. Its copy of the picture is
deleted.

## Removing a member's pictures

[Purging](/moderation/purge) a Discord member deletes every entry they posted, waiting or
decided.
