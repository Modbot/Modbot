---
title: Modbot roles from Discord
description: Let a Discord role give a Modbot role, so staff get and lose their Modbot permissions when they get and lose their Discord staff roles.
---

If your team already decides who is staff with Discord roles, Modbot can follow them. You say which
Discord role gives which Modbot role, and from then on somebody given that Discord role gets the
Modbot role within about a minute, and loses it within about a minute of losing the Discord role.

Nothing happens until you set it up. There are no mappings until you make one, and every mapping
names one Discord role, one Modbot role and which way it works. A role you have not mapped is never
looked at.

## Before you start

You need both **Manage roles** and **Manage users**. A mapping changes who holds a role, so it asks
for what changing somebody's roles by hand asks for.

The [Discord bot](/discord/bot-setup) must be in your server. It only needs to read the member list,
which it already does, unless you use **Both ways** (below).

## Who it reaches

Only staff accounts that already exist and have **proven their Discord account** from their account
page (**Connect Discord**, see [Staff accounts](/accounts/staff-accounts#email-and-discord-account)).
Everybody else is left alone:

- A Discord account that was only typed in, not proven, never counts here, not even before
  1 November 2026.
- **Administrators are never touched**, and nor is the owner. A Discord role can't give
  Administrator, or any role that includes it.
- Disabled accounts are left as they are.
- **Modbot never makes accounts.** Somebody who holds a mapped Discord role and has no Modbot account
  shows up in the preview under **Left alone**, so you know whom to [invite](/accounts/staff-accounts).

## Where to set it up

The same list, in two places. A mapping made in one shows in the other.

- **Settings → Discord → Modbot roles from Discord**: every mapping, the switch, and **Apply**.
- **Settings → People and roles → Roles**: open a role, and its **Discord roles** are listed under
  its permissions.

## Add a mapping

<Steps>

<Step>

### Pick the roles

Pick a **Discord role** and, on the Settings card, a **Modbot role**. @everyone and roles that
belong to bots can't be used. One Discord role gives one Modbot role, but several Discord roles can
give the same Modbot role, and holding any of them is enough. Fourteen department roles can all give
**Moderator**.

You can only map a Modbot role that is below your own highest role and allows nothing you can't do
yourself: the same rule as [giving somebody a role by hand](/accounts/roles-and-permissions#who-can-give-what).

</Step>

<Step>

### Choose which way it works

| Which way | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Discord decides** | The Discord role is the answer. Modbot gives and takes the Modbot role to match, and never changes anything in Discord. |
| **Both ways** | As above, and giving or taking the Modbot role in Modbot gives or takes the Discord role too. |

</Step>

<Step>

### Look at who would change, then save

**Add** shows **Who would change** before anything is saved: who would get the role, who would lose
it, and whether they had it from Discord or were given it by hand. Press **Save**, or **Cancel** to
back out. Changing which way a mapping works shows the same list.

</Step>

<Step>

### Switch it on

**Give Modbot roles from Discord roles** is off to begin with. Turning it on shows the list once
more, for every mapping, then **Turn on**. While it is off nothing is given or taken, and on the
Roles page the mappings show **Off**.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What happens

| They… | Modbot… |
| --- | --- |
| get a mapped Discord role | gives the Modbot role |
| lose it (and hold no other Discord role that gives the same Modbot role) | takes the Modbot role away; the account stays, even with no role left |
| leave the Discord server | takes the Modbot role away |
| take their Discord account off their Modbot account | takes away every role a mapping gave them |
| hold the Modbot role from before, given by hand, but not the Discord role | takes it away: Discord decides |

On an account a mapping reaches, a role that follows Discord can't be given or taken by hand on the
**Users** page: its box is greyed out. A **Both ways** role can, and the change goes to Discord.

**Removing a mapping takes nothing away.** The roles it gave stay as ordinary roles.

A change shows on the person's next click; they don't need to sign in again.

## Both ways

The bot needs **Manage roles** in your server, and its own role must be above the Discord role.
When it can't give the role, the mapping says **Not set up**, and until you fix that it works as
**Discord decides**: changes in Discord still reach Modbot, and the role can't be changed by hand in
Modbot.

A **Both ways** Modbot role can have only one Discord role, so Modbot knows which one to give.

Whichever side changed is copied to the other, and a change Modbot made itself never bounces back.
The first time a mapping is checked, whichever side holds the role gives it to the other.

## Stopped: too many at once

If one check would take roles away from more than five accounts at once, or from more than half of
the accounts it reaches (and at least three), Modbot takes nothing, the card says **Stopped**, and
**Apply** appears. This catches a Discord role deleted by mistake, or the bot losing sight of the
member list. Press **Apply** to see the list, then **Apply** again to carry it out.

Giving roles is never stopped this way.

Modbot also waits after the bot reconnects until it has read the member list again, so it never acts
on roles from before the bot went away.

## What goes in the audit log

In the operational log:

- **Roles changed**, with no person as the one who did it, saying Modbot changed the roles to match
  Discord.
- **Discord role given a Modbot role** and **Discord role no longer gives a Modbot role**, saying
  who made or removed the mapping.
- **Roles from Discord stopped**, once, when too many would have lost a role at once.
- Turning the switch on or off, like any other setting.

For **Both ways**, a Discord role Modbot gives or takes is recorded against the person like a
[role sync](/discord/role-and-ban-sync) copy, and a refusal from Discord as a failed copy.
