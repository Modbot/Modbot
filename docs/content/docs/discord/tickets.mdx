---
title: Tickets
description: Let members press a button to open a private thread with staff. Staff or the member close it, and a text transcript goes to a channel of your choice.
---

A ticket is a private thread. A member presses a button on a ticket panel, Modbot opens a thread that
only they, the staff role you picked and people who can manage threads can see, and the opening
message you wrote is the first thing in it. When the ticket is closed, Modbot posts its transcript in
a channel you choose.

Everything is off until you add a ticket type and post a panel.

## Set it up

You need the **Change settings** permission. The bot should be set up first
([Setting up the bot](/discord/bot-setup)).

Open **Settings → Discord** and find the **Tickets** card.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Button** | The words on the panel's button, and the start of the thread's name. Up to 80 characters. |
| **Staff role** | The role asked into every ticket of this type. It may close and delete them. |
| **Opening message** | The first message in the ticket. Up to 1,500 characters. |
| **Private images** | Turn on for a type where members may post photos or documents Modbot must not keep, such as an ID for an age check. |
| **Transcript channel** | Where a closed ticket's transcript goes. Leave it empty to keep no transcript. |

You can add up to five ticket types. Each saves on its own with its own **Save**; the arrows change
the order of the buttons, and **Delete** removes a type.

Then post the panel: pick a **Channel**, write the **Text**, and press **Post panel**. The panel is
one message with a button for each ticket type, in the order shown. Tickets open as threads under that
channel. Post the panel again after you change the types, since an old panel keeps the buttons it was
posted with.

## What the bot needs

In the channel the panel is in:

- View Channel, Send Messages and Read Message History
- **Create Private Threads**, **Send Messages in Threads** and **Manage Threads**

In the transcript channel: View Channel, Send Messages, Embed Links and Attach Files.

The staff role is mentioned in the first message of a ticket, which is what brings its members into
the thread. So the role needs to be mentionable, or the bot needs **Mention All Roles**. The role
picker marks roles the bot cannot mention.

## Opening, closing and deleting

- A member has **one open ticket of each type** at a time. Pressing the button again links to the one
  they have.
- **Close** can be pressed by the member who opened the ticket and by staff. Modbot posts the
  transcript first and only then locks and archives the thread. If the transcript cannot be read or
  posted, the ticket stays open and says why, so a ticket is never closed with its record lost. With no
  transcript channel set, it closes and says no transcript was kept.
- **Delete** is for staff only. It removes the thread and posts no transcript.
- Staff means someone with the type's staff role, or anyone who can manage threads. If you delete a
  ticket type, its open tickets stay open and only people who can manage threads can close or delete
  them.
- A closed ticket is not reopened.

## The transcript

The transcript is a text file posted in the transcript channel, with a card naming the ticket type, who
opened it, who closed it and the number of messages. It lists every message in the thread, oldest
first, with the time and author. Attachments are listed by name and link.

Modbot reads message text through the Message Content intent. If your server does not give it, the
transcript says at the top that the text of the messages is missing.

## Private images

For a type with **Private images** on:

- The transcript writes `[image removed]` where a message had an attachment, and never names or links
  it.
- Modbot keeps no attachment names or addresses from those tickets in its own copy of messages either.
- The member's photos or documents stay in the Discord thread only.

Tickets already open keep the rule they were opened with if you change the switch later.

## What Modbot keeps

For each ticket Modbot keeps the ticket type's name, the member's Discord id and name, the thread, and
when it was opened, closed or deleted and by whom. The audit log shows each setting change, and the
fact log shows every ticket opened, closed and deleted.
