---
title: Setting up the bot
description: Create a Discord bot, switch on the intents it needs, invite it to your server and connect it to Modbot.
---

Modbot can run a Discord bot for your community's server. Once it is connected it:

- posts events to the Discord channels you choose ([Event channels](/discord/event-channels));
- announces your group's open instances in a channel ([Instance announcements](/discord/instance-announcements));
- lets members link their Discord and VRChat accounts, and gives linked members a role ([Account linking](/discord/account-linking));
- keeps a copy of the server's messages, members and voice time, for the **Discord members** list and the **Discord** analytics page ([Messages and analytics](/discord/messages-and-analytics));
- answers five slash commands, and a sixth for members when you switch it on (see [Slash commands](#slash-commands));
- lets moderators look people up, write notes, and kick, ban or answer join requests from right-click menus and buttons under cards ([Acting from Discord](/discord/acting-from-discord)).

The bot is optional. Until you give Modbot a token and a server id, the bot does not start and the
rest of Modbot works as normal.

You need the **Change settings** permission to set the bot up.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Create the application

1. Open the [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications) and click
   **New Application**. Any name works; "Modbot" is fine.
2. Open **Bot** and click **Reset Token**. Copy the token and keep it somewhere safe for a
   moment. Discord shows it only once.

</Step>

<Step>

### Switch on the privileged intents

An *intent* is a kind of update the bot asks Discord to send it. Most are open to every bot. Two
of the ones Modbot asks for are *privileged*, which means you have to switch them on yourself.

Still under **Bot**, find **Privileged Gateway Intents** and switch on:

- **Server Members Intent**
- **Message Content Intent**

Leave **Presence Intent** off. Modbot does not ask for it.

</Step>

<Step>

### Invite the bot to your server

The easiest way is the **Bot invite link** Modbot builds for you. It appears on the
**Account linking** card under **Settings → Discord** once you have saved the **Client id**
there (the Developer Portal shows the client id under **OAuth2**). It asks for exactly the
permissions the features you have set up need. See [Account linking](/discord/account-linking)
if you want to fill in that card now.

You can also build the link yourself under **OAuth2 → URL Generator**:

- Scopes: **bot** and **applications.commands**. Without **applications.commands** the bot
  connects, but no slash commands appear.
- Bot permissions: see [Permissions the bot needs](#permissions-the-bot-needs) below.

Open the link, pick your server and confirm. Do not give the bot **Administrator**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Find your server id

In Discord, turn on **Developer Mode** (User Settings → Advanced). Then right-click your server's
icon and choose **Copy Server ID**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Enter the token and server id in Modbot

You can do this in the [setup wizard](/setup-wizard) or later in Settings.

- **In the setup wizard**, the step **Public address, Discord and email** has a **Discord bot**
  section with **Bot token** and **Server ID**.
- **In Settings**, open **Settings → Discord**. On the **Bot** card, fill in **Bot token** and
  **Server id**, then press **Save**.

The token is stored encrypted and is never shown again. Once one is saved, the **Bot** line on
the card says **Token stored**. When you change something else later, leave **Bot token** empty:
an empty field keeps the token you already have.

Also set a **Public address** under **Settings → Server**. Modbot builds every link it
sends to Discord from that address and nothing else. Without it, event posts have no link back to
Modbot, and account linking cannot work.

</Step>

</Steps>

Modbot looks at its Discord settings every ten seconds, so the bot connects within a few seconds
of saving. Nothing needs restarting, now or when you change these settings later.

## Intents the bot asks for

These are all the intents Modbot requests, and what each one is for.

| Intent | Switch in the Developer Portal | What Modbot uses it for |
|---|---|---|
| Servers | none | The server's name, channels and roles, and slash commands. |
| Server messages | none | Messages as they are sent, edited and deleted. |
| **Message Content Intent** | yes | The text of those messages. |
| **Server Members Intent** | yes | The member list, joins and leaves, nickname and role changes, timeouts, and the message that asks new joiners to link their accounts. |
| Voice states | none | Who joins, leaves or moves between voice channels, for voice time. |
| Bans and moderation | none | Bans and unbans, and new entries in the server's audit log as they happen. |

### If a privileged intent is off

Discord refuses the whole connection when a bot asks for a privileged intent that is switched off,
without saying which one. Modbot works out which ones are off, then connects again **without
them**, so posting events and the slash commands keep working.

While that is happening, the **Discord bot** card on the **Health** page shows
**Intents off in the Developer Portal:** followed by the switches to turn on.

What you lose without each one:

- **Without Message Content Intent**, messages are still stored, but with no text.
- **Without Server Members Intent**, Modbot does not read the member list or see people join and
  leave, and new joiners are not asked to link their accounts.

After you switch an intent on, Modbot asks for it again the next time Modbot restarts, or when you
change the **Bot token**, the **Server id**, or the **Prompt new joiners to link their VRChat
account** switch.

## Permissions the bot needs

These are Discord permissions. You give them when you invite the bot, or later in your server's
role and channel settings.

| Permission | Needed for |
|---|---|
| **View Channels** | Everything the bot reads or posts. |
| **Send Messages** | Event posts, instance announcements, calendar posts, insights, and the backup channel for account linking. |
| **Embed Links** | Event posts, instance announcements and insights, which are sent as cards. |
| **Read Message History** | Reading back each channel's older messages, and updating instance announcement cards. |
| **View Audit Log** | Knowing *who* banned, kicked, timed out or changed somebody's roles, and catching up on what happened while the bot was offline. |
| **Manage Roles** | Only if you set a **Linked role** or an **18+ role** for account linking, or turn on the [join gate](/discord/join-gate). The bot's own role must also sit above those roles and the gate's **Member role**. |
| **Kick Members** | Only if the [join gate](/discord/join-gate) removes people who do not finish (**Remove after** is not **Never**). |
| **Manage Server** | Only if the [join gate](/discord/join-gate) may pause your server's invites (**Allow pausing invites**). |
| **Manage Events** | Only if a [calendar](/moderation/calendar) event has **Discord event** ticked. The invite link Modbot builds does not ask for it. |
| **Manage Messages** and **Timeout Members** | Only if you set [AutoMod rules](/moderation/automod) to delete messages or time people out. The invite link Modbot builds does not ask for these. |

The **Bot invite link** asks for View Channels, Send Messages, Embed Links, Read Message History and
View Audit Log. It adds Manage Roles once a linked role or 18+ role is saved or the join gate is
turned on, Kick Members once the join gate removes people, and Manage Server once it may pause
invites. If you turn on a
feature later that needs more, open the link again to give the bot the new permission.

Whenever you pick a channel in **Settings → Discord**, a channel where the bot lacks a permission
that setting needs is marked with what is missing, for example `Missing Send Messages, Embed Links`.
Fix it in Discord's channel settings, or pick another channel. The channel and role lists come from
the bot, so they are empty until it has connected once.

## Checking that it is connected

Open **Health** from the **Discord** row at the bottom of the sidebar. It needs
**See the operational log**. The
**Discord bot** card shows one of these:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **not set up** | No token or no server id is saved. This is not a fault. |
| **connecting** | Signing in to Discord. |
| **working** | Connected. It also shows how long it has been connected and how many commands were registered: the slash commands and the right-click menus. |
| **reconnecting** | The connection dropped and Modbot is getting it back. This usually clears within a minute. |
| **stopped — needs you** | Discord refused to let the bot sign in, usually because the token is wrong. The bot waits until you change the settings. |

The card also shows how many posts have gone to Discord since Modbot last started, each event
channel with a problem, how far reading back message history has got, and the last problem the bot
ran into. See [Checking a deployment](/self-hosting/health) for the rest of that page.

You can also run `/modbot` in Discord.

## Slash commands

The bot registers its commands on your server only. Every reply is visible only to the person who
ran the command, except `/events`, which answers in public unless the member asks otherwise. One
Discord bot can serve several Modbots: each one answers only in its own server.

| Command | What it does | Who can use it |
|---|---|---|
| `/lookup user: discord:` | Looks up one person in what Modbot has stored. Fill in one of the two: `user` takes a VRChat display name or id, and suggests names from Modbot's records as you type; `discord` takes a member picked from Discord's list. If the person [linked their accounts](/discord/account-linking), the reply covers both. It shows their VRChat profile as VRChat shows it — their name, their picture, their banner, and the group they represent — with whether they have been seen as 18+ verified and when the profile was last refreshed; their Discord account; whether they are banned in the group and on Discord (with the reason Discord holds), or timed out on Discord; how many bans, kicks and warns in the group; how many bans, kicks and timeouts on Discord; how many times Modbot's rules flagged them; and their latest moderation events, with the reason where there is one. Notes are included for people with **See the audit log**, and join requests for people with **See join requests**. Somebody known only on Discord gets a reply headed by their Discord name and picture. Nothing is fetched from VRChat or Discord. If several VRChat names match, it lists them with their ids so you can run it again with one. | **See profiles** |
| `/recent count:` | The latest moderation events, in the group and on Discord, newest first. Every name opens that person in Modbot. `count` is 1 to 25; 10 if you leave it out. | **See the audit log** |
| `/modbot` | Whether the bot is connected, whether any channel is set to receive events, and the web app's address. | Anyone who connected Discord to their Modbot account |
| `/note text: member: vrchat:` | Writes a note about one person, the same note as in the web app. `text` is 1 to 2,000 characters. Fill in one of the other two: `member` takes a member picked from Discord's list, and `vrchat` takes a VRChat display name or id and suggests names from Modbot's records as you type. The reply, seen only by you, is "Note saved." with an **Open in Modbot** button on their profile. If several VRChat names match, it lists them with their ids so you can run it again with one. Without **See profiles**, `vrchat` takes an exact id only: no names are suggested or searched, and no reply names the person. | **Write notes**, and a VRChat account linked to your Modbot account |
| `/watch reason: for: member: vrchat: follow-up:` | Starts watching one person, the same watch as the **Watch** button in the web app. `reason` is 1 to 200 characters. `for` is **1 day**, **1 week**, **30 days** or **Until stopped**. `follow-up` is **None**, **Tomorrow** or **In a week**, and is optional. Fill in one of `member` and `vrchat`, as for `/note`. The reply, seen only by you, says who you are watching and until when, with an **Open in Modbot** button. If somebody is already watching that person it says so. Names work as for `/note`. | **Write notes**, and a VRChat account linked to your Modbot account |
| `/live` | The group's open instances right now, one card each: the world, how many people are in (such as `12/40`), and when it opened. Names, up to 20 and then "and N more", appear only while a moderator's Companion App is watching that instance, the same rule as the Live page; otherwise you see the count only. No flags and no watched tags. An **Open Live** button opens the Live page. Seen only by you. | **See live instances** |
| `/ban member: vrchat:` | Bans one person, with the same meaning as **Ban** in the web app. Fill in one of the two: `member` takes a member picked from Discord's list, and `vrchat` takes a VRChat display name or id and suggests names from Modbot's records as you type. A VRChat person, or a member who has [linked their accounts](/discord/account-linking), is banned from the VRChat group, and their linked Discord account is banned with them, as for any ban made in Modbot. A member with no VRChat link is banned from the Discord server only. Nothing is sent until you have sent the form (the group's reasons and a note for a group ban; a reason and **Delete their messages from** — **None**, **1 day** or **7 days** — for a Discord ban) and pressed the red button on the confirmation. Never the bot, the server's owner or a Discord account linked to a Modbot staff account. Without **See profiles**, `vrchat` takes an exact id only: no names are suggested or searched, and no reply names the person. See [Ban and kick](/discord/acting-from-discord#ban-and-kick). | **Ban** for a VRChat person or a linked member; **Ban on Discord** for a member with no VRChat link; and a VRChat account linked to your Modbot account |
| `/kick member: vrchat: from:` | Removes one person. Name them as for `/ban`. A Discord member is removed from the Discord server and a VRChat person is kicked from the group, unless `from` says otherwise: **Discord server**, **VRChat group** or **Both**, for a person with both accounts linked. The form and the confirmation work as for `/ban`; **Both** is one confirmation that acts in both places. Seen only by you. | **Kick** for the group; **Remove from Discord** for the Discord server; both for **Both**; and a VRChat account linked to your Modbot account |
| `/gate waiting`, `/gate let-in member:`, `/gate hold`, `/gate lift` | The [join gate](/discord/join-gate) from Discord. `waiting` lists up to 10 people at the gate, oldest first, with when they joined and a **Let in** button for each; if more are waiting it says "and N more". `let-in` takes a member picked from Discord's list and gives them the member role now, whatever steps they have left. `hold` holds new joiners at once, with nothing to confirm first, and the reply has a **Lift hold** button; `lift` lifts the hold. They do what the buttons on the [join gate](/discord/join-gate) page and under a join spike alert do, and write the same entries to the audit log under the name of your Modbot account. Seen only by you. | **Manage the join gate**, and a VRChat account linked to your Modbot account; `waiting` also needs **See members**, as the page does |
| `/event open event:`, `/event cancel-date event: date: say-so:` | The calendar from Discord, for event hosts. `event` suggests your upcoming events as you type. `open` opens the event's instance now, with the same refusals as **Open now** on the calendar page (for example "It is too early to open the instance."). `cancel-date` suggests the event's next ten dates, asks "Cancel **Title** on *time*?" with **Cancel this date** and **Keep it**, and cancels only that date when you press the red button. For an event that does not repeat, its only date is the whole event, and the button says **Cancel the event**. `say-so` yes also posts, once, that it is cancelled in the event's channel, as the calendar page's tick does; an event with no channel refuses it. The date is cancelled once however many times the button is pressed, and the audit log has the same entries as for the calendar page, under the name of your Modbot account. Suggestions need **See calendar** as well, as the calendar page does. Seen only by you. | **Manage calendar**, and a VRChat account linked to your Modbot account |
| `/post new channel:`, `/post list` | Marketing posts from Discord, for event hosts. `new` takes a channel picked from Discord's list and opens a form with a **Title** (optional) and the **Text**. You then see a private preview of the message exactly as it will be posted, with **Post now** and **Cancel**. **Post now** saves a post that is due now, the same as **Post now** on the Marketing tab, and Modbot's Discord sender posts it within about twenty seconds: it tries once, and a press, a second press or a restart can never make a second message. The post then shows on the Marketing tab like any other. It is refused, and nothing is saved, while posting is paused, Discord posts are switched off or Discord is not set up. `list` shows the next ten posts waiting to go out, soonest first, and the posts that failed, each with its time. Discord only; VRChat and Bluesky are chosen on the Marketing tab. Seen only by you. | `new`: **Manage posts**, and a VRChat account linked to your Modbot account. `list`: **See posts** |
| `/events private:` | The next five dates in the next 14 days, soonest first: the event's name, its time in each reader's own time zone, how long until it starts, the world's name, and **Open now** while its instance is open. Only events already published in this Discord, as a Discord event or a post in a channel; never a draft, a cancelled event or a deleted one. A **Join** button (at most five) opens VRChat for an event anyone can join whose instance is open; events only members can join have none. The answer is public unless `private` is yes. If a public `/events` was already posted in that channel in the last minute, the answer is private instead. Each person can ask five times a minute. Only there when **/events** is on the **Commands** card. | Anyone in the server |
| `/remindme event: before:` | Sends you one direct message before an event, so you don't miss it. Pick the event from the list under `event`, which suggests only the events `/events` would show. `before` is **15 minutes**, **1 hour** (if you leave it out) or **1 day**. Running it with an event is the whole opt-in: Modbot sends you a confirmation direct message at once, with a **Stop** button, and the reply you see says when the reminder will come. If your direct messages are closed, it tells you so and keeps nothing. Run it with no event to list your reminders, each with a **Stop** button. The reminder says "**Title** starts" and the time in your own time zone, and has a **Join** link only if anyone can join the event and its instance is open. A repeating event is reminded for its next date only. The reminder follows a date that moves, is dropped if the date or event is cancelled, and is not sent if it is more than 15 minutes late. You can have one waiting reminder per event date and ten in all. Only there when **/remindme** is on the **Commands** card; see [What Modbot keeps for reminders](#what-modbot-keeps-for-reminders). | Anyone in the server |
| `/link` | A **Link VRChat account** button that opens the account linking page. | Anyone in the server |
| `/me` | What Modbot holds about the member who ran it, with **What Modbot keeps** and **Ask to delete my data** buttons. Only there when **/me** is on the **Commands** card; see [What members can see: /me](/discord/account-linking#what-members-can-see-me). | Anyone in the server |
| `/verify code:` | Connects the Discord account that runs it to a Modbot account, with the code from **Connect with a code** on that account's **Your account** page. See [Letting moderators use the commands](#letting-moderators-use-the-commands). | Anyone in the server |
| `/help` | The commands the person who ran it can use, and what each does. A member who has not connected Discord to a Modbot account sees `/link`, `/me`, `/events`, `/remindme`, `/verify` and `/help` only; a moderator also sees the commands their Modbot permissions let them run. | Anyone in the server |

### Switching commands on and off

**Settings → Discord → Commands** has one switch for every command and right-click menu, labelled with its
name. A command that is off is not registered: nobody sees it in Discord. Press
**Save commands**; the bot registers the new list within a few seconds, without reconnecting.
Everything is on except `/me`, `/events` and `/remindme`, which stay off until you turn them on. The commands you never touched
keep following their default if a later version changes it. Nothing can be saved in the demo.

### What Modbot keeps for reminders

`/remindme` is off until you turn it on. Each reminder is one row in Modbot's database: the member's Discord
id, the event, the date it is about, how long before it was asked for, when it is due and its state
(waiting, sending, sent, stopped, skipped or failed), with the times they changed. No name and no message text.
Every reminder is deleted in the end: sent, stopped, skipped and failed ones 30 days after they last changed,
one stuck while sending after a day, and one nobody handled (the command was off) 30 days after it was due.
Just before it sends, Modbot checks again that the event is still one `/events` would show, and skips the reminder
if it is not, so a message never names an event taken off Discord after you asked. [Purging a person](/moderation/purge) deletes all of theirs. What leaves your server is two direct messages
through Discord to that member: the confirmation, and the reminder. Turning `/remindme` off stops the
reminders being sent too; **Stop** keeps working. Reminders are separate from
[event invites](/moderation/calendar#inviting-people): asking for one signs nobody up for the other, and
stopping event invites under `/me` does not stop a reminder. Each member can run `/remindme` five times a minute.

### Who sees the commands in Discord

`/lookup`, `/recent`, `/modbot`, `/note`, `/watch`, `/live`, `/ban`, `/kick` and `/gate` are shown only to members who may time others out (Discord's
**Timeout Members** permission). `/event` and `/post` are shown only to members who may manage events
(**Manage Events**), which event hosts usually have and moderators often do not. Everyone else does not see
them in the command list. To change
who sees them, open **Server Settings → Integrations → Modbot** in Discord and pick roles or
members for each command. `/verify` is shown to everyone, because many moderators don't hold
Timeout Members; without a code from a Modbot account it does nothing.

Seeing a command does not let anyone use it: Modbot still checks the person's Modbot account and
permissions every time, as described below.

### Letting moderators use the commands

Every command except `/link`, `/me`, `/events`, `/remindme`, `/verify` and `/help` only answers people Modbot can match to
a Modbot account. Each moderator connects their own Discord account from their **Your account**
page in Modbot, under **Your Discord account**, in one of two ways. Either way, the card then says
**Connected as** and their Discord name. Nobody can do this for somebody else, and nobody can type
an id in: Modbot knows the Discord account is theirs because Discord told it.

- **Connect with a code** works once the bot is set up. The card shows a code such as `K7P-42Q`,
  with **Copy**, and the command to run: `/verify K7P-42Q`. Run `/verify` in your Discord server and
  paste the code into `code`. Discord tells Modbot which account ran the command, and the bot
  replies "Your Discord account is now connected to Modbot." The card changes by itself within a few
  seconds. A code works once, for 15 minutes; **New code** replaces it. A wrong or old code gets
  "That code isn't right or has expired." Each Discord account can try five codes a minute, and a
  code stops working after five wrong tries at it.
- **Connect Discord** signs in to Discord: Discord asks them to sign in and allow Modbot to see who
  they are, then sends them back. It uses the same Discord application as
  [Account linking](/discord/account-linking), so it is only offered once the **Account linking**
  card's client id, client secret and redirect address are saved.

If the bot isn't set up either, **Connect Discord** stays, and pressing it says "Discord sign-in is
not set up on this server."

A Discord account can be connected to one Modbot account. If it is already connected to another
one, nothing changes: the account page says so, or `/verify` replies "This Discord account is
connected to another Modbot account." **Disconnect** on the same card takes it off; the bot then
treats that Discord account as nobody.

If someone without a connected Modbot account runs a command, the bot replies "Connect Discord on
your account page in Modbot first." If they lack the permission, the reply names the permission they
need. Permissions come from the person's Modbot roles, the same as in the web app. See
[Roles and permissions](/accounts/roles-and-permissions).

<Callout type="info">
Before connecting existed, moderators typed a **Discord user id** into their account, and an
administrator could type one for them. Modbot never checked those. An account that still has one
shows it as **Not proven**, with the day it stops working: the bot answers it, and sends direct
messages to it, until **1 November 2026**, and then never again. Connecting before then, with
**Connect with a code** or **Connect Discord**, replaces it. Where two accounts typed the same id,
the bot answers neither.
</Callout>

Every command run, answered or refused, is recorded in the audit log as **Discord command used**.
The names suggested while you type into `/lookup user:`, `/note vrchat:`, `/watch vrchat:`, `/ban vrchat:` and `/kick vrchat:` are not
recorded; the command you then run is, and so is every question you answer and every button you press under `/event` and `/post`. The events and dates suggested under `/event` are not recorded either. Names are only suggested to people that command would answer, and for every one but `/lookup` only to people who also have **See profiles**.

### Right-click menus

The bot also registers two menus, under **Apps** when you right-click a member:
**Look up in Modbot** and **Add a note**. They follow the same rules as the
commands: a connected Modbot account and the matching permission. Only members with Discord's
**Timeout Members** permission see them until a server admin changes that under **Server Settings →
Integrations**. See [Acting from Discord](/discord/acting-from-discord).

## Turning the bot off

Clear **Server id** on the **Bot** card and press **Save**. The bot disconnects within a few
seconds. Your channels and other Discord settings are kept for when you turn it back on.

## If something is wrong

Start with the **Discord bot** card on **Health**.

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **stopped — needs you** with "Discord rejected the bot token." | Reset the token in the Developer Portal and paste the new one into **Bot token**. |
| **Intents off in the Developer Portal:** … | Switch the named intents on under **Bot → Privileged Gateway Intents**. |
| **reconnecting** for a long time | Check whether Discord is having problems. After a few minutes Modbot signs in again from scratch by itself. |
| **connected**, but no slash commands appear in Discord | The bot was invited without the **applications.commands** scope. Invite it again with it. |
| A problem registering the slash commands | Check that **Server id** is your server's id and that the bot is in that server. |
| A moderator does not see `/lookup`, `/recent` or `/modbot` | They lack Discord's **Timeout Members** permission. Show them the commands under **Server Settings → Integrations → Modbot** in Discord; see [Who sees the commands in Discord](#who-sees-the-commands-in-discord). |
| A channel marked **Missing** … or **Removed** | Give the bot the named permissions in that channel, or pick a different channel. |
