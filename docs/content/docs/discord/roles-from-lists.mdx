title: Roles from lists
description: Give a Discord role to everybody in a saved list, such as @Regular for "visited 5 or more times", and take it away again when they leave the list.
---

Pair a [saved list](/moderation/lists) with a Discord role, and everybody in the list who is in your
Discord server gets the role. When somebody stops being in the list, Modbot takes the role away
again. "Visited 5 or more times" can give **@Regular**; "18+ on VRChat" can give **@18+**.

## What Modbot changes in Discord

One thing: whether a member holds a role you paired here. Modbot gives it, and takes it away, and
nothing else. No other role, no nickname, no message.

- **It only takes away a role it gave.** Modbot writes down everybody it gives the role to. Somebody
  who already had the role, or was given it by hand, keeps it whatever the list says.
- **It does not fight your moderators.** If somebody takes the role off a member by hand, Modbot
  does not give it back while they are still in the list.
- **Each change says why in Discord's audit log**: `Modbot: in the list “Regulars”` or
  `Modbot: no longer in the list “Regulars”`.
- **Everything is off until you switch it on**, and each list's role has its own switch too.

## Who gets the role

A Discord role can only go to somebody in your Discord server. A list can hold VRChat accounts,
Discord members, or both:

| Somebody in the list who is | Gets the role? |
| --- | --- |
| In your Discord server | Yes |
| A VRChat account with no [linked](/discord/account-linking) Discord account | No |
| Linked, but not in your Discord server | No |

**Show who would change** counts the last two as **No linked Discord** and **Not in the server**.
Somebody who unlinks their accounts is no longer in a list that asks about their VRChat account, and
loses the role the next minute.

## Which roles a list can give

Lists are for community roles. A list cannot give:

- **@everyone**, or a role that belongs to a bot or an integration;
- a role the bot cannot give: it needs **Manage Roles**, and its own role above that one;
- a role with any staff permission: Administrator, Manage Server, Manage Roles, Manage Channels,
  Manage Messages, Ban Members, Kick Members, Timeout Members, Mention @everyone, @here and All
  Roles, Manage Webhooks, Manage Nicknames, Manage Threads, Manage Events, Manage Expressions, View
  Audit Log, Mute Members, Deafen Members or Move Members;
- a role something else already gives: a role paired with a group role in
  [role sync](/discord/role-and-ban-sync), the linked role and the 18+ role from
  [account linking](/discord/account-linking), or a role another list gives.

If somebody adds a staff permission to a role after you paired it, Modbot stops giving it and says
why on the card.

## Before you start

| To do this | You need |
| --- | --- |
| Set it up, switch it on and off | **Manage role and ban sync** and **Manage settings** |
| See who would change | **See members** and **See profiles** as well |
| **Apply**, when a list stopped | **Run role and ban sync**, with **See members** and **See profiles** |

## Give a role from a list

**Settings → Discord → Roles from lists**. From a list, **Give a Discord role** opens the same card
with the list picked.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Pick the list and the role

Pick a **List** and a **Discord role**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Look first

**Show who would change** lists everybody who would be given the role, and counts who already has it
and who cannot get it. Nothing changes. **Give this role** appears once you have looked.

</Step>

<Step>

### Switch it on

Switch on **Give roles from lists**. The first time, **Save** becomes **Show who would change**:
it lists what every list's role would do, then **Turn on** makes it happen. Modbot checks once a
minute from then on, and changes at most 50 roles a minute.

</Step>

</Steps>

## When a list would take the role from many people

If one check would take the role from more than 25 people at once, or from more than half of the
people who hold it, Modbot changes nothing for that list and the card says **Stopped**. That usually
means the list's rules changed, or something went wrong: it is a chance to look before anybody
loses a role.

Press **Show who would change** on that list, read who would lose the role, then press **Apply**.
Modbot carries on, taking the role from the people you saw. If more people would lose it than you
saw, Apply is refused; look again.

Giving the role is never stopped this way. Look before you switch a list's role on.

## Removing one, or switching it off

Switching a list's role off, or removing it, takes nothing away. Everybody keeps the role they have.
Once it is removed, Modbot forgets who it gave the role to, so it will never take those away.

A list that gives a role cannot be deleted, and changing its rules needs **Manage role and ban sync**
as well as **Manage lists**, because changing the list changes who holds the role.

## Erasing somebody

[Erasing a person](/moderation/purge) also erases Modbot's note that it gave them the role. The role
stays with them in Discord, and Modbot will not take it away. Remove it in Discord if you want it
gone.

## What goes in the audit log

| Entry | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Discord role given for a list** | Modbot gave somebody the role. Names the list and the role. |
| **Discord role taken away for a list** | Modbot took it away, because they left the list. |
| **Role from a list stopped** | A list would have taken its role from many people at once, and is waiting for **Apply**. Operational log. |

Setting up, switching on and off, removing and **Apply** are settings changes, with who did them.
