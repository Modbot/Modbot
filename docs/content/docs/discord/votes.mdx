A vote is a question the bot posts in one of your channels, with a button for each answer. A member
presses a button to vote, and presses another one to change their mind. A vote has no time limit: staff
close it.

Make one with `/vote` in Discord or on the **Votes** page in Modbot. Both do the same thing.

## Make a vote

On the **Votes** page you need the **See posts** permission to look, and **Manage posts** to make and
close a vote. Press **New vote**.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Question** | One line, up to 200 characters. It is the title of the message. |
| **Channel** | Where the vote is posted. The bot needs to see the channel, send messages and embed links in it. |
| **Only for role** | Only people holding this role can vote. Left empty, everybody in the server can. |
| **Options** | One on each line, at least two. Two options may not say the same thing. |
| **Show counts while open** | See [Counts](#counts). Off to start. |
| **Anonymous** | See [Anonymous](#anonymous). On to start. |

In Discord, type `/vote` and pick the **channel**. You can also pick the **only_for** role,
**show_counts** and **anonymous** (yes when you leave it out). Then fill in the form: the question, and the options with one on
each line. `/vote` is for event hosts and needs **Manage posts** and a VRChat account linked to your Modbot
account. It is off until you switch it on in the **Commands** card ([Setting up the bot](/discord/bot-setup)).

The vote is posted the moment you send it. If Discord refuses the message, nothing is saved.

## Buttons or lists

Up to 25 options become buttons. More than that become drop-down lists of 25 choices each, and a vote
can hold up to 125 options. A button holds at most 80 characters.

## How people vote

A member presses an option. Only they see the answer: what they voted for, or that it changed. Pressing
another option changes their vote; pressing the same one again changes nothing. Each person has one
vote.

If the vote is limited to a role and the member does not hold it, they are told which role they need.

## Counts

With **Show counts while open** on, the message shows the number of votes for each option while the vote
is open. Modbot rewrites the message a few seconds after people vote, so a busy vote is updated every
few seconds and not once for every press.

With it off, the message shows no numbers until the vote is closed.

## Anonymous

With **Anonymous** on, nobody sees who picked what, on the Votes page or in Discord.

With it off, the Votes page lists the names under each option. Discord never lists voters: the message
shows counts only.

Modbot keeps every person's pick either way, and not only the totals. That is how each person gets one
vote and can change it. Anonymous decides what is shown, not what is kept. A vote is made one way or the
other and cannot be switched from anonymous afterwards.

## Close a vote

Press **Close vote** on the Votes page, then **Close for good**. The bot rewrites the message with the
result, each option with its votes and share, and the buttons go away. A closed vote takes no more
votes and cannot be opened again.

The vote is closed in Modbot even when Discord refuses to rewrite the message. The page then says why,
and the old message may still show its buttons; pressing them says the vote is closed.

## What Modbot keeps

The question, the options, who made it and when, and each person's pick. Picks are deleted when a
[purge](/moderation/purge) removes the person. Nothing is sent to Modbot Cloud. See
[Privacy](/privacy).
