title: Member milestones
description: Count the events each person came to, mark their first event, when they became a Regular and when they came back on their profile, and optionally post each moment in Discord and give a Regular role.
---

Modbot counts the events each person came to and shows it on their profile. Three moments stand out: the
person's first event, the event that makes them a Regular, and a return after a long time away. Each can also be
posted in a Discord channel, and a Regular can be given a Discord role.

## What counts

An event counted is one date of a [calendar](/moderation/calendar) event that Modbot opened an instance for. A date
is counted once its instance has closed, and a person counts once for each date, however often they joined.

A return is any visit to one of the group's instances, not only an event. The visit counts as a return when the
person had not been seen in the group's instances for the number of days in **Welcome back after days** or more.
A person's first visit is never a return.

Modbot counts only the people in instances where a moderator's Modbot app was present. Somebody who was in the
instance while no moderator's Modbot was there is not counted, so the numbers can be lower than the real turnout.
Counting starts from the oldest history Modbot has, so people are counted for events from before you set this up.

A Regular here means somebody who came to the number of events you set. It is not the same as the
[Regulars page](/analytics#regulars), which looks at weeks.

## On the profile

The **Milestones** panel on a person's profile, for staff with **View audit log**, shows:

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Came to N events** | How many events they came to. |
| **Nth event on a date** | The date of the event that was their first moment. |
| **Regular since a date** | The date of the event that made them a Regular. |
| **Back after N days on a date** | The latest return after a long time away. |

The panel needs no setting and is hidden for somebody with no event and no return. Changing the numbers changes
the lines straight away, with no new count.

## Settings

You need the **Change settings** permission. Open **Settings → Discord** and find the **Member milestones** card.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **First event number** | The event number that is a person's first moment. Starts at 1. |
| **Regular at event number** | The event number that makes a person a Regular. Starts at 5. |
| **Welcome back after days** | How many days away make a visit a return. Starts at 60. |
| **Post channel** | Where the posts go. |
| **Post first events**, **Post new Regulars**, **Post welcome backs** | Which moments get a post. All off to start. |
| **Regular role** | The Discord role given to a Regular. None to start. |

Press **Save member milestones**.

## The posts

Each moment has its own switch and posts in the one channel you pick. A post is a card that names everyone who
reached that moment since the last one, as VRChat shows them, so forty people at a first event make one post. The
names are text on a card, so nobody is pinged.

Turning a post or the role on never acts for people who had reached the moment before. Only moments reached after
you turned it on are posted, and a moment is posted once for a person. If Discord asks the bot to wait, Modbot tries
again a short while later. If Discord refuses a post, Modbot does not send it again, and the card shows what went
wrong.

In the channel the bot needs **View Channels**, **Send Messages** and **Embed Links**.

## The Regular role

Modbot gives the role to a Regular who has a linked Discord account ([Account linking](/discord/account-linking))
and is still in the server. It never takes the role away. The role must be one the bot can give, and the card
refuses a role that holds a permission the [permission watch](/discord/permission-watch) tells staff about.

The bot needs **Manage Roles**, and its own role must sit above the Regular role. The **Bot invite link** in
[Setting up the bot](/discord/bot-setup#permissions-the-bot-needs) asks for **Manage Roles** once a Regular role is
saved; open the link again after you choose one.

## What Modbot keeps

For each person and date they came to, the VRChat user id, the event and the date. For each person, the time they
were last seen arriving in a group instance, the latest return, and which moments were already dealt with so
nothing is posted twice. Nothing about member milestones is sent to Modbot Cloud. What leaves your server is the
posts and the role, through Discord.

A [purge](/moderation/purge) removes all of this for the person, but can't remove a post already in Discord.
