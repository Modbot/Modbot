---
title: Role and ban sync
description: Keep VRChat group roles and Discord roles in step, and copy bans between the two, with a look at what would change before anything does.
---

Role and ban sync keeps your VRChat group and your Discord server in step with each other:

- a **role pair** says that one group role goes with one Discord role, and which side decides;
- **ban sync** copies a ban in one place into the other.

Everything here is **off** until you switch it on, and each direction is its own switch. A ban made
directly in VRChat and a ban made directly in Discord stay separate until you switch copying on.
The one exception is a ban made in Modbot, which reaches both places without any switch: see
[Bans made in Modbot](#bans-made-in-modbot).

<Callout type="warn">
This only ever touches people who have [linked their accounts](/discord/account-linking). Somebody
in your Discord server with no VRChat account tied to them is never given a role, never has one
taken away, and is never banned by this. That is most of a Discord server, and it stays that way.
</Callout>

## Before you start

You need the **Manage role and ban sync** permission to set any of this up, and **Run role and ban
sync** to copy what is already different.

The bot needs permissions in your Discord server, and only the ones you actually use:

| What you switch on | What the bot needs |
| --- | --- |
| Keep paired roles in step | Manage roles, and its own role above every role you pair |
| Copy group bans into Discord, as a ban | Ban members |
| Copy group bans into Discord, as a removal | Kick members |
| A ban or unban made in Modbot reaching Discord (no switch) | Ban members, which the invite link does not ask for: give it to the bot in Discord yourself |

Re-invite the bot with the link on **Settings → Discord → Account linking** after switching one on:
the link asks for exactly what your settings need.

A ban made in Modbot also needs **Ban members** to reach Discord, and no switch here asks the link
for it. If you want those bans to reach Discord, give the bot **Ban members** yourself; until you do,
the ban still stands in the group and Modbot tells you Discord refused.

## Pair a role

**Settings → Discord → Role and ban sync**.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Pick the two roles

Pick a **group role** and a **Discord role**. A role can be in one pair only.

</Step>

<Step>

### Choose who decides

| Who decides | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **VRChat** | The group role is the answer. The Discord role is given and taken away to match. |
| **Discord** | The Discord role is the answer. The group role is given and taken away to match. |
| **Nobody** | Nothing is changed. A difference is written to the audit log and nothing else. |

Start on **Nobody** if you are not sure. You can see the differences it finds in the audit log and
change your mind later.

</Step>

<Step>

### Switch role sync on

**Keep paired roles in step**, then **Save sync**. Modbot checks once a minute from then on.

</Step>

</Steps>

## When somebody unlinks

Unlinking takes back the **Discord** roles this sync gave them, within a minute. It only takes a role
Modbot's own records show it gave to that Discord account, and only while nothing has taken it back
since. A role somebody gave them by hand, or one they held before the sync ever ran, stays, and so
does a role on a pair you have switched off or removed.

The **group** role stays too: the person is still in your group. **Show what would change** lists
the roles it would take back, like any other change.

## Copy bans

Two switches, one for each direction, and a third that only appears while the second is on.

- **Copy group bans into Discord** — somebody banned from your VRChat group is banned in Discord, or
  removed from the server, whichever you pick.
- **Copy Discord bans into the group** — somebody banned in Discord is banned from your VRChat group.
- **Copy bans made by other bots** — shown while the switch above is on, and **off** by default.
  Bans made by another bot in your server (a moderation bot with rules of its own) are skipped,
  and so are the unbans it makes. Switch it on if you want those copied as well. Modbot's own bot is never copied, whatever this
  says.

A ban made by a person is copied whichever way that switch is set. When Modbot cannot tell who made
a ban, it treats it as a person's.

Each copied ban records the reason the moderator typed in Discord, when there was one, in the
**Ban copied from the other platform** entry of the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log). VRChat's
ban has nowhere to put a reason, so that entry is the only place it is kept.

Unbans follow the same switches. If group bans are copied into Discord, lifting a group ban lifts
the Discord one too.

<Callout type="warn">
A Discord ban is often for something said in chat, by a moderator with no say over your VRChat
group. Copying it into the group widens the punishment. Some communities want exactly that; decide
on purpose.
</Callout>

### Ban, or remove

A group ban can either **ban** the person in Discord or **remove them from the server**. A removal
can be rejoined, so a later group unban does nothing in Discord — the person simply comes back.

### What crosses over, and from when

Switching a direction on starts from that moment. Bans from before are not copied, so turning a
switch on cannot ban a hundred people at once. Use **Copy what is different** when you want the
older ones as well.

Modbot never copies a ban it made itself, in either direction.

## Bans made in Modbot

A ban or unban made **in Modbot**, by a moderator or by an [AutoMod](/moderation/automod) rule, is
made on both platforms: in your VRChat group, and on the person's linked Discord account, when
Discord is set up and they have [linked their accounts](/discord/account-linking). No switch on this
page is involved.

Somebody with no linked Discord account is banned in the group and nothing else happens. If Discord
refuses, for instance because the bot has no **Ban members**, the group ban still stands: the
confirmation says what Discord answered, and the audit log has a **A copy between platforms failed**
entry.

Modbot does not copy that Discord ban back into the group, or the group ban into Discord a second
time.

## See what would change first

**Show what would change** lists every difference and does nothing at all — no bans, no role
changes, nothing written down. Read it before you press anything else.

**Copy what is different** also lists every difference first, without changing anything, then turns
into a red **Copy it** and a **Cancel**. Press **Copy it** to make the changes, or **Cancel** to back
out. On a server that has been running for a while this is the press that moves the most people, so
look at the list first.

The first run only ever bans. It never lifts a ban, because "there is no ban here" and "the ban here
was lifted" look the same before Modbot has any history of its own.

## When something is wrong

The card shows when each half last ran and what went wrong.

| What it says | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The bot cannot assign a role | Give the bot Manage roles, and move its own role above that one in Discord |
| The bot may not ban in this server | Give the bot Ban members, and keep its role above the people it bans |
| The bot may not remove people from this server | Give the bot Kick members |

A ban that could not be copied because of a missing permission is copied on the next check once you
fix it.

## What goes in the audit log

Every copy is recorded against the person it happened to:

- **Ban copied from the other platform**, **Unban copied from the other platform**. A ban or unban
  made in Modbot and made in Discord as well is recorded the same way, and says who made it.
- **Removed from Discord for a VRChat ban**
- **Role given to match the other platform**, **Role taken away to match the other platform**

A copy that failed, and a difference found on a pair nobody decides, go in the operational log
instead.
