---
title: Birthdays
description: Let members tell the bot their birthday with /birthday, post the day's birthdays in a channel each day, and give a role for the day.
---

Members tell the bot their birthday with `/birthday`. Each day at the hour you choose, Modbot posts the
names of the members whose birthday it is in a channel you pick. It can also give those members a role for
the day.

Modbot keeps a month and a day. It never asks for the year, so it holds no age.

## Turn it on

You need the **Change settings** permission. The bot should be set up first
([Setting up the bot](/discord/bot-setup)).

`/birthday` is off until you turn it on. Open **Settings → Discord → Commands**, switch on `/birthday` and
press **Save commands**.

Then open **Settings → Discord** and find the **Birthdays** card.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **On** | Posts the day's birthdays. Off to start. |
| **Birthday channel** | Where the post goes. The bot needs to see the channel and send messages in it. |
| **At** | The hour of the day the post goes out. |
| **Time zone** | The time zone the hour and the day are in. UTC if you leave it empty. |
| **Message** | What the post says. `{members}` stands for the members with a birthday. Empty gives `Happy birthday {members}!`. |
| **Birthday role** | A role the members hold for their birthday. Optional. See [The birthday role](#the-birthday-role). |

Press **Save birthdays**. Turning it on, or moving the hour, never posts for a time that has already
passed: the first post is the next time the card names.

## What members type

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/birthday set month: day:` | Sets your birthday. Pick the month from the list and type the day. A date that does not exist, such as 30 February, is refused. |
| `/birthday show` | Tells you your birthday. Only you see the answer. |
| `/birthday remove` | Forgets your birthday. If you hold the birthday role, it comes off at once. |

Every answer is seen only by the member who ran the command. A member can run it five times a minute.

## The post

Once a day, at the hour on the card, Modbot posts one message that names every member whose birthday it
is. A post names at most 40 members; more are named in a second post.

- Members who have left the server are left out.
- Someone who set **29 February** is named on 28 February in a year with no 29th.
- The names are mentions, so they show as the members' names, but nobody is pinged.
- Modbot posts a day once, however often it restarts. If the bot is not connected when the hour comes, or
  Discord asks it to wait, Modbot tries again a few minutes later. If Discord refuses the post, Modbot does
  not send it again, and the card shows what went wrong.

## The birthday role

Pick a **Birthday role** and Modbot gives it to the members it names in the post. It takes the role back at
the hour of the next day, even if you have turned birthdays off or picked another role since.

- The bot needs **Manage Roles**, and its own role must sit above the birthday role. The card says so when
  it does not.
- A role that holds **Administrator** or another permission that
  [permission watch](/discord/permission-watch) tells you about is refused. So is `@everyone`, and so is a
  role that belongs to a bot or an integration.
- Roles Modbot gives are not reported by permission watch.

## What Modbot keeps

For each member who sets a birthday: their Discord id, the month and the day, when they set it, and, while
they hold the birthday role, which role and which day it was given. No name, no year and no age. It is kept
until the member removes it. [Purging a person](/moderation/purge) deletes it; if they still hold the
birthday role at that moment, the role comes off at the next daily check and the last of the row goes with it.

Nothing about birthdays is sent to Modbot Cloud. What leaves your server is the post in your channel, and the
role changes, through Discord.
