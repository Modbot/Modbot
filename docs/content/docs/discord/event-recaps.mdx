---
title: Event recaps
description: Post a recap in a Discord channel when an event's instance has closed, with who came, the most people there at once, a photo thread and the next date.
---

When the instance Modbot opened for a [calendar](/moderation/calendar) event has closed, Modbot posts a recap
in a channel you pick. Each date of an event that repeats gets its own recap.

## Turn it on

You need the **Change settings** permission. The bot should be set up first
([Setting up the bot](/discord/bot-setup)).

Open **Settings → Discord** and find the **Event recaps** card.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **On** | Posts a recap for each event instance that closes. Off to start. |
| **Recap channel** | Where the recap goes. |

Press **Save event recaps**. Turning it on never posts for an instance that closed before: only instances that
close after you turned it on get a recap.

## The permissions the bot needs

In the recap channel the bot needs **View Channels**, **Send Messages**, **Embed Links**, **Read Message History**
and **Create Public Threads**. Without **Create Public Threads** the recap still goes up, but with no photo
thread, and the card says so. The **Bot invite link** in
[Setting up the bot](/discord/bot-setup#permissions-the-bot-needs) asks for **Create Public Threads** once event
recaps are on; open the link again after you turn them on to give the bot the new permission.

## The recap

One post in the channel, as a card with the event's title:

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Came** | How many people were seen at the instance, with their names below, as VRChat shows them. |
| **Most at once** | The most people in the instance at one time. |
| **Next date** | When the event happens next, as a Discord time, so everyone sees their own time zone. Left out when the event does not repeat again, or is cancelled. |
| **Photo thread** | A public thread started on the post, named after the event and the date, for people to share their pictures. |

- A long list of names is cut short and ends with how many more came.
- The names are text on a card, so nobody is pinged.
- A date nobody was seen at, with no head count, gets no recap.

## When it posts

Modbot posts the recap when the group's instance list no longer shows the instance. If it never sees the
instance close, it posts 12 hours after the date was due to end.

A date gets one recap, however often Modbot restarts. If Discord asks the bot to wait, Modbot tries again a
short while later. If Discord refuses the post, Modbot does not send it again, and the card shows what went wrong.

## Who it counts

Turnout counts only the people in instances where a moderator's Modbot app was present. Somebody who was in the
instance while no moderator's Modbot was there is not counted, so the numbers can be lower than the real turnout.

## What Modbot keeps

For each date, the time its recap was posted, so it is not posted twice. The people and the head count come from
what Modbot already keeps about the instance. Nothing about recaps is sent to Modbot Cloud. What leaves your server
is the post and its thread in your channel, through Discord.
The post names the people who came as VRChat shows them, and a [purge](/moderation/purge) can't remove that post or its thread.
