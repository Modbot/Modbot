---
title: Server snapshots
description: Save your Discord server's roles, channels and permissions on a schedule or by hand, see what changed between two snapshots, download one as a file, and put one role's or one channel's permissions back.
---

A snapshot is a saved copy of your Discord server's roles, channels and permissions at one moment.
The **Discord** page's **Snapshots** tab lists them, shows what changed between two, downloads one,
and puts a role's or a channel's permissions back the way a snapshot had them. **Discord** stays lit
in the sidebar while it is open. The command palette finds it as **Discord snapshots**. Its address
is `/discord/snapshots`.

## Taking snapshots

Press **Take snapshot** to save one now. To have Modbot save them for you, pick **Weekly**, **Every 2
weeks** or **Monthly** under **Save** on the **Server snapshots** card in **Settings → Discord**. It
is **Off** until you do. Modbot checks every ten minutes: when the newest snapshot, however it was
taken, is older than one period (or there is none), it takes one. While the bot is not connected
nothing is taken, and the next check tries again. The tab shows the schedule and when the next one is
due.

Modbot keeps at most 100 snapshots. The schedule keeps its 24 newest and deletes older scheduled ones
as it takes new ones; snapshots taken by hand are never deleted by the schedule. Past 100, **Take
snapshot** is refused until you delete one. **Delete** asks you to confirm.

## What a snapshot holds

- **Roles:** name, colour, position, the server-wide permissions, whether it is shown separately in
  the member list, whether anyone can mention it, and whether Discord manages it (a bot's role).
- **Channels and categories:** name, type, category, position, whether it is age-restricted, and what
  each role's own settings allow and deny in it.

A snapshot holds no members and no Discord ids of people. Settings a channel has for a single member
are not saved, and a restore leaves them alone. Channels the bot cannot see are not in the snapshot.

## What changed

Under **What changed**, pick **From snapshot** and **To snapshot** (another snapshot, or **Now**, the
server as it is at this moment) and press **Compare**. The list names each role and channel that was
**Added**, **Removed** or **Changed** between the two, with one line for each difference, such as a
rename, a moved position or a permission allowed or denied. Roles and channels are matched by their
Discord id, so a rename shows as a change, not as one removed and one added.

## Download

**Download** on a row saves that snapshot as a JSON file: every role with its permissions in words,
and every channel with its role settings. It is the same for everyone who can open the tab.

## Restoring one role or one channel

Restore is **Off** until you turn on **Allow restore** on the **Server snapshots** card in
**Settings → Discord**. Then, under **Restore**, pick a snapshot, **A role** or **A channel**, and
which one, and press **Preview**. Modbot reads your server as it is now and lists the exact change a
restore would make, one line each. Nothing has changed yet. Press **Restore** to make it.

- For a role, a restore sets the role's server-wide permissions to the snapshot's.
- For a channel, a restore sets the channel's settings for each role to the snapshot's, and removes
  the settings for roles the snapshot did not have. A role that is gone from the server is skipped.
- It never makes a deleted role or channel again, never renames or moves anything, and never touches
  the settings for a single member.
- If the server changed between the preview and the press, the restore is refused with a message and
  the preview is drawn again from the new state.
- If nothing would change, the preview says **Nothing to change.**

Each restore is recorded in the audit log as **Discord permissions restored from a snapshot**, with the role or
channel and every line of the change.

## Permissions

| Who | Needs |
|---|---|
| Opening the tab, comparing, downloading | **See analytics** |
| Taking, deleting, restoring, and the card in **Settings → Discord** | **Change settings** |

| The bot needs | For |
|---|---|
| **Manage Roles** | Putting a role's permissions back, and a channel's settings for roles. Taking snapshots and comparing need no extra permission. |

Discord only lets the bot change roles below its own highest role, and only give permissions the bot
has itself. When a restore cannot be made for one of those reasons, the preview says what is
missing. The bot's invite link asks for Manage Roles only while **Allow restore** is on; after you
turn it on, open the **Bot invite link** again to give the bot the permission. See
[Permissions the bot needs](/discord/bot-setup#permissions-the-bot-needs).
