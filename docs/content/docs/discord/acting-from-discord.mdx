---
title: Acting from Discord
description: Look people up, write notes, and kick, ban or answer join requests from Discord, with the same permissions and records as the web app.
---

Moderators can act on what they see in Discord without opening Modbot: right-click a member, or
press a button under a card the bot posted. Everything happens as the moderator's own
Modbot account, with the same permissions, reasons and records as the web app.

Every reply, form and confirmation is visible only to the person who pressed. The one thing the
channel sees is a line above a card once somebody has acted on it, such as **Banned by alice**.

## Who can use it

The same people who can use the [slash commands](/discord/bot-setup#slash-commands): a moderator
who has pressed **Connect Discord** on their **Your account** page. Each action then needs the
permission it needs in the web app:

| What | Needs |
|---|---|
| **Look up in Modbot** | **See profiles** |
| **Add a note**, the **Add note** button | **Write notes** |
| **Kick** | **Kick** |
| **Ban** | **Ban** |
| **Approve**, **Reject** | **Answer join requests** |

Anything that writes — a note or an action — also needs a VRChat account linked to the moderator's
Modbot account, as the web app does. Without one the bot replies "Link your VRChat account in
Modbot first." Without the permission, the reply names it, for example
`You need the "Ban" permission in Modbot.`

Permissions are checked again when a form is sent and when a confirmation is pressed, so a
permission taken away in between stops the action.

## Right-click menus

Right-click a member, then **Apps**:

| Menu | On | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Look up in Modbot** | a member | A card about them. For a member who has linked their VRChat account, it is their VRChat profile, the same card `/lookup` shows, with **Add note**, **Kick** and **Ban** buttons for the ones you may use (no **Kick** or **Ban** for somebody already banned). For a member with no link, it shows their Discord name, **VRChat: Not linked**, how many notes they have, and how many timeouts, Discord bans and kicks Modbot has recorded, with an **Add note** button. |
| **Add a note** | a member | A form with one box. The note is written about their Discord account and shows on their person view in Modbot. |

The menus refuse Modbot's own Discord account.

### Who sees the menus

The menus are shown only to members with Discord's **Timeout Members** permission. To change that,
open your server's **Server Settings → Integrations**, pick the bot, and choose the roles or members
for each menu. This only changes who sees them: Modbot still checks the moderator's Modbot
permissions on every press.

## Buttons under cards

When an [event channel](/discord/event-channels) posts a message with one card in it, the card gets
buttons for the person it is about:

| Card | Buttons |
|---|---|
| Something a VRChat person did or had done to them: joined, left, warned, kicked, role changes, profile changes, flags, notes | **Add note**, **Kick**, **Ban** |
| A ban | **Add note**, and **Open case** when a case file covers the ban and a public address is set |
| **Asked to join the group** | **Approve**, **Reject**, **Add note** |
| Something that happened to a Discord account: joined, left, timed out, banned from Discord | **Add note** |
| Anything else: instances, the group, settings | none |

A message that holds several cards, which happens when many events arrive at once, has no buttons:
Discord puts buttons under the message, so they could not say which card they are for.

Everybody who can read the channel sees the buttons. Pressing one without the permission is refused.

## Kick, ban, approve and reject

None of these happen on one press.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Press the button

**Kick**, **Ban** and **Reject** open a form with the group's [reasons](/moderation/case-files#the-reason-list)
for that action and a note. A ban always needs a reason; a kick or a rejection needs one when your
group has switched on **Require a reason on kicks, unbans and rejected join requests**. Discord
shows at most 25 reasons, the first 25 in your list's order. **Approve** has no form.

</Step>

<Step>

### Send the form

Modbot checks everything it checks in the web app before anything is sent: the reasons are on the
list and ticked for this action, a reason that needs a note has one, and the person is not the
account Modbot signs in as. Anything wrong is said now.

</Step>

<Step>

### Confirm

A message asks, for example, "Ban **jessie** from the group?", with the reasons and the note, a red
**Ban** and a **Cancel**. Only you can press it, and it runs out after 15 minutes, or if Modbot
restarts; press the card's button again to start over.

</Step>

</Steps>

Once VRChat answers, the confirmation says what happened. **Nothing is recorded unless VRChat
accepted it**, and a refusal says what VRChat said. A ban writes its case file and bans the
person's linked Discord account, exactly as a ban in the web app does.

One confirmation acts once. Pressing it twice, or from two devices, sends one action and answers
the second press with the first answer.

When the action was started from a card, the card gets a line above it, such as
**Banned by alice**, and loses its **Kick**, **Ban**, **Approve** and **Reject** buttons. **Add note**
stays.

## What is recorded

Notes and actions from Discord are the same records as from the web app: the same note, the same
kick or ban in the audit log under the moderator's name, the same case file. Every menu, button and
form that finishes or is refused is also recorded as **Discord command used**, which shows that it
came through Discord.
