[Account linking](/discord/account-linking) only helps for the people who have done it. The
**Discord** page's **Account links** tab shows how many have, and lets staff send the rest one
reminder. **Discord** stays lit in the sidebar while it is open. The command palette finds it as
**Discord account links**. Its address is `/discord/account-links`.

Seeing the tab needs **See analytics**. Starting and stopping the reminder needs **Change settings**.
The tab shows counts only; it never names anyone.

## The two numbers

| Number | Counts |
|---|---|
| **Group members with a linked Discord account** | The people in your VRChat group now, and how many of them have a Discord account linked. |
| **Discord members with a linked VRChat account** | The people in your Discord server now, and how many of them have a VRChat account linked. Bots are not counted. |

Each shows a percentage and "N of M". A link that has been ended with **Unlink** does not count.

The tab says **Account linking is not set up.** until the OAuth client, its secret and the public
address are saved. See [Account linking](/discord/account-linking#set-it-up).

## The link reminder

The **Link reminder** panel sends each Discord member who has not linked one direct message with a
**Link VRChat account** button that opens your [link page](/discord/account-linking#how-a-member-links).
It reads: "Link your VRChat account to" your server's name "with the button below."

Press **Start reminder** to begin and **Stop reminder** to stop it. The panel shows:

| Row | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Would be sent** / **Left to send** | How many members it would reach now, or how many are left of those it began with. |
| **Sent, ever** | Members it has been sent to. |
| **Not reached, ever** | Members Discord would not let it reach, because their direct messages are closed or the account is gone. |
| **Not confirmed, ever** | Members whose message may or may not have arrived: Discord timed out or failed on its side, or Modbot stopped before it heard back. |
| **Last sent** | When the latest message went out. |

How it behaves:

- **Slow on purpose.** One message goes out every 30 seconds, because a burst of direct messages from
  a bot is what Discord punishes. A server with 3,000 members to remind takes about a day. The pace is
  kept from the time of the last message, so restarting Modbot does not send one at once.
- **Once per person, ever.** Modbot keeps a note for each member it has reminded, or tried to. Stopping
  and starting again, or starting it again next year, never sends the same person a second reminder.
  A member whose direct messages are closed is marked **Not reached** and is not tried again.
- **Only people already in the server when you press Start.** Anyone who joins later is asked by the
  [join prompt](/discord/account-linking#asking-new-joiners-to-link) or the
  [welcome button](#the-welcome-button) instead.
- **Not for the linked, bots or people who left.** A member who unlinked since is reminded once like
  anyone else.
- **Never sent again, whatever happened.** If Discord times out or fails on its side, the message may
  already have arrived, so the member is counted under **Not confirmed** and is not tried again. A
  missed reminder is better than a second one.
- **It stops by itself** when everyone has been dealt with. It also stops, at once, if Discord asks the
  bot to slow down: the tab says **The reminder stopped** with Discord's words, the member it was
  about to message is counted under **Not reached** and is not tried again, and nothing goes out until
  you press **Start reminder** again.
- **Start is refused** when linking is not set up, no Discord server is saved, or nobody is waiting.

Each message is recorded as **Asked to link on Discord**, and starting or stopping the reminder is in
the audit log as a settings change.

The bot needs the **Server Members Intent** to know who is in the server (see
[Intents the bot asks for](/discord/bot-setup#intents-the-bot-asks-for)).

## The welcome button

On **Settings → Discord**, the **Welcome steps** card has a switch, **Add a Link your VRChat button**.
It is off until you turn it on. When it is on, the [welcome message](/discord/welcome-steps) gets one
more button, **Link your VRChat**, after the step buttons, for a member who has not linked. It goes
with the direct message and with the channel post. Nothing is added when linking is not set up or the
member has already linked.

## What it replaces

If you ran a separate VRChat Linking bot, Modbot covers the same ground: a link page where members
sign in with Discord and prove their VRChat account with a code in their bio, a linked role and an 18+
role, the `/link` command, a join prompt with a backup channel, and the share numbers and one-time
reminder on this page.

## What Modbot keeps

For each member reminded, one row: their Discord id, whether the message was sent or could not reach
them, when, and, if it failed, Discord's reason as a sentence. It is kept so nobody is reminded twice.
A [purge](/moderation/purge) deletes it, and no retention window touches it. See
[Privacy](/privacy). Nothing is sent to Modbot Cloud.
