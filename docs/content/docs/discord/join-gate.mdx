---
title: Join gate
description: Let new members into your Discord server only once they have agreed to your rules, and if you like linked their VRChat account or shown they are 18+ on VRChat. Remove the ones who never finish.
---

The join gate decides when somebody who joins your Discord server gets in. Until they do the steps
you ask for, they see one channel, the **gate channel**. When they are done, the bot gives them the
**member role**, the role that opens the rest of the server.

It can replace a captcha bot. It is not a captcha: a button does not prove a person is human.
Discord's own verification level and Raid Protection keep doing that job alongside it.

Everything is off until you turn it on.

## Set it up

You need the **Change settings** permission. The bot should be set up first
([Setting up the bot](/discord/bot-setup)).

<Steps>

<Step>

### Make the member role and the gate channel in Discord

In your server's settings, give `@everyone` access to one channel only, the gate channel, and give
the member role access to the rest. Modbot never changes channel permissions; this is yours to set.

The bot needs **Manage Roles**, and its own role must sit above the member role in **Server Settings
→ Roles**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Fill in the Join gate card

Open **Settings → Discord** and find the **Join gate** card.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Join gate** | **Off**, **Watch only** or **On**. See [Running it beside another bot](#running-it-beside-another-bot) for Watch only. |
| **Member role** | The role the bot gives when somebody is done. A role the bot cannot give is marked **Bot cannot assign** and is not saved. |
| **Gate channel** | Where the bot posts one message with a **Get in** button, and keeps it there. |
| **Message** | Your own words above the button, such as your rules. Empty shows your server's name. |
| **Link VRChat account** | A step: link a VRChat account on the [link page](/discord/account-linking). Needs account linking to be set up. |
| **18+ on VRChat** | A step: the linked VRChat account is marked 18+ verified. Needs **Link VRChat account**. |
| **Remove after** | How long somebody may take before the bot removes them: **Never**, 30 minutes, 1, 6, 12 or 24 hours, or 2, 3 or 7 days. |
| **Hold new joiners on a join spike** | See [Join spikes](#join-spikes). |
| **Allow pausing invites** | See [Join spikes](#join-spikes). Needs **Manage Server** for the bot. |

**I agree** is always a step. Press **Save join gate**.

</Step>

</Steps>

Only people who join after you turn the gate on are gated. Everybody already in your server is left
alone.

## What a new member sees

1. The bot sends them a direct message, "Welcome to" your server, with a **Get in** button. If they
   do not accept direct messages, the bot mentions them once in the gate channel instead.
2. **Get in** (in the message, or in the gate channel) shows them, privately, each step and whether
   it is done: **Rules**, and if you ask for them **VRChat account** and **18+**. Buttons for what is
   left: **I agree**, **Link VRChat account**, and **Check** to look again after linking.
3. When the last step is done, they get the member role and see "You're in."

If your server has Discord's own rules screening, they accept that first; the bot waits for it.
Somebody who got in through the gate before and comes back has already agreed.

While the gate is on, it sends this message instead of the
[account linking prompt](/discord/account-linking#asking-new-joiners-to-link), so a new member gets
one message, not two.

## Not finishing

With **Remove after** set, the bot warns somebody once, halfway through: a direct message saying when
they will be removed, with the **Get in** button. At the end, the bot removes them from the server.
They are not banned, and can join again.

**Time only counts while the hold-up is theirs.** It stops while:

- the bot is offline, or Modbot is down;
- the bot cannot give the member role (it lost Manage Roles, or the role moved above the bot's);
- VRChat is refusing Modbot's requests, for a gate that asks for a linked account;
- they have done every step and are waiting on a hold.

So nobody is removed because Modbot could not let them in. When Modbot is down, new members wait in
the gate channel; nobody gets in and nobody is removed until it is back.

The bot removes at most 10 people a minute and stops removing once 30 were removed in an hour. The
**Discord bot** card on **Health** then says so.

## Seeing who is waiting

**Discord → Members** shows an **At the gate** card above the member list while the gate is not
off: who is waiting, when they joined, which steps they have done, and when they will be removed.

With the **Manage the join gate** permission you also see:

- **Let in**: gives them the member role now, whatever steps they have left.
- **Remove**: removes them from the server now.
- **Hold new joiners**, or **Lift hold** while they are held.
- **Pause invites**, when allowed.

Seeing the card needs **See members**; whether each person has linked needs **See profiles** as
well. **Manage the join gate** is not in the built-in Moderator or Viewer roles.

**Giving the member role by hand in Discord counts as let in.** The person leaves the list, recorded
as let in in Discord.

## Join spikes

When the **People joining Discord** [alert](/ai/alerts) fires, the alert is posted as usual and the
gate keeps working. What else happens is your choice:

- With **Hold new joiners on a join spike** on, the bot holds new joiners by itself: nobody gets the
  member role until staff lift the hold. With **Allow pausing invites** on as well, it also pauses
  your server's invites for 24 hours, the most Discord allows.
- With it off, the alert's Discord post carries a **Hold new joiners** button, and **Pause invites**
  when allowed, for staff to press.

The hold is lifted with **Lift hold**, on the At the gate card or on the bot's reply after a hold.
Everybody who is done then gets in within a minute. A button pressed in Discord counts as the Modbot
account that connected that Discord account, and needs **Manage the join gate**.

## Running it beside another bot

**Watch only** does nothing in Discord: no message, no role, no removal, no hold. It records who
joined, which steps they did not do, when they would have been warned and removed, and whether they
got the member role some other way. Run it next to your old captcha bot for a while and compare,
with the same member role on both.

When you are happy, set the gate to **On**, then turn the old bot's verification off in its own
dashboard. Changing the mode ends everybody's current time at the gate, so Watch only never turns
into removals.

## What is recorded

These appear in the audit log and can be sent to [event channels](/discord/event-channels):

| Event | When |
|---|---|
| **Passed the join gate** | Somebody did the steps and got the member role. |
| **Let in at the join gate** | A moderator let them in from Modbot, or they got the member role in Discord. |
| **Removed at the join gate** | The bot removed them for not finishing, or a moderator removed them. |
| **Warned at the join gate** | They were warned halfway. |
| **New joiners held** | A moderator, or a join spike, held new joiners. |
| **Hold on new joiners lifted** | A moderator lifted the hold. |
| **Discord invites paused** | A moderator, or a join spike, paused the server's invites. |

For each person at the gate Modbot keeps their Discord id and name, when they joined, when they
agreed, how long has counted against them, when they were warned, and how it ended.

## If something is wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Nobody gets the member role | Give the bot **Manage Roles** and move its role above the member role. **Health** says why. |
| There is no message in the gate channel | Check the bot has View Channel, Send Messages, Embed Links and Read Message History there, and that the gate is **On**. |
| **Link VRChat account** cannot be turned on | Set up [account linking](/discord/account-linking) first. |
| New members are not seen | Turn on the **Server Members Intent** (see [Intents the bot asks for](/discord/bot-setup#intents-the-bot-asks-for)). |
