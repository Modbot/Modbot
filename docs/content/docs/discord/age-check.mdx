---
title: 18+ check
description: Mark a member 18+ verified with /ageverified in a ticket or from their profile, give and take away the roles you chose, and delete the ticket afterwards.
---

A member who shows their VRChat 18+ badge, or a photo ID in a ticket, is checked once and marked **18+
verified** in Modbot. The same step gives and takes away the roles you chose in Discord, gives a VRChat
group role, and can delete the ticket.

Modbot never sees the photo ID. It keeps the **method** (badge or photo ID), the **date** and who did
the check, and nothing else: no image, no text from it and no number from it.

## Turn it on

You need the **Change settings** permission. The bot should be set up first
([Setting up the bot](/discord/bot-setup)).

`/ageverified` is off until you turn it on. Open **Settings → Discord → Commands**, switch on
`/ageverified` and press **Save commands**.

Then open **Settings → Discord** and find the **18+ check** card.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Discord roles to give** | Roles the member is given. Add them one at a time; press **×** on a role to take it out of the list. Roles that hold a permission the [permission watch](/discord/permission-watch) warns about are not offered. |
| **Discord roles to take away** | Roles the member loses, such as a waiting role. The **Linked role** and the **18+ role** from [Account linking](/discord/account-linking) cannot be taken away here, because Modbot gives them back. |
| **VRChat group role to give** | A role of your VRChat group the member's VRChat account is given. Optional. |
| **Delete the ticket afterwards** | Deletes the ticket once the check is done. Off to start. |
| **Ticket category** | A Discord category whose channels may be deleted. Optional. |

A role can be in one list only. The bot needs **Manage Roles**, and its own highest role must sit above
every role it gives or takes away. A Discord role that is also part of a pair in
[Role and ban sync](/discord/role-and-ban-sync) is kept in step by that pair, so leave those roles out
of both lists.

## Check someone in a ticket

In the ticket, run `/ageverified member: method:`.

| Option | What it takes |
|---|---|
| `member` | The Discord member, picked from Discord's list. |
| `method` | **Badge** or **Photo ID**. |
| `vrchat` | Their VRChat profile link. Needed only when the member has not [linked their accounts](/discord/account-linking); a member who has is checked as the account they linked. |

Modbot then:

1. Marks the VRChat account **18+ verified** and writes the check to the audit log, with the method and
   your Modbot account as the checker.
2. Gives and takes away the Discord roles you chose.
3. Gives the VRChat group role, if you chose one.
4. Answers with one line for what it did and one line for each thing it could not do. A role that fails
   does not undo the check: the member stays marked.
5. Deletes the ticket, if **Delete the ticket afterwards** is on. The answer is sent first.

A member with no linked account gets both the Discord roles and the VRChat group role, from the
VRChat profile link you typed. If a member has linked their accounts and you type a different VRChat
profile, Modbot refuses and changes nothing.

You need the **Edit 18+ verified** permission and a VRChat account linked to your Modbot account, and
your Discord account must be [connected to your Modbot account](/discord/bot-setup#letting-moderators-use-the-commands).
The command is shown to members who may time others out.

### What is deleted

Modbot deletes only a ticket thread that Modbot opened, or a channel directly inside the
**Ticket category** you chose. Run in any other channel, the command checks the member and leaves the
channel alone, and says so. To delete a channel in the category, the bot needs **Manage Channels**.

A ticket thread deleted this way is logged like one deleted with the ticket's **Delete** button, with
you as the person who deleted it.

## Check someone from their profile

On a person's profile, open the **Not seen as 18+ verified** badge and press **Mark 18+ verified**. It
asks **Badge** or **Photo ID**, then does the same as `/ageverified`, except that it does not delete a
ticket. The Discord roles go to the member linked to that VRChat account; with no one linked, only the
mark and the VRChat group role are done, and the answer says so.

**Clear flag** still asks for a reason and does nothing else: it gives and takes away no roles. See
[The 18+ verified mark](/moderation/profiles#the-18-verified-mark).
