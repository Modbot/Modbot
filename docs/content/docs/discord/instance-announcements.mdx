---
title: Instance announcements
description: Post a card in a Discord channel for each instance your group opens, kept up to date while it runs.
---

Instance announcements turn a Discord channel into a notice board for your group's open instances.
When the group opens an instance, the bot posts one card for it. The card is updated as people
come and go, and updated one last time when the instance closes.

This is meant for your members ("we are in here, come along"), so it usually goes in a different
channel from the [event channels](/discord/event-channels) your team reads.

## Set it up

You need the **Change settings** permission, and the bot must be connected (see
[Setting up the bot](/discord/bot-setup)).

1. Open **Settings → Discord** and find the **Instance announcements** card.
2. Pick a channel in **Announce open instances in this channel**.
3. Optionally, write a **Message above each announcement**, for example "Come and join us!". It is
   posted above every card. It can be up to 2,000 characters. Mentions in it do not ping anyone.
4. Choose whether to **Show names** (see [Names on the card](#names-on-the-card)). It is on by default.
5. Press **Save**.

To stop announcing, set the channel to **None** and save.

## Which instances are announced

- **Only your group's own instances.** An instance appears when it shows up in the group's list of
  open instances. A moderator's own private or public instance is never announced.
- **Only instances that opened in the last 15 minutes.** Turning announcements on, or Modbot
  starting up again after being down, does not post cards for instances that have already been
  running for a while.
- An instance that closes before its card was posted gets no card.

## What the card shows

While the instance is open, the card's title is the world's name, and the card shows:

- **People here now**, and **Most at once** when more people were there earlier;
- **Open for**, such as `2h 14m`;
- **Who can join**: **Group members**, **Members and their friends** or **Anyone**;
- **Region**, the world's **Capacity** when Modbot knows it, and the **Instance** number;
- **Who is here**, when names are shown (below);
- the world's picture, when Modbot has it;
- a **Join** button. It opens VRChat's launch page for that instance. VRChat still checks who is
  allowed in, so the button lets in only the people who could already join.

The card is green while someone is in the instance and grey while it is empty.

When the instance closes, the card changes to **This instance has closed.**, shows
**People at the end** and **Ran for**, and loses its **Join** button. After that it is never
changed again.

Cards are updated at most once a minute each, so the numbers can be up to a minute behind.

## Names on the card

Modbot only knows who is inside an instance while someone from your team is in it with the
[companion](/companion/install) running. While that is true and **Show names** is on,
the card lists the display names of the people there under **Who is here**: up to 20 names, then
"and N more".

When nobody from the team is in the instance, or **Show names** is off, the card shows only the
head count. Turn **Show names** off if your community would rather not have names posted.

## What the bot needs in the channel

The bot needs **View Channel**, **Send Messages**, **Embed Links** and **Read Message History** in
the announcement channel. Read Message History is needed because the bot has to fetch its own card
again each time it updates it. The channel picker marks a channel where any of these is missing.

## Good to know

- If you change the channel, cards already posted stay in the old channel and keep being updated
  there until their instances close. New instances go to the new channel.
- If someone deletes a card in Discord, or the bot loses access to the channel, Modbot stops
  updating that card and does not post it again.
- If Discord refuses a post, Modbot waits two minutes and tries again.
