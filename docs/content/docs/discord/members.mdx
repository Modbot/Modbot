title: Discord members
description: The Discord server's member list, and the Discord person popup with a member's details, history, messages and activity.
---

The **Discord members** page lists the people in your Discord server, as the bot sees them. It sits
in the sidebar just below **Members**, which is your VRChat group's list. They are separate lists
because most people are in one place and not the other, and most never
[link their accounts](/discord/account-linking).

The page needs **See members**. The bot must be set up and connected
([Setting up the bot](/discord/bot-setup)).

## The list

Above the list, Modbot says when it last read the member list and how many people are in the
server. Instead, you may see:

- **No Discord server set.** — no **Guild id** is saved on the **Bot** card.
- **The Discord member list has not been read yet.** — the bot has not read the full list. Until it
  has, the list only holds people the bot has seen some other way. Reading the list needs the
  **Server Members Intent** (see [Intents the bot asks for](/discord/bot-setup#intents-the-bot-asks-for)).

### Search and filters

- **Search by name or id** — matches the server nickname, the Discord display name, the username
  and the user id. Capital letters, accents and fancy lettering do not matter (see
  [Names](/moderation/names)).
- **Status** — **In server** (the default), **Left**, or **All**.
- **Role** — **Any role**, or one of your server's Discord roles.
- **Linked** — **All**, **Linked** or **Not linked**, by whether the person has linked a VRChat
  account. Only shown to people with **See profiles**.

The number of people matching is shown on the right. Newest joiners come first, 50 to a page, with
**Previous** and **Next** at the bottom.

### Columns

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Person** | Picture and the name the server shows: nickname, else display name, else username. Bots are marked **bot**. |
| **Username** | The Discord username. |
| **Roles** | The Discord roles they hold. |
| **Joined** | When they joined the server. |
| **Left** | When the bot saw them leave. Only shown when **Status** is **Left** or **All**. People who left are greyed out. |
| **Timed out until** | When a timeout ends, if they are timed out now. |
| **VRChat** | The VRChat account they linked, if any. Click it to open that VRChat person's popup. Only shown to people with **See profiles**. |

Click a row to open the Discord person popup.

## The Discord person popup

The **Discord person** popup shows one Discord account. It opens from a row on **Discord members**,
and from a Discord person's name anywhere else in Modbot: an audit log entry, **My Server**, or the
**Discord** card in a VRChat person's popup.

It is a separate popup from the VRChat person popup, even for someone who has linked. Each shows
its own account's history and points to the other when there is a link. There is no case files tab,
because case files are written for VRChat bans.

The popup's subtitle is the Discord user id.

### Left side

**Who they are in the server** (needs **See members**):

- picture, server name, Discord display name and `@username`;
- marks: **In server** or **Left**, **Bot**, **Pending** (they have not passed the server's
  membership screening yet) and **Timed out**;
- **Joined**, **Left**, **Timed out until** and **Boosting since**, when they apply;
- **Roles**.

If the bot never saw the person in the server, it says **Not seen in the server.** This happens for
someone named in an event who was never a member.

**VRChat** card (needs **See profiles**): the VRChat account they linked, with when they linked.
Click the name to open the VRChat person popup. People with **Manage Discord links** see
**Unlink** here (see [Unlinking](/discord/account-linking#unlinking)). The card is not shown when
there is no link.

### Tabs

| Tab | Needs | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Logs** | **See the audit log** or **See the operational log** | The latest 50 events about this account or done by it, newest first. You see the kinds of event your permissions allow in the [audit log](/moderation/audit-log). |
| **Messages** | **Read Discord messages** | Everything the person wrote that Modbot has stored, deleted messages included. See [Who can read messages](/discord/messages-and-my-server#who-can-read-messages). |
| **Metrics** | **See profiles** | Their activity, below. |

**Messages** and **Metrics** are hidden from people without their permission. **Logs** is always
there, but only loads for someone who can see the audit log.

### Metrics

- **Activity** — **Messages, 30 days**, **Voice, 30 days**, **Messages, all time**,
  **Voice, all time**, **First seen** (when Modbot first saw them in the server) and **Joined**.
- **Messages per day** and **Voice minutes per day** — each of the last 30 days.
- **Joined and left** — each time they joined or left the server that Modbot still has on record.
  When Modbot only found out afterwards, for example while the bot was offline, it shows the window
  the change happened in.

The counts come from Modbot's daily totals, so **all time** can go back further than the stored
messages do.
